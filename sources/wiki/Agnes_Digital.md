I love Umamusume! A super versatile otaku.

A veritable Umamusume otaku. She came to Tracen Academy to be close to all of her favorite runners. She does respect boundaries, however, and tries to stay out of trouble. She's eager to get up close and personal with as many Umamusume as possible, and to that end she has revealed surprising ability on both dirt and turf.

## Appearance

Agnes Digital wears her bubblegum-colored hair in two half-up pigtails that come to a curl at the end, while the rest of her hair lays straight down her back. Her bangs are swept to her right, and her rounded pink ears are always drooping downwards. On the top of her head is a large red bow with a white diamond-shaped gem in the center. Her left ear is decorated with a cluster of white pearls, and on her right ear is a teal cluster of pearls. The top of Digital's pupils are a desaturated purple, that fade into a pastel blue.

Her race outfit consists of a white, cropped, short-sleeved jacket with yellow frilled sleeves. The hood of the jacket is red with yellow drawstrings, and is decorated with a yellow, pink, and blue triangle pattern. Underneath the jacket, she is wearing a white lacy tube top, laced with a baby blue ribbon. Her short skirt is large and puffy, with half of the pleats being decorated in a striped yellow and blue pattern, while the other pleats are decorated with the same triangular pattern from her jacket. In between these pleats is polka-dotted blue and yellow fabric, and the edge of the skirt has white frills, and a yellow half-petticoat underneath. The skirt is held up with a bright red belt, and is adorned with a giant red ribbon with a yellow, and blue striped star in the center. Underneath the skirt, she wears short white bloomers with red trim on the legs. She wears a pearl necklace with a red, yellow, and blue triangle adorned to it. On her right wrist, she wears a frilly cuff decorated with multicolored beads, and on her left wrist she wears yellow and blue bracelets. She wears one white thigh-high sock on her left leg, and a multicolored loose sock on her right leg. Her shoes are white sneakers with red and yellow trims, tied up with bright cyan laces. Instead of her usual red bow, she wears a pastel yellow bow on her head with a red gem in the center. Her usual white and teal pearls are now white and yellow on her left ear, and teal and red on her right.

## Relationships

* Agnes Tachyon - Friend and roommate. Many Umamusume fear this room pairing as they are already eccentric individually. Tachyon finds Digital's odd behavior intriguing, while Digital admires Tachyon. 
** IRL, they are owned by Takao Watanabe. Agnes Digital (1997–2021) was one year older and never raced against Tachyon due to the latter's early retirement.
* Air Shakur - Friend of the same generation. 
* Meisho Doto - Friend and upperclassman. Doto is one of Digital's seniors that she particularly idolizes as an oshi due to being a year above her. Digital helps assuage Doto's nervousness and low confidence.
* T.M. Opera O - Friend and upperclassman. Opera O basks in the adoration that Digital heaps upon all Umamusume and Digital often gets involved in projects based around improving Opera O's public standing.
** In real life, Digital would beat both Opera O and Doto in the 2001 G1 Tenno Sho Autumn, claiming his first 2000m victory. 
* King Halo - Friend and upperclassman. King often tries to help Digital, only for it to turn into a tutoring session. Digital admires King greatly, and even helps King's fanbase cheer for her properly. While King is worried that she lost her fighting spirit in races, Digital continues to support her, because she loves how hard she works and how she does not give up. Though King initially thinks that Digital is blindly worshiping her, King eventually befriends her, and they hang out with each other on occasion.
** In real life, the two would race against each other at the 2000 Mile Championship, which Digital unexpectedly won in an upset victory. 
* Tap Dance City - Friend of the same generation. Tap wants Digital to live in her castle in the future.
** In real life, the two raced against each other at the 2003 Takarazuka Kinen, with Tap Dance City finishing 3rd while Digital finished 13th. 
* Haru Urara - Friend. Digital finds Urara's naivete adorable, and likes being around her. 
* Zenno Rob Roy - Friend. Both of them like to read, and they get along well. Digital also notices Rob Roy while others don't, which surprises Rob Roy. 
* Chrono Genesis - Friend. They both get along for their love for race history, albeit they have very different reasons for being invested. 
* Smart Falcon - Friend. Digital admires Falco as an Umadol, and supports her at her concerts. Falco notices her as a fan of hers, and Falco thinks that Digital's bow is cute, much to the latter's shock. 
* Loves Only You - Friend. Digital likes to watch Loves' streams, and she uses an anonymous online username to watch them. Loves eventually picks up on who she is, and appreciates her as a fan. 
* Special Week - Friend and upperclassman. To Digital's embarrassment, Spe is overtly curious in the works Digital makes. 
* Shinko Windy - Friend. Though Windy tries to scare her, Digital is unbothered by her, and in fact finds her adorable. This scares and frustrates Windy, and she gets mad when Digital is enamored by her.

## Trivia

* Her initial character profile was uploaded to the official [https://x.com/uma_musu/status/875547352538284032 Twitter] account on June 15, 2017.
** This tweet was a character name prediction quiz, that encouraged fans to try and guess which horse she's named after. The hint was "She is an all-rounder who can handle any race, any environment, and even the most demanding schedules with ease. She is beautiful girl with a rather unfortunate personality."
* In Loves Only You's solo song, (Oyasumi Haishin) Minna to Love Hojuu Shita~i, Digital makes an appearance at the 2:51 mark.
* In chapter 4 of her character story, when the trainer mentions the idea of being an "all-rounder" to her, she yells "AND IIIIIII!" before quickly stopping. This is a reference to the 1974 song "I Will Always Love You" by Dolly Parton. In the Global version, this reference is kept, though the line is changed to "AND I WILL ALWAYS LOVE UMAS!".
** However, considering how Agnes Digital screams the line out loud, it is likely a reference to the 1992 cover sung by Whitney Houston instead of Dolly's original. As Whitney sung the song with a powerful tone, while the original song was sung softly.
* During her career, when she wins the Mile Championship Nambu Hai, she tells the audience that their support allowed her to assume her power level 530,000 final form. This is a reference to Frieza from Dragon Ball, though the power level in question is for Frieza's first form rather than his final form.
* In the flavor text of her SSR Power card {{Game Support|30297}}, there are online fans of Digital talking about her and racing career. All of their usernames are named after her IRL counterpart's major wins.
** ＠Mal_pip1119 - 2000 Mile Championship (G1)
*** "Mal" and "pip" hint at "Mile Championship", while "1119" is the date, November 19th.
** ＠108reimentabetai - 2001 Mile Championship Nambu Hai (JpnI)
*** "108" is the date, October 8th. "reimentabetai" means "I want to eat cold noodles" (reimen), a local specialty dish in Morioka, where the race takes place.
** ＠ten-aki01 - 2001 Tenno Sho (Autumn) (G1)
*** "ten" for Tenno Sho, and "aki" means autumn. "01" marks the year 2001.
** ＠tensin-1216 - 2001 Hong Kong Cup (G1)
*** "tensin" is a romanization of the Sha Tin racecourse, where the Hong Kong Cup takes place. "1216" is December 16th, the date of the 2001 Hong Kong Cup.
** ＠02217February - 2002 February Stakes (G1)
*** "022117" gives the date, February 17th 2002. "February" references the races name, the February Stakes.
** ＠68YS03 - 2003 Yasuda Kinen (G1)
*** "68" reads as "ya-su", and together with "YS" both reference Yasuda. "03" marks the year 2003.
* She designed her racewear so that it wouldn't stand out compared to other Umamusume.
* Unlike the majority of the other careers in the game, when Quick Mode is used on Agnes Digital's trainee scenarios, instead of the player, Agnes Digital will narrate her own career from her perspective.
* Despite having a visible marking in real life, this was not adapted in her Umamusume design.
* Agnes Digital's Valentines chocolate teased the future release of Jungle Pocket at the time. The chocolates in the box on top are designed after Admire Vega, Narita Top Road, Meisho Doto and T.M. Opera O respectively. Underneath it is a second box with chocolate representing Agnes Tachyon and Manhattan Cafe, with an obscured third piece, hinting at the 2001 Classics.
* Her father running a printing company is a nod to real life, in which Agnes Digital's owner, Takao Watanabe, owned a printing business.
