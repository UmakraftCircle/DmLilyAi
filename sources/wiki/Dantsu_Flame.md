## Appearance

Dantsu Flame wears her rosy-brown short hair in a half-up ponytail, with two ahoges on the top of her head. She has blunt bangs, with white side bangs on both sides of her face. On her ears, she wears two pink covers with a silver trim, and there is a green and pink checkered ribbon on her right ear. Her eyes are round with amber-colored pupils.

In her race outfit, she's sporting a pink undershirt, with a white collar fastened with a large golden zipper, and a white ribbon tied at the bottom. On top of this is a black long sleeved top with a pink checkered pattern going across the right sleeve, and a green checkered pattern on the left. The sleeves are rolled up, and have a pink and green checkered pattern. Underneath the hoodie sleeves are the black sleeves of a compression shirt. She wears a pair of black joggers with pink stripes going down the side, and on top of this, a pair of pink shorts with a white belt. The belt has pink and green tassels coming down either side, and the belt has a golden checkered buckle. Her shoes are plain pink and white sneakers.

## Trivia

* She was announced during the 3rd Anniversary PakaLive TV stream on February 22, 2024.
** Among all who was introduced during the stream, she was the last one to receive a playable version.
* During 5th EVENT ARENA TOUR GO BEYOND -NEW GATE- Day 2, her voice actress, Haruna Fukushima, revealed that she used to be a backup dancer for Sprinters' Story before she was cast as Dantsu Flame.
* During 6th EVENT The New Frontier, she was announced to be the next playable trainee during the event. This is the first time a playable Umamusume was announced in the middle of a major live event.
* The quartet consisting of Dantsu Flame, Jungle Pocket, Agnes Tachyon, and Manhattan Cafe is often referred to as "JAMD."
* She really likes dairy products, especially cheese.
** This is a reference to Dantsu Flame's irl dam, Inter Pyrenees, who is named after the cheese Tomme des Pyrénées. Dantsu Flame's body was also compared to a cow's, hence the reference too.
