## Appearance

Gentildonna has dark brown hair, styled into slightly curly long bangs that neatly frame her face and two braids pinned into loops. She wears black ear covers; the left one features a golden crown design around the base, and attaches to a red heart-shaped ornament with white frilly trim. She also wears a matching pair of black and red hair ribbons with gold edges. Her eyes are a deep, regal shade of red.

The core piece of her racing outfit is a short black dress, with yellow stripes on the bodice and skirt, sheer black mesh along its halter neckline, and layers of frills and lace underneath. The detached sleeves are covered in detail - copper lace, red and black puff sleeves, winglike embellishments at the elbows, black and yellow stripes below these, and a pair of neat white gloves. Over the dress, Gentildonna wears the most extravagant piece of her outfit - a red overskirt attached to a gold-trimmed corset, which makes the whole ensemble look more like a royal gown. The skirt also features black panels with yellow stripes, cascades into black ruffles closer to the hem, and is adorned with a pair of peachy-pink, flowerlike bows. The open front panel of the skirt shows off white thigh-high stockings adorned with gold lace, as well as her shoes - one black, one white, and both decorated with gold trim and a red gem on the vamp.

## Trivia

* Gentildonna was first mentioned during Season 3 episode The Dream Never Ends, where Gold Ship announces her intentions to challenge her and Orfevre in the Dream Trophy League after her last race in the Twinkle Series.
* She was then properly introduced during the 3rd Anniversary PakaLive TV stream on February 22nd, 2024.
* She is an incredibly physically strong Umamusume. This is likely a reference to her IRL counterpart being a muscular horse that could surpass stallions and bump opponents while racing.
** A running gag showcasing her strength is her apparent effortless feat to compress iron balls with her bare hands, while complaining that the iron balls weren't hard enough and won't do for strength training.
*** In the third anniversary game story, according to KUSOLAB, effortlessly crushing an iron ball 12cm in diameter down to 2cm results in a force of about 1.14 million tons. In comparison, an average human can manage about 37kg. 
*** Although incredibly strong, Gentildonna still trains her power under the premise that pure power means nothing if one can't control it precisely.
** She can split a watermelon with just one finger. A similar feat was listed as one of her secrets, being able to pulverize walnuts with her toes.
** She can light up a campfire through friction with just one decisive spin of the stick, as showcased in the Design Your Island scenario. 
* Her pose is similar to Verxina's due to the IRL horses having a rivalry during their classic years.
* Gentildonna is a semi-limited character. This meant that while she is permanently added to the gacha pools, she cannot be chosen with paid 3-star trainee vouchers.
** She is also the second semi-limited Umamusume to receive an event alternative unit that is not semi-limited.
* While activating her wedding outfit's unique skill, the gender of her partner will change depending on the trainer's gender in-game.
* Her wedding outfit is a reference to the pink race cap that her IRL counterpart wore during her retirement ceremony instead of the black race cap used during her races - making her a "noble lady" instead of a "demoness."
* Gentildonna's last listed objective is the senior year Japan Cup. However, the senior year Arima Kinen will be added as a new objective after the Japan Cup.
** In The Twinkle Legends scenario, the senior year Arima Kinen is listed as an objective from the start.
* In real life, Gentildonna had a white marking that was not incorporated into her Umamusume design.
* She likes to eat carrot tarts at restaurants.
* In her career story, Gentildonna makes note of how the Junior Queen Umamusume of last year is getting the most attention at the Oka Sho.
** This Umamusume is a reference to the real horse [https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/ジョワドヴィーヴル Joie de Vivre], who won the Hanshin Juvenile Fillies in 2011. 
*After the senior year Japan Cup, Gentildonna's father asks the trainer to resign, and the trainer briefly gives Verxina training advice instead. This is a reference to how Yasunari Iwata was replaced as Gentildonna's main jockey after the 2013 Tenno Sho (Autumn), and in the Japan Cup, he rode Verxina while Ryan Moore rode Gentildonna.
