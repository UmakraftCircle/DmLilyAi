This, combined with some of her peers from the same generation having a stronger impression, often results in her being left out of the spotlight, but her prowess is nevertheless legitimate. ## Appearance

Curren Bouquetd'or has muted gray coming into beige eyes and long brown hair with a small white strand at the front, with loose twin curly braids at her sides slightly below her shoulder with straight bangs that cover her forehead. She wears a black ear cover on her right ear and an ear cover with a multicolored floral pattern on her left. She also wears a frilled beige headband with a large bow at her left ear adorned with a black tag that reads her name, "Curren Bouquetd'or."

Her race outfit resembles an upside-down floral bouquet, consisting of a layered black and white dress with a high-low hemline, shorter in the front and longer in the back. The inside lining of the skirt is vibrant and colorful, featuring a floral pattern with various flowers in shades of pink, yellow, blue, and green; both dress layers feature some gold accents. It is tied with a black sash on the empire waistline with a large black bow at the side with a small golden flower on the bow. She wears shoes with mismatched colors, with a black shoe on her right foot and a white shoe on her left, both with red ankle straps at the top. Curren Bouquetd'or also wears long white gloves that reach above the elbows, with the right glove's hand being colored in black.

## Relationships

*Yamanin Zephyr - Friend and roommate. They both get along as fellow calm Umamusume, and Zephyr particularly likes the tea Bouquet makes.
*Almond Eye - Friend and upperclassman. They are close friends, and Almond Eye looks out for Bouquet. Bouquet looks up to her as her upperclassman, and aspires to be as strong as her.
**Curren Bouquetd'or is Almond Eye's stablemate in real life.
*Chrono Genesis - Friend and classmate. As rivals, Bouquet wishes to catch up to Chrono. Off the racetrack, they are friendly with each other, and connect as they are both soft-spoken.
*Loves Only You - Friend and classmate.
*Gran Alegria - Friend and classmate. They sometimes take care of flowerbeds together.
*Curren Chan - Friend. They have been friends ever since childhood, and Curren was the one who initially inspired Bouquet to race after she watched her run. They both look out for each other, and Curren tries to encourage Bouquet on being more assertive. She is also the one who helped her with her racewear.
*Kawakami Princess - Friend. On occasion, they go to cafes together, and Kawakami likes to talk to her about Princess Fighter. Bouquet also makes her flower tiaras.
*Lucky Lilac - Friend and upperclassman. Bouquet finds her funny, and they sometimes hang out together.

## Trivia

* She was announced during the 4th Anniversary PakaLive TV stream on February 22nd, 2025.
* Curren Bouquetd'or's design has multiple references to an actual bouquet, including:
** A tag with her name written on it, similar to a price tag.
** Her racewear flipped upside down resembles that of a bouquet.
*The frills on her headband are a nod to how in real life, she had to wear a shadow roll while she was active.
*The days always being sunny when she's with her mother is a reference to her IRL dam's name, which is Solaria. Another nod to the IRL dam is Bouquet's mother being from Chile, much like the real horse.
*She likes small and cute animals.
*In her career story, Chrono mentions that a certain Australian Umamusume attempted to win a long-distance race, but failed to do so. 
**This Umamusume is a reference to the Australian racehorse Makybe Diva, who placed 7th in the 2005 Tenno Sho (Spring).
*She is particularly good at brewing tea, especially chamomile milk tea.
*She is not afraid of ghosts, and instead feels sympathy for them.
*Her dislike for shiitake mushrooms is a reference to her jockey, Akihide Tsumura, as he also dislikes them.

## Real-life page (excerpt)

Source: https://umamusu.wiki/IRL:Curren_Bouquetd'or

'  is a Japanese racehorse that was active from 2018 to 2021. Known to fans as the "Silver Collector", Curren Bouquetd'or finished runner-up in three Grade 1 races and boasts lifetime earnings of 458,057,000 yen despite only winning 2 races. Her earnings rank 2nd best among JRA horses with 2 or fewer wins, only behind Sounds of Earth.

### Name Origin

Her owner Suzuki Takashi described the meaning of her name as "Crown name (Curren) and a golden bouquet (Buddhism). I hope the flowers of victory will bloom." In Buddhist religion, golden flowers symbolize merit and purity, offered to the Buddha to express reverence and devotion. Bouquetd'or also means "golden bouquet" in French.

### Background

Curren Bouquetd'or was born on April 23rd, 2016, on Shadai Farm. Her sire is Deep Impact, the winner of the 2005 Triple Crown. Her dam, Solaria, was successful in Chile, winning 3 G1 races there. As Curren Bouquetd'or got older, she was sent to train under Sakae Kunieda at Miho Training Center.

### Racing career

=== Two-year-old season (2018) ===

Curren Bouquetd'or made her debut on October 8th, in a 1,600-meter turf race on Tokyo Racecourse. Despite utilizing a strong spurt, she could not catch up and placed 2nd to Danon Kingly, who went on to win the Yasuda Kinen in 2021. She attempted to break out of her maiden once again on November 18th, but she lost once again and placed 3rd. On December 16th, she finally won, breaking out of her maiden. 
=== Three-year-old season (2019) ===

On February 11th, Curren Bouquetd'or challenged her first graded race, the Queen Cup (G3). Though she fought hard, she lost momentum and placed 4th to Chrono Genesis. Because of this, she didn't have enough prize money to enter the Oka Sho, so she competed in the open-class Sweetpea Stakes on April 28th instead. While she won this race, this was the only race she would ever win at that point. 

She was entered into the Japanese Oaks on May 19th, as the 12th favorite. Running within the pack, Curren Bouquetd'or ran alongside Chrono Genesis in the final straight. However, Loves Only You surged from the back, and overtook both of them. Though unpopular, she had placed 2nd despite it, which gained attention. On September 7th, she challenged the Shion Stakes (G3), where she lost and placed 3rd. In the Shuka Sho, on October 13th, she fought hard in the stretch to place 2nd to Chrono Genesis. Curren Bouquetd'or then challenged the Japan Cup on November 24th, where she placed 2nd once again to Suave Richard. 
=== Four-year-old season (2020) ===

Her 2nd place streak continued on into the year, as she lost the Kyoto Kinen (G2) to Chrono Genesis on February 16th. After the race, Curren Bouquetd'or was sent to the United Arab Emirates to compete in the Dubai Sheema Classic, a foreign G1 race. She arrived to the country and prepared for the race, but due to the COVID-19 pandemic, all races in Dubai were cancelled, and she was sent home.

For seven months, Curren Bouquetd'or did not race since the cancellation. She made her return on September 27th in the All Comers (G2), where she placed 2nd. She was then entered into the Japan Cup on November 29th, which had particular attention to it due to the competitors in the field. Daring Tact, the first undefeated Triple Tiara winner, alongside the undefeated Triple Crown winner Contrail, were competing.
