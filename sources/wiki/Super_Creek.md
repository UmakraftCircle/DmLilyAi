## Appearance

Creek’s hair is a deep, brownish auburn, kept in an extra long braid that reaches below her back, with the end tied by a large black ruffle. The sides of her hair curl up, framing her face, with strands of hair reaching just above her shoulder. She wears another braid above her forehead, with a single white strand of hair passing through her braid. Over her right ear, she wears a cerulean bow, with four silver circles arranged in a diamond shape in the center. Her blue down-turned eyes showcase her gentle and motherly personality. 

Her racing uniform is simple, yet feels elegant. For starters, she wears a dark blue scarf around her neck. She wears a flowing dress with tank-top sleeves. The dress itself is light blue, with the center being white. The dress is split down the middle, both sides with framed with silver-blue trim, buttoned up by three distinct dark blue clasps, with blue diamonds on either side. Underneath her dress is a white tube dress, the top embellished with blue gemstones. As for accessories, she wears a dark blue bow around her tail, and around her waist is a dark blue purse with an intricate design on the front, featuring a silver emblem with blue gemstones, as well as two strings of pearls underneath. She wears a pair of thigh-high boots, each shoe having two diamond-shaped cutouts, with blue gems and silver trimming. Her right shoe is solid blue, whereas the right fades into a light gray gradient.

## Trivia

* Her initial character profile was uploaded to the official [https://x.com/uma_musu/status/857787799415668736 Twitter] account on April 27, 2017.
** This tweet was a character name prediction quiz that encouraged fans to try and guess which horse she's named after. The hint was "She is a calm and gentle sisterly-type with a mature aura. Her hobby is to train promising younger trainers into becoming full-fledged professionals."
* "Umamusume: Pretty Derby Artworks Vol.1" explains why her character designer made specific choices when designing her in relation to her real-life counterpart, which were as follows:
** Super Creek's real life jockey's racing silks had a blue sash down the middle, this is represented in her design by the blue cross-body bag.
** Her name represents a river, which are known for both their strength, and ability to travel long distances. This was showcased in her race outfit by giving off the impression of a fantasy "water healer."
** Her real-life counterpart had a single white sock on his back hoof, which is why Creek wears one white boot.
* In her bio, Creek's weakness is drawing, or that she dislikes drawing, however in an [https://x.com/umamusume_eng/status/1954301754038006239?s=20 official illustration for the Tenno Sho (Spring)], Creek is seen using a drawing tablet, having drawn Oguri, Tamamo and Inari remarkably well, and seems happy with her work.
* In her classic year, Super Creek will get Under the Weather - a debuff that gives her a flat 5% fail rate in training after racing in the Sumire Stakes until she wins the Kikuka Sho, making her Career Mode very likely to experience failures. The only way to circumvent this is to play a scenario where character events are unavailable, like Trackblazer.
** This debuff event is likely a reference to real life, as after winning the Sumire Stakes, an injury in Super Creek's left front leg forced him to rest until the fall.
*** On a related note, the debuff is called 小さなほころび (chiisana hokorobi, meaning "a little crack") in the Japanese servers, likely referencing the injury.
*Ironically, despite her motherly personality in Umamusume, the real life Super Creek wasn't well known for his breeding success. In fact, he sired no graded race winners, and was the damsire of only one G3 winner. 
*Super Creek's motherly personality is likely a reference to how in real life, Super Creek and Yutaka Take, his main jockey, had a close bond and was said to be the horse that "nurtured" Take to become the prodigious jockey he is today. This is also shown in Cinderella Gray, where her trainer Fumino Nase acts caring towards Super Creek and vice versa.
**Take would win his first of many G1s with Super Creek, the 1988 Kikuka Sho. He has since ridden and won with many other Umamusume in the series.
