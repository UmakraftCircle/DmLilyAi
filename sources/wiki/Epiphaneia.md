## Appearance

Epiphaneia has short, curly brown hair that transitions into a lighter chestnut color at the tips, fashioned into a chin-length bob with two protruding tufts, and braided into a ponytail with a blue ribbon at the back. Her eyes are a vibrant gradient that changes from violet to cyan, with diamond-shaped pupils. She has a large, puffy ahoge, along with a prominent light tan-colored blaze in the shape of a comet with a long trail that flows down to her bangs. Her long, ruffled ears have a blue interior, decorated with a silver ring on her right ear. The ring is adorned with charms in the shape of two four-pointed stars and a four-leaf clover; the rightmost charm has a purple gem, while the other two have green ones.

Epiphaneia's race outfit is a sleeveless vest primarily of a teal color with white stripes, as well as gold trimming, buttons, and a red bow with a golden stripe on her collar. She wears white gloves with gold cuffs, while green fabric armbands with white triangle patterns, crimson edges, and navy blue cuffs with gold diamond-shaped buttons wrap around her shoulders. A navy blue ribbon with a thin white stripe wraps around her chest, decorated with a golden four-leaf clover ornament positioned between two diamond-shaped buttons. She wears a cape that drapes from the left side of her chest, forms a scarf around her neckline, and drapes down the right side of her back. The lining of the cape is white with a golden stripe along the hem, while the exterior features a gradient going from white to teal towards the hemline and has a white stripe. The cape's edges are a deep navy blue with another golden stripe, and features a navy blue collar around the lower neckline, decorated with golden fleur-de-lis decals. The cape is fastened by a crimson aiguillette adorned with a circular brooch with a black gemstone on the left side, and a golden brooch in the shape of a chrysanthemum on her right.

She wears a pleated skirt with a gradient transitioning from teal to white, a black hemline, and a navy blue underlayer with a golden button shaped like a four-pointed star, as well as black spats underneath the skirt. The star is accentuated by two striped white-and-golden trails resembling a comet, opening to reveal another white fabric underlayer beneath it. Her white knee-high socks have jagged edges and red trimming. Finally, she wears boots with golden soles and a collar adorned with a small diamond. Her left boot is white, while the right is colored black.

## Relationships

* Cesario - Friend. Cesario, alongside Symboli Kris S, mentors Epiphaneia throughout her career. Cesario had been watching over her races for a while, and sees great potential in her. 
** IRL, Cesario is Epiphaneia's dam.
* Special Week - Friend.
** IRL, Special Week is Epiphaneia's damsire.
*Symboli Kris S - Friend. Alongside Cesario, Symboli Kris S mentors Epiphaneia, and watches over her races. She trains alongside Epiphaneia, and Epiphaneia greatly looks up to her. Epiphaneia wants to surpass both her and Cesario.
**IRL, Symboli Kris S is Epiphaneia's sire.
*Rulership - Friend and roommate. On occasion, they both play games together. However, they tend to not get along, since Rulership finds Epiphaneia loud and annoying at times.
** IRL, they both shared the same jockey, Yuichi Fukunaga. They also both competed in the Hong Kong Queen Elizabeth II Cup, where Rulership ran in 2012, while Epiphaneia ran in 2014. They have both competed in Dubai as well, where Rulership ran in the Dubai Sheema Classic in 2011, while Epiphaneia ran in the Dubai World Cup in 2015.
* Daring Tact - Friend and underclassman. Epiphaneia feels fated to Daring Tact, and wants to guide her like how Cesario and Symboli Kris guided her. 
* Efforia - Friend. When Efforia is feeling trouble, Epiphaneia wants to help her right away. She also feels fated to her, and wants to guide her like how Cesario and Symboli Kris S guided her.
** IRL, Epiphaneia sired both Daring Tact and Efforia.
* Logotype - Friend and rival. Epiphaneia is highly passionate and headstrong toward Logotype, and feels like their relationship is sticking together with some special kind of bond. As Logotype was Epiphaneia's first rival, Epiphaneia holds her in high regard. Logotype likes the hyperactive side of Epiphaneia.
** IRL, they are in the same generation of 2013.
* Win Variation - Friend. During summer camp, Win Variation helps Epiphaneia train her body by doing ballet. Epiphaneia's attitude encourages Win Variation to keep going and to work harder.
* Gentildonna - Rival. Gentildonna finds Epiphaneia noisy, but is amused by her behavior.
** IRL, They have raced against each other 3 times, and in retirement they had a foal together.

## Trivia

* She was announced during the fifth anniversary PakaLive TV stream on February 22nd, 2026.
* Epiphaneia is a semi-limited character. This meant that while she is permanently added to the gacha pools, she cannot be chosen with paid 3-star trainee vouchers.
* While there are many other father-child and mother-child pairs, Epiphaneia completes the first father-mother-child trio in the franchise with Symboli Kris S and Cesario.
** This relation is referenced in Epiphaneia's design, as the belt on her racewear shares the same four-leaf clover with Cesario, Buena Vista and Efforia, all of them being grandchildren of Special Week; alongside her ears having a similar shape to Symboli Kris S'. Another nod to the relation is the cross on her ear piece being similar to Symboli Kris S' ear piece.
***Although Efforia has the same four-clover motif, it is designed differently from the characters mentioned above. Which is an indicator that while Efforia is a descendant of Special Week in real life, he is only related to him via the sire-line as Special Week is Efforia's paternal great-grandfather.
* Her love for Galette des rois is a nod to her name origin, as Epiphaneia means "Epiphany", also known as the holiday "Three Kings' Day". Galette des rois (known as king cake), is eaten on this day.
*If the player decline her request to be her trainer on her character story, the player will be sent back to the home screen. This also marks the first time a two-choice option is used in an Umamusume's character story.
* In Epiphaneia's career story, there are many allusions to her classics rival in real life, Kizuna.
** In her career, after Epiphaneia loses the Japanese Derby, the Prix de l'Arc de Triomphe theme begins to play. This is a reference to Kizuna, as after he won the 2013 Japanese Derby that Epiphaneia lost, he went to France to compete in the Prix Niel and the Prix de l'Arc de Triomphe.
** At the Fan Fest, during a game of passing the baton, Epiphaneia grabs a baton with the word "dog" on it. This is a reference to Tatsuya Aoshima's race commentary of the 2013 Japanese Derby, where as Kizuna is taking the lead, Aoshima comments "a dog?" in confusion.

## Real-life page (excerpt)

Source: https://umamusu.wiki/IRL:Epiphaneia

' is a Japanese racehorse that was active from 2012 to 2015. He is well known for his victories in the 2013 Kikuka Sho and the 2014 Japan Cup.

### Name Origin

"Epiphaneia" (ἐπιφάνεια) in Greek means the epiphany of the Lord, twelve days after Christmas. This was inspired by the same Shakespearian play "the Twelfth Night" as his mother, Cesario.

### Background

Epiphaneia was born on February 11th, 2010, Northern Farm. His sire, Symboli Kris S, won both the Tenno Sho (Autumn) and Arima Kinen twice from 2002-2003. His dam, Cesario, won the Japanese Oaks and the American Oaks in 2005. He was purchased for six million yen by Carrot Farm, and began training under Katsuhiko Sumii at Ritto Training Center.

### Racing career

=== Two-year-old season (2012) ===
Epiphaneia made his debut on October 21st in a 1,800-meter turf race on Kyoto Racecourse. Winning the race by three lengths, he was set to compete in the open-class Kyoto Nisai Stakes on November 24th. Entered as the overwhelming favorite, he won the race by one length and three-quarters. This was also the first time facing off his future rival, Kizuna.

His final race of the year was the Radio Nikkei Hai Nisai Stakes (G3). Competing with Kizuna once again, he overtook him and won the race, marking his first graded victory.

=== Three-year-old season (2013) ===
The first race of the year for Epiphaneia was the Yayoi Sho (G2) on March 3rd. Because Yuichi Fukunaga, Epiphaneia's jockey, was suspended at the time, William Buick took his place.Despite accelerating at the final straight, he lost and placed 4th, marking the end of his win streak.

With Fukunaga returning as his jockey, Epiphaneia was set to compete in the Satsuki Sho, the first leg of the Triple Crown. However, due to Epiphaneia being in a rough mood, he rushed into the front instead of waiting in the middle of the pack. As he tried to hold onto his position, Logotype began to overtake him, and set a record time of 1:58.0, going unbeaten until Dee Majesty beat him by 0.1 seconds in 2016. With Epiphaneia placing 2nd, he was then set to challenge the Japanese Derby on May 26th, aiming for the second leg.

Once again, Epiphaneia was in a rough mood during the race, and nearly caused Fukunaga to fall. Taking the lead for a brief moment, he was suddenly overtaken by Kizuna, and he lost and placed 2nd. In a 2018 interview on the television show Umanchu, Fukunaga lamented about the loss, and stated that "there was no reason to continue riding him if he couldn't handle a horse like this", referring to Epiphaneia and his temperament.

After months of rest, Epiphaneia made his return as the overwhelming favorite in the Kobe Shimbun Hai (G2) on September 22nd. Winning by two lengths and a half, he earned his second graded victory. Next, he went on to challenge the Kikuka Sho, the final leg of the Triple Crown. With Logotype tackling middle-distance races and Kizuna preparing for overseas racing in France, Epiphaneia did not get to face his rivals in the final leg.

On October 20th, the Kikuka Sho began. Enduring a heavy track caused by the rain, Epiphaneia got off to a good start and ran in the front of the pack. At a steady pace, he began to accelerate in the final straight. With an easy lead, Epiphaneia won the race by five lengths, and he achieved his first G1 victory.
