## Appearance

Satono Crown has dark gray hair, styled into a wavy side-ponytail on the right side of her head, tied by a white ruffled ribbon and a chain of several white diamonds. Her bangs are fringed, and parted to the left, exposing the right side of her forehead. At the center of her bangs, she has a prominent white marking, shaped like a crown, with four distinct points. Her ears are scalloped, resembling ear fluff, and fade into dark gray at the tips, and she wears an elaborate silver crown around her right ear. Her eyes are a de-saturated green.

Her race outfit consist of a dark green top with vertical stripes, and black shorts with suspenders. The collar above her shirt is black with blue diamond decor at the tips, and she wears a yellow tie with dark yellow floral decor. Her suspender shorts have elaborate gold trimming, and a cutout in the center with X-shaped lacing connecting either side. The end of her shorts have a yellow and green diamond pattern. She wears a pair of black full-length gloves, with gold trim at the top. On her hip, she wears a bright green ribbon. Underneath this ensemble she wears black tights. Above those tights is a pair of black thigh-high boots, with gold trimming at the top, on her toes, and down either side.

## Trivia

*During the 1st Anniversary PakaLive TV stream on February 22, 2022, her character design was shown, but her name was concealed as ???. Her name wasn't revealed until November 5, 2022.
*She is not easily scared by haunted attractions.
*She is able to speak Chinese, more specifically Cantonese.
**This is a reference to the IRL horse winning his first G1 in Hong Kong, as Cantonese being the most spoken language in Hong Kong.
*She is very talented at chess.
*She loves to eat spicy food.
*Her interest to Magic is a reference to the Jockey Joao Moreira, dubbed as "The Magic Man', who partnered the IRL horse winning the Hong Kong Vase in 2016.
