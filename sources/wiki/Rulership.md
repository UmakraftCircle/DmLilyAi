## Biography

<blockquote style="font-style: italic; border-left: 3px solid #3a4c5c; padding-left: 1em; color: #beab08;>"Checkmate. The game's already in the palm of my hand."</blockquote>

A hardcore board game enthusiast who enjoys the genre as a whole, from classics like Reversi to modern ones. She's an intellectual who likes to plan and scheme her way into dominating the flow of the game. The ingenuity serves as an asset in races as well, but...her frail ego is her greatest weakness. When her plans derail by accident, she instantly buckles and folds, hiding away in her room and refusing to come out.

## Appearance

Rulership has pale blue eyes and short, unkempt hair arranged in a messy, chin-length bob with a slight ahoge on top. Her hair is mostly a light umber color accented by a white undertone, with a prominent zig-zag-shaped blaze to match. She wears two alternating black-and-white triangular hairclips on the left side of her bangs. She also dons a green, diamond-shaped ring over her right ear; it is adorned with two circular black and white earrings, which also happen to resemble miniature reversi game pieces.

Rulership's race outfit reflects her affluence and braggadocious persona, consisting of a white halterneck dress shirt underneath a black waistcoat and high-waisted shorts. She dons a cape resembling a jacket worn over the shoulders, with detached black sleeves decorated with black and white feathers. The cape is primarily yellow, with a striped black-and-yellow lining, and has longer strips of fabric and black belts dangling off the sleeves. The back of the cape is dark grey, with a pair of red belts forming a saltire connected by a silver, cross-shaped buckle.

The shirt is fastened with a bolo tie featuring a black-and-white color scheme and a green square-shaped charm with a checkerboard design similar to a reversi board. The waistcoat features a red saltire on its right, while the left is decorated by a yellow stripe and an assortment of pins: one featuring a corner of a reversi board with matching black and white pieces, a green capital letter "R", a rook chess piece, and a triangular pin embossed with a die icon. Her shorts have eight silver buttons, as well as a thin pair of belts connected by a diamond-shaped buckle with a cross emblem. Attached to these belts is a yellow tassel on the left side; on the right, she wears a key ring with an assortment of trinkets: a pair of charms consisting of another rook chess piece and a pair of dice, and a chain that wraps around her waist. She wears white gloves with a striped green trim and triangular black buttons. Her footwear is a pair of black thigh-high boots, each of which are adorned with two green straps and a long, white stripe that stops just above the ankle.

Rulership looks up to Pisa, and is somewhat envious of her good qualities, such as her kindness. She also views Pisa as inspiring, and was brought to tears when Pisa won the Dubai World Cup.

## Trivia

* She was announced during the fifth anniversary PakaLive TV stream on February 22nd, 2026.
* As a reference to how Air Groove is her dam in real life, Rulership's racewear has feather patterns on her right and left shoulders.
* Rulership's favorite tailor being from Italy is a possible reference to either her real life damsire, Tony Bin, or the Italian jockey Umberto Rispoli, who jockeyed the IRL horse to win the 2012 Hong Kong Queen Elizabeth II Cup.
* During training, one of the in-game events includes an earthquake that references the same earthquake that happened in Japan on 11th March, 2011 in real life. Because of the scale of the disaster, which may lead the reference to cause discomfort in individuals affected by it, the official Umamusume X account posted a warning about the reference.
* In her unique skill animation, the knight chess piece she uses is an armored Umamusume instead of a horse, as horses do not exist in the Umamusume world.
* Her younger brother's piano music is a reference to a horse named Chopin, who was Rulership's younger brother in real life.
* She has a liking for sweets. She also tends to eat dessert when she is upset after losing a game.
* Her dislike for cats is likely a reference to the real horse Neko Punch, who defeated Rulership in the 2012 Nikkei Sho. Win Variation also references this horse, as she mentions that a cat punched her earlier.
* She likes going to amusement parks when there's no one else around.
* In her career and character story, when she is upset or frustrated, she tends to repeat words over and over.
* Her tendency to suffer late starts in races is a reference to the real horse, as Rulership was infamous for starting late in many of his races.
** Her skills also reference this tendency:
*** Her unique skill will either activate immediately if she does not get a late start, or will activate mid-way through the race if she does get a late start, and is still at the back of the pack.
*** Her evolved version of the skill Concentration will make her significantly more delayed following a late start, in turn giving her a great boost in speed around the middle part of the race.
* In episode four of her character story, Rulership notes that three Umamusume from the 5th, 9th, and 12th gate are fighting for the lead during a race she's competing in. This is a reference to the 2011 Kinko Sho that Rulership won. Captain Thule was in the 5th gate, Earnestly was in the 9th gate, and Unrivaled was in the 12th gate.
* She shows interest in opening a board game inn with her trainer.
* According to Air Groove in the lobby, Rulership tends to be late for exams, causing her to fail them.
* She likes to read manga in internet cafes. This is likely a nod to Kiseki, as she also likes manga.
* In her classic year, she will get two debuffs that will greatly reduce her performance and mile aptitude before the Mainichi Hai (G3). This is a reference to Rulership's 5th place in the Mainichi Hai (G3) on 2010, where he not only suffered a late start, but another horse-The Taiki-began to break down near him, causing his performance to worsen.
* She heavily dislikes being outside, and prefers to be indoors.
* She is very knowledgeable about visual novels, such as dating simulators.  
* In the homescreen, Rulership mentions that Air Groove constantly asks her if she ate lunch. Rulership also tends to eat junk food, and has an unhealthy diet in general. Her eating habits are likely a reference to how the real horse did not eat very much, especially when he was preparing for the Hong Kong Queen Elizabeth II Cup.
* In the lobby, Rulership and Gold Ship do a pose that looks like a prancing horse while playing a game. This pose is a reference to the 2012 Arima Kinen, where Rulership stood on his hind legs like he was a prancing horse, which caused him to suffer a late start by ten lengths.

## Real-life page (excerpt)

Source: https://umamusu.wiki/IRL:Rulership

' is a Japanese racehorse active from 2009 to 2012. He's best known for his habit of late starting, and is said to have passed it down to his son, Kiseki. He also won the Hong Kong Queen Elizabeth II Cup in 2012.

### Name Origin

Means "the authority to rule, the ruler". Likely inspired by his sire's name, "King" Kamehameha.

### Background

Rulership was born on May 15th, 2007, on Northern Farm. His sire was King Kamehameha, who was the first horse to win both the NHK Mile Cup and the Japanese Derby in 2004. His dam, Air Groove, won the Japanese Oaks in 1996 and the Tenno Sho (Autumn) in 1997, which earned her Japanese Horse of the Year. He was Air Groove's eighth foal. Because of his pedigree, there were high expectations for Rulership as a racehorse.

Rulership was a large horse as a yearling, weighing around 470 kilograms. He began training under Katsuhiko Sumii at Ritto Training Center.

### Racing career

=== Two-year-old season (2009) ===
Rulership made his debut on December 27th in a 2,000-meter turf race on Hanshin Racecourse. As the overwhelming favorite, he won the race by three lengths. This was his only race in the year. Though he was set to debut earlier in the year, an injury in his right hind leg delayed his debut.

=== Three-year-old season (2010) ===
Rulership competed in the open-class Wakagoma Stakes on January 23rd, where he fought hard in the stretch and placed second to Hiruno d'Amour. Despite initial plans to have him compete in the Saintpaulia Sho, he contracted a fever that prevented him from entering. Plans to have him challenge the Sumire Stakes were also cancelled due to him contracting phlegmon.

On March 7th, he entered the open-class Armeria Sho. Despite losing his balance during the race due to Tamura Sky's interference, he won the race by half a length. His first graded race was the Mainichi Hai (G3) on March 27th, where he entered as the overwhelming favorite. However, not only did he suffer a late start, The Taiki began to break down right by him, and Rulership could only manage to place 5th. Because of this loss, he was unable to compete in the Satsuki Sho. 

After a break, Rulership entered the open-class Principal Stakes on May 8th. Because Yasunari Iwata, his usual jockey, was going to ride Admire Tenku at the Kyoto Shimbun Hai (G3), he was temporarily replaced by Norihiro Yokoyama. Winning by four lengths, he gained priority entry to the Japanese Derby on May 30th. 

Due to Iwata jockeying Victoire Pisa at the Japanese Derby, Hirofumi Shii was Rulership's jockey instead. Though he ran in a good position, he was cut off by Gestalt, and he lost momentum at the stretch. Placing 5th, and losing to Eishin Flash, he took a break until the fall. Making his return in the Naruo Kinen (G3) on December 4th, where Iwata rode him for the final time. Gaining momentum, he won the race by half a length. His final race of the year was the Arima Kinen, where Christophe Lemaire was his jockey. He lost the race to Victoire Pisa, and placed 6th.

Though it was planned to have him enter the American Jockey Club Cup (G2), it was instead decided to have him enter the Nikkei Shinshun Hai (G2) on January 16th. With Umberto Rispoli as his jockey, Rulership took the lead at the final straight and won the race by two lengths.
