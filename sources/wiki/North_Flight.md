## Appearance

North Flight has light brownish-orange hair styled in a layered bob-cut, that fades into a light tan at the end. Her fluffy hair is decorated with blonde and pink streaks, and three black and pink hair clips on the right side of her bangs. She wears a magenta boater hat around her left ear, decorated with black trim, and tied with a black and teal ribbon, a white frilled ribbon, as well as a golden "N" accessory. Part of her inner ears are magenta, and she has two triangle-shaped golden clips on her left ear. Her eyes are light magenta, that fade into a pastel yellow. 

Flight's racing outfit is equal parts chic and stylish. She wears a form-fitting bodice, decorated in black and magenta sections, with a skirt that flows out, the ends cut to resemble feathers. Her dress has golden buttons on the bodice, as well as several other golden embellishments. On the right side of the dress' skirt is a laced up blue ribbon, and red bows clipped to where the ribbon intersects. Around her waist, she wears a thin light blue belt, with an emblem resembling wings with a heart in the center. Under her dress' skirt is another skirt, but this time in white, with the ends also cut to resemble feathers. She wears a multicolored striped scarf tied into a ribbon around her neck, striped in black, magenta, and light blue. Decorating her tail are two large white plumes of feathers. She wears short black and magenta detached sleeves on her upper arms, and black lacy gloves tied with pink and blue ribbons on her hand. She wears black low-denier tights, with simple black heels decorated with magenta ribbons and a golden winged charm in the center.

## Relationships

*Aston Machan - Roommate. In her SR support card, she is shown making clothes for Machan's mini doll.
*Sakura Bakushin O - Friend, classmate, and rival. Though they may have different aptitudes in distance, Flight and Bakushin O are fierce rivals all the same. A rivalry strong enough to shake Bakushin O's confidence at first. On the track, it's no holds barred, but off the track, they are very close friends who have a deep understanding of each other despite their differences in hobbies and sensibilities.
** In real life, they held a rare stallion-mare rivalry. Sakura Bakushin O never yielded the sprint to North Flight, and in turn North Flight never yielded the mile to Sakura Bakushin O. They retired one by one, considered by the public to have firmly defended their titles.
*Royce and Royce - Friend. Flight helps Royce out with fashion from time to time, which aids Royce in finding new character appeals for fanservice.

## Real-life page (excerpt)

Source: https://umamusu.wiki/IRL:North_Flight

' was a Japanese racehorse who ran from 1993 to 1994. She was named the Queen of Mile, with her emerging victorious on all five mile races she had participated in including two G1s. She was well known for her rivalry with the King of Sprint - Sakura Bakushin O, whom she never won against in sprint races. Though their roles would reverse when it comes to mile races, with Sakura Bakushin O never winning against her. Their dominance on both sprint and mile races lead to JRA's restructuring for sprint and mile grade races.

### Name Origin

Her name was a combination of "North" and "Flight". "North" originated from Taihoku Farm, while "Flight" originated from her dam, Shadai Flight.

### Background

Before North Flight was born, Taihoku Farm was in a slump period for over twenty years. Founded in 1935, the farm had their first major race winner thirty years later in 1965, when Hatsuyuki won the Oka Sho. However, it took 24 years for the farm to have their second G1 winner, when Light Color won the Japanese Oaks in 1989. Because the owner of the farm, Toshio Saito, was inspired by this result, Saito went to an auction marketed hosted by Shadai Farm in hopes of finding a good broodmare in January 1990. He purchased Shadai Flight, a broodmare that was pregnant with Tony Bin's foal.

Later that year, on April 12th, 1990, North Flight was born on Shadai Farm. She was born without the help of humans, and when Saito went to see her, she was already able to stand properly. Though she had a good physique, due to her sire being Tony Bin, there was the presumption that she would be inconsistent as a racehorse. Due to this, she struggled to find a buyer, and Taihoku Farm ended up being her owners throughout her career. However, Saito had hopes for her as a racehorse. As she grew older, North Flight began training under Keiji Kato at Ritto Training Center.

### Racing career

=== Three-year-old season (1993) ===

North Flight made her debut on May 1st, in a 1,600-meter turf race on Niigata Racecourse. As the overwhelming favorite, she won the race by nine lengths, earning her first victory. Two months later, on July 25th, she competed in the open-class Adachiyama Tokubetsu, where she won by an overwhelming eight lengths. 

After a few months, she returned to challenge the open-class Shubun Tokubetsu, where she lost for the first time and placed 5th. This loss was due to factors such as her feeling sick, alongside going through estrus. It was also her first time running in a medium-distance race.However, on October 17th, she won the Fuchu Himba Stakes (G3), and she attained her first graded victory. This granted her priority entry in the Queen Elizabeth II Cup,where she fought hard and placed 2nd. Her last race of the year was the Hanshin Himba Tokubetsu (G3) on December 19th, which she won. 
=== Four-year-old season (1994) ===
North Flight started off the year by running in the Kyoto Himba Tokubetsu (G3) on January 30th, where she won by six lengths. Later, on March 6th, she won the Yomiuri Milers Cup (G2), and it was then decided to have her continue running in miles throughout her career. On May 15th, she competed in the Yasuda Kinen, where she first encountered Sakura Bakushin O. Despite suffering a late start, she gradually accelerated at the final corner, and she won the race by two lengths. This became her first G1 victory.
