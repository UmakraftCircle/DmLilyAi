Bursting with energy! This girl works hard for her family.

An Umamusume with a friendly personality befitting an elder daughter. She's known for her quick start and strong sense of responsibility. Despite working multiple part-time jobs to help support her two younger twin sisters, you will never find her complaining about it. Her mother granted her greatest wish by sending her to Tracen Academy, and she aims to repay that kindness in spades by showing excellent results.

## Appearance

Reliable big sis Ines is here! Her vibrant green eyes sparkle with energy, and her short hair is impeccably maintained. Though partially obscured by her sporty visor, her bangs are neatly parted as always. Her right ear is adorned with a tidy green wrap, and her unique side ponytail is perfectly secured with a tie. 

Her outfit is as sporty as she is reliable, she wears a short-sleeved, zipped pink jacket with white sleeves, layered over a sleek pink and white midriff shirt with green stripes and the letters "AF" printed on it. A white wristband adorns her left wrist. A roped bracelet and a larger wristband matching her colors sit on her right. She wears her own unique choker with an X hanging below the middle. 

Her skirt, pleated with a pink top layer and a ruffled green and white underlayer. The skirt has belt loops allowing for a white belt with a star buckle to fit in. The loops also allow for two other straps to attach, these straps carry 2 massive stars on them. Beneath the skirt, she wears black safety shorts, and completes her look with ruffled black socks and stylish running shoes that match her color scheme.

## Real-life page (excerpt)

Source: https://umamusu.wiki/IRL:Ines_Fujin

' was a Japanese racehorse that was active from 1989 to 1990. He is one of 4 horses to win the Japanese Derby by frontrunning, and holds the Derby race record prior to Tokyo Racecourse's remodel. Additionally, by holding the Japanese race attendance record at 196,517 spectators, he is seen as one of the contributors for turning the perception of horse racing from gambling to a sport in Japan.

### Name Origin

"Ines" is the crown name of owner Eimei Kobayashi. "Fujin" was a suggestion by his daughters, meaning God of Wind.

### Racing career

=== Two-year-old season (1989) ===
Ines Fujin debuted on the 10th of September in a 1600 turf maiden race in the Nakayama Racecourse. He took two 2nd places, and while jockey Eiji Nakano thinks Ines Fujin can attempt a tokubetsu class race, the team nonetheless entered them into a third maiden race. Ines Fujin ran in front and won with a 1 3/4 length margin in his third maiden race. They rested him afterwards due to fatigue in his back. While their next race was scheduled to be the conditional race Habotan Sho, jockey Nakano suggested that they should instead run in the Asahi Hai Sansai Stakes (G1) (1600m). While Ines Fujin's trainer, Shobo Kato, wanted to extend his running distance and planned on entering the 2000 meter Hopeful Stakes (OP), he decided his should test their strength and accepted Nakano's suggestion. Out of the gate, the 2nd favorite Sakura Saezuri decided to take an early lead, and Ines Fujin chased after following a mediocre start. The two broke away from the pack and had a 5 length lead at the 3rd corner, with a blistering pace of 56.9s at the 1000m mark. The two entered the straight side by side, and jockey Nakano took time to check behind him. While he didn't accelerate in the mean time, Sakura Saezuri wasn't able to pull a lead, and the pack was unable to catch up to them. Ines Fujin passed Sakura Saezuri at around the 150m mark and extended his lead into a 2.5 length finishing margin. The winning time of 1:34:4 tied the record set by Maruzensky in 1976, which was called "Unbreakable" at the time. Jockey Nakano referred to the G1 victory as "easy". With this victory, well known racing analyst and commentator Keijiro Okawa, said Ines Fujin was "a great talent" and "the number one horse to pay attention to in Kanto". He was awarded the Best Two-Year-Old Colt with a 112/172 vote at the end of the year.

=== Three-year-old season (1990) ===
Ines Fujin started his three year old season with the Kyodo News Hai (G3) on the 11th of February. He entered at the 1st favorite of 8 in the rainy race. Starting at the front, he held his lead comfortably and finished with a 3 length margin. Jockey Nakano said, he wanted to let Ines Fujin experience a race where he's reigned in, so he intentionally started late, but the horse's speed was a different level to other horses in the field so he still ended up frontrunning. Ines Fujin entered the Yayoi Sho (G2) on the 4th of March, again as the 1st favorite. As the turf condition on the day was soft, jockey Nakano determined that the inside of the course would be too muddy for Ines Fujin's usual running style, and he would run on the firmer ground on the outside. They was frontrunning as usual, but was unable to extend a lead and lost speed. They were passed by Mejiro Ryan at the 200m mark and subsequently Tsurumaru Mr. O and White Stone, with Ines Fujin finishing in 4th.
