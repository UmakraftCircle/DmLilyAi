## Lead

Buena Vista is a character in Umamusume: Pretty Derby.

## Biography

<blockquote style="font-style: italic; border-left: 3px solid #c0af3b; padding-left: 1em; color: #583d3e;

A top student with a most pure and innocent heart. She's cheerful, upfront, and affable; not afraid to be affectionate toward anyone, she's empathetic to the point of caring about others' business like it's her own. One time, while spectating a race, she witnessed a 'scenery' like no other. To reach that impeccable view, and to fulfill a precious "promise", she runs on in the Twinkle Series.

## Appearance

Buena Vista has fluffy light brown hair that flows just past her shoulders, and both her hair and tail fade into a lighter brown at the tips. She has a small white tuft of hair on her bangs. She wears a brown and yellow checker headband, with yellow ribbons on the end that braid the sides of her hair. On her ears, she wears yellow ear covers, with her left ear cover decorated in the same checkerboard pattern, and a red "X" in the center. She has cheery light pink eyes.

Her racing uniform has a two-piece dress. Her top is a white blouse with a frilled collar, and a black tie with a red "X" in the center. The bottom half is a brown skirt with red buttons along her waist, and three prominent pleats. The center pleat is red, while the two others are white, with criss-crossed red ribbon lacing to her waist. The end of the skirt has yellow frills. Atop of her dress, she wears a dark brown cropped jacket with medium-length puffy sleeves. The collar has a yellow and dark yellow checkerboard pattern, and she wears a clover-shaped medal on the left side. Attached to the jacket are two yellow straps. Her dark brown puffy sleeves have frills at the end, and she wears a white glove on her right hand, with a black on her left. Both have gold trim. She wears two different brown socks, her left leg's being knee-high with a thigh belt at the top, and her right leg having a thigh-high sock with white frills at the top, held up by a garter strap. She wears heels with "X" belting on the front, and straps going up her shin. The right heel is red, while the left is white.

## Relationships

* Gentildonna - Roommate. Buena looks up to and admires Gentil's strength, while Gentil respects Buena as her senior in the Tiara series. The two often train together.
** Buena Vista and Gentildonna have the same owner, Sunday Racing. Gentildonna is officially the first and only horse to win the Japan Cup in two consecutive years. That being said, Buena Vista has unofficially achieved this feat in the two years prior to Gentildonna's Japan Cup races, but was demoted to second place in the 2010 Japan Cup with the runner-up Rose Kingdom being promoted to 1st.
* Special Week - Friend. Buena Vista admires and looks up to Special Week.
** IRL, Special Week is Buena Vista's sire.
* Cesario - Friend.
** IRL, Buena Vista and Cesario are both sired by Special Week.
* Maruzensky - Friend. Maruzensky acts as a reliable senior to Buena Vista.
** IRL, Maruzensky is the sire of Special Week's dam, Campaign Girl, while Special Week sired Buena Vista, making them related.
* Red Desire - Friend and rival. Though not fully understanding Red Desire's oddities, Buena Vista gets along with her quite well. Red Desire views her as the "absolute heroine" of the world, and wishes to surpass her in races.
** They are part of the same 2009 generation, with Red Desire being Buena Vista's primary rival during her Triple Tiara journey. Red Desire lost to Buena Vista in the Oka Sho and Japanese Oaks by second place, but managed to win first in the final leg of the Triple Tiara series, Shuka Sho while Buena Vista got demoted to third.
* Tosen Jordan - Friend and rival.
** They are part of the same 2009 generation, and ran against each other multiple times, with one of their most notable showdowns being the 2011 Autumn Tenno Sho.
* Nakayama Festa - Friend and rival. Nakayama always tries to cheer Buena up when she tears up, though sometimes her attempts don't land well. The former deeply respects Buena’s talent and skills, and along with that, whenever she finds Buena lost or walking in the streets at night, she always makes sure Buena gets to her destination safely. Despite Nakayama being a ‘lone wolf', Buena’s optimism always somehow makes Nakayama a bit soft around her, and Buena always sees the good inside Nakayama.
** They are part of the same 2009 generation. They raced each other twice. In particular, Nakayama Festa beat her and other top class racehorses in 2010's Takarazuka Kinen, netting him his first and only G1 win in quite a flashy way as he was considered the underdog as the 8th favorite.
* Transcend - Friend. 
** They are part of the same 2009 generation. Despite mainly running on dirt, Transcend raced together with Buena Vista in the Dubai World Cup 2011, where Transcend took second place to another Japanese horse, Victoire Pisa, making it a historic Japanese 1-2 Finish, Buena Vista finished 8th in the race due to her poor aptitude in dirt races.
* Wonder Acute - Friend.
** They are part of the same 2009 generation, though they never ran in a race together as Wonder Acute primarily ran in dirt races. Coincidentally however, they share the same birthday.
*Dream Journey - Friend and rival. Dream Journey has described Buena Vista as "dazzling" and shows admiration for her. The 19th Japan Cup is when Dream Journey found her admiration for Stay Gold, and Buena found her admiration for Special Week, so the two feel as if they understand one another.
** The two raced together multiple times, with the 2009 Arima Kinen being Dream Journey's last G1 win in his career, followed by Buena Vista in second in that race.

** They raced together multiple times during their careers, two times at the Japan Cup, two times at the Arima Kinen, and once at the Dubai World Cup. Victoire Pisa triumphed against Buena Vista in the 2010 Arima Kinen and snagged Japan's first ever victory in the 2011 Dubai World Cup, while Buena Vista bested Victoire Pisa in the 2011 Japan Cup.
* Eishin Flash - Friend and rival. Eishin Flash teaches Buena Vista the German language so the latter can write to her German grandma in the language. 
** They raced together multiple times during their careers, twice at the Japan Cup, twice at the Arima Kinen, and once at the Takarazuka Kinen.

**They raced together multiple times, and travelled together to Dubai in 2011 alongside Victoire Pisa and Transcend, although the two were in different races. Their IRL counterparts also had a foal together in 2024, a colt named Pytheas.

* Fusaichi Pandora - Friend and classmate. They sometimes hang out together. 
* Kawakami Princess - Friend.
** The two raced together once, in the 2009 Queen Elizabeth II Cup. They are also both double tiaras.

## Trivia

* She was announced during the PakaLive TV stream Vol.42 in June 24, 2024.
** Like Orfevre, it is believed that she was one of the first Umamusume to be announced back in 2016, when the project first started. During this time gap, fans assumed a design to have been made for Buena Vista.
** Inori Minase, her supposed voice actor for that original design, was never recasted as another Umamusume as of now.
* Buena Vista is currently the only Umamusume explicitly stated to be the trainer's childhood friend, which was mentioned in the introduction of her training session and a vital part of her character story.
** This is theorized to be a reference to how her IRL counterpart has a strong attachment to humans, especially a stablehand named Yamaguchi, as she would start neighing if he was absent for a long period of time.
* In Buena Vista's random event where she gets a headache, the Alps is mentioned as part of a quiz show.
** This is a reference to her IRL dam, Biwa Heidi, who is named after the character "Heidi" from the show "Heidi, Girl of the Alps".
* In Buena Vista's fan letter event, a young girl writes to her saying she was inspired by Buena Vista's racing, and says that she would write again if she got accepted into a trainer training school.
** This is possibly a reference to one of the fan letters Buena Vista's IRL counterpart received, from a mother whose daughter had been absent from school for two years. Her daughter was so impressed by Buena Vista racing against male horses that she started going to school again. The daughter herself later wrote a letter in which she reported that she had started riding horses and wanted to take care of horses like Buena Vista in the future.
* Buena Vista being bad with dates could be a reference to how her IRL counterpart lost both of her races in Dubai, placing 2nd in the Dubai Sheema Classic and 8th in the Dubai World Cup. Dates are the national fruit of the United Arab Emirates, which explains the connection.
* Buena Vista's dislike of traffic jams is a possible reference to when her IRL counterpart got agitated before the Oka Sho when her assigned stall faced a road with many horse transport vehicles.
* Buena Vista is known to shed tears easily, including when she wins a G1 race, referencing her IRL counterpart where the horse shed tears during her retirement ceremony.
* She has a tendency to get lost easily. 
* Her liking of salad dressings and salad bowls in general could be a reference to how her IRL counterpart favors eating green grass.

** This is a reference to when her IRL counterpart received the JRA Award for Best Two-Year-Old Filly.

## Real-life page (excerpt)

Source: https://umamusu.wiki/IRL:Buena_Vista

' is a Japanese racehorse that was active from 2008 to 2011. She famously won the Oka Sho and Japanese Oaks in 2009, but was denied the Triple Tiara by Red Desire, placing only 3rd in the Shuka Sho. Despite this, she went on to win numerous G1 races, including the 2010 Victoria Mile and Tenno Sho (Autumn), and the 2011 Japan Cup.

### Name Origin

Her name literally means "Good View" in Spanish.

### Background

Buena Vista was born on March 14th, 2006. She was born on Northern Farm to Biwa Heidi, a mare who won the Hanshin Juvenile Fillies in 1995. Buena Vista's sire, Special Week, won four G1 races, which includes the Japanese Derby in 1998, both versions of the Tenno Sho and the Japan Cup in 1999.

Buena Vista was then bought by Sunday Racing for 40 million yen. There were high expectations for her ever since she was a foal, though she had a minor issue with her ball node in her left leg before her debut.

### Racing career

=== Two-year-old season (2008) ===

Buena Vista made her debut on October 26th, 2008, in a 1,800-meter turf race on Kyoto Racecourse. She lost and place 3rd to the future Satsuki Sho winner Unrivaled. However, in her next race, she won by three lengths, breaking out of her maiden. On December 14th, she entered her final race of the year, the G1 race Hanshin Juvenile Fillies, as the most popular. As she raced from the back, she eventually surged past the other horses, and won by two lengths, making this her first G1 victory. It also marked a mother-daughter win since Biwa Heidi's participation at the year 1995. At the end of the year, she won the JRA Award for Best Two-Year-Old Filly.

In a post-race interview, the interviewer asked Hiroyoshi Matsuda, her trainer, on whether or not Buena Vista would aim for the Japanese Derby in her classic year. Matsuda denied this notion, and reiterated that Buena Vista will be taking the Triple Tiara route, as it will be easier on the horse. 

=== Three-year-old season (2009) ===

On March 7th, 2009, Buena Vista entered the Tulip Sho (G3). From the third corner, she began to accelerate, and won the race by a quarter of a length. On April 12th, she entered the Oka Sho as the overwhelming favorite. She evaded Red Desire who was charging from the outside and won the race by half a length, making this her second G1 victory. On May 24th, Buena Vista entered the Japanese Oaks as the most popular once again. Red Desire, who was ahead at the time, was aiming for the finish, when Buena Vista suddenly surged behind her, crossing the finish line with her at the same time. After a close examination, it was determined that Buena Vista won the race, as she was slightly ahead of her. 

With these victories, Buena Vista had the Double Tiara, and after this, she rested at Northern Farm until the fall. On August 23rd, she entered the Sapporo Kinen (G2). This race would determine if she would enter the Prix de l'Arc de Triomphe, a French G1 race that no Japanese horse has been able to win. If she could not win this race, she would be unable to travel abroad and participate. She attempted to accelerate from the third corner, but she was unable to catch Yamanin Kingly, and she placed 2nd. Because of this loss, the plan to have her race in the Prix de l'Arc de Triomphe was quickly cancelled, and Buena Vista continued to aim for the Shuka Sho, the final leg of the Triple Tiara. An issue in the hoof of her right front leg was also discovered, but since it was mild, the symptoms were quickly dealt with
