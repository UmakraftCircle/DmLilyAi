## Appearance

Meisho Doto has short brown hair that curls inwards to frame her face. She has a large white patch in the middle of her bangs, as well as a white curled ahoge that zig-zags at the base. Her eyes are purple, with swirled pupils in place of normal pupils. Her ears are often depicted as drooping downwards and she wears a blue cord bow with a good luck knot on her right ear along with a pink headband.

Her race outfit is reminiscent of a mail carrier with a collared white top and a blue overall dress. Her top has a ruffled center and a collar with two black stripes right above the bottom edge. The sleeves have lace ruffles that are tied off with a pink ribbon. She wears white gloves with the same lace ruffles that have pink cuffs with a white stripe in the middle. The bow on her collar resembles a wax postage stamp and has a horseshoe imprint.The two ribbons attached to it are red with a vertical yellow stripe. Her messenger bag is brown with gold edge and a pink strap that crosses diagonally across her body. On the center is another bow and a carrot keychain dangles from the right side. Her dress has golden buttons and accents, with a dark stripe along the bottom. The underside of the pleats are red and lace peaks out from under the dress. She wears plain white loafers with pink ribbons and stockings with alternating blue and red carets.

## Trivia

* Her initial character profile was uploaded to the official [https://x.com/uma_musu/status/870472255947415556 Twitter] account on June 1, 2017.
** This tweet was a character name prediction quiz that encouraged fans to try and guess which horse she's named after. The hint was "She is a strong competitor but also incredibly shy. In races, she is always beaten by "that classmate" who calls herself the strongest, and she seems to have little self-confidence.
* Her ability to speak English with her family, alongside her family traveling a lot, is a reference to how Meisho Doto was born in Ireland in real life.
* She is often shown with various animals. These are based on animals the real Meisho Doto lived with in his retirement.
** The cat is based on Meto, a popular cat living on Northern Lake Farm, who Meisho Doto became close with.
* In the mobile game, she has a series of special events if the player make her run the Triple Crown while still with her "Not Ready" debuff.
* As revealed in one of her costume events, Meisho Doto has an extreme spice tolerance built up due to accidentally ordering spicy foods consistently. Even El Condor Pasa and Hishi Amazon, both known to be spice enthusiasts, are challenged by her tolerance, with Doto finding foods they struggle with only a slight bit spicy.
* She dislikes summer, but likes winter.
* Meisho Doto's father is a jeweler, and she oftentimes stares at hard candy rather than eating it as it reminds her of him.
