## Biography

<blockquote style="font-style: italic; border-left: 3px solid #52606b; padding-left: 1em; color: #cb363d;>"All is for the sake of Our family's dearest desire: to found the Kingdom of Rose."</blockquote>

Born to the distinguished 'Rose Family', she received elite education to grow into a noble Umamusume befitting of her place. Her mission in racing is to elevate her family's standing in the world of racing to a height beyond merely prestigious; essentially, she seeks to become the 'king' who leads her family. She's had such a noble upbringing that worldly things are rather foreign to her.

## Appearance

Rose Kingdom’s hair is styled in an evenly parted wavy bob that falls to her shoulders. Her hair color is a dark gray that transitions into a red gradient near the ends, with a subtle ahoge at the crown. The base of her right ear is adorned with a red rose accented by several leaves and a small bead, while the ear itself is wrapped in a golden thorned rose. Her eyes are slightly downcast and a pale pink in color, and her pupils appear to be encircled by a circular pattern, giving them an almost emblem-like appearance. 

Rose’s race outfit resembles a formal court uniform inspired by classical European aristocracy. It is primarily black, with a deep red panel running down the center, adorned with gold braided dress cords and rose ornaments. A golden rose embellishment rests at the neckline of her embroidered collar. A larger golden rose brooch secures her cape. The cape itself features a layered panel design resembling stylized feathers each one decorated with rose symbols, and a final golden rose charm dangles elegantly from the cape’s end. Her sleeves are puffed and black, with white diamond-quilted undersides. Gold-colored trim runs along the length of the sleeves to the wrists, which are fitted and adorned with additional golden rose accents. She also wears white gloves. A red sash with silver trim runs diagonally over her torso, secured at the hip with a large red rose.

She wears tailored white fitted pants and black boots that reach up to her thigh. A few more red roses decorate the cuffs of her boots.

## Relationships

* Victoire Pisa - Friend and rival. While at first, Rose is closed off, Pisa reaches out to her, and shows her that friendship and connection is a good thing. They get along well, and they train together from time to time.
* Eishin Flash - Friend and rival.
* Rulership - Friend and rival. Rulership tends to heavily exaggerate her speech when around Rose, which confuses the latter. Rulership also tries to get Rose to try "normal" food, such as hamburgers.
** In real life, Eishin Flash, Rose Kingdom, Rulership and Victoire Pisa were rivals throughout their careers, starting with their classic years.
* Tanino Gimlet - Friend and senior. Rose admires Gimlet and is intrigued by her behaviours, to the point of keeping a notebook of the language Gimlet uses to better understand her. In turn, Gimlet mentors Rose, teaching her about the art of fence destruction. 
** In real life, Rose Kingdom and Tanino Gimlet are stablemates at Versailles Resort Farm. Rose Kingdom has been seen imitating Gimlet’s fence-destroying habits, much to the chagrin of farm staff, who attributed this behaviour to Gimlet taking Rose Kingdom as his disciple.

### Name Origin

"Rose Kingdom" is a combination of his sire's name "King" from King Kamehameha and his dam's name "Rose" from Rosebud.

### Background

Rose Kingdom was born on May 10th, 2007, on Northern Farm. His sire was King Kamehameha, a horse who became the first to win the Unconventional Double Crown in 2004, consisting of the NHK Mile Cup and the Japanese Derby. His dam was Rosebud, who had mild success in graded races, winning the Fillies' Revue in 2001 and the Mermaid Stakes in 2003. She was sired by Sunday Silence, an American horse who had major success as a stallion in Japan.

Rose Kingdom was purchased by Sunday Racing for 80 million yen. As a yearling, he initially had a low weight, though he had a steady improvement in his training. He was trained under Kojiro Hashiguchi at Ritto Training Center.
