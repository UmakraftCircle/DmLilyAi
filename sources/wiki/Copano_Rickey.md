Rickey ☆ Lucky ☆ Super sweet feng shui guru ☆

A bright and cheerful Umamusume known for her amazing luck. Her knowledge of feng shui is second to none, and she uses it to navigate not only her daily life, but also her approach to training and races. She insists on making those around her happy with the power of feng shui, and her advice for attaining good fortune is highly sought after.

## Appearance

Copano Rickey has light orange hair that falls past her shoulders, and a large ahoge coming off the top of her head. She wears her hair in two round buns on either side accompanied by red and yellow tassels, and the center of her bangs are solid white. She wears ear covers on both of her ears, the left side being plain black. However, her right ear cover is yellow with a diagonal red stripe, and the base of the ear cover is a bagua octagon, representing her interest in feng shui. Her eyes are bright pink, and she has a fang on the left side of her mouth. 

Rickey's race outfit is bright, and full of ruffles. She wears a layered off-shoulder dress with a sweetheart cut, decorated with red trim at the neckline. The dress' skirt is decorated with two large four-petaled flowers, both being white with red outer petals. Underneath her yellow dress skirt are two ruffled layers, the first being red, and the second being white. She wears a pair of light blue bloomers underneath. Her white collar is accompanied by a large red rope, tied into a ribbon, with a blue rounded diamond in the center. Rickey's yellow sleeves are detached from her dress, and have red bands around both the top and bottom, with long, frilly, bell sleeves. She wears blue fingerless gloves on either hand, both with octagonal holes at the back, and wears a blue pearl bracelet on her left wrist. As for her boots, they frill outwards at the top, almost making a heart shape. Both boots have red interiors, red out-soles, and blue ribbons on the back of her heels. However, her left boot is white, while the left is yellow.

## Relationships

* Hokko Tarumae -  Rival and close friend. Rickey's obsession with good luck and fortune often irritates the more logical Tarumae, but they maintain a very close bond despite this, and Rickey respects Tarumae's racing immensely. 
* Wonder Acute - Rival and close friend. Acute is very supportive of Rickey's feng shui, and she makes sure that Rickey and Tarumae don't become too rowdy. 
* Smart Falcon - Friend. As Rickey's senior, Falco usually takes it upon herself to help Rickey when needed, and Falco also tends to follow Rickey's feng shui advice. Likewise, Rickey respects Falco's seniority and follows her advice when it comes to being an Umadol.
* Espoir City -  Roommate and Friend. Rickey often embarrasses Espoir City by being incredibly energetic and clingy around her, but deep down Espoir is happy with Rickey's kindness.
** IRL, Copano Rickey, Espoir City and Smart Falcon were all sired by Gold Allure, and went on to become successful dirt racers.
* Transcend - Friend. Transcend is often left stunned by Rickey's feng shui. Regardless, she thinks very highly of Rickey's sharp mind, and her desire to help people no matter what.
* Furioso - Friend. The two talk often enough that Rickey's feng shui is subconsciously stuck inside of Furioso's head. Furioso does her best to keep hold her at arm's length, but Rickey's sheer energy typically powers through anyway.
* Seiun Sky - Friend. Rickey admires and praises Seiun regularly, as noted by Seiun herself. The two sometimes fish together.
** IRL, Seiun Sky and Copano Rickey's owners are good friends.
* Kitasan Black -  Childhood friend.
** IRL, Kitasan Black and Copano Rickey's owners are good friends.
* Curren Chan -  Friend.
* Matikanefukukitaru - Friend. Fukukitaru often seeks Rickey's help in misfortunate situations through her expertise, calling her "Master" Rickey.

## Trivia

* She was announced during 4th EVENT SPECIAL DREAMERS!! on day one of the Yokohama leg, which took place on May 4th, 2022.
* She chose red for her racewear for good luck, while she also chose yellow for good fortune.
* Her racing outfit was designed under supervision by her real life counterpart's owner - Dr. Copa (real name Sachiaki Kobayashi), to incorporate Feng Shui elements.
* She has a tendency to say the phrase "Copa Copa" randomly.
* She is not afraid of ghosts.
*During late June on her Classic Year, she will get Ominous Portent - a debuff the description claims that winning races become difficult, but seemingly does not actually do anything that hinders her shape in racing, making the debuff mostly decorative.
**The debuff gets cured after winning the February Stakes, though the story event will be different depending on whether Rickey had won a G1 race beforehand.
**In Japanese, the debuff is named 怪しい雲行き (ayashi kumoyuki, meaning "things are looking grim"), the description of it merely claims that "disrupted qi flow makes it easier for bad things to happen", leaving its effects completely vague.
*If Rickey successfully wins 11 dirt G1 races in her Career Mode, the secret event "Eleven Stars in the Sky" will trigger, giving her substantial stat bonuses and skill hints.
**This is a reference to the real life Copano Rickey, who currently holds the Japanese record for highest combined G1 and Jpn1 victories.

### Name Origin

Copano Rickey's owner, Sachiaki Kobayashi, has stated that he had already decided that he would name Copano Nikita's foal Rickey prior to Copano Rickey's birth.

### Racing career

=== Two-year-old season (2012) ===

Copano Rickey debuted on December 22, 2012, at Hanshin Racecourse's 6th race of the day. He was the 8th favorite to win and finished 8th place in a field of 11 horses.

=== Three-year-old season (2013) ===

In January of 2013, he won a maiden race in Kyoto as the 8th favorite. Afterwards, he proceeded to win his second race in a row, which was an allowance race in Tokyo. After the race, his jockey Cristopher Lemaire remarked that it was an easy victory, and that he was looking forward to Copano Rickey's future. He followed these victories with a third place finish as the favorite to win at the Hyacinth Stakes and the third win of his career at the Fukuryu Stakes.

Copano Rickey proceeded to win the first graded race he participated in, the Hyogo Championship. During this race, he took the lead from gate to wire, and ended up beating the second place finisher, [https://en.netkeiba.com/db/horse/2010101349/ Lord Crusader], by six lengths. After this race, despite his lack of experience running on turf, it was planned for him to participate in the Japanese Derby.  However, it was soon found that he had a fracture in his right forearm, and he was forced to drop out of the race. Due to this injury, he rested for six months.

After recovering, his next race would be the Shimotsuki Stakes on November 17th, ran in Tokyo. Despite being the first favorite, he finished tenth in a field of sixteen horses. His final race for the season would be the 2013 Farewell Stakes on December 23rd, ran on Nakayama. He was the third favorite and finished ninth.

=== Four-year-old season (2014) ===

Copano Rickey began his 2014 season with the first G1 of his career, the February Stakes. He had won his spot in this race through a 50/50 lottery draw against [https://db.netkeiba.com/horse/2010104996/ Keiai Leone], a horse who, at the time, had four wins out of fourteen starts. His trainer, Murayama made comments about Copano Rickey's improvement.  Despite this, Copano Rickey entered the race as the least favorite in the field of 16 horses, with the odds of 272.1. During the race, Copano Rickey remained in second place, before taking the lead on the final stretch and managing to stave off the second favorite, Hokko Tarumae. With an L3F time of 35.3, the third fastest on the field, Copano Rickey won the 2014 February Stakes. The payout for a win bet, ￥27,210, is one of the highest payouts in JRA history. After the race, his jockey, Hironobu Tanabe, expressed disbelief, calling Copano Rickey a lucky horse.<br />
