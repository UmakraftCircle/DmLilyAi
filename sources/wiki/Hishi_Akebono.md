## Appearance

Standing high above many of her colleagues at 180cm tall, Akebono wears her dark brown hair into two big pigtails tied by small white ribbons. Her bangs frame her face, and she has a pointed forelock in the center. Bono's large ears have dark brown tips, and she has pink inner-ears. She has two blue round beads on her right ear. She has cheery red eyes that fade into a light pink.

When wearing her racing outfit, she wears a small soda jerk style hat on her right ear, mostly white with a dark blue top, and adorned with pink and blue stripes. The beads that were once there are now attached to her right pigtail.

Her racing outfit is a blue and white striped dress with short puffy sleeves. The cuffs of her sleeves are pink with a blue stripe in the center. The center of her dress has six large gold buttons, and she wears a cute yellow ribbon with a cupcake in the center on the right side of her chest, and above that is a mint and white polka-dotted ribbon. Around her neck is a white choker with a simple bow tie, and a gold medallion in the center. Her waist is tied with a blue ribbon, and she has a white apron with blue frills above her skirt, decorated with pictures of donuts and candies. She wears a white frilled petticoat under her skirt. She wears two different tennis shoes, the left is white with gold heels and red laces, decorated with small accessories of fruits, while her right shoe is brown with dark brown laces, and cookie decorations. She wears one puffy sock on her right foot.

## Trivia

*Her initial character profile was uploaded to the official [https://x.com/uma_musu/status/862861118863065089 Twitter] account on May 11, 2017.
**This tweet was a character name prediction quiz, encouraging fans to try and guess which horse she was named after. The provided hint was: "She is a very large Umamusume in both mind and body. She loves to eat. Her racing performance is truly impressive, taking full advantage of her extraordinary physique."
*Her name is based on Yokozuna Akebono Taro, a famous sumo wrestler who was also born abroad. Her large size and proclivity for eating are tied to this moniker.
** Chanko Nabe is a hotpot meal consumed by those looking to put on weight, and so is often associated with sumo wrestlers.
*** The fact that Hishi Akebono mentions researching on Hawaiian food like taro, alongside the fact that she has been to Hawaii before, is also a reference to the wrestler, as Akebono Taro was from Hawaii.
*At 180 cm, Hishi Akebono is the tallest Umamusume in the game.
**Akebono's height was featured mostly on Umayuru anime as she was depicted as a super giant Umamusume, typically only appearing in the backgrounds or behind buildings.
*When she wakes up in the mornings, the first thing she does is stomp her feet, though her pajamas get torn in the process.
*She is good at entertaining and playing with children.
*She believes that aliens exist.
*She has nostalgia for the fairy tale Alice in Wonderland. This is because she admired Alice from the story.
