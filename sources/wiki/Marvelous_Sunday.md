## Appearance

Marvelous Sunday's dark hair is styled into two massive twintails on each side of hear head, held in place by leather straps. A red headband bearing a ribbon also sits on her head. Two longer wisps of hair at the sides of her head curl down into drills. Her golden eyes shine under her star-shaped pupils. She also has a singular fanged tooth.

Her race outfit is a fiesta-style dress adorned with bright flowers across the skirt, multicolored frills at the hemline, and a yellow trim at both the hem and sleeves. A multicolored corset-like top sits over her white shirt. A red ribbon is tied around her neck, and a necklace with three six-point stars sits below it. Three pairs of stringed ball lights additionally embellish the dress. A pair of multicolored frilly bands wrap around her wrists, and flower-like bands sit at her ankles. She wears long white socks which reach the thigh, and a pair of purple-heeled shoes.

## Trivia

*Her initial character profile was uploaded to the official [https://x.com/uma_musu/status/862861613992300544 Twitter] account on May 11, 2017.
**This tweet was a character name prediction quiz, that encouraged fans to try and guess which horse she's named after. The hint was "She is a high-tension and high-spirited girl. Due to her moody personality, she quickly loses her cool when it comes to being number one. She loves to wear red ribbons."
*She uses the bathroom before races.
**In real life, Marvelous Sunday had a similar trait, where he used to pee in the paddock before races.
*She is able to send other Umamusume to her Marvelous Dimension.
*She is talented at knitting.
