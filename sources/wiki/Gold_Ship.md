## Biography

<blockquote style="font-style: italic; border-left: 3px solid #E9DA36; padding-left: 1em; color: #DA3C57;>"So long as it's fun, right? Right?!"</blockquote>

Wild and unpredictable, this golden runner is unlike anybody else!

An unpredictable firebrand born with an extra helping of free spirit. She's prone to some dramatic swings in mood and motivation—one moment she could be setting up an elaborate punchline, and in the next you would be hard-pressed to get her to move. She seems to get along with anyone and everyone, but it is unclear just how she views her bonds with others. Truly, one could call her Tracen's greatest mischief-maker.

## Relationships

* Mejiro McQueen - Friend. Gold Ship often pulls pranks on McQueen and drags her into various antics. McQueen's dignified Mejiro family upbringing makes her the perfect target for Gold Ship. Some notable pranks include: kidnapping McQueen into Spica by putting her in a sack, putting hot mustard in her snacks, and fake crying to get McQueen to race with her. McQueen calls Gold Ship "a pest who won't leave me be" and is known to flee when she hears Gold Ship's voice approaching. However, she is also there for McQueen to help cheer her up when life gets her down. The two are teammates on both the anime-only team, Team Spica, and the game-only team, Team Sirius. 
** IRL, McQueen was the maternal grandfather of Gold Ship.
* Nakayama Festa - Friend. They often play games and entertain Orfevre together, along with that they both work together in order to prank Fenomeno.
* Orfevre - Friend, classmate and rival. Orfevre is one of the few people who is completely unbothered by Gold Ship's antics, instead finding great entertainment in them.
*Dream Journey - Friend. Dream Journey is grateful to Gold Ship for being friends with Orfevre.
*Fenomeno - Friend and rival. Often a victim of Gold Ship's pranks. Gold Ship likes to prank and tease Fenomeno, since she tends to react poorly every time. Fenomeno feels frustrated about her, since Gold Ship is a bad influence yet she is popular with children, while Fenomeno tries to be a good role model but gets little attention. Despite the pranks and the teases, Gold Ship does care about Fenomeno; as before, she has gifted Fenomeno a bouquet of flowers and is one of the primary reasons why Fenomeno continues to race. Fenomeno grows to admire Gold Ship for her strength, and they are both viewed as a comedic duo by racing fans. Fenomeno also defends a child who likes Gold Ship, and acknowledges her as a valid inspiration for others even if she's unconventional.
*Stay Gold - Friend and roommate. She once referred to Stay Gold as "Konjiki Oyaji" ("Golden Pops" in English). She also seems to value Stay Gold's opinions, being one of the very few people that Gold Ship deeply respects. Gold Ship also watches Stay Gold's races, as before Stay Gold was implemented, Gold Ship was said to have cried when a certain "silver-medal Umamusume" won her first G1 race overseas, which is a reference to Stay Gold. Stay Gold is amused by Gold Ship's strange behavior, and doesn't mind her antics.
** IRL, Gold Ship, Nakayama Festa, Dream Journey, Orfevre and Fenomeno are all sired by Stay Gold.
*** Gold Ship, Nakayama Festa and Orfevre ran in the Prix de l'Arc de Triomphe in real life, with Gold Ship finishing 14th on his run and both Nakayama Festa and Orfevre coming close to winning but not quite clinching it.
***Stay Gold and Gold Ship was planned to be stablemates, that is until Stay Gold died earlier before Gold Ship's move to Big Red Farm.
* Tosen Jordan - Rival. Out of all of the Umamusume in Tracen, Gold Ship seems to like messing with Jordan the most, as Gold Ship will come out of nowhere to try and kick Jordan or mess with her head, though the two hold no bad blood between each other and can still be amicable to one another.
** IRL, Jordan was presided as stable boss in the same place Gold Ship resided at. As such, he would often harass and kick him.
*Eishin Flash - Friend and classmate. Flash is somewhat confused by Gold Ship's antics, but still sees her as a rival.
*Verxina - Friend. Verxina finds Gold Ship's antics amusing sometimes.
*Gentildonna, Fuji Kiseki, Hishi Amazon, and Smart Falcon - Classmate.
**Gold Ship likes to try and get Gentildonna's attention, which greatly amuses the latter. Gentildonna also acknowledges Gold Ship's strength, as she is undoubtedly a strong racer despite her antics.

* Grass Wonder - Friend. Despite their clashing personalities, they get along well.
**IRL, the two of them were good friends during their time in Big Red Farm, as Gold Ship would always stomp his foot, greeting Grass Wonder.
*Special Week, Silence Suzuka - Teammates on both the anime-only team, Team Spica, and the game-only team, Team Sirius.
*Tokai Teio, Vodka, Daiwa Scarlet, Kitasan Black - Teammates on the anime-only team, Team Spica.
**Vodka and Daiwa Scarlet tend to get annoyed by Gold Ship.
*Rice Shower, Winning Ticket, Narita Brian - Teammates on the game-only team, Team Sirius.
**Gold Ship defends Rice Shower when onlookers insult her for defeating Mihono Bourbon, and tries to cheer her up.
*Rulership - Friend. Gold Ship likes to tease Rulership, and vice versa. When playing games together, Rulership likes tricking Gold Ship.
*Chrono Genesis - Friend and underclassman. Despite Golshi's eccentricities, Chrono Genesis appreciates her guidance, while Golshi keeps note on each others' similarities.
**IRL, both of them were gray horses who won the Takarazuka Kinen twice, and coincidentally, they share the same birthday.

## Trivia

* She acts as the primary host, virtual YouTuber (VTuber) and the self-proclaimed PR manager for [https://www.youtube.com/@UMAMUSUME_official PakaTube] (ぱかチューブっ!), the official YouTube channel for the Umamusume: Pretty Derby franchise.
** Gold Ship insists the title is official despite various videos indicating she is self-proclaimed, thus she constantly asks the staff to remove the parenthetic addition.
* Her initial character profile was uploaded to the official [https://x.com/uma_musu/status/837135396928540672 Twitter] account on March 1st, 2017.
* Gold Ship is often associated with the number 564. This stems from Japanese wordplay, wherein the number 5 can be read as go, 6 can be read as ru, and 4 can be read as shi; when put together, these three numbers spell out Gorushi, Gold Ship's nickname.
* "Umamusume: Pretty Derby Artworks Vol.1" explains why her character designer made specific choices when designing her in relation to her real-life counterpart:
** The golden emblem on her leg strap is designed to represent a ship's anchor, in reference to her name, Gold Ship.
** Her outfit is red with white trim, reflecting the color of her real-life counterpart's jockey silks. Likewise, the white and red bands around Gold Ship's arms reflect the white bands on the sleeves of these jockey silks.
** The six golden buttons on her dress represent the six G1 wins of her real-world counterpart.
*** On a similar note, Gold Ship is often associated with Rubik's cubes, as they have six sides. She is often seen playing with a Rubik's cube in the anime.
* In the anime, despite Gold Ship suffering numerous injuries and mostly in the eyes, she is always seen in perfect shape as if those injuries never happened.
** This is a reference to her in-real-life counterpart being in perfect form and only received minor injuries during the course of his career.
* Gold Ship's signature racewear has multiple red ribbons on it, most likely a reference to her in-real-life counterpart wearing one during his career. In horse racing, a horse with a red ribbon tied to its tail is usually a sign meaning, "careful, I kick!". Additionally, Gold Ship was known to kick without warning.
