## Appearance

Calstone Light O, the fastest, has straight, long black hair that reaches her waist, and a distinct solid white cowlick swept to the right side of her head. She wears yellow ear coverings on both ears, and has a yellow tassel shaped ornament adorning her right ear cover. Her eyes are a piercing yellow, with black pupils.

Calstone Light O's racing outfit consists of a dark blue double-breasted jacket with long, frilled sleeves, and a white frilled skirt. The front of the jacket is adorned with golden buttons, and red buckled decorations on the upper arm of both sleeves. The inside collar of the jacket matches the golden buttons with a bright yellow diamond-shaped inner pattern. The jacket also has red colored shoulder decorations, matched by the red colored ascot that Calstone Light O wears. Above the skirt is a leather belt with golden decorations, with a single blue diamond shaped gem at its center. A yellow string is tied around her waist under the belt, the knot sitting neatly where the white frilled skirt is. Under the white frilled skirt are a pair of white shorts and black pantyhose, with a black garter on her right thigh, adorned with a golden pattern running across it. She wears mismatched black and white heeled shoes, with the right shoe resembling a boot that almost reaches her knee, and her left shoe reaching just past her ankle.

## Trivia

* She was announced during the PakaLive TV Vol.42 stream on June 24th, 2024.
* Calstone Light O is tied with Kiseki and Victoire Pisa, having the 5th shortest time gap from introduction to being playable trainee, with 25 days between when she was first announced and when she was added as a trainee, behind Phalaenopsis at 20 days, Transcend at 19 days, Gran Alegria at 17, and Dream Journey's immediate banner.
*Her love for cats is a possible reference to her real life ancestor, Godolphin Arabian, who was noted to be friends with a cat named Grimalkin.
*She tends to yell loudly in her sleep.
*She has the ability to run on water.
*She celebrates New Year's as early as December.
*She tends to dream herself racing against animals like jaguars and pronghorns in her mind.
*She tends to lose her wallet due to her speed.
*In her good ending, Light O shows intense interest in overseas racing, and Durandal and Believe briefly help her out with planning her trip. This is a reference to how all of three of them have competed in Hong Kong at some point, with Believe having competed in 2002, while both Calstone Light O and Durandal competed in 2004.
*She likes to watch underwater diving videos.
*One of her favorite snacks is yokan.
*She is particular about her skincare products, because she wants to reduce friction in her skin.
*She has the ability to catch fish with her bare hands.
*Her goal to be faster than a pronghorn is a reference to an NHK quiz program that aired on May 24th, 2019, where one of the questions "what is the second-fastest land animal?" The answer was pronghorn, and Calstone Light O was used as a reference regarding speed.
*Her father is a legal prosecutor, while her mother is an optical scientist.
*In her solo song, O-Light-O, the lyric "53 byō 7" is a reference to her IRL counterpart's former record time, 53.7 seconds, in the 2002 Ibis Summer Dash. Excluding the intro, the song's main section also lasts exactly 53.7 seconds as a tribute to that record.
**This record, however, was beaten by Puro Magic by 0.2 seconds in the 2026 iteration.
*Her white ahoge curving to the right is a reference to how the real Calstone Light O had a tendency to lean his head to the right when racing.
*In her classic year, it is noted by the trainer that the Sprinters' Stakes is being held at Niigata Racecourse due to repairs. While this did happen in real life, this happened in 2002 in which Believe was the victor of the race, which was Calstone Light O's four-year-old season.
**In her classic year, she also achieves her speed record of 53.7 seconds in the Ibis Summer Dash (G3), though that happened in the four-year-old season in real life as well.
*While at the hot springs, Calstone Light O tells her trainer that pumice stones can be used for foot care. This is a reference to her traditional Chinese name (specifically the one on the TW servers instead of the HKJC one, as it was different), which means "Pumice Light O".
