## Biography

<blockquote style="font-style: italic; border-left: 3px solid #DFC8E7; padding-left: 1em; color: #00B5BE;
>"I'll bring honor and glory to the Mejiro family!"</blockquote>

The crown jewel of the prestigious Mejiro clan. A classy and poised young lady.

Born into a family of racing legends, Mejiro McQueen is a long-distance runner who is the envy of her peers. She has class and decorum befitting her prestige, and is determined to take first at the Tenno Sho, like many Mejiro racers before her. On her days off, she enjoys having afternoon tea with the many other residents of the Mejiro estate.

## Relationships

* Tokai Teio - Friend and rival. The two are also teammates on the anime-only team, Team Spica. McQueen’s relationship with Teio is the central emotional and competitive focus of season 2. Though they are close friends and teammates within Team Spica, their bond is shaped by a fierce rivalry. Their relationship reached its competitive peak during the Spring Tenno Sho, where Teio sought to maintain her undefeated streak while McQueen aimed to defend her title. McQueen's victory in that race saw Teio defeated for the first time, earning Teio's respect. Despite their rivalry, McQueen harbors deep affection and concern for Teio. In their everyday interactions, McQueen often acts as the "straight man" to Teio’s impulsive and energetic antics.
** IRL, Mejiro McQueen and Tokai Teio only faced each other once, that being the 1992 Tenno Sho (Spring). After retirement, they later became stable neighbors.
*Stay Gold - Friend. Stay Gold often comes to visit the Mejiro family, and has respect for Mejiro McQueen. Stay Gold also entrusts Mejiro McQueen to watch over Dream Journey in her career.
** IRL, Stay Gold mated with 24 of Mejiro McQueen's daughters, with two of the most famously known ones being Oriental Art and Point Flag. A combination commonly known as "Stema Combination".
*Dream Journey - Friend. McQueen watches over Dream Journey from her debut race, having been asked by Stay Gold to support her.
* Gold Ship - Friend. The two are teammates on both the anime-only team, Team Spica, and the game-only team, Team Sirius. Gold Ship calls McQueen "Big Mac" and "Mac-Chan". Gold Ship loves pulling pranks on her often, often singling McQueen out. McQueen, who prides herself on the dignity of the Mejiro family, usually reacts with exasperation and/or scolding. Despite the frequent squabbles, they are close friends. Gold Ship is often one of the first to notice when McQueen is upset and cheers her up or distract her from her worries in her own unique way.
* Orfevre - Friend - Similar to Gold Ship, Orfevre also occasionally messes with McQueen in her own way.
** IRL, Mejiro McQueen is the damsire of Dream Journey, Gold Ship and Orfevre.
* Ikuno Dictus - Friend and roommate.
** IRL, McQueen sired one of Ikuno Dictus's foals and was seemingly fond of her as well.
* Satono Diamond - Friend. Satono Diamond admires Mejiro McQueen and is her main inspiration for racing. Both girls share several similitudes, such as coming from prestigious families and having great expectations placed on their shoulders.

*Mejiro Ryan - Family and rival.
*Mejiro Palmer, Mejiro Dober, Mejiro Ardan, Mejiro Bright, Mejiro Ramonu - Family.
* Special Week, Silence Suzuka - Teammates on both the anime-only team, Team Spica, and the game-only team, Team Sirius.
* Kitasan Black, Vodka, Daiwa Scarlet,   - Teammates on the anime-only team, Team Spica.
* Rice Shower, Winning Ticket, Narita Brian - Teammates on the game-only team, Team Sirius.
** McQueen looks out for Rice, and wants her to become stronger and more confident. When she notices how Rice feels sad due to people judging her for winning the Kikuka Sho, she proposes the idea to Rice to have her challenge the Tenno Sho (Spring) with her. Rice also likes McQueen's way of speaking, because it reminds her of the princesses in the storybooks she reads.

## Trivia

* Her initial character profile was uploaded to the official [https://x.com/uma_musu/status/849818975311503364 Twitter] account on April 5, 2017.
* "Umamusume: Pretty Derby Artworks Vol.1" explains why her character designer made specific choices when designing her in relation to her real-life counterpart, which were as follows:
** McQueen's real counterpart originally had a gray coat that turned white as he aged. This was implemented into her design by making her race outfit black with white accents.
** As her real life counterpart was a hall of fame inductee with the "Mejiro" name, she wears an armband on her right arm to show pride for the Mejiro family.
** Since her real life counterpart's jockey silks had teal sleeves, her socks are mainly teal.
*** McQueen is one of the many cases where the jockey's silk's colors were altered to create a more coherent design.
*She is one of the few Umamusume, like Haru Urara, Smart Falcon, and Almond Eye to have a career event if she loses her debut.
**Unlike Haru Urara and Almond Eye, the real Mejiro McQueen won his debut race.
