## Appearance

Bamboo Memory has short, primarily brown hair with a distinctive white streak running through the middle. A teal ribbon wraps around her right ear, and she wears a headband with the kanji "夢" (Yume), meaning "dream," symbolizing her dreamer spirit. Her blue eyes and sharp brows perfectly reflect her competitive nature. 

Her race outfit is complex, adorned with numerous gold accents and metallic designs. She sports a black jacket with a green lining on the inside, highlighted with intricate gold trims and bamboo-like patterns near the hem. The jacket's style evokes a sense of power and prestige, reminiscent of formal, militaristic attire. It features white buckles with gold latches, and a waist chain adds further detail. An armband on her left arm bears the kanji "風紀" (Fuuki), meaning "morals" or "discipline." She also wears long white gloves secured by three straps. 

Underneath the jacket, Bamboo Memory wears a green skirt with detailed gold hems, and a white vest edged in gold, buttoned at the center. Beneath the vest is a gray button-up shirt and bike shorts. Her boots are white with metallic gold detailing near the toes and a green stripe down the center, fastened with white straps and gold accents. She finishes the look with black socks trimmed in even more gold.
