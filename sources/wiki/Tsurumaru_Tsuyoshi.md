## Appearance

Tsuyoshi has a short brown bob-cut, with bangs parted in the center, and a small white streak of hair on her forehead. On the left side of her bangs is a red zig-zag shaped hair ornament, and on her right ear is a silver charm wrapped around her ear. Underneath her right ear she wears a hair-clip, featuring a yellow circle, with gray wings coming off the top. She has de-saturated light-purple eyes, and small but thick eyebrows.

Her racing uniform consists of a multi-colored purple, black, and red outfit. The top portion of her outfit is a dark purple sleeveless coat overlaid with golden trim and accents around the fabric's perimeter, and a red interior fabric, and attached to the ends of the coat are several golden diamonds. The jacket is clasped in the center with a golden corded rope. She wears a black high-neck top, with a triangle shaped cutout at the neckline. Over top, she wears a light pink corset-like piece around her waist. Underneath, a pair of simple white shorts. Her sleeves are detached from the rest of the ensemble, held to her upper arms by silver metal cuffs with zig-zag engravings. The sleeves themselves are purple with gold trim, left half open, revealing red compression sleeves. Around her right leg, she has a purple zig-zag shaped thigh belt with golden trim. She wears black heeled sandals with golden vamps, cutouts exposing her heel and toes. Underneath, she wears thick purple ribbon around her lower shins.

## Trivia

*Her design was shown with a redacted name on March 6, 2022, and her name was revealed on March 17, 2022, as she was a part of the mobile game's main story.
* She is the Umamusume with the third longest time to be trainable the game, with 1,076 days between when she was first announced and when she became trainable.
** The record holder is Biko Pegasus, who took 2,476 days to become trainable.
** Daring Tact overtook Tsurumaru's place on July 8, 2025, and currently it has been more than 1,461 days and counting.
*Like the horse IRL, Tsurumaru Tsuyoshi also possesses a weak constitution as an Umamusume. Though it is portrayed in more exaggerated ways, such as coughing harshly when she doesn't have sufficient energy to maintain a high success rate in training, and is shown to feel sick in her non-G1 victory animation.
