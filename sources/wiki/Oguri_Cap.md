## Biography

<blockquote style="font-style: italic; border-left: 5px solid #c85f63; padding-left: 1em; background-color: #fef9d8; background-color: #fffbe6; color: #3A7AD2;">"I'm going to win this for the folks back home... But first, sustenance."</blockquote>

A coolheaded dreamer from the countryside.

Originally hailing from the countryside, she enrolled in Tracen Academy after dominating the racing scene back home. She works to make her hometown proud, but try as she might, her clumsy side always comes out. At the academy, she's known as a fearsome gourmand, able to empty an entire pot of rice in the blink of an eye.

===Cinderella Gray Anime Introduction===
A gray-haired Umamusume that enrolled in Kasamatsu Tracen Academy. Oguri's a hearty eater that lives life at her own pace. On the track, she's a strong competitor who competes in a variety of intense races against her many rivals.

## Relationships

* Tamamo Cross - Friend, rival, and roommate. Tamamo lit a fire in Oguri when it came to racing, and they both get fired up when competing against each other. While Tamamo gets exasperated by Oguri's airheaded nature from time to time, she still genuinely cares for her as a friend. Oguri also appreciates her friendship, and would even eat less if Tamamo said she couldn't buy her a lot of food for her birthday. 
* Super Creek -  Friend, rival, and classmate. Part of the "Heisei Big Three".
* Inari One -  Friend and rival. Part of the "Heisei Big Three".
* Bamboo Memory - Rival from the same generation.
* Yaeno Muteki - Friend and rival. Yaeno has respect for Oguri as a racer, and they both get along. Oguri believes that Yaeno is strong, and vice versa. However, Yaeno also feels jealous of Oguri, and feels anger towards her because to her, Oguri took all of the attention away from the other racers of their generation like Ardan and Chiyono. Yaeno vows to defeat Oguri, and Oguri lights a fire into Yaeno that she cannot suppress.
* Sakura Chiyono O - Classmate from the same generation.
* Mejiro Ardan - Rival and classmate from the same generation.
* Special Week - Oguri's underclassman. They often compete against each other on food championships.
* Symboli Rudolf - Oguri's upperclassman in Cinderella Gray, she was the one who scouted Oguri back in Kasamatsu.
* Belno Light - Oguri's best friend in Cinderella Gray, classmates while in Kasamatsu.
** Belno Light supports Oguri Cap's racing goals, often by providing her with the right equipment and advice, as well as analyzing possible strategies on Oguri's races, most notably on her first run at the Arima Kinen. She is also her roommate in the manga.
* Jo Kitahara - Oguri's trainer in Cinderella Gray while she was in Kasamatsu. He became Oguri's Sub-Trainer after passing the National Exams. He was Oguri's stand-in Trainer for Yasuda Kinen and for her last race at the Arima Kinen.

* Ginjirou Musaka - Oguri's trainer in Cinderella Gray after moving to Tracen Academy.
* Fujimasa March - Oguri's friend in Cinderella Gray, and rival during her time in Kasamatsu.
* Dicta Striker - Rival and classmate in Cinderella Gray.
* Norn Ace - Friend and classmate/roommate while Oguri Cap was in Kasamatsu. Originally bullied Oguri Cap by making her sleep in the dorm's storage room. Norn would eventually become Oguri's big fan even after Oguri's transfer to the Nationals.
* Mini the Lady, Rudy Lemono - Friends and fellow classmates while Oguri Cap was in Kasamatsu.
* Obey Your Master - Rival. After racing against each other in the previous Japan Cup, Oguri can see through Obey's true self behind her disguise.
** IRL, Obey Your Master is based on Pay the Butler who ran on both Japan Cups in 1988 and 1989 against Oguri Cap.

* T.M. Opera O - Opera got Oguri's attention after a stunning performance at Mainichi Hai and her pursuit on winning the Satsuki Sho.
** IRL, T.M. Opera O was one of the racehorses who benefited from the "Oguri Cap Rule", allowing him to be able to run in the Classics by paying a huge entrance fee, despite registering late. He was also the first horse to win a Classic crown through this option. The Mainichi Hai is also one of the first graded races won by Oguri after transferring to Nationals and the first graded race that Opera won.

## Trivia

* Her initial character profile and announcement was uploaded on the official [https://x.com/uma_musu/status/832061971641966592 Twitter] account on February 15th, 2017.
* "Umamusume: Pretty Derby Artworks Vol.1" explains why her character designer made specific choices when designing her in relation to her real-life counterpart, which were as follows:
** As her real-life counterpart found success after transferring from Kasamatsu Racecourse to JRA central racing, the star she wears on the center of her collar represents the "the Star of Kasamatsu".
** The yellow diamond crown atop her head is reminiscent of the gold diamonds found on her real-life counterpart's jockey silks.
** Her sturdy boots are in reference to her real-life counterpart's historical story that, while facing injuries and ailments, making a spectacular comeback at the end of his career.
* Her Cinderella Gray costume is a semi-limited trainable unit, meaning that it could be pulled from gacha but cannot be chosen with paid 3-star trainee vouchers. 
** This is the first time a certain trainable unit from an already existing Umamusume became semi-limited. The initial trainable units of other semi-limited characters released prior to Oguri Cap's costume alt were all semi-limited.
* Oguri Cap has a tendency to shake herself from side-to-side, a trait that is present from her in-real-life counterpart which often means he is in a good mood. While it is subtle in her in-game G2 or lower victory animations, it is in full display in the anime and manga of Cinderella Gray and her idle animation in the costume of the same name during the Career mode.
* In the Global version of the game, she has a line where she talks about a debate she had with Tamamo Cross earlier on whether pineapple goes on pizza or not. However, in the Japanese version, the original line was talking about whether squid should be grilled whole or not.
* Her Christmas alternative outfit is based on when a fan gave the real life counterpart a Christmas-themed hood around the time of his final run at the Arima Kinen.
* Her love for food is a reference to the real horse, as he was known for having a voracious appetite.
* In her career story, Oguri Cap befriends a cat that she named "Hatsuratsu". This cat's name is a reference to the real Oguri Cap's nickname as a foal, which was "Hatsuratsu".
* Her voice actress was originally going to be Ayane Taketatsu, before she left the project for unknown reasons.
* Oguri Cap loves hot springs. This is because her IRL counterpart enjoyed equine hot springs.
** While the real Oguri Cap loved hot springs, he was not a fan of pool training, and was a terrible swimmer. This is referenced in her stamina training, as she is one of several Umamusume who uses a kick board while swimming.
