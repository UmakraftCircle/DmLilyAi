## Appearance

Yaeno-san, as fierce as fire and as calm as water, has focused brown eyes with a subtle greyish hue. Her short brown hair, calm in a bob-cut style, carries her fiery spirit with its spiked layers and a striking white blaze streak through her pointy bang. Her ears carry a unique and rare lightening at the tips. At the base of her right ear is a flower pinwheel with a hanging black ribbon. This pinwheel may symbolize the passage of life and growth in Shinto beliefs, represent good fortune, or simply just reflect her unique fashion sense.

Yaeno’s outfit draws inspiration from a Japanese Yukata, but with her own battle-ready spirit imbued. It’s multilayered and designed for mobility, featuring a sharp collar and a more flexible structure. The main dress is black and white with geometric red patterns near the chest, and it has a cutout design along with a sleeveless style. A traditional Obi belt is tied at the back, but Yaeno adds a separate red sash on top of it. She wears white sleeves tied to her arms with blue ribbons, and red fight gloves with metallic knuckles.  

Her skirt is ruffled black, are white at the hem, and features a large tie at the top. The black and white parts of the skirt appear to be stitched together, possibly as separate pieces when you see the sides. Yaeno’s socks have a gradient design from dark to light, and her shoes combine two classic Japanese styles. The right shoe is a Waraji, a lightweight sandal worn during labor, while the left is a Geta sandal, a platform shoe commonly worn daily. Imbalanced footwear reflecting her journey to find balance and harmony within herself.

## Trivia

* Her first appearance was in a chapter of Cinderella Gray on October 21st, 2020, but made her appearance on the website on February 9th, 2021.
* Her wild behavior from when she was younger is a reference to how in real life, Yaeno Muteki was an unruly horse ever since he was a foal.
* She likes puppy cafes, and in general finds animals cute.
* She is good at farming and tending to vegetables, such as carrots.
* She is easily embarrassed by romantic stories.
