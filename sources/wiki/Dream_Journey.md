## Appearance

Dream Journey has long and messy warm-gray hair, with a white underdye. Her fringed bangs almost cover her eyes, and she has several white and yellow streaks scattered throughout her hair. In the center of her bangs is one very large white strand of hair, and on the strand next to it, a diamond-shaped marking, and on top of her head, a large warm-gray ahoge. Her ears are large and fluffy, with yellow on the inside. On her right ear, she wears a metal ring with a diamond charm protruding upwards, attached is a white tassel that fades into a yellow gradient. Her narrow eyes are very unique, with her light purple irises fading into baby blue, and a dark blue pupil in the center. She wears a pair of oval-shaped glasses, with a thin, silver wire under-rim.

Her racing outfit is befitting of someone as cool and composed as her. The main piece of this ensemble is a piece of attire that is equal parts dress and jacket. It is colored a warm gray, similar to her hair. The jacket's collar is yellow on the right side, and black on the left. The dress is tied around her waist with a black leather belt, with several silver chains coming off of it. Underneath the jacket, she wears a dark red button-up shirt, with a line of several silver rings held together by leather straps going down the center of her chest. Her short and puffy sleeves are decorated with vertical yellow stripes. She wears black leather compression sleeves on both arms. On her left hand, several silver rings, and on her right, an elegant lacy glove. The dress' skirt reaches down to her ankles, with two slits on either side that reach above her thighs. The dress has a prominent red X design on the front, and is laced up with red ribbon at the front, and on the left side of the dress. She wears black heeled thigh-high boots with golden trim on the bottom.

## Relationships

* Orfevre - Younger sister and roommate. Family is the most important thing to her, and she does everything to make sure her family is happy and healthy. She is in charge of planning all of the travel-related endeavors when Orfevre races overseas, befitting her role in the Expedition Support Committee. The sisters share a room in Ritto dorm, where Dream Journey dotes intensely on Orfevre. Examples include preparing chamomile tea for her before bed, looking after her hair and clothes, and quietly watching over her sleeping face in the morning. Dream Journey affectionately calls her "Or," while Orfevre addresses her as "Ane-ue"(姉上). Orfevre respects and appreciates Dream Journey as her older sister and admires her terrifying strength. Orfevre sees her as reliable to the point where she lets her handle her schedule. 
** IRL, both of them were full brothers, sharing both sire Stay Gold and dam Oriental Art (Mejiro McQueen's daughter). Trainer Yasutoshi Ikee trained both Dream Journey and Orfevre, reflected in the sisters' close bond. Jockey Kenichi Ikezoe rode both siblings as their main jockey, earning their trust through patience with their violent temperaments.

* Gold Ship - Friend. The two have developed a friendly and easygoing relationship, and Dream Journey is grateful for Gold Ship being Stay Gold's confidant. Despite Gold Ship's eccentric and unpredictable personality, Dream Journey appreciates their casual bond. Gold Ship once referred to Stay Gold as "Konjiki Oyaji" (Golden Pops) and appears to deeply respect her, a sentiment Dream Journey shares.  
** IRL, the two horses never raced against each other due to Dream Journey retiring in early 2011 and Gold Ship debuting in July 2011. Their careers bookended different eras of Stay Gold's legacy. Dream Journey proving the bloodline's domestic excellence, and Gold Ship confirming its continued dominance.

* Nakayama Festa - Friend. Similar to Gold Ship, the two have developed a casual friendship. Apparently the Ochazuke in Nakayama's hometown is a favorite between Journey and Orfevre. Despite contrasting personalities, with Dream Journey being a "suave model student" who treats everyone with courtesy and formality compared to Nakayama's apathetic and lazy attitude, both connect with each other as they have their own special reasons for racing. Journey races as she was inspired by Stay Gold, while Nakayama races for the thrill and for her teacher's sake. Dream Journey approves of Nakayama's friendship with Orfevre, seeing her as a "good influence on her younger sister", despite her "wild" nature. 
**IRL, Nakayama Festa notably defeated Dream Journey in the 2010 Takarazuka Kinen, where Nakayama Festa won by a dramatic upset while Dream Journey placed 4th.
*Fenomeno - Friend. As Journey is the most polite out of the Umamusume in the Expeditionary Support Committee room, Meno rarely has any problems with Journey and they usually don't have qualms with each other. However, when it comes to prying too much, Journey easily manipulates situations so that Meno doesn't look into certain things, like when Meno questioned what exactly Journey was doing in suspicious areas around the school. Regardless, Journey acts cordial towards her, since Orfevre likes having her around so that she can tease her with Gold Ship and Nakayama Festa.

* Stay Gold - Friend and mentor figure. Dream Journey refers to Stay Gold as "Anego". This term is commonly used in yakuza circles to address a boss's wife or female relative, reflecting her deep reverence for her. The "big sis" mentioned in Dream Journey's profile who influenced her in childhood is Stay Gold, and Dream Journey races with the purpose of seeing the "journey's end" that Stay Gold spoke of. When asked about her inspiration, Dream Journey recalls: "At the end of the journey, I saw a streak of gold. A long, endless light stretching far beyond... I'll chase after it and go on another journey." Stay Gold appears to enjoy Dream Journey's tea, and though Journey says "I can never meet her when I want to," their bond remains the foundation of her racing philosophy. Before her arrival in Tracen Academy, Stay Gold asked her old ally Mejiro McQueen to watch over Dream Journey. The term "Anego" may also be wordplay, combining "ane" (sister) with Stay Gold's "go." Dream Journey's name itself is an allusion to Stay Gold's Hong Kong racing name, 「黃金旅程」(Golden Journey). Both the trainer and Dream Journey were connected by cheering on Stay Gold's run together.
** The game references the 19th Japan Cup in Dream Journey's character story, which is when Dream Journey found her admiration for Stay Gold. Dream Journey was sired by Stay Gold and out of Oriental Art (by Mejiro McQueen), making him a full brother to Orfevre, the 2011 Triple Crown winner. Dream Journey would also become Stay Gold's first G1 race winner, a monumental achievement that validated Stay Gold's prowess as a sire. Dream Journey retired to become a stud at Shadai Stallion Station in 2012, where Stay Gold was also stationed from 2003-2015.
*** IRL, Dream Journey, Gold Ship, Fenomeno, Nakayama Festa, and Orfevre are all sired by Stay Gold.

* Buena Vista - Friend and rival. Dream Journey says that when she looks at Buena Vista, she "dazzles so brightly that it's almost blinding". The 19th Japan Cup is when Dream Journey found her admiration for Stay Gold, and Buena found her admiration for Special Week, so the two feel as if they understand one another. Journey also finds Buena's innocent personality adorable, but Buena feels somewhat envious of Journey and Orfevre's sisterly bond as she is an only child.
* Win Variation - Friend. While Win Variation feels inferior in racing ability compared to Orfevre, Journey sees the talent in her. In fact, she even envies her for the fact that she gets to compete with Orfevre, while she is unable to due to the fact that she had stopped racing at that point.

* Vodka - Friend. The two have raced against each other several times. Journey has an appreciation for Vodka's straightforward nature. While others are confused by Vodka's desire to run in the Japanese Derby, Journey doesn't look down on her for it, and instead encourages her to run her own path. Vodka finds Journey cool as her upperclassman, and they get along well. Vodka is also one of the few people to not be afraid of Journey, as she doesn't notice anything suspicious about her.

* Daiwa Scarlet - Friend. They play chess together, and Dream Journey gives her chess tips and tricks.

* Kawakami Princess - Friend. Dream Journey always gives her tea, and in return, Kawakami Princess gives her information about Princess Fighter.

* Curren Chan - Friend. Curren Chan visits the Expeditionary Support Committee from time to time, and even comes up with a collaborative stream with Dream Journey to bring more publicity to the club. Curren also helps Journey with choreography during dance lessons.

* Mejiro McQueen - Friend. McQueen watches over Dream Journey from her debut race, having been asked by Stay Gold to support her.
** IRL, Mejiro McQueen is the damsire of Dream Journey.

* Aston Machan - Friend. Both of them shop for perfume together. Dream Journey also finds Machan's creativity intriguing and fascinating.

* Tosen Jordan - Friend. Dream Journey finds her interesting, while Tosen Jordan does her nails for her on occasion, like for her birthday.
* Red Desire - Friend. She helps Red Desire by giving her advice on overseas racing, and they both relate to each other as racers who have a major dream. While Red Desire dreams of taking on overseas races so that she could gain glory as a saint, Dream Journey dreams of reaching Stay Gold by racing.
* Sweep Tosho - Friend and underclassman. Sweep is jealous of Journey's prowess at persuading people, and asks her for help on how to manipulate people better.
* Lucky Lilac - Friend and underclassman. They get along well, and Lucky Lilac also views her as a strong senior. Dream Journey also appreciates Lucky Lilac's admiration of Orfevre.
** IRL, Dream Journey is the paternal uncle of Lucky Lilac.
*Seeking the Pearl - Friend. Pearl's dancing tends to exhaust Journey, and Journey can't handle Pearl's extroverted nature.
*Durandal - Friend. Durandal shows interest in the Expeditionary Support Committee, which Journey appreciates. She also gives Durandal advice on overseas racing.
*Rulership - Friend and rival. Journey, alongside Orfevre, finds Rulership's strategies to be cunning. Journey also acknowledges Rulership's strength as a racer.

## Real-life page (excerpt)

Source: https://umamusu.wiki/IRL:Dream_Journey

Dream Journey  is a Japanese racehorse that was active from 2006 to 2011. He was the first G1 winner for decorated Japanese sire Stay Gold. Known for his small and light stature, having debuted at a weight of 414 kg, Dream Journey is the lightest horse ever to have won a G1 JRA turf race. His major wins include the 2006 Asahi Hai Futurity Stakes, the 2009 Takarazuka Kinen, and the Arima Kinen. 
He is the full older brother to the 2011 Triple Crown winner Orfevre.

### Name Origin

Means "dreamlike journey". His name is an allusion to his sire, Stay Gold, Hong Kong name of 黃金旅程 (Golden Journey).

### Background

Dream Journey was born on February 24th, 2004 on Shiraoi Farm, and came from Stay Gold's second crop of foals. He was the first foal of Oriental Art, a mare who won a few dirt races, and was sired by Mejiro McQueen. Mejiro McQueen was a stayer who won four G1 races, including the 1990 Kikuka Sho, 1991-1992 Tenno Sho (Spring), and the 1993 Takarazuka Kinen. Dream Journey's sire, Stay Gold, won the Hong Kong Vase in 2001.

As he began his training under Yasuo Ikee at Ritto Training Center, it was noted that Dream Journey had a poor temperament, to the point where he would even injure his stablehands.

### Racing career

=== Two-year-old season (2006) ===

Dream Journey’s two-year-old season was impressive for a debuting Japanese thoroughbred, culminating in a major Grade 1 victory and recognition as a precocious talent. Dream Journey kicked off his career with a debut and maiden victory at Niigata Racecourse under jockey Masayoshi Ebina, followed by another win in the Fuyou Stakes.  In his first graded stakes race, the Tokyo Sports Hai Nisai Stakes, a slow break and a tendency to rush affected his performance, resulting in a third-place finish. Dream Journey would return to run in the Asahi Hai Futurity Stakes. Despite breaking poorly, Dream Journey surged past his rivals on the final stretch to secure his first group race win. At just 416 kg, he set a record as the lightest horse to ever win the race, utilizing his compact build and quick legs to clock the fastest final three furlongs in each of his four juvenile season starts. His victory in the Stakes also earned him the 2006 JRA Award for Best Two-Year-Old Colt.

=== Three-year-old season (2007) ===
Dream Journey opened the season in the Yayoi Sho, coming in as the second favorite behind Shinzan Kinen champion Admire Aura. Here, he remained among the midfield throughout much of the race and made a late charge in the final stretch, but was hampered by other horses and ultimately finished third. Dream Journey would appear at the Satsuki Sho as the third favorite, but unfortunately landed in eighth place. Later on, at the 74th Japanese Derby, he showcased impressive finishing speed yet placed fifth behind Vodka. 

After a summer break, his first autumn start was the Kobe Shimbun Hai with a new jockey, Yutaka Take, who had previously ridden key horses in Dream Journey’s lineage. In this race, Dream Journey came from the rear, passing competitors on the final stretch to claim his second graded race victory.

He then contested the 68th Kikka Sho as the second pick, making a move from the back at the third corner but was unable to catch the front runners, finishing fifth behind Asakusa Kings.
