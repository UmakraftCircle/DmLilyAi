## Biography

<blockquote style="font-style: italic; border-left: 3px solid #3DD1D7; padding-left: 1em; color: #D4C200;>"I've just gotta be the coolest to ever do it!"</blockquote>

Headstrong and full of life! A roadster with a heart of gold.

Dedicated to being the coolest, this Umamusume tries her best to act like a delinquent. She takes after her motorcycle-loving father, emulating his hobbies and attitudes down to the letter. Her grandstanding nature sees her charge daringly into any challenge, never afraid of what she might face. She has a bit of a cat-and-dog relationship with her roommate, Daiwa Scarlet.

## Relationships

* Daiwa Scarlet - Friend, roommate, rival, and classmate. The two first met after being paired together as roommates, but their conflicting personalities led to them bickering constantly, which drove them to become rivals on the track.
** Their relationship is inspired by the pair's rivalry in real life, racing against each other in five graded races, with both horses taking home two wins each: the 2007 Tulip Sho and 2008 Tenno Sho (Autumn) for Vodka, the Oka Sho and Shuka Sho for Scarlet in 2007, and one draw in the 2007 Arima Kinen.
** The two are also teammates on the anime-only team, Team Spica. The anime's official website describes the two's relationship as: "Her and her teammate, Daiwa Scarlet, are always fighting because they're polar opposites: a delinquent and an honor student. However, Scarlet is her best friend, comrade in arms, and the one she understands the most."
* Tanino Gimlet - Role model. To Vodka, Gimlet is the embodiment of what it means to be "cool". She idolizes her, often going as far as to mimic Gimlet's mannerisms.
** IRL, Vodka was sired by Tanino Gimlet. Both horses were also ridden by the jockeys Yutaka Take and Hirofumi Shii. The two are also the only father-daughter duo to both win the Japanese Derby in real life, winning in 2002 and 2007 respectively.
* Aston Machan - Friend and classmate. While they didn't get to race each other much, Vodka admires the fact that Machan chooses her own path, with Machan choosing to run in shorter distances, while Vodka chooses to run in the Japanese Derby instead of the Japanese Oaks. Machan appreciates her friendship, and makes her participate in the films she makes. 
** In real life, the two raced against each other twice, with Vodka winning once.
* Kawakami Princess - Upperclassman.
* T.M. Opera O - An Umamusume she thinks is really cool.
* Narita Brian - An Umamusume she thinks is really cool. In the game, Vodka often asks her for advice.
** This connection is most likely because Narita Brian's sire is Brian's Time, who is one of Vodka's grandsires.
* Dream Journey - Friend and rival. She is an Umamusume that she thinks is really cool. The two have raced against each other several times. Journey has an appreciation for Vodka's straightforward nature. While others are confused by Vodka's desire to run in the Japanese Derby, Journey doesn't look down on her for it, and instead encourages her to run her own path. Vodka finds Journey cool as her upperclassman, and they get along well. Vodka is also one of the few people to not be afraid of Journey, as she doesn't notice anything suspicious about her. 
** In real life, the two raced against each other four times.
* Red Desire - Friend and rival. Red Desire admires and idolizes her deeply. Vodka finds Red Desire to be cool, and they both get along as fellow "outcasts". Vodka encourages Red Desire to be herself, and to take on the world with her.
** IRL, the two raced each other twice in their careers. They both ventured to Dubai to pursue the Dubai World Cup, only for Vodka to be withdrawn due to a nosebleed after the Al Maktoum Challenge R3. With Vodka leaving Dubai early, staff posted a picture of Vodka in front of Red Desire's stable to ease her loneliness during that time.
*Sweep Tosho - Friend. Vodka finds her cool for the fact that she changed the reception of the Tiara races from "elegant" to beyond that, showing a raw and rough element of the Tiara route. She also helps her out with her racewear photos, and Sweep sometimes ropes her into "magical" situations.
* Special Week, Silence Suzuka, Tokai Teio, Kitasan Black, Gold Ship, Mejiro McQueen - Friends and teammates on the anime-only team, Team Spica.

## Trivia

* Her initial character profile was uploaded to the official [https://x.com/uma_musu/status/834598683794550784 Twitter] account on February 22, 2017.
* Vodka's real life counterpart began getting nosebleeds near the end of her career, which led to her no longer being able to run in the Grand Prix. As a reference to this event, Vodka tends to get nosebleeds when faced with anything related to romance. 
** She also tends to get nosebleeds when she gets overexcited before a race, as depicted in her Christmas outfit story.
* During the midpoint of Vodka's career, she has the goal of running, not winning, the Arima Kinen. Due to the fact that Vodka's base aptitude for long is F, it is essentially impossible to have her win without good legacies. While winning the Arima Kinen with Vodka is in fact possible, it is proven to be difficult.
** This is in reference to the real horse running her only Arima Kinen in 2007, but placing 11th.
* As depicted in her in-game comic, her flask is full of barley tea, not alcohol.
* Vodka's final race with Daiwa Scarlet was during the Tenno Sho (Autumn) in 2008. Vodka beat Daiwa Scarlet by a margin of 2cm, which is why Vodka is 2cm taller than Daiwa Scarlet.
* Vodka's blaze is reminiscent of the blaze on her real-life counterpart. However, in her race outfit, both of her sleeves appear to be white, this is a reference to her real-life counterpart having two white socks, though the real horse's socks are on her back left, and front left, hooves.
* While playing as her during Beyond Dreams scenario, Red Desire shows her admiration to her during the scenario's win animation.
* Despite being a mare in real-life, Vodka uses the masculine personal pronoun 俺 (Ore). Believe is the only other real-life mare who uses a masculine personal pronoun.
** In Japanese, 俺 is a very rough personal pronoun that is very rarely used by women, considered rude in formal situations but oftentimes considered cool in casual situations, which suits Vodka's personality. Vodka using 俺 might also be a connection to why Tanino Gimlet (real life sire) uses the same pronoun.
* "Umamusume: Pretty Derby Artworks Vol.1" explains why her character designer made specific choices when designing her in relation to her real-life counterpart, which were as follows:
** Due to her jockey's silks being yellow with a blue diagonal sash, the colors and pattern can be found throughout her entire design.
** Due to her real-life counterpart being a mare whose large feat was defeating stallions in the Japanese Derby, her outfit is designed to be reminiscent of a biker, to emanate the "coolness" of her feat.
** The clock on her necklace reads 3:40, which was the starting time of the 2007 Japanese Derby.
*Vodka is currently the only member of Team Spica to not have an official nendoroid made out of her.
