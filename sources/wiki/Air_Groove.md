## Biography

<blockquote style="font-style: italic; border-left: 3px solid #FAE053; padding-left: 1em; color: #0092CB;>"Just as my esteemed mother once did, I must train to perfection and lead others along my path!"</blockquote>

The coolly calculating Empress. Highly gifted and ambitious.

The vice president of Tracen Academy's student council, known to the other students as the "Empress". The daughter of an accomplished racer, she constantly strives to live up to her mother's racing prowess and ability to guide others. To this end, she has joined her friend and confidant, Symboli Rudolf, in the student council. Despite her strict schedule, she still makes time to tend the flowers in the garden.

## Relationships

* Duramente - Childhood friend and underclassman. While Groove gets exasperated by Duramente's nature, she still cares for her as a friend. Duramente looks up to Groove, and calls her "Guru-nee" out of respect.
** IRL, Air Groove is Duramente's granddam.
* Fine Motion - Roommate and friend. Air Groove often worries after her due to Fine Motion's obsession with ramen and her strange behavior due to her royal upbringing.
* Admire Groove - Friend and underclassman. Air Groove treats her like a little sister and dotes on her. Admire Groove acts like she does not need affection or love, but Air Groove changes this belief by caring for her during her career, and looking out for her. Secretly, Admire Groove also gets excited when Air Groove texts her, much to her own dismay.
* Rulership - Friend and underclassman. Groove is aware of how Rulership gets when she's upset, so she is a bit soft yet firm with her. Rulership looks up to Groove, and she is one of the few Umamusume that she uses the honorific "-san" for.
** IRL, both Admire Groove and Rulership were foaled by Air Groove.
* Symboli Rudolf, Narita Brian - Friends and student council co-members. Teammates on the anime-only team, Team Rigil.
* Silence Suzuka, Matikanefukukitaru - Close friends and rivals.
** Silence Suzuka is a friend and rival of hers. While Air Groove is a bit strict with her, considering how spaced out Suzuka can be, she still cares for her. They have a friendly rivalry, and Groove gets very excited watching Suzuka race, much to her embarrassment when others like Fukukitaru point it out.
** Matikanefukukitaru is also a friend and rival. Despite being strict with her due to Fukukitaru's antics and general personality, both Groove and Fukukitaru can get along, and both of them support Suzuka at her races. Fukukitaru however tends to blurt out things about Groove that embarrass her, like how she was actually intending to watch Suzuka's Tenno Sho (Autumn) in support rather than just for studying Suzuka's racing style.
* Taiki Shuttle - Close friend. Teammates on the anime-only team, Team Rigil.
* Mejiro Dober - Underclassman and rival. At first, Air Groove encourages Dober to see her as ideal to strive towards when she’s lost. As Dober’s career progresses, Air Groove eventually starts treating her as a rival and equal. 
* Daiwa Scarlet - Underclassman. Scarlet admires Air Groove even though her fearlessness can be intimidating, and often turns to her for guidance and to confide her troubles.
* Kiseki - Friend and underclassman. Air Groove considers Kiseki a straightforward Umamusume who always gets back on her feet, and admires her cheerful personality. Kiseki wants to live up to her expectations, and she also considers Air Groove as a main character.
** IRL, Air Groove is Kiseki's paternal grandmother.
* El Condor Pasa, Grass Wonder, T.M. Opera O, Hishi Amazon, Fuji Kiseki, Maruzensky - Fellow members of the anime-only team, Team Rigil.
* Matikanetannhauser, Winning Ticket, Daiichi Ruby, Dance Limerick - Fellow members of the Star Blossom exclusive team, Team Regulus.
** All of the members' real-life counterparts were trained by Yuji Ito.
* Bubble Gum Fellow - Rival and friend. The two were seen as their generation's potential Triple Tiara and Triple Crown winners. However, those hopes went unfulfilled. Bubble missed the Satsuki Sho and Japanese Derby due to injuries and later did not participating in the Kikuka Sho, while Air Groove missed the Oka Sho due to illness and lost the Shuka Sho. Air Groove is one of Bubble's main inspirations and visited her in the hospital after Bubble's injury. Air Groove ended Bubble's goal of winning the Autumn Tenno Sho twice.
** IRL, they were from the same generation and raced each other twice, in the 1997 Tenno Sho (Autumn) where Air Groove finished first and Bubble Gum Fellow second, and in the 1997 Japan Cup where Air Groove finished second and Bubble Gum Fellow third.
* Eishin Flash - Friend. Together they form the Ladybug Management Association. They are both ladybug enthusiasts.
* Air Messiah - Friend and underclassman. Messiah looks up to Air Groove as a strong racer, especially since she won the Japanese Oaks.
** IRL, both were owned by Lucky Field Co. Ltd, and shared Northern Taste as their damsire.
* Sweep Tosho - Underclassman. Sweep sees Air Groove quite often for disciplinary concerns. While Air Groove is strict with her, she sees great potential in Sweep as a racer. Though she initially views her as just an underclassman, she changes her approach when seeing Sweep's strong running and treats her as an equal. Despite her behavior, Sweep has respect for her deep down.

## Trivia

* Her initial character profile was uploaded to the official [https://x.com/uma_musu/status/862502678936641536 Twitter] account on May 10th, 2017.
* "Umamusume: Pretty Derby Artworks Vol.1" explains why her character designer made specific choices when designing her in relation to her real-life counterpart, which were as follows:
** On her race outfit, two belts with crown-like designs at the end represent her two G1 wins.
** Her real-life counterpart had a yellow bridle and reigns, which is why her sleeves have yellow threading.
** The feather motif (as seen on her left shoulder) was also implemented in Duramente's design, as Duramente is Air Groove's grandchild.
*** The feather motif is also present in her children's racing outfits such as Rulership and Admire Groove.
*Air Groove's dislike of flashing things comes from the 1996 Shuka Sho, as prior to the race, Air Groove was startled in her paddock by the photographers' camera flashes, causing her to injure her right foreleg and place 10th in the race.
**This caused the JRA to ban flash photography at races from that moment onward.
**This was also referenced in Season 2 of the anime, where Air Groove was startled by a young Umamusume's camera flash when she was giving her a tour of the school.
*Despite the real horse having a visible blaze, this was not adapted in her Umamusume design.
*Her like for Chopin's music is a reference to her final foal in real life, named Chopin.
*Air Groove sticking her tongue out in her original illustration is likely a reference to how in real life, Air Groove would lick humans.
*Her tolerance for ladybugs is likely a reference to her sire Tony Bin, an Irish-bred Italian racehorse. In Italy, ladybugs are known for good luck.
