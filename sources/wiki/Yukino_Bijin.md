Yukino Bijin is a character in Umamusume: Pretty Derby.

## Biography

<blockquote style="font-style: italic; border-left: 3px solid #C8D8EB; padding-left: 1em; color: #AF7E51;>"I wanna run in the Twinkly Series and become a spiffy city girl!"</blockquote>

She's gonna be a real city girl! Just a hardwork'n girl from the country.

A simple girl from the Iwate countryside, she would often lend a hand on the family farm. Yet despite her humble country background, her true desire is to be a cosmopolitan city girl and compete in the Twinkle Series. Her rural northern upbringing has made her a sincere Umamusume who doesn't back down from a challenge. She sees Gold City as that very ideal.

## Appearance

Yukino Bijin's looks suit her simplistic country bumpkin personality. Her large round eyes are light brown, and she has a simple brown bob haircut, with her bangs pushed to her left. She has a braid wrapped around the backside of her head. She wears a white headband, with two white earmuffs to boot, the left muff has a pink lace pattern at the base.

In her race outfit, the previously mentioned pink lace is now red. Around her neck is a white choker with a snowflake charm. She wears a white long sleeved dress with dark blue accents and simple floral patterns along the skirt and near her collar. The dress' collar is dark blue with white lace decor along the trim, and the dress is fastened with two rows of brown buttons down the center. On the top of either sleeve is two blue bands, and her lacy frilled sleeves are tied with a thin blue ribbon. She wears white fingerless gloves on either hand. Around her waist is a brown belt with gold buckles, and tied to the belt is a white ribbon with blue chevrons acting as a background to a floral pattern. Her box pleated skirt has dark blue stripes along the base, with floral patterns above. The fabric under the box pleat has yellow and red vertical pleats, with a fluffy petticoat underneath. Her white boots have dark blue heels and brown X-shaped ribbon laces, with the same red floral pattern on the outside. The boots' top edges are folded inward, with a cream cable knitted pattern on the inside, adorned by blue bows on the outside, and frills coming out of the top.

## Relationships

* Gold City - Close friend. She aspires to be a pretty and modern girl like her, while Gold City envies Yukino's simplistic way of life.
* Manhattan Cafe - Friend and roommate. The two frequently spend time together, and it seems that Yukino's bright demeanor tends to put Cafe at ease.
** IRL, Yukino Bijin and Manhattan Cafe had a foal in 2006. The foal was never registered, so is only listed as Yukino Bijin's 2006.
* Winning Ticket - Friend.
** IRL, Yukino Bijin and Winning Ticket had a foal in 2004 named Maruhachi Dolan.
* Mayano Top Gun - Friend.
* Haru Urara - Rival in competitions of hometown pride, with Yukino representing Iwate.
* Sakura Bakushin O - Friend. Bakushin reminds Yukino of her father, and talking to her helps to put her at ease.
** IRL, both were sired by Sakura Yutaka O.

## Trivia

* Her initial character profile was uploaded to the official [https://x.com/uma_musu/status/845471625889796097 Twitter] account on March 24th, 2017.
** This tweet was a character name prediction quiz, that encouraged fans to try and guess which horse she's named after. The hint was "An Umamusume from Tohoku! She's going to do her best in the Twinkle Series so she can bring glory to her hometown!"
* Her talented hair braiding is a reference to real life, as the real Yukino Bijin always had her mane braided when she was active.
* Yukino Bijin is one of several Umamusume to narrate their careers themselves in quick mode rather than have them be narrated by their trainer.
** Her self-narration is also unique in comparison to others, with it being her writing letters back home about her time at Tracen rather than simply being her thoughts.
* In her career story, Yukino Bijin faces off with an NPC Umamusume named Harp Alpha in the Triple Tiara route. Harp Alpha is based off the real life horse Vega, who won the Oka Sho and Japanese Oaks in 1993.

## Real-life page (excerpt)

Source: https://umamusu.wiki/IRL:Yukino_Bijin

' was a Japanese racehorse who was active between 1992 and 1993. She was popular among female fans, and was considered a particularly good-looking horse.

### Name Origin

"Yukino" (kanji: 雪乃) means "of the snow," and "Bijin" (kanji: 美人) means "beautiful lady."

### Background

Sakura Yutaka O, Yukino Bijin's sire, won the 1986 Tenno Sho (Autumn). He was a son of Tesco Boy, who retired from racing in Britain to become a successful foundational sire in Japan. Yukino Bijin's dam was Fatima, a daughter of American G1 winner Royal Ski.

### Racing career

=== Two-year-old season (1992) ===

Yukino Bijin won her debut race on August 10th, 1992, over 850 meters on dirt at Morioka Racecourse in Iwate. She spent the remainder of the year running short-distance dirt races; some sources claim that due to the ongoing racing boom at the time, her owner, Yukikatsu Arai, had to keep her in regional races while waiting for room to transfer her to central JRA stables. Yukino Bijin's only loss in Iwate was the listed Nambukoma Sho, where she placed 5th behind Ebisu Sakura.

=== Three-year-old season (1993) ===

Early in 1993, Yukino Bijin transferred to central racing. She won the open-class Crocus Stakes, her first turf race, on February 27th, 1993. On April 11th, she entered the Oka Sho, the first leg of the Triple Tiara. She began to accelerate, but could not reach the pace-setter Vega and placed second by only a neck.

On May 23rd, Yukino Bijin again placed second to Vega in the Japanese Oaks, this time by almost two lengths. While there were originally plans to have her race in the Radio Nikkei Sho (G3) after the Oaks, it was decided to have her rest until the fall.

After her break, Yukino Bijin entered the Queen Stakes (G3) on October 3rd as the favorite. She beat Hokuto Vega by two lengths, earning her first and only graded stakes victory. After this, she entered the Queen Elizabeth II Cup on November 14th, the final leg of the Triple Tiara at the time. She placed tenth, the lowest placement in her career, behind Hokuto Vega.

Yukino Bijin then won the open-class Turquoise Stakes against Hokuto Vega on December 18th. However, due to a fracture in her left front leg, she was retired as a racehorse in September 1994.
