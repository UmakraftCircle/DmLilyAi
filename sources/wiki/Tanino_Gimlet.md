## Appearance

Tanino Gimlet's short, crimson colored hair bears a single white stripe that runs down across her bangs. A black eyepatch sits below it, on her right eye. The inside of her ears are teal, and there is a small patch of golden hair on the left of her face. Her eyes are yellow, and her ringed pupils are brown. There is a golden ornament fixed onto her right ear.

Her race outfit consists of a yellow sleeveless undershirt with a flared collar, a ruffled blue striped top, and a navy-blue vest decorated with gold baubles atop them. A teal neckpiece resembling a tie hangs at her neck, under a choker adorned with a jewel. A pair of navy-blue sleeves with white and gold accents are attached to the undershirt with straps. She also wears a pair of color-mismatched gloves. A pair of white shorts are sectioned off by a navy-blue half skirt fastened down by a blue belt that spans asymmetrically across her waist. An arrowhead-shaped jewel and silver arrowhead-shaped ornaments wrap around the side of her waist, each connected by a ring on the belt. She wears fishnet-patterned tights on both legs. Blue thigh garter wraps around her right leg. A pair of belts wrap around her lower legs, just above her tall black boots, decorated with belts and straps of their own.

## Trivia

* She was announced during 4th EVENT SPECIAL DREAMERS!! on day two of the Yokohama leg which took place on May 5th, 2022.
* Tanino Gimlet's habit of kicking down fences is based on her real life counterpart, who is notorious for destroying his enclosure's fencing.
* Character Design Notes from Umamusume: Pretty Derby Artworks Vol.04:
** From the etymology of "Gimlet", the outfit is designed to give a military uniform or bartender impression. The outfit is kept dark to highlight the colors of original horse's jockey silk.
** The white gloves and soles of the boots reflect the markings on the motif horse's legs, while the black arm cover and boots are taken from his dark coat color.
** As she often kicks and breaks fences as the "God of Destruction" in Umamusume, she is given long, sturdy looking boots to hope to protect her legs.
*In her career story, the term "MC Rotation", used to describe running both the NHK Mile Cup and the Japanese Derby back-to-back, is a reference to the real term "Matsukuni Route". This term is based on how trainer Kunihide Matsuda would train horses like Tanino Gimlet and King Kamehameha, where he would make them run both the NHK Mile Cup and the Japanese Derby to test their strength.
