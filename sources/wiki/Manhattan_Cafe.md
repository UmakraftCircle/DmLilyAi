## Appearance

Cafe's most prominent feature is her long, jet-black, hair. The ends are cut perfectly blunt, with bangs framing her face. She has incredibly long bangs, swept to the side covering her left eye. Atop her head is a large curved white ahoge. On the right side of her jet-black ears, she wears a gold earring with a rectangular teal charm attached to it. She has pale yellow eyes with a simple, yet eerie-looking, black pupil in the center.

As for her race outfit, she wears a black knee-length overcoat with intricate gold embellishments on either side, and gold pockets. She wears a black long sleeved shirt underneath, with gold stripes on the cuff, and black gloves. Underneath this shirt, she wears a white collared shirt, tied by a yellow tie with black stripes at the end, two stars, teal and black, in the center. There is a simple black choker around her neck. She wears a black pleated skirt with gold decor, and white trim around the edge. There's a black belt with gold chains tied around her waist, over her shirts. On her legs, she wears black stockings, the right shin decorated with golden diamonds. She wears simple white loafers with black heels.

## Trivia

* Her initial character profile was uploaded to the official [https://x.com/uma_musu/status/850174601208279040 Twitter] account on April 6th, 2017.
** This tweet was a character name prediction quiz, that encouraged fans to try and guess which horse she's named after. The hint was "A beautiful Umamusume with jet-black hair. Her speed is outstanding, but she can't run well on rainy days. She's also an active actress."
*** Manhattan Cafe is no longer an actress, as that aspect of her character was discarded shortly before the game's release. This former setting was given to her due to her in-real-life counterpart playing the role of his sire, Sunday Silence, due to their similar physical appearances in the documentary "Fortunate Encounter, The Dream for Thoroughbreds".
**** This scrapped aspect of her character was referenced in Red Desire's biography, as Red Desire used to be a child actress.
* Cafe is said to be "haunted" by a ghost she considers her friend. All students at Tracen that have said they have seen this ghost say she looks incredibly similar to Manhattan Cafe herself. Many liken this ghost to be Sunday Silence, Cafe's sire in real life, as the two looked very similar. 
** This is further supported in an event in Jungle Pocket's career story, where Manhattan Cafe begins to speak gibberish. However, unscrambling this text reveals the names in the pedigree of the real-life Sunday Silence and includes the names: Halo (sire), Hail to Reason (grandsire), Cosmah (dam of sire), Wishing Well (dam), Understanding (sire of dam/damsire), and Mountain Flower (granddam).
* The trio consisting of Jungle Pocket, Agnes Tachyon, and Manhattan Cafe is often referred to as "JAM". But with Dantsu Flame, they are also known as the quadruplet often referred to as "JAMD".
* She hates black tea, which contrasts with Agnes Tachyon's hatred for bitter flavors such as coffee.
* She is shown to like scary stories, as she tells Zenno Rob Roy and Rice Shower them. This is also referenced in a Pakatube video series, where she told scary stories from [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tQrKvUY0w0E August 13th] to [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nXTWVpbNGdk&t=52s August 15th, 2025].
** The first scary story, posted on August 13th, corresponds with the date of Manhattan Cafe's death on August 13th, 2015. This is likely why her kimono has red spider lily patterns, as they symbolize death in some Asian cultures.
** The third scary story, posted on August 15th, corresponds with her voice actress' birthday, Yui Ogura.
*She likes to go to cat cafes, and cats tend to play with her belt.
*In her career story, her weight tends to fluctuate rapidly. This is a reference to the real horse, as his weight decreased rapidly when traveling for races, causing him to miss the Satsuki Sho and Japanese Derby due to him no longer meeting the weight requirements.
