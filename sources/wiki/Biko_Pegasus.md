## Appearance

Biko Pegasus has short, spiky brown hair, with tufts of it framing her face, and wears a winged star hairclip on her right ear. She has thick eyebrows and round blue eyes.

Her racing outfit resembles a tokusatsu superhero’s gear. She wears a short-sleeved zip-through top with diagonal white and purple stripes and bright red cuffs, and puffy white shorts with red-trimmed purple panels running down the sides. Her outfit is cinched at the waist by a chunky white and silver belt that widens in the center; the buckle is a large, glowing winged horseshoe with a star on it. A short red neckerchief with gold and white stripes is tucked into her collar, with the ends trailing behind her. She wears white and purple fingerless gloves, with a silver gauntlet on her right hand. Her shoes are white and purple ankle boots with red trim around the top. Lastly, she wears a different winged star hairclip on her right ear, with faceted white wings and a gold star edged with pale blue.

## Relationships

Category:Characters Without Relationships 

* Hishi Akebono - Friend, rival, and roommate. While Biko sees Akebono as a strong rival, they both get along well off the track, and they both roleplay as Carrotman characters together. Because of the size difference between them, people gawk at Akebono online, which angers Biko. However, Akebono assures Biko that she is fine, as she is confident in her size.
* Shinko Windy - Rival and Friend. She looks up to her and really admires Windy's racing spirit. The two are also often depicted as "Hero and Villain" duo, playing and watching hero shows together.
* Curren Chan - Rival and Friend.
* Hishi Amazon - Rival and Friend.
* Narita Brian - Rival and Friend.
* Mayano Top Gun, and Sweep Tosho - Friends and classmates.
* Fenomeno - Friend. Biko and Fenomeno spar in Tracen Academy's dojo sometimes, and they hang out together in the New Year. Fenomeno also holds Biko's hands when in crowds so she doesn't get lost or kidnapped.
* Samson Big - Friend and upperclassman.

## Trivia

*Her initial character profile was uploaded to the official [https://x.com/uma_musu/status/860325977313783808 Twitter] account on May 4, 2017.
**This tweet was a character name prediction quiz that encouraged fans to try and guess which horse she's named after. The hint was "She is a passionate and energetic Umamusume. Although she is small in stature, she has the ability to surpass even the greatest heroines."
*She is the Umamusume who took the longest time to be implemented into the game, with 2,476 days between when she was first announced and when she was added as a trainee.
**Meanwhile, Dream Journey was implemented as a trainable character in the game on the same day she was announced, having the "shortest" time gap from introduction to being playable trainee.
*She is very popular on Umatok.
