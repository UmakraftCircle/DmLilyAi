## Appearance

Royce and Royce has long brown hair, tied into a bun at the back of her head, and decorated with braids. Underneath this bun, she wears a diagonally-striped bow, colored purple and dark purple. The first layer of her side bangs are cut bluntly, and she has two long strands of hair falling past her shoulders on either side. She has a purple ear cover on her right ear, accompanied by a golden ear clip, with a golden diamond linked to it. She wears green over-rim glasses, and has de-saturated red eyes.

Her racing uniform consists of a multi-colored and multi-layered outfit. A reddish-orange jacket with white puffed sleeves and black cuffs is worn over a white button-up top. She wears a yellow layered half-skirt with a purple interior seen in the ruffles. Underneath this is a white pencil skirt, with purple "X" shaped laces on either side, and teal slits in the front. On the right side of her waist, she has a teal piece of fabric attached to a black belt. She also wears black tights, and black heeled boots with gold accents.

## Relationships

*Nice Nature, Twin Turbo, Matikanetannhauser, Ikuno Dictus, Sounds of Earth - Teammates on the anime-only team, Team Canopus.
**Royce is very supportive of Ikuno as a racer, and wants to have a fandom the way she does one day. She also makes fans to support her at races.
**Royce views Nature as a potential idol considering her traits. They both get along well as they both admire Tokai Teio, yet want to defeat her one day.
*Shinko Windy - Friend and roommate. Royce makes note of Windy's charms and quirks, and learns them to her advantage. Meanwhile, Windy gets intimidated when Royce imitates her mannerisms, like when she roars. However, Windy struggles to sleep in her dorm room if Royce isn't around, showing a bond between the friends. Windy also bites Royce when she's asleep.
*Biwa Hayahide - Friend and rival. Royce views Hayahide as a strong and popular racer, and wants to surpass. She even goes so far as to temporarily remove her glasses while training, as she doesn't want her and Hayahide to "overlap" in traits. She also finds Hayahide's friendship with Winning Ticket and Narita Taishin interesting as their personalities mesh according to her. Both Hayahide and Royce get along quite well, as they are both data analysts and bond over how glasses can be a struggle while racing.
**In real life, Royce and Royce and Biwa Hayahide have competed with each other numerous times, such as the 1993 Kikuka Sho and 1994 Sankei Sho All Comers (G3). Biwa Hayahide won both of these races.
*North Flight - Friend. Flight helps Royce out with fashion from time to time, which aids Royce in finding new character appeals for fanservice.
*Sakura Chitose O - Friend. Chitose O gives medical advice to Royce, and helps her maintain a healthy and nutritional diet as Royce is a late bloomer.

## Real-life page (excerpt)

Source: https://umamusu.wiki/IRL:Royce_and_Royce

'  was a Japanese racehorse that was active from 1992 to 1996. He is best known as a "bronze collector", who placed well in graded races despite not winning them.

### Name Origin

The initial name proposed was "Rolls-Royce", after the car brand; However, it was rejected by the JRA since brand names are not allowed as to prevent advertising. The name then became "Royce and Royce".

### Racing career

=== Two-year-old season (1992) ===
Royce and Royce made his debut on December 13th, in a 1,600-meter turf race on Nakayama Racecourse. He placed 2nd, and he placed 2nd once again in his second maiden race attempt on December 26th.

=== Three-year-old season (1993) ===
For months, Royce and Royce was unable to win a maiden race, and on May 1st, he was even entered into the open-class Aoba Sho, only to place 3rd. On June 6th, he finally won his maiden race after numerous attempts. He attempted to win a graded race in the Radio Tampa Sho (G3) on July 4th, but lost and placed 3rd.

Instead of resting throughout the summer, he ran throughout the rest of August and September, where he placed 2nd in three open-class races. As he placed well in the St. Lite Kinen (G2), he was granted entry to the Kikuka Sho on November 7th. He advanced during the race, but couldn't catch up to Biwa Hayahide and placed 7th. He then ran in the open-class Toji Stakes on December 18th, where he placed 5th.

=== Four-year-old season (1994) ===

While he lost and placed 2nd in the open-class Kumpu Stakes on May 7th, he won the open-class Murasaki Sho weeks later on May 29th. He then lost and placed 8th in the Epsom Cup (G3) on June 11th, but on August 6th, he attained his final victory in the open-class Sarobetsu Stakes. Throughout the rest of the year, he went on to place 3rd in three consecutive graded races, in the Sankei Sho All Comers (G3), Tenno Sho (Autumn), and the Japan Cup. 
=== Five-year-old season (1995) ===
Royce and Royce continued his losing streak, as he placed 4th in the Osaka Hai (G2) on April 2nd. After a long break, he returned to place 3rd in the open-class Fuji Stakes on November 12th, and he placed 7th in the Japan Cup weeks later on November 26th. He then challenged the Arima Kinen on December 24th, where he placed 7th to Mayano Top Gun.

=== Six-year-old season (1996) ===
Royce and Royce only ran in three races in his six-year-old season. On January 21st, he competed in the American Jockey Club Cup (G2), where he placed 4th. His placements only worsened, as he placed 8th in the Nikkei Sho (G2) on March 17th, and placed 11th on April 21st to Sakura Laurel in the Tenno Sho (Spring).

==== Death ====
In the summer break, Royce and Royce developed intestinal torsion as he was grazing. At six years old, he passed away due to complications on June 14th, 1996.
