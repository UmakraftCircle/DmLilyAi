## Biography

<blockquote style="font-style: italic; border-left: 3px solid #FFCD00; padding-left: 1em; color: #EA504A;>"The track's like a dance floor, and I'm feelin' groovy today!"</blockquote>

Keeping it old-school! A real hot rod!

A dashing Umamusume with a sisterly demeanor and a retro way of speaking. She is a speed demon who adores the pure joy of the wind in her face. On the road, her hot rod is the one burning rubber, but in a race, it's her own two feet tearing up the track. Despite her tremendous power, she isn't pompous, and is well-liked by everyone.

===Cinderella Gray Anime Introduction===
An Umamusume attending Tracen Academy, who has the aura of an older sister. She often advises Symboli Rudolf. She has a friendly personality and is loved by everyone, even if her way of speaking tends to be a bit dated.

## Relationships

* Sakura Chiyono O - Friend and underclassman. Maruzensky is someone Chiyono looks up to a lot, and Maruzensky is supportive towards her in return. Maruzensky is Chiyono's main reason for running in the Asahi Hai Futurity Stakes and the Japanese Derby, as she wants to emulate her. 
** IRL, Sakura Chiyono O is sired by Maruzensky.
* Special Week - Friend and underclassman. Maruzensky makes sure to be someone she can rely on, particularly when Spe first transferred to Tracen Academy.
* Rice Shower - Friend and underclassman. Maruzensky usually invites Rice for drives, even when they occasionally get stuck in traffic.
* Winning Ticket - Friend and underclassman. Like many others, Maruzensky invites her out for fun.
** IRL, Maruzensky is the damsire of Special Week, Rice Shower and Winning Ticket.
* Tazuna Hayakawa - A good friend of Maruzensky's. The two often hang out together at bars and lounges after school hours.
* Symboli Rudolf - Teammates on the anime-only team, Team Rigil. Friends in Cinderella Gray.
* Air Groove,  El Condor Pasa, Grass Wonder, Taiki Shuttle, T.M. Opera O, Narita Brian, Fuji Kiseki - Teammates on the anime-only team, Team Rigil.
* Hishi Amazon - Teammates on the anime-only team, Team Rigil. Often being used as the team's Goal post.
* Mr. C.B. - Classmate and friend. They hang out together, and C.B. is one of the few people to notice the sadness Maruzensky feels over not getting to compete in the classic races. 
* Katsuragi Ace - Classmate and friend.
* Smart Falcon - Founder of the Umadol Group Runaway Sisters that Maruzensky joins.
* Ines Fujin, Mihono Bourbon, Silence Suzuka - Fellow Runaway Sisters.
* Mejiro Ardan - Friend. Maruzensky looks out for Ardan sometimes, and notices when she feels down.
* Mejiro Ramonu - Friend. Maruzensky is completely unbothered by Ramonu's standoffish behavior, and views her as a good friend of hers.

## Trivia

* Her initial character profile and announcement was uploaded on the official [https://x.com/uma_musu/status/826988616861704193 Twitter] account on February 1, 2017.
* Her penchant for speaking with old-fashioned slang is due to her real life counterpart being the only playable Umamusume character that raced in the 1970's.
* As of this point, in terms of real life racing eras, Maruzensky is the oldest playable Umamusume to be added to the game.
** While there are other Umamusume in the game based on real horses that are older than Maruzensky, such as Saint Lite, Haiseiko and Speed Symboli, they are all non-playable Umamusume, with Maruzensky acting as somewhat of a stopgap.
* Her unique skill on her initial trainable unit is named "Red Shift/LP1211-M" (紅焔ギア/LP1211-M), with LP referring to a Lamborghini, and "1211" referencing the time in which her in-real-life counterpart reached the 1,400 meter mark during the 1976 Asahi Hai Sansai Stakes (1:21.1).
* Maruzensky owns a Lamborghini Countach nicknamed Tata or Ta-chan. Not only is it the sports car that popularized the Italian Wedge, but it was also first produced in 1974, the IRL horse's birth year.
** In Japan, a person must be 18 to legally acquire a license to own and drive a car. While Umamusume do age differently in comparison to humans, it can be presumed Maruzensky must be equivalent to a human adult to legally own the car.
* In her Career Mode, there are only five runners in the Spring Stakes, and Maruzensky is always stationed at the furthest gate when she enters the Japanese Derby.
** The small field in the Spring Stakes references the fact that as the real Maruzensky became more and more successful, owners began withdrawing their horses from races he'd entered because they feared losing to him. One open-class race he entered was nearly canceled.
** Maruzensky's jockey, Seiichi Nakanowatari, begged for him to be able to run the Derby, saying "I don't care if the horse starts from the furthest outside. We won't interfere [with] the other horses. We don't need the prize money. We just want to see how the horse would do." The JRA upheld their ruling that he was ineligible, because even horses transported in utero from abroad were not allowed to enter the Triple Crown races at the time, but the restriction was later relaxed thanks in part to the discussion surrounding Maruzensky's exclusion.
* "Umamusume: Pretty Derby Artworks Vol.1" explains why her character designer made specific choices when designing her in relation to her real-life counterpart, which were as follows:
** Due to her real-life counterpart's silks being red with yellow sleeves, her race outfit contains several elements featuring yellow trim.
** The horse was described as a "Supercar," due to him being so fast, which led to the artist designing an outfit that reminded them of a bright red sports car.
** Due to the previously mentioned nickname, the emblem on the side of her garter ring was designed to represent a sports car emblem.
