## Biography

<blockquote style="font-style: italic; border-left: 3px solid #6F2678; padding-left: 1em; color: #63666A;>"Grawr! Better not look away, or I’mma bite’cha!"</blockquote>

A total prankster! Watch out: she bites!

A wild wolf who'll chomp ya good—or so she says. This Umamusume is a rascal who loves pranks, likely owing to the fact that she craves attention from others. Her vicious facade and munchy tendencies are clear pleas for affection, though she hasn't realized it herself. She also speaks with a similarly wild flare.

## Appearance

Shinko Windy has short brown  hair, with parts that curl up from the bottom. A white tuft sits at the center near her forehead, and above that lies a thick ahoge which angles to the left. Her head is framed by dark-brown patterns in her hair that could be likened to a pair of pincers, at the sides of her head. A white band with purple accents sits at the base of her right ear. Her eyes are a dark purple, but grow lighter near the bottom. Her sharp pupils are a darker shade of purple. Additionally, her teeth are pointy.

Her race outfit consists of a purple cropped jacket bearing a fur collar, under the jacket is a black tattered top, resembling a long corset, cut off at the upper body. She wears a necklace adorned by sharp, fanged teeth. Her wrists, along with her ankles, are wrapped in a furry trim, which secures both her gloves and her boots. A series of black bands is tied around her left arm. Her black, cropped shorts are topped with fur trim at the thighs and side along with a purple studded belt with five fanged teeth at the buckle. She wears ripped tights secured to her legs by thigh garters. Her black boots have a purple trim at the flats of the feet.

## Relationships

* Agnes Digital - To Windy's dismay, Digital is very into Windy's mannerisms, and finds her adorable rather than menacing.
* Biko Pegasus - Friend and arch nemesis. They play the role of Hero and Evil villain together very much like kids.
* Hishi Amazon - Dorm leader. As the head of Miho dormitory, Hishi Amazon has seen and dealt with Windy's antics on a regular basis. Amazon tries to control her when Windy is on a biting spree.
* Taiki Shuttle - Friend.
* Royce and Royce - Friend and roommate. Royce makes note of Windy's charms and quirks, and learns them to her advantage. Meanwhile, Windy gets intimidated when Royce imitates her mannerisms, like when she roars. However, Windy struggles to sleep in her dorm room if Royce isn't around, showing a bond between the friends. Windy also bites Royce when she's asleep.
* Seeking the Pearl - Friend. As a team, they call themselves "Worldwide Windy".

## Trivia

*Her initial character profile was uploaded to the official [https://x.com/uma_musu/status/867935576531521536 Twitter] account on May 25, 2017.
**This tweet was a character name prediction quiz, that encouraged fans to try and guess which horse she's named after. The hint was "A naughty and mischievous girl. She is a problem child who always bites other Umamusume due to her abundant fighting spirit and guts."
*Before the Champion's Cup in her career, Windy is asked about a "new generation" emerging in the dirt racing sphere, specifically mentioning "a strong person from Funabashi" and "a chestnut-haired visitor from Morioka" as two regional Umamusume who have been garnering a lot of attention. 
**These are likely in reference to Abukuma Poro and Meisei Opera, the NAR Grand Prix Horse of the Year winners in 1997-1998 and 1999 respectively.
