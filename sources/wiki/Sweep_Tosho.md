## Lead

Sweep Tosho is a character in Umamusume: Pretty Derby.

## Biography

<blockquote style="font-style: italic; border-left: 3px solid #BC204B; padding-left: 1em; color: #926DDA;>"I can wield magic, you know! 'Cause I'm Magical Girl Sweepy!"</blockquote>

A totally spoiled brat! She swears she'll be a witch someday!

A stubborn grandma's girl, she aspires to one day become a magical girl. Hoping to match her grandmother, a self-proclaimed witch, in magical prowess, she truly believes that if she keeps on winning races, her magic will also level up in turn. Since she's still learning magic, she often falls back on the good old-fashioned way of solving problems—with force. When she starts crying, it's hard to get her to stop.

## Appearance

Sweep Tosho has striking violet eyes. She has notably sharp eyebrows, and auburn hair that cascades into soft, uneven bangs. Her signature braids are styled into elegant loops, and she has flowing twintails that are adorned with gleaming gold and crimson ornaments at the ends. 

Her witch’s cap, a most treasured gift from her grandmother, features golden bands and ear pockets tailored for her ears tipped with the same ornaments you can find on her hair and capped off with a cute red ribbon. However, the ears on her hat are fake and merely decorative, and move in sync with her actual ears inside the hat. Her robe has a black exterior that contrasts with a rich purple lining, golden embroidery stitched down along the hem, and seamlessly blending with a striking red and yellow diamond pattern that can also be seen near her chest. Her open robe is fastened at the top and cinched by two belts—one practical, one decorative—and it reveals a red cardigan over a shirt and tie. She also wears white gloves with her outfit. And finally, she wears a layered white skirt with red trims, and thigh-high black boots capped with golden heels.

## Relationships

* Air Groove - Upperclassman. Sweep sees Air Groove quite often for disciplinary concerns. While Air Groove is strict with her, she sees great potential in Sweep as a racer. Though she initially views her as just an underclassman, she changes her approach when seeing Sweep's strong running and treats her as an equal. Despite her behavior, Sweep has respect for her deep down.
* Fuji Kiseki - Upperclassman. Dubbed the wizard to Sweep's witch for handling her mischievousness and seemingly always being one step ahead of her. She also tries to encourage Sweep to eat her vegetables, much to the latter's dismay.
* Kitasan Black - Friend. She is seen hanging out with Sweep, and shows genuine interest in her magic. Kitasan sees her as a nice girl deep down, while Sweep finds Kitasan to be interesting.
** In real life, Sweep Tosho had a foal sired by Kitasan Black in 2019 named Piedra de Luna.
* Kawakami Princess - Friend and rival. They watch Princess Fighter every Sunday morning together, and they bond together as the witch and the princess. Sweep also encourages Kawakami when she's in a slump, and wants her to run in her own way regardless of the risk.
** In real life, Sweep Tosho and Kawakami Princess challenged each other a few times. Most notably, they competed with each other in the 2006 Queen Elizabeth II Cup, where Kawakami Princess obstructed Yamanin Sucre and got herself disqualified.
* Rhein Kraft - Friend. Rhein Kraft looks up to Sweep, and got inspired to pursue the Triple Tiara because of her insistence that Tiara route runners are strong. She also helps Sweep out with magic from time to time. Sweep finds her annoying sometimes, but does help her out when it comes to mentorship.
** In real life, both Rhein Kraft and Sweep Tosho were sired by End Sweep.
* Zenno Rob Roy - Friend and rival. They are like-minded individuals (not in personality) who are obsessed with fantasy, and are seen together regularly. While Rob Roy is obsessed with being a folk hero, Sweep is obsessed with being a witch, and they both like to read fantasy books in the library. Sweep enjoys teaching Rob Roy about magic, and Rob Roy herself seems to believe in her power and strength. Rob Roy admires Sweep as a friend and rival, and vows to defeat her in future races.  
** In real life, they challenged each other in the 2005 Takarazuka Kinen, where Sweep Tosho defeated him in an upset win. They also challenged each other in the 2005 Tenno Sho (Autumn), where they both lost to the long-shot mare Heavenly Romance.
* Orfevre - Friend. Sweep gets easily annoyed by Orfevre's standoffish personality. Orfevre tolerates her interactions and does not object to drinking her potions from time to time, and they occasionally hang out together.
* Curren Chan - Friend. Curren wants to borrow Sweep's magical girl clothes, because it would look good on Umastagram.
* Durandal - Friend. Sweep likes to cast magic on her, especially on her birthday. Durandal greatly respects Sweep as a witch, and even addresses her as Witch-dono out of reverence.
* Dream Journey - Friend and upperclassman. Sweep is jealous of Journey's prowess at persuading people, and asks her for help on how to manipulate people better.
** In real life, all of them were jockeyed by Kenichi Ikezoe, and were known for being the most difficult horses Ikezoe had to deal with.

During summer camp, Sweep teaches her a "magic spell" so that Pandora could cool off, which in reality was just her spraying her with water.

## Trivia

* Her initial character profile was uploaded to the official [https://x.com/uma_musu/status/865398672225320960 Twitter] account on May 18th, 2017.
** This tweet was a character name prediction quiz, that encouraged fans to try and guess which horse she's named after. The hint was "She calls herself “Magical Girl Umamusume”. She rarely listens to her trainer, and if she is not in the mood, she will not move an inch for 30 minutes."
* Her primary race outfit, obsession with magic, and grandma are likely inspired by the American sitcom Bewitched.
** This in itself is likely a reference to the fact that the real-life Sweep Tosho's dam, Tabatha Tosho, and granddam, Samantha Tosho, were both named after characters from the show.
* She is secretly afraid of ghosts. She is also scared of haunted houses and monsters, like zombies.
* She despises vegetables. In her SR Speed card, her most hated vegetables are green bell peppers and onions.
** Despite this, she is shown to like carrots, and has no qualms with eating them. This is a nod to the real-life counterpart, who enjoyed eating carrots.
* According to her SSR Speed card, she can make medicinal cough drops out of black licorice, albeit they taste like tires.
* She tends to be late to her classes because she practices her magic, which causes her to get extra classes.
* According to her SSR Wit card, she owns a camellia flower hair accessory that her grandma gifted to her.
* In her career mode, there's a random event called "Doing Things My Way!" that will lock the player out of training options. 
** This is a reference to the real life Sweep Tosho, where she was known for refusing to train on a whim.
* While her real life counterpart had a visible blaze and a white sock on her left hind leg, this was not incorporated into her Umamusume counterpart's design.
* Because her grandmother tends to flowers and has a garden, Sweep's "spells" are actually named after flowers. For example, the chapters in her character story are named after flowers. In her solo song, she also yells out "alstroemeria", known as the Peruvian lily.
* Her grandmother is also good at engineering, and added mechanics to Sweep's hat.
* In her career story, there is an NPC Umamusume titled "Umamusume With a Light Blue Ribbon" who is seen hanging around Sweep, but got injured after running with Kawakami Princess.
** This NPC is a reference to the real horse Yamanin Sucre, who raced with Sweep Tosho in the classics and beat her in the 2003 Hanshin Juvenile Fillies. However, she developed tendonitis after the 2006 Queen Elizabeth II Cup due to Kawakami Princess' obstruction during the race.
*** Another nod to this connection is in Sweep Tosho's optional Arima Kinen event, where she races with Tokai Teio. In real life, Tokai Teio is the sire of Yamanin Sucre.
* After she wins the Takarazuka Kinen, a fan hopes that a Tiara route Umamusume wins the Tenno Sho (Autumn) coming up.
** This Umamusume is a reference to the real horse Heavenly Romance, that won the Tenno Sho (Autumn) in 2005.
* She is very good at playing Daruma-san ga Koronda, the Japanese version of the children's game Statues.
* She is ticklish, despite threatening to tickle others.
*She is talented at making facial masks for skincare.
* She loves to eat sweets, and especially likes parfaits.
* Sweep Tosho’s father is based on her real life jockey, Kenichi Ikezoe. He has acknowledged this on [https://x.com/0723kenchan/status/1539087496109207553?s=46 Twitter] and jokingly stated he had become her Papa, and unlike real life, she actually listens to the player in Umamusume. 
** Sweep Tosho’s secret of never listening to her father references how the real horse frequently refused to listen to Ikezoe. Infamously, this includes the 2005 Tenno Sho (Autumn) with then Emperor Akihiko watching, in which she refused to move into the starting gates, eventually being guided in by stable hands to prevent delays.
** In the homescreen, she mentions that her dad really likes anmitsu. This is another reference to Kenichi Ikezoe, in which during the Hokkaido TV program "Dosanko Wide", Ikezoe drew a picture of anmitsu during a segment centered around pedestrians drawing.

## Real-life page (excerpt)

Source: https://umamusu.wiki/IRL:Sweep_Tosho

Sweep Tosho was a Japanese racehorse that was active from 2003 to 2007. In 2005, she became the first mare to win the Takarazuka Kinen in 39 years since Eight Crown last did in 1966. She was one of the first mares to succeed in major G1 races against stallions after an era of stallion dominance.

### Name Origin

Tosho is the crown name of Tosho Sangyo Co., Ltd., and Sweep references her sire's name, End Sweep.

### Background

Before Sweep Tosho's birth, Tosho Farm, owned by Tosho Sangyo Co., Ltd., had been in a slump period. The success of their sires, Social Butterfly and Tosho Boy, began to dwindle. Though Tosho Boy sired the 1983 Triple Crown winner Mr. C.B., the stable's successful horses continued to be sired by other horses other than him until 1991, when his daughter Sister Tosho won the Oka Sho.

Tosho Farm was focused on reviving the dam-line of Social Butterfly, but to no avail. There were also hopes for Sister Tosho to have notable offspring, but none of her foals had any major victories. The new manager of Tosho Farm, Yoshio Shimura, began to reform the ranch, and imported foreign mares for the sake of finding a line that could surpass the lines of Social Butterfly and Tosho Boy. 

In 1993, Tabatha Tosho was born from her dam, Samantha Tosho, and her sire, Dancing Brave. After six races, she retired and became a broodmare at Watanabe Stables. By 2000, foreign stallions, specifically End Sweep, were transported to Japan for stud duty. Tabatho Tosho was bred to End Sweep, and on May 9th, 2001, she gave birth to Sweep Tosho at Tosho Farm.

As a foal, there were high expectations for Sweep Tosho, and they saw a lot of potential in her as a racer. However, Sweep Tosho had a poor temperament, and heavily disliked running and leaving her stable for training. She was sent to train under Akio Tsurudome at Ritto Training Center.

### Racing career

=== Two-year old season (2003) ===

Sweep Tosho made her debut on October 18th, 2003, in a 1,400-meter turf race on Kyoto Racecourse. She won the race by surging through and chasing the horses from behind, winning by three lengths. After this, she won the 1,400-meter Fantasy Stakes (G3) on November 9th in a similar fashion on the same racecourse, beating the favorite Royal Selangor by a quarter of a length. 

After this, on December 7th, she participated in her first G1 race, the 1,600-meter Hanshin Juvenile Fillies. Though she was the favorite in the race, she placed 5th and lost to Yamanin Sucre. She tried to chase in the final corner, but she found her path blocked. 

=== Three-year old season (2004) ===
On January 18th, 2004, Sweep Tosho returned to Kyoto Racecourse and won the open race Kobai Stakes, making it her third win and her last race with her jockey Koichi Tsunoda. As Sweep Tosho was transferred from Watanabe Stable to Tsurudome Stable, her new permanent jockey was Kenichi Ikezoe. Sweep Tosho entered the 1,600-meter Tulip Sho (G3) on March 6th at Hanshin Racecourse, where she stalled and refused to enter the starting gate. However, she won this race, which qualified her for the Oka Sho, a G1 classic race for three-year-old fillies. On April 11th, she entered the Oka Sho, once again stalling and delaying the race due to not entering the gate. Though she chased from behind, she placed 5th, losing to Dance in the Mood.
