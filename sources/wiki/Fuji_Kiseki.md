## Biography

<blockquote style="font-style: italic; border-left: 3px solid #33B839; padding-left: 1em; color: #444745;>"Don't cry, my darling pony. If there's something on your mind, speak!"</blockquote>

Always at your service. The classiest racer around.

A dashing performer who never hesitates to take another runner gently by the hand. You won't catch her flirting, however. Instead, she helps her dear ponies in her capacity as leader of Ritto Dormitory. As a runner, she hopes to captivate audiences just like her mother, a stage actress. Her ultimate stage: the Triple Crown.

=== Umayon Manga Introduction ===
A popular, beautiful Umamusume. Being at Ritto Dorm for a long time, she's very caring to the others.

## Appearance

Fuji Kiseki has a short black pixie cut, parted to her left. She has a black ahoge on the top of her head, and a white strip of hair on the edge of her bangs. Her ears are pointed with a de-saturated pink inner-ear. On her right ear, she wears two diamond gold piercings, and a gold chain with a blue gem on the end. She has sharp piercing blue eyes, with a gentle expression.

Her race outfit resembles that of a suave magician. She wears a black blazer with yellow and green stripes on the shoulders, gold buttons, and golden cuff links on both arms. The blazer is left open, with a brown belt with gold metal holding either side together underneath her chest. She wears a white collared frilled shirt, with the center buttons left open, and a black tie with a green stripe at the end. She wears black gloves that leave the top of her hands exposed, with gold trim. On her legs are simple black slacks with flared ends. Attached to her pants are a brown belt with gold chains hanging off the end, and a yellow and green striped cloth on her left hip. She wears black heels with gold adornments.

## Relationships

* Jungle Pocket - Fuji's ambitious underclassman. Fuji's running is what inspired Pocket to enter into the Twinkle Series, and Fuji does her best to support her however she can.
** IRL, both horses share the same owner, trainer, stable staff, and main jockey.
* Special Week - Friend. Fuji assisted Special Week when she first enrolled in Tracen, helping her get on her feet.
* Hishi Amazon - Friend and fellow dorm leader over at Miho. Teammates on the anime-only team, Team Rigil. Often being used as the team's Goal post. The two help plan special events and surprises for all of the students in their respective dorms.
* Agnes Tachyon - Friend. Fuji sometimes asks Tachyon for assisting in her magic acts, and Tachyon sometimes has Fuji try out new concoctions.
** IRL, they are both sons of Sunday Silence who were undefeated in 4 starts, had 1 G1 each, and retired due to tendon injuries, both being dubbed "Phantom Triple Crowns". 
* Air Groove, El Condor Pasa, Grass Wonder, Symboli Rudolf, Taiki Shuttle, T.M. Opera O, Narita Brian, Maruzensky - Teammates on the anime-only team, Team Rigil.
* Marvelous Sunday, Genuine - Teammates on the manga-only team, Team Betelgeuse.
* Sweep Tosho - Underclassman. Dubbed the wizard to Sweep's witch for handling her mischievousness and seemingly always being one step ahead of her. She also tries to encourage Sweep to eat her vegetables, much to the latter's dismay.
* Hishi Akebono - Friend and underclassman. As Akebono is her junior, Fuji likes to cheer her up.
