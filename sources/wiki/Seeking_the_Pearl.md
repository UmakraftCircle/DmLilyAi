## Lead

Seeking the Pearl is a character in Umamusume: Pretty Derby.

## Biography

<blockquote style="font-style: italic; border-left: 3px solid #1690D4; padding-left: 1em; color: #B02D40;>"The world is brimming with possibility! It's like an oyster that bore a pearl such as me!"</blockquote>

She's taking on the whole world, ready to make the impossible look easy.

An active older-sister type who insists on standing out and demonstrating that anything is possible on the world stage. Owing to the fact that she has lived in many countries growing up, she embodies confidence and unique methods of self-expression. She is a talented soul who can accomplish all she sets out to do while eliciting laughter from those around her. She has a hopeless grasp of the English language, but she does a good job of compensating for it with her body language.

## Appearance

Seeking the Pearl has long, dark brown hair that dynamically cascades far past her back. A single strand of hair falls past her eyes. Atop her head she wears bright red sunglasses, and on her left ear is a red scrunchie, decorated with gray trim, and accessorized with a string of pearls and gold charms. Her eyes are purple, and she has a mole under her right eye. She wears lipstick, so her lips are noticeably fuller than other Umamusume.

Her racing outfit consists of an off-shoulder red dress, with various white accents and gold trimming. The collar of the dress folds downwards, tied at the center with a white, red, and black ribbon, and she has two white stars on either side of her chest,  The dress' sleeves reach her lower arm, and puff out at the end. She wears a pair of black gloves with gold trimming, with a red band around her wrist. On her right arm is a pearl bracelet, and on her neck is a pearl necklace, with gold and purple gems. Underneath, she wears a cropped black tank top. Her white pleated skirt has a red stripe at the base, and a layer of black pleats underneath. She wears several decorative belts, one black, one white and red, and one purple and red. All meet in the  center of her waist, attached with an intricate golden buckle. She wears a pair of black heels, laced above the top line, with a golden toe box. She wears two pearl anklets and a red ribbon around her right ankle, and laced up her left shin is a thick red ribbon.

## Relationships

* Kawakami Princess - Roommate and friend.
* Stay Gold - Friend. They respect each other as fellow travelers. As they are both carefree, they get along well. Seeking the Pearl is also the only one that calls Stay Gold "Stay", showing a close friendship between them.
* Mejiro Dober - Friend and rival. Pearl considers her a part of her "family", while Dober is intrigued by her eccentricities, and invites her to tea parties sometimes. 
* Mejiro Bright - Friend and rival. Pearl also considers her a part of her "family".
* Grass Wonder - Friend and underclassman.
* El Condor Pasa - Friend and underclassman. As a fellow American, El deeply admires Pearl, and wishes to get on her level. 
* Taiki Shuttle - Friend and rival. Pearl sees Taiki as a rival she must surpass, and feels an odd familiarity when around her. They both communicate to each other in English sometimes, with Pearl pretending that she is fluent.
** IRL, Seeking the Pearl and Taiki Shuttle were the first American-bred and Japanese-trained horses to win an international G1 in France.
* Shinko Windy - Friend. As a team, they call themselves "Worldwide Windy". 
* Dream Journey - Friend. Pearl's dancing tends to exhaust Journey, and Journey can't handle Pearl's extroverted nature. 
* Hishi Akebono -  Friend. Akebono is inspired by Pearl's attitude, and it inspires her to pursue the possibilities of "Buono". 
* King Halo - Friend.

After watching a cooking show with her trainer, Seeking the Pearl will ask the player to adjust her training schedule so she can make a bento lunchbox similar to the one shown on TV.  When it occurs, training for all attributes but one will be restricted for the next turn.
** This is presumably a reference to an interview with Yutaka Take, Seeking the Pearl's primary jockey, conducted by journalist Akihiro Shimada. During the 1996 Niigata Sansai Stakes, Seeking the Pearl veered to the outer rail shortly after leaving the gate. After the race, Shimada asked Take about the cause of the incident, to which Take jokingly replied: "She just went off to buy a bento lunchbox."
* In her career story, there's an event in her classic year called "A Sudden Entrapment!" where she gets diagnosed with epiglottis entrapment shortly after the Rose Stakes. 
** In real life, this happened to the real Seeking The Pearl, where she was forced to rest for months and miss the Shuka Sho because of this illness.
* In her career story's normal ending, Seeking The Pearl not only goes to the United States on a whim, she also claims that she nearly got struck by lightning.
** In real life, during her five-year-old season, Seeking The Pearl's team suddenly announced that she was traveling to the United States to compete in races there. After that, she was set to compete in Japanese races in the fall, before her team deregistered from the JRA suddenly and sent her to the United States once again. Not only that, her death in 2005 was suspected to be caused by lightning, as there were no apparent injuries on her body that said otherwise.
* Her parents give her a pearl every year.
* New students mistake her for a teacher when she isn't wearing her uniform.
* She sings in her sleep.

## Real-life page (excerpt)

Source: https://umamusu.wiki/IRL:Seeking_the_Pearl

'  was an American-bred Japanese racehorse that was active from 1996 to 1999. She was the first Japanese-trained horse to win a G1 race overseas in Europe.

### Name Origin

Her name was inspired by her sire, Seeking the Gold, as well as her buyer Masako Uenaka's birthstone, pearl. Tomoko Uenaka, Masako's daughter who would later inherit Seeking the Pearl, reportedly chose the name with the intention of saying "the horse found you [Masako]"

### Background

Seeking the Pearl was foaled on April 16th, 1994, at Lazy Lane Stables in Upperville, Virginia. Her sire, Seeking the Gold, won 8 races in 15 starts which included the then-G1 Dwyer Stakes, and had also found significant success as a sire. Her dam, Page Proof, was sired by Seattle Slew, the winner of the 1977 American Triple Crown.

In July 1995, Seeking the Pearl was entered into the Keeneland July Select Yearling Sale, where she was designated as RNA and therefore went unsold. However, Masako Uenaka had her eye on Seeking the Pearl since she had first appeared in the pre-sale catalog, and after learning that she had not been purchased, went to inspect the filly in person. Uenaka first encountered Seeking the Pearl sticking her head out of the stall window, and stated: "While most young horses look anxious, she seemed to be deep in thought. She looked very intelligent." Uenaka also remarked that this first meeting with Seeking the Pearl was a "fateful encounter", and decided to purchase her for $180,000 USD. 

After being purchased by Uenaka, Seeking the Pearl was relocated to Stone Farm in Paris, Kentucky to continue her development, and was considered to be a "strong and beautiful horse" by many of the Stone Farm staff. She was then transported to Northern Farm in Hayakita, Hokkaido, Japan, to begin training as a racehorse, before entering the stable of Shozo Sasaki at the Ritto Training Center.

### Racing career

=== Two-year-old season (1996) ===
Seeking the Pearl made her debut on July 20th, 1996. Entering her first race as favourite, she took the lead right after the start and won by 7 lengths. 

In the subsequent G3 Niigata Sansai Stakes, she was the second favorite behind Shinko Splendor, but veered towards the outside immediately after the race began, forcing her to start at the back of the pack. Despite a strong finish on the final stretch, she finished 3rd.

However, in her next race, the G3 Daily Hai Sansai Stakes, she displayed a much calmer temperament, beating Mejiro Bright by five lengths as favourite, claiming her first graded stakes victory, and setting the record time for the race, which she held for 24 years.

At the Hanshin Sansai Himba Stakes, her first G1 race, Seeking the Pearl was the favourite just as in her previous race. However, in the race, she lost momentum on the final stretch and finished 4th, behind Mejiro Dober. 

She finished her junior season with two wins in four races, but was labelled as "hot-tempered" due to the puzzling nature of her two defeats. Yutaka Take, who served as her jockey for all four races, stated that Seeking the Pearl was "a lovely racehorse who would never bite, act out, or object to anything done to her in the stables, but once mounted, she would transform."
