## Biography

She dreams of being a princess! A bold spitfire of an Umamusume.

A scrappy girl who aspires to ascend from the confines of ordinary life into royalty. She was always a bundle of energy growing up, but after watching a certain anime in which a princess fights hand-in-hand with a prince, she's been able to channel her zeal toward her future dreams—the least of which includes walking the path of a princess and finding her own prince one day. She admires King Halo as a mentor.

## Appearance

Kawakami Princess has long pinkish-brown hair, that fades into a saturated pink at her wavy tips. Her hair is parted down the center, with very large hair intakes, exposing her forehead completely, and has her hair tied into braids on either side of her head. She has large ears, and on her left ear is a silver ring with upside-down heart shaped etchings, and a red and blue striped bow. Her eyebrows are small and thin, and she has large bright blue eyes, with vertically long pupils in the center.

Kawakami Princess' racewear is a vibrant pink princess dress is held up with a dark pink halter top with a cutout on the chest, embellished with white and dark pink decor. Around her neck is a white choker with three green diamonds. Her poofy pink dress that is decorated with green diamonds has a cutout in the center, revealing two pleated layers, one pink, and one white with a pink stripe. She wears detached sleeves that frill out at the ends, with white trim on either arm, both arms also sport a pink and green cuff around her upper arm. She wears white thigh-high socks with a 'V' shaped cutout, each with a pink line across the top, and a green diamond in the center. Her boots, also sporting a "V" cutout are bright pink with elaborate white trims.

## Relationships

* Seeking the Pearl - Roommate and friend.
* Sweep Tosho - Friend and rival. They watch Princess Fighter every Sunday morning together, and they bond together as the witch and the princess. Sweep also encourages Kawakami when she's in a slump, and wants her to run in her own way regardless of the risk.
** IRL, Sweep Tosho and Kawakami Princess challenged each other a few times. Most notably, they competed with each other in the 2006 Queen Elizabeth II Cup, where Kawakami Princess obstructed Yamanin Sucre and got herself disqualified.
* Fusaichi Pandora - Friend and rival. Fusaichi Pandora views Kawakami Princess as a rival she must surpass, as they are both rivals in the Triple Tiara route. Though she loses to her multiple times, she eventually finds the strength to beat her. Outside of racing, they are good friends. They both enjoy watching Princess Fighter, and have nostalgic memories of watching the show.
* King Halo - Role model. Kawakami admires King because she is similar Princess Fighter's main character, Shiny Princess. Despite King finding Kawakami rather inconsolable, she tries to help her, especially to help her become more ladylike, albeit with limited success.
** IRL, Kawakami Princess is sired by King Halo,  and is named "Princess" because she's the daughter of the "King".
*Daiwa Scarlet - Underclassman and friend.
*Vodka - Underclassman and friend.
*Biko Pegasus - Friend.
*Mejiro Dober - Friend. Kawakami inspires her to be the best version of herself and stands up for Dober.
* Katsuragi Ace - Upperclassman. Ace can be seen giving training advice and encouragement to Kawakami.
** IRL, Katsuragi Ace's main jockey, Nishiura Katsuichi, had also trained Kawakami Princess.
*Dream Journey - Friend. Dream Journey always gives her tea, and in return, Kawakami Princess gives her information about Princess Fighter.
*Curren Bouquetd'or -  Friend. On occasion, they go to cafes together, and Kawakami likes to talk to her about Princess Fighter. Bouquet also makes her flower tiaras.
*Nishino Flower - Friend. They both watch Princess Fighter together in their free time.

## Trivia

* Her initial character profile was uploaded to the official [https://x.com/uma_musu/status/852713044345892864 Twitter] account on April 13, 2017.
** This tweet was a character name prediction quiz, that encouraged fans to try and guess which horse she's named after. The hint was "A beautiful girl who came to Tracen to become the strongest princess. She's a great senior to Vodka and Daiwa Scarlet, and is a strong-willed young lady that has her trainer under her thumb."
* The anime that influenced Kawakami Princess, Princess Fighter, is based on the Pretty Cure series.
** More specifically, the series that motivates Kawakami Princess is based on the franchise's 12th installment, Go! Princess Precure!
** Her unique skill's {{Game Skill|100391}} animation references various elements of the franchise:
*** The overall animation resembles a magical girl transformation and fight, which makes sense as Pretty Cure is a magical girl franchise.
*** The shadow-like monster she fights resembles the shadow-like [https://prettycure.fandom.com/wiki/Zakenna Zakenna] monsters from the original series, Futari Wa Pretty Cure.
*** Kawakami Princess defeats the monster via headbutting it. The Pretty Cure series is well-known for its use of physical combat.
**The real-life Kawakami Princess had a rivalry with a racehorse named after the Pretty Cure series, [https://en.netkeiba.com/db/horse/result/2003101046/ T.M. Precure], though the references to Pretty Cure in-game are likely purely coincidental.
* Unlike the majority of the other careers in the game, when Quick Mode is used on Kawakami Princess' trainee scenarios, instead of the player, Kawakami Princess will narrate her own career from her perspective, and in her case, being similar to that of magical girl anime previews with the protagonist narrating the events of an episode, with most events ending with the name of a special attack.
* Before Gentildonna's introduction, Kawakami Princess was deemed the one to possess the most brute strength. That strength is likely a reference to her IRL counterpart having damaged her stall by kicking the walls and making cracks and holes on them.
* The warning signs on her bed in her second 1koma is a reference to how warning signs had to be placed around Kawakami Princess' stall due to her temperament.
