## Biography

<blockquote style="font-style: italic; border-left: 3px solid #99e397; padding-left: 1em; color: #405a5f;

An artistic Umamusume who loves to read & draw manga. Drawing up new drafts in her room every day, she also dreams of drawing her legs across the racetrack to create a race that's just as exciting as a manga. An avid creator who loves the art enough to skip meals for it, her goal is to become a 'protagonist'.

## Appearance

* Kiseki has emerald green eyes and short deep blue-green hair with some small white strands at the front and lower half of the hair in the back is turquoise styled in a shoulder-length medium bob cut.
* In her race outfit she wears:
** a white cropped jacket with green accents. The jacket has a zipper down the front and is worn open, revealing the green top underneath. The sleeves are puffed and gathered slightly just after the elbows, with green ruffled cuffs at the ends. The jacket also features a small green patch with her name on it on the left chest area.
** Underneath the jacket, she has a fitted green top with a high collar and a zipper that runs down the front. This top is accented with two white, belt-like straps positioned around the ribcage area, just below the bust
** dark brown shorts that are cuffed at the hem. Attached to the shorts is a decorative silver chain with a square pendant featuring a star-like design and a green gem
** knee-high white socks with two green horizontal straps with silver buckles near the top.
** brown boots with beige toes and heels, lace-up style, and lined with fluffy fur at the top
** white gloves with green accents
** On her head, she has a small beige beret adorned with a green and white striped ribbon and a green gem.

## Relationships

* Almond Eye - Friend and rival. Kiseki views her as a natural-born protagonist, and wishes to surpass her. 
** IRL, both faced off a handful of times, including the 2018 and 2020 Japan Cup.
* Marche Lorraine - Roommate.
* Kitasan Black - Friend. Kiseki looks up to her as an ideal protagonist, and is inspired by how popular she is.
** IRL, both of them won the Kikuka Sho. Kitasan Black won it in 2015, while Kiseki won it in 2017. 
* Lucky Lilac - Friend and classmate.  Kiseki believes that she's cool, and that her racing career could make for a great story in a manga.
* Blast Onepiece - Friend and classmate.
* Air Groove - Upperclassman. Air Groove considers Kiseki a straightforward Umamusume who gets back on her feet, and admires her cheerful personality. Kiseki wants to live up to her expectations, and she also considers Air Groove as a main character.
** IRL, Air Groove is Kiseki's paternal grandmother.
* Vivlos - Friend. Vivlos looks forward to reading the manga Kiseki publishes. Kiseki considers her as a rival in her "Ultimate Showdown Arc", and wants to compete with her in races.
* Chrono Genesis - Friend. Chrono Genesis lets Kiseki borrow her history books, which she finds cool.
* Loves Only You - Friend. They both enjoy manga together. Loves also helps her make a drawing livestream.
* Sounds of Earth - Friend. Earth invites her to classical music concerts occasionally.
* Still in Love - Friend. Kiseki helps her with updating her social media, since Still in Love doesn't use it very often. 
** This connection is based on real life details, in which Still in Love was the first G1-winning filly of Shimokobe Farm while Kiseki was the first G1-winning colt of Shimokobe Farm.
*Rulership - Friend and upperclassman. Rulership shows interesting in mentoring Kiseki once she finishes her racing career. Kiseki finds Rulership's strategies in games cool and intense.
** IRL, Kiseki is Rulership's son.

## Trivia

* She was announced during the PakaLive TV Vol.60 stream on December 24th, 2025.
* Kiseki is tied with Calstone Light O and Victoire Pisa, having the 5th shortest time gap from introduction to being playable trainee, with 25 days between when she was first announced and when she was added as a trainee, behind Phalaenopsis at 20 days, Transcend at 19 days, Gran Alegria at 17, and Dream Journey's immediate banner.
* A version of Almond Eye akin to her superboss version in The Twinkle Legends scenario is present in Kiseki's senior year Japan Cup. Her stats are higher, with some differences in aptitude.
** This is in reference to Almond Eye winning Japan Cup in 2018 and 2020, where Kiseki not only ran but also set the pace for.
* In the Main Story - Act 2, there is a mob Umamusume that represented Kiseki in Japan Cup before her eventual release. This mob Umamusume was replaced afterwards.
* Kiseki's story titles are references to various manga:
** Chapter 1 - Naruto
** Chapter 2 - Kyō Kara Ore Wa!!
** Chapter 3 - Hunter × Hunter
** Chapter 4 - Hajime no Ippo
** Chapter 5 - Crows
** Chapter 6 - Ushio & Tora
** Chapter 7 - Zom 100: Bucket List of the Dead
*She has younger siblings, and she used to wear matching clothes with them when she was little.
*Her mother sends her daily movie recommendations on LANE.
*When she was little, she had to wear an apron because she was a messy eater.
*In the Kyoto Daishoten in her career mode's senior year, the commentator focuses significantly on her, commenting in awe "Ah, Kiseki".
**This is a reference to the real life 2021 Kyoto Daishoten that Kiseki participated in, where it became infamous for how the commentator mainly commented on him throughout the race, and how the commentator exclaimed "Ah, Kiseki".
*Kiseki's racewear is resistant to water and mud. This is a reference to Kiseki's Kikuka Sho victory in 2017, where he won the race while it was raining. This is also referenced in Kiseki's casual outfit, where there is an umbrella symbol on her shirt.
*She tends to doodle in class.
*Her younger sister was the one who suggested putting a ribbon on her beret.
**This is a possible reference to her IRL younger sister named Big Ribbon.
*She believes that glasses can boost intelligence.
*Her love for manga is a reference to her owner in real life, Tatsue Ishikawa, who is the CEO of Digimerce. Digimerce runs an online manga distribution service.
*Her title, "神業の軌跡" (kamiwaza no kiseki, Trail of Divine Work), although not the original intended meaning, references her HKJC name "神業".
*Her 1-koma references various mangaka, like Riyoko Ikeda and Hirohiko Araki.
