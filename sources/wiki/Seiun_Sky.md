## Appearance

Seiun Sky embodies an easygoing lifestyle, reflected in her relaxed appearance. She has blue down-turned eyes with short, fluffy, muted green hair. Her right ear is adorned with a green cover featuring soft cloud patterns and fluffy white puffs (most likely to resemble clouds) at the base. She wears a bright yellow dandelion hair clip on the left side of her bangs.

Her outfit complements her laid-back demeanor with a fashionably simple yet charming basic white blouse accented with subtle hints of green. The blouse features somewhat "wild" ruffles at the chest, sleeves, and waist, with the waistline flaring out in a skirt-like fashion. Paired with this are green shorts adorned with cute cloud-like patterns that echo the same design as her ear. A set of straps and a yellow-and-green band wrap her left thigh. She also wears a brown choker around her neck, decorated in the center with a silver horse-shoe shaped emblem. And finishing her look are basic gray heels, embellished with fluffy cloud-like puffs that, like her shorts, mirror the ones on her ear.

## Trivia

* Her initial character profile was uploaded to the official [https://x.com/uma_musu/status/845470700039127040 Twitter] account on March 24th, 2017.
** This tweet was a character name prediction quiz that encouraged fans to try and guess which horse she's named after. The hint was "Special Week's classmate that tends to go at her own pace. She hates entering gates!"
* She can fall asleep in three seconds.
* In various livestreams of Sokosoko PakaLive TV, it is revealed that Seiun Sky was the most auditioned Umamusume among the voice actresses until Akari Kito landed the role.
* If the player makes her win the Triple Crown in her career story, there's a secret event where her grandfather gifts her a bamboo fishing rod, a fishing chair, sea bream, and fishing bait for winning.
** This is a reference to Masayuki Nishiyama's best friend, Juzo Yamasaki, a manga artist who's hobby is fishing. Juzo Yamasaki was also a fan of Seiun Sky.
*Her dislike for gates is a reference to how Seiun Sky in real life had trouble entering the gate, such as in the 1999 Tenno Sho (Autumn).
