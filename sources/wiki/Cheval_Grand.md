## Appearance

Though she is the quiet middle child of the family, Cheval is a soul that dreams big. Like her sisters, her eyes are blue with white pupils, but hers are a much lighter shade compared to their deep blues. Her hair is simple yet distinct—a grayish-brown, medium-length layered bob with soft bangs. It carries a slightly messy charm, with the ends jutting out unevenly. The most striking feature is a platinum streak that runs along the right side of her hair, setting her apart from other Umamusume, who often have center streaks. Cheval's sailor cap might as well be her prized possession, as she wears it everywhere. The white cap features a blue band, her family's crest and neat ear pockets, perfectly tailored for her ears.

Cheval wears a distinctive white jacket over a black underlayer. The jacket subtly resembles a classic sailor uniform, with light blue collars and cuffs, while incorporating elements of naval jackets, such as the double rows of gold buttons. Her underlayer features a neat yellow ribbon neck tie. Draped over her shoulders is a cape. The cape's interior is a deep blue, reminiscent of the night sky, while the exterior is a lighter blue adorned with unique markings. Its design can be interpreted as wings—a metaphor for her desire to grow and become the person she aspires to be, much like a butterfly emerging from its cocoon. Another interpretation could be her personal voyage under the stars.

She pairs her jacket with slightly baggy black shorts, detailed with gold buttons. A unique belt with metallic gold highlights runs through her shorts, and a blue strap hangs from her shorts, displaying her name "Cheval Grand." Completing her robust look, she wears knee-high black socks with white stripes and sleek, modern white shoes with strap designs.

## Trivia

*During the 1st Anniversary PakaLive TV stream on February 22nd, 2022, her character design was shown, but her name was concealed as ???. Her name wasn't revealed until November 5th, 2022.
*Cheval Grand was known to hold onto her cap to calm down whenever she feels anxious, which has become a muscle memory. 
**In fact, this was shown in her summer alt's ultimate animation, where she became flustered and tried to hold onto the cap she wasn't wearing at the time, becoming more agitated as a result. Before races, she holds onto her "cap" while she is idle.
*Character Design Notes from Umamusume: Pretty Derby Artworks Vol.04:
**As Cheval Grand shares the letter V in their name with her sisters Verxina and Vivlos, the letter V is sprinkled in as a motif on her accessories.
**As only the right foreleg of the motif horse has a white marking, she wears a white glove on her right hand, while she has no glove on her left.
**Given the Umamusume Cheval Grand's dream of "spreading her wings into the world", she wears a big, wing like cape.
*In her career story, she has a childhood friend named Shine Praise who goes to Australia during her senior year, but gets injured while she is there.
**This Umamusume is a reference to the real horse Admire Deus, who was a year older than Cheval Grand and raced with him in the Hanshin Daishoten. In 2017, he was transferred to Australia, where he suffered a catastrophic injury and passed away on November 25th, 2017.
*She admires a certain Umamusume who beat an undefeated Triple Crown winner in the Arima Kinen, and looks up to her as an underdog. Meanwhile, her sisters admire the "hero" Triple Crown Umamusume.
**The underdog Umamusume that Cheval admires is based on Heart's Cry, a horse who beat Deep Impact, the undefeated Triple Crown winner of 2005, in the Arima Kinen in that same year. Heart's Cry is the sire of Cheval Grand, while Deep Impact is the sire of both Verxina and Vivlos.
*She likes to watch fishing videos.
*She prefers salty mochi in contrast with her sisters who like sweet mochi.
*Her love for baseball is a reference to the real life Cheval Grand, in which his owner, Kazuhiro Sasaki, is a former professional baseball relief pitcher.
