## Biography

<blockquote style="font-style: italic; border-left: 3px solid #d8d2d9; padding-left: 1em; color: #d85081;>"That's right, dearie... The key is to never give up."</blockquote>

Slow and steady wins the race. An Umamusume with a backbone of steel.

A gentle Umamusume whose sweet attentiveness puts others at ease. Many even find themselves warmly recalling their grandmothers when speaking with her. As a race approaches, however, you can feel a stoic discipline well up from within as she faces the track with a steely resolve.

## Appearance

Wonder Acute has messy warm-gray hair that reaches below her mid-back. Her bangs are rather fringed, with a white stripe down the middle of her hair, and a white streak on the left side of her bangs. Her right ear has an ear cover over it, with pastel red and blue vertical lines. The ear cover has two red and blue colored bands wrapped around it in a V-shape, and two white pom-poms with red and blue tops in the center. The pom-poms each have a red, white, and blue ribbon tied to them. Her gentle and down-turned eyes are blue and fade into a pastel yellow.

Her race outfit consists of a long-sleeve pink dress. Her off-the-shoulder collar is dark brown with white trims around the edge. Her sleeves have a diamond shaped pattern on the top, with white gem in the center, and her sleeves have a large layer of white frills underneath. She wears dark brown fingerless gloves with a diamond cutout on the top of her hand, with a silver decorative trim around the edge. Her pink dress flows outwards, and underneath is a pastel red and yellow striped large pleated skirt that flows down to her knees. Around her neck, she wears a frilled choker with two white pom-poms in the center, and underneath her dress she wears a dark brown tank top with frilled sleeves. She wears dark brown stockings, with lighter brown boots over top. Her high boots have a pink and white gradient down the center, and are tied by white laces. The heel of her right boot is white, while the left side is dark brown. Over her boot is a light brown leg strap with a silver diamond emblem in the center.

## Relationships

* Smart Falcon - Rival and close friend. Wonder Acute shows great respect towards Falco's talent and skill on the dirt, and Falco respects her for being one of the few who can keep pace with her, deeply trusting her abilities.
** In 2011, the year Smart Falcon went undefeated IRL, Wonder Acute came the closest to ending his win streak, finishing second by a nose.
* Copano Rickey - Rival and close friend. Wonder Acute often has to play the mediator between Rickey's feng shui and Tarumae's practicality, but Rickey shows great respect toward Acute, and Acute looks over Rickey as a gentle senior.
* Hokko Tarumae - Rival and close friend. Wonder Acute often has to play the mediator between Rickey's feng shui and Tarumae's practicality, but Tarumae especially reveres Acute's veteran status, and deeply admires her running, while Acute continues to be a dependable senior.
** The four of them are part of an idol group called the "Tetra Mystics".
* Transcend - Rival and close friend. Transcend is her roommate, and she often teaches her how to use technology, while Wonder Acute's gentle and unabashedly kind nature often disarms Transcend's more aloof behavior.
** They raced against each other 3 times IRL, most notably when Transcend defeated Wonder Acute at the Japan Cup Dirt in 2011.
* Espoir City - Rival and friend. Wonder Acute sees through the surface of Espo's rashness and thinks of her as a very kind girl, which Espo vehemently denies, though she does help Acute carry her bags luggage.
* Furioso - Rival and friend. Wonder Acute's gentle kindness is seen as a great danger by Furioso, though it hardly dissuades Acute from treating her with the same kindness she does everyone else, and so she helps Furioso feel at home at Central.
* Buena Vista - Friend.
* Nakayama Festa, Tosen Jordan - Classmates, and friends. 
** Nakayama sometimes asks to borrow her notes, Acute also offers to study together.
** Buena Vista, Nakayama Festa, Tosen Jordan, Wonder Acute, and Transcend are in the same racing generation of 2009, though only Buena Vista and Transcend have raced against each other once as Wonder Acute and Transcend mainly raced on dirt.
*** Coincidentally, Wonder Acute and Buena Vista share the same birthday. 
* Victoire Pisa - Friend and classmate. They do arts and crafts together, such as beadwork.
* TM Opera O - Friend. Acute would give her stage play ideas.
** IRL, Both of their main jockeys were Ryuji Wada.

## Trivia

* She was announced during the PakaLive TV Vol.19 stream on July 27, 2022.
* Wonder Acute is the only Umamusume who uses a flip phone instead of a smartphone.
** This is a reference to the fact that the real horse is the oldest to ever win a G1/Jpn1 race, especially when the horse has been racing since the flip phone era.
* She loves painting, which references her in-real-life counterpart being a skilled artist.
* She knows the basics of boxing as her parents own a boxing gym.
* The Tracen Meet that Wonder Acute competes in in her character story may be a reference to the 2011 Tokyo Daishoten. Both the Tracen Meet and Tokyo Daishoten were the 10th race of the day, dirt, 2000 meters, sunny, and were races in which Wonder Acute barely lost to Smart Falcon.
