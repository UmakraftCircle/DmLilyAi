## Appearance

Mejiro Ryan's design depicts the vibe of someone who is sporty and practical. Spiky short brown hair that flows down with a white tuft that stands out at the center. She has uneven bangs that frame her face and her right ear is adorned with a metallic piece matching her color scheme and two piercings to boot. 

For her racewear, Mejiro Ryan wears a cropped jacket, mixed with white and teal stripes, rolled up sleeves, and on one wrist she wears a golden bracelet and on the other a black bracelet with metal studs. Beneath her jacket is a striped tied up shirt with the name "Ryan" on it. She wears high waisted black shorts that seem to be tied down by a unique looking belt. Both sides of her upper thighs seem to be wrapped by a brown belt with gold trims, and she wears practical and plain looking white racing boots.

## Trivia

* Her initial character profile was uploaded to the official [https://x.com/uma_musu/status/852713162876911616 Twitter] account on April 13, 2017.
** This tweet was a character name prediction quiz, that encouraged fans to try and guess which horse she's named after. The hint was "A tomboy with her trademark very short haircut. Due to her cheery and refreshing personality, she's very popular at school. She is said to be favored by the Umamusume gods...?" 
*** To further add onto this, Ryan was a very popular horse in real life. His popularity actually surpassed even McQueen's despite McQueen being more accomplished. This is most evident during the 1991 Takarazuka Kinen, the race where Ryan was at his peak.
* "Umamusume: Pretty Derby Artworks Vol.1" explains why her character designer made specific choices when designing her in relation to her real-life counterpart, which were as follows:
** Her jacket is white with teal stripes, in reference to the real horse's jockey silks. However, as with all of the Mejiro horses, the original green has been changed to teal.
** Since she is named after the Major League Baseball player Nolan Ryan, she wears a shirt that resembles a baseball player's uniform.
** Her real-life counterpart had white markings on both his front and back hooves, which is why she wears both white boots and white gloves.
* Ryan's belt in her racing outfit is also present in Mejiro Dober and Mejiro Bright's outfits, reinforcing their IRL counterparts as Ryan's children.
* Her real-life counterpart was known to have a very short mane as to prevent rashes, which is why she has short hair. The short mane has been described as a unique charm point which was very popular with the fans. It even has its own nickname, ライアンカット (Ryan Cut).
* Ryan's athletic personality comes from her real-life counterpart being more muscular than most other horses.
* Ryan was in the first season of the anime, and raced in the Autumn Tenno Sho where Silence Suzuka broke her leg. However, the real life Ryan retired years before Suzuka's debut, the inclusion was meant to be a stand in for Mejiro Bright (who actually raced in the same Tenno Sho), who wasn't introduced as an Umamusume yet.
* Ryan is the only Mejiro member to be a 1-star unit initially, while all the other Mejiro members were 3-star units.
* In her career mode, following the Senior Year Arima Kinen, a "Gentleman with a Beret" is noted as being particularly ecstatic about Ryan's victory. This NPC is likely based on horse racing critic Keijiro Okawa, nicknamed "the God of Horse Racing" for achieving four perfect predictions. He is best known for shouting Ryan's name during the 1990 Arima Kinen, as the horse faced off against Oguri Cap on the final straight.
