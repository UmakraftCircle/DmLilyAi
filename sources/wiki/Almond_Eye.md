## Lead

Almond Eye is a character in Umamusume: Pretty Derby.

## Biography

<blockquote style="font-style: italic; border-left: 3px solid #cc3300; padding-left: 1em; background-color: #fffbe6; color: #69a9e9;>"Of course I like racing; the whole thing is fun, after all. From competing to winning."</blockquote>

Excelling at both academics and athletics, she also has the makings of a star; she is the ultimate model student, flawless in all aspects. She was only able to achieve this, however, thanks to her defining trait of absolutely hating to lose, a trait which must be prefaced with no fewer than nine "really"s. She's competitive to a point of perfectionism, and the one flaw in her shining qualities is that she often pushes herself beyond her body's limits.

## Appearance

Almond Eye has long, smooth light brown hair that cascades past her shoulders, with a gentle inward curl at the tips. The inner layer of the hair is light blue with a soft lavender gradient and small sparkles. A single, tight braid wraps from the back to the side of her head, secured with a black ribbon. She has a headband with a large white bow that has black tips, framing her left ear. Her bangs part neatly at the center, revealing a white highlight in the middle of her forehead that resembles a small cross-like sparkle. Small hair clips and accessories decorate the right side of her hair. Her eyes are lavender with sparkling blue gradients that grow lighter toward the bottom. Her irises feature intricate star-shaped pupils.

Her race outfit is a detailed pastel blue idol dress. The bodice is a pale blue, corset-like top with white frills along the neckline and down the chest, tied at the neck with a choker-style ribbon. Red, button-like accents trail down the front, contrasting the cool color scheme. Puffy, off-shoulder white sleeves are adorned with yellow bows, and a set of blue fingerless gloves cover her hands. The skirt is multilayered, alternating between white, blue, and black ruffled tiers, with a large red bow at the front of her waist and a smaller black bow beneath the left hip bearing her name in blue. Two long, diamond-patterned tails hang from her hips, giving the impression of stylized banners. White thigh-high stockings. trimmed with sky-blue straps with pink gradient, rise up her legs, while her ankles are wrapped in curling pastel pink and blue loops. Her shoes are vibrant blue high-top sneakers with white soles and gold emblems on the tongues. Red bows adorn the laces.

** IRL, they are both Triple Tiara winners and are the only horses who have won the Japan Cup twice.

** IRL, Gran Alegria was one of the few horses to defeat Almond Eye during her racing career.

** IRL, Both ran and won the Dubai Turf race, with Vivlos winning in 2017 and Almond Eye winning in 2019, where both ran against each other.

** IRL, both faced off a handful of times, including the 2018 and 2020 Japan Cup.

** IRL, both are Triple Tiara winners, and faced off in the 2020 Japan Cup.
* Rhein Kraft - Friend.

** IRL, both were extremely dominant during their racing careers, with Almond Eye holding the record for number of G1 wins by a Japanese-bred horse with nine, and Smart Falcon holding the record for the number of race wins by a Japanese-bred horse for about ten years.
*Mejiro Bright and Seiun Sky - Friends and upperclassman. Both Mejiro Bright and Seiun Sky teach Almond Eye how to relax and have fun. In turn, Almond Eye learns from them that relaxation can lead to greater success.

## Trivia

* She was announced during the 4th Anniversary PakaLive TV stream on February 22nd, 2025.
* Almond Eye is a semi-limited character. This meant that while she is permanently added to the gacha pools, she cannot be chosen with paid 3-star trainee vouchers.
* In Almond Eye's playthrough, during the Senior Year Japan Cup, she will encounter all Umamusume whose real life counterparts won 7 G1 victories. These include Vodka, Symboli Rudolf, T.M. Opera O, Kitasan Black, and Gentildonna. This scenario is also present on Chapter 1 of her character story.
** This is in reference to the real life horse having 9 G1 wins, a record set in 2020.
* Her eyes have 9 stars inside it, representing all 9 G1 wins that the real horse won. 
* She wears a fluffy hairband to reflect the shadow roll worn by her real-life counterpart.
* She can bake brownies.
* She's a huge fan of the mascot character Kyumaru, a kappa who likes cucumbers as much as she does. 
** This is a reference to a pun of her fan nickname, 九冠馬 (kyuukanba, "Nine Crown Horse"), in which the pronunciation is the same as the word cucumber.
* She is prone to heatstroke and fevers.
** This is likely a reference to her expedition in Hong Kong for the 2019 Hong Kong Cup, where she ended up developing a fever, thus canceling her participation to the race.
*Her racewear was based on a drawing she did as a kid.
*She likes watching murder mystery shows at night.
*She is one of the few Umamusume, like Haru Urara, Mejiro McQueen and Smart Falcon, to have an alternate event if she loses her debut race in the game. This is because in real life, she notably lost her debut to a horse named Nishino Urara.
*In her career story, Almond Eye gets frustrated when the trainer tells her that a certain Umamusume has beaten her record. This is a reference to the 2025 Japan Cup, where Calandagan beat her record time by 0.3 seconds. The current record time is now 2:20.3.
*Her love for porridge in the morning is a reference to how her trainer, Sakae Kunieda, would make porridge for the horses he trained, including Almond Eye.

## Real-life page (excerpt)

Source: https://umamusu.wiki/IRL:Almond_Eye

' is a Japanese racehorse that was active from 2017 to 2020. Considered the most successful Japanese racemare of all time, Almond Eye is the previous record holder of the fastest time over 2400 meters on turf in the world, the 5th racehorse to achieve the fillies' Triple Tiara, and the first Japanese racehorse to surpass career earnings of over 1.9 billion yen, surpassing Kitasan Black's earnings. 

Almond Eye was named Japanese Horse of the Year in both 2018 and 2020, and also earned the titles of Best Three-Year-Old Filly in 2018 and Best Older Filly or Mare in 2020. In 2023, she was inducted as the 35th horse to receive JRA Hall of Fame honor, and became known as the “Nine-Crown Queen” for her remarkable tally of nine Grade 1 victories.

### Name Origin

Her name means "An eye shape of a face considered beautiful". Almond eyes are considered naturally attractive for beauty standards, known for their symmetry and balance.

### Background

Almond Eye was born on March 10th, 2015. She was born on Northern Farm, and she came from Lord Kanaloa's first crop of foals after becoming a sire. Lord Kanaloa won six short-distance G1 races, including two consecutive Hong Kong Sprint victories in 2013 and 2014. Almond Eye's mother, Fusaichi Pandora, won the Queen Elizabeth II Cup in 2006 after Kawakami Princess' disqualification. 

Almond Eye, according to the head of the farm, was "good-looking and a strong horse body, though her body didn't feel like she was a mare." She was also weaned off normally, and was able to train with ease. As she grew into a yearling, she was bought by Silk Racing for 30 million yen,, and it was decided to have her train under Sakae Kunieda at Miho Training Center.

### Racing career

=== Two-year-old season (2017) ===

Almond Eye made her debut on August 6th, 2017, in a 1,400-meter turf race on Niigata Racecourse. As the most popular, despite chasing in the final straight, she could not catch up to Nishino Urara and placed 2nd. However, two months later, she won her next race by three and a half lengths. 
=== Three-year-old season (2018) ===

Almond Eye entered the Shinzan Kinen (G3) on January 8th, 2018. Because her usual jockey, Christophe Lemaire, was suspended from riding at the time, Keita Tosaki rode in his place instead. Though Almond Eye suffered a late start and had to race from the back, in the end, she overtook the leading horses and finished first, marking the race as her first graded victory. She shortly took a break at Northern Farm until the spring.

Three months later, on April 8th, she entered the Oka Sho, the first leg of the Triple Tiara. Christophe Lemaire returned as her jockey in the race, where Almond Eye faced the popular Hanshin Juvenile Fillies winner from the previous year, Lucky Lilac. Almond Eye remained in the back of the race, as Lucky Lilac led the rest of the pack. In the final 200 meters, Lucky Lilac began to break out of the pack, but Almond Eye caught up to her just in time, and won the race by three-quarters of a length. 

A month later, on May 20th, she returned to compete in the Japanese Oaks, aiming for her second Tiara. She had a good start, waiting in the pack until she ran alongside Lucky Lilac in the last 400 meters. At the remaining 200 meters, she surged past her and Lily Noble, winning by two lengths and attaining her second Tiara. She became the 14th Double Tiara winner in six years since the Triple Tiara mare Gentildonna in 2012, and she rested up back at Northern Farm
