## Appearance

Gran Alegria has medium-length brown hair, tied into high twin-tails that flare out in playful arcs. A striking white patch marks the center of her bangs, cutting through her fringe like a painted streak. Above it, a soft, subtle ahoge springs upward. On left side of her bangs is an x-shaped hairclip. Her ears are deep brown with crimson interiors, with multiple gold earrings on her left ear. Her eyes are a rich amber-orange.

Her race outfit is mostly black and crimson with gold detailing. She wears a cropped vest with golden tassels and red plaid accents layered over a red halter top. A pair of decorative pins—including a yellow smiling face with "Ohamile" written on it and a gold megaphone with black and crimson letters "GA"—are attached to the left side of her chest. Her shorts are trimmed with red and gold, cut high and asymmetrical, with belts, chains, and tassels draping off her hips for added flair. A matching belt cinches her waist, adorned with a circular, gold-accented buckle. Black and red bracelets adorn her wrists. Around her thighs, she wears black garters and a golden strap on her left leg, paired with knee-high black socks that are decorated with white stripes. On her feet are oversized white sneakers, thick-soled and cartoonishly large. A red ribbon and gold tassels sways behind her, decorating the tail.

## Relationships

* Almond Eye - Friend and rival. Gran Alegria admires her running, while Almond Eye gets frustrated when she loses to her in miles.
** IRL, Gran Alegria was one of the few horses to defeat Almond Eye during her racing career.
* Loves Only You - Friend and classmate. Gran watches her streams after she finishes her homework.
* Curren Bouquetd'or - Friend and classmate. They sometimes take care of flowerbeds together.
* Chrono Genesis - Friend and classmate. Chrono admires her strength and adherence to her "MiRules", and takes note of how physically fit her body is.
* Taiki Shuttle - Friend and upperclassman. Gran admires Taiki as a fellow miler, and wishes to surpass her. They also both tend to forget to convert miles in meters sometimes. 
* Vivlos - Friend. When it snows, they make snowmen, sled, and have snowball fights with each other.
* Gentildonna - Friend and upperclassman. Gran Alegria admires how strong Gentildonna is, but she also sees the cute side of her. Gentildonna acknowledges her strength. They both like to compete with each other. 
* Bubble Gum Fellow, Symboli Kris S, and Zenno Rob Roy - Friends and upperclassmen. They all look out for Gran Alegria, and help her train. They also give her racing advice, and hang out with her.
** IRL, all three of them won the Tenno Sho (Autumn). Bubble Gum Fellow won it in 1996, Symboli Kris S won it in both 2002 and 2003, and Zenno Rob Roy won it in 2004. Meanwhile, Gran Alegria attempted to win it in 2021, but she lost and placed 3rd. They were also all trained by Kazuo Fujisawa.

## Trivia

* She was announced during the 4th Anniversary PakaLive TV stream on February 22nd, 2025.
* Gran Alegria has the second shortest time gap from her initial introduction to being added as a playable trainee, with 17 days between when she was first announced and when she was added as a trainee. The first is Dream Journey, who was added as a trainee on the same day as her announcement.
* She watches go-kart videos when she isn't hungry.
* She carries a pedometer with her to keep track of her miles.
* She begins and ends her day with salad.
* She likes airplanes.
* Her family doing "English days" is a possible reference to Gran Alegria's mother in real life, Tapitsfly, as she was an American horse.
* Unlike the real life counterpart, Gran Alegria in Umamusume has multiple siblings. The real life counterpart had only one younger brother named Blutgang, who passed away at an early age. 
* Her height being 160cm is possibly a reference to her love of miles, as 160 is 1600 divided by 10, and 1600m is a mile.
* Her love for listening to the radio is a reference to the real horse, in which she would listen to talk shows about diets on the radio at her stable.
* In the senior year Yasuda Kinen, Gran Alegria gets something stuck in her eye. This is a reference how in the 2020 Yasuda Kinen, Kenichi Ikezoe, Gran Alegria's jockey, got hit by a clod of turf in his right eye before the fourth corner and was given a bag of ice by Almond Eye's jockey, Christophe Lemaire during the post-race interview.

## Real-life page (excerpt)

Source: https://umamusu.wiki/IRL:Gran_Alegria

'  is a Japanese racehorse that was active from 2018 to 2021. She is best known for being the first Japanese horse in history to win all JRA mile G1 mile races for older horses, which includes the Yasuda Kinen in 2020, the Victoria Mile in 2021, and the Mile Championship in both 2020 and 2021.

### Name Origin

"Gran Alegria" means big cheers in Spanish.

### Background

Gran Alegria was born on January 24th, 2016 at Northern Farm, and she came from her sire Deep Impact's ninth crop of foals. Deep Impact was the 2005 Triple Crown winner, and won seven G1 races in total. Gran Alegria's dam, Tapitsfly, was an American racehorse who won three American G1 races, which include the Breeders' Cup Juvenile Fillies' Turf, Just a Game Stakes, and First Lady Stakes. After being sold for $1,850,000 USD at the Fasig-Tipton sale, she was exported to Japan to be used as a broodmare. However, Tapitsfly was declared legally dead in 2018 by the time Gran Alegria reached her two-year-old season, and her only sibling, Blutgang, died shortly after his debut race in July 2019 due to cervical spinal stenosis.

Gran Alegria was purchased by Sunday Racing when she became a yearling, and was trained under Kazuo Fujisawa at Miho Training Center. He had trained numerous successful horses previously, including horses like Taiki Shuttle and Zenno Rob Roy.

### Racing career

=== Two-year-old season (2018) ===

Gran Alegria made her debut on June 3rd, 2018, in a 1,600-meter turf race on Tokyo Racecourse. She won her maiden by two lengths, and a few months later she entered the Saudi Arabia Royal Cup (G3) on October 6th. Despite suffering from a late start, she eventually caught up to the horses in front and overtook them, winning by three lengths. 

Instead of entering the filly-only G1 Hanshin Juvenile Fillies, she participated in the Asahi Hai Futurity Stakes on December 16th instead. This was because her main jockey, Christophe Lemaire, was scheduled to race in the Hong Kong International Races the same week. Because of her victories so far, she was the most popular as she was expected to be the first filly in 38 years since Tenmon in 1980 to win the race. Though she was in second throughout the middle of the race, Admire Mars overtook her in the end, and she placed third.
=== Three-year-old season (2019) ===
Avoiding the trial race, she immediately entered the Oka Sho on April 7th, 2019. During the race, Gran Alegria ran in the middle of the pack. At the third corner, she began to accelerate, and in the final stretch, she overtook the horses ahead of her and was in the lead. She won by two lengths and a half, and her time of 1:32.7 beat the record set by the previous year's Triple Tiara winner Almond Eye, her record later beaten by Sodashi in 2021. Not only that, but her 111-day break before the Oka Sho also beat Almond Eye's record break of 89 days.

After that, she entered the NHK Mile Cup on May 5th. She couldn't chase as well as she did in the Oka Sho, and she lost to Admire Mars and finished fourth. However, it was discovered that she interfered with Danon Chaser's path, so her placement was demoted to fifth.

As she rested in the summer, it was initially planned for her to compete in the Sprinters' Stakes, but due to pus accumulation in her left front leg, her break continued.
