## Lead

Nishino Flower is a character in Umamusume: Pretty Derby.

## Biography

<blockquote style="font-style: italic; border-left: 3px solid #F6EB61; padding-left: 1em; color: #E26ECB;>"All flowers want to be by someone's side. Even the smallest, quietest one of them all."</blockquote>

Her smile brightens the room. She's a very thoughtful and kind Umamusume.

A softspoken, adorable and innocent Umamusume. Though she's still a bit unsure of her future, she aims to bloom into an elegant lady. She's very thoughtful, and even if she's caught up with her own responsibilities, she's always considerate of those around her. The season of full-bloom is surely just around the corner.

## Appearance

Flower has dark purple hair in a simple bob with blunt bangs. She wears magenta ear covers, and white and pink ribbons attached to her left leg. She wears a pink headband and has "X" hair clips on either side of her hair. She has round purple eyes.

Her race outfit is a short sleeved dress with a poofy skirt. The white top of her dress is frilled at both the sleeves and collar. On her chest is a large pink and yellow striped ribbon, with a large pink flower in the center. The center of her dress is white with yellow sides, yellow buttons, and purple trim. Her poofy skirt has white and yellow fabric, cut to resemble the petals of a flower. The ends of the flower petals each have a flower design on the tips. Underneath the flower petals is a yellow and purple layered petticoat, with a large white petticoat underneath. She wears pink cuffs around her wrists with white frills on either side. Her yellow boots have one white stripe down the center, and light purple heels. The top cuffs of her boots are red, and have a purple and yellow ribbon with a white flower in the center.

## Relationships

*Seiun Sky - Close friend. She is mostly seen hanging out with Seiun Sky and is very close to her. Seiun Sky looks out for her, and usually doesn't pull pranks on Flower due to her being so nice. Sometimes, she invites Flower to nap with her outside. Despite Seiun Sky's antics, Flower sees the good in Sky, and admires her for being herself.
**In real life, both Flower and Seiun are from the same stable and owned by Masayuki Nishiyama. The two were very close in real life, had a foal together named Nishino Mirai, and were buried next to each other when they passed on.
***They also have a great-grandson named Nishino Daisy, who is referenced in Flower's SR support event, where she makes a crown of daisies for Seiun Sky.
*Mihono Bourbon - Roommate.
*Mayano Top Gun - Friend and classmate. Mayano sometimes gives flower-styling advice to her, and gives Flower the idea to make heart-shaped flower beds.
*Sweep Tosho - Friend and classmate. Sometimes, Sweep teaches her spells after class. Flower also calls her "Sui-chan", showing a close friendship. Sweep also ropes Flower alongside her other classmates into "magical" hijinks on occasion.
*Super Creek - Friend. Creek tends to look after Flower often, and has done so ever since Flower came to the academy, especially when Flower struggles with problems.
*Tosen Jordan - Friend.
*Eishin Flash - Friend.
*Hishi Akebono - Friend. Akebono likes to make large bowls of chanko for Flower.
*Sakura Bakushin O - Friend and fierce rival. Flower may find Bakushin O's silly and unpredictable behavior hard to keep up with at times, but she gets along with her relatively well. Despite being younger, Flower can hold her own against Bakushin O in a race.
**In real life, Nishino Flower's 1992 Sprinters Stakes win marked the only time Sakura Bakushin O was defeated in a distance of less than 1400m. The two of them also debuted in the same 1992 generation.
*Yamanin Zephyr - Friend and rival. They go on picnics together, and train together. Flower looks up to her as an older competitor.
*Air Groove - Friend and upperclassman. They both tend to flowers together.
*Taiki Shuttle - Friend and upperclassman. Taiki likes to play games with Flower, especially hide and seek. 
*Grass Wonder - Friend and fellow member of the Groundskeeping Committee. Flower inspires Grass for how assertive yet calm she is, and asks her for advice. 
*Kawakami Princess - Friend. They both watch Princess Fighter together in their free time.

## Real-life page (excerpt)

Source: https://umamusu.wiki/IRL:Nishino_Flower

' was a Japanese racehorse that was active from 1991 to 1993. She was a successful sprinter of the early 1990s alongside Yamanin Zephyr and Sakura Bakushin O, and became the first three-year-old filly to win the Sprinters Stakes after it became a G1 in 1990.

### Name Origin

"Nishino" is a crown name for horses owned by Nishiyama Farm.

### Background

Before Nishino Flower was born, Shigeyuki Nishiyama had been in the process of reforming Nishiyama Farm. While he was working on constructing new breeding ranches and hiring advanced veterinarians for his horses, he was also trying to purchase new broodmares for the farm. 

He invested 200 million yen into buying broodmares from Kentucky in the United States, one of whom was Duplicit who was already pregnant with Majestic Light's foal. Majestic Light was a successful American sire, who won four G1 races. Duplicit's sire was Danzig, an American horse who became a successful sire in the United States. While Duplicit and the other mares Nishiyama purchased were set to arrive in Japan in the autumn of 1988, their arrival was delayed to February 1989 due to issues with quarantine.

Two months later, on April 19th, 1989, Nishino Flower was born on Nishiyama Farm. Though her dam was American, Nishino Flower was born in Japan, which classified her as a domestic horse. Though there were high expectations for her, she had a small and slender body, which caused numerous trainers to reject her. The last trainer to see her was Masashiro Matsuda, and though he was hesitant to take her in, he saw something intuitive about her physique and saw potential. Nishino Flower was then trained under him at Ritto Training Center in 1991. It was noted that she was good at training, so they were still expectations for her.

### Racing career

=== Two-year-old season (1991) ===

Nishino Flower was originally going to debut on June 8th, but due to soreness in her legs from intense training, her debut was pushed to July 7th. In a 1,000-meter dirt race on Sapporo Racecourse, Nishino Flower won her debut. Since the soreness in her legs was resolved, it was decided that she would compete in turf races from now on.

While it was initially planned that her next race would be the Hakodate Sansai Stakes, it was then decided that she would compete on Sapporo Racecourse again due to the rougher turf on Hakodate Racecourse. She competed in the Sapporo Sansai Stakes (G3) on July 28th. Running within the front of the pack, she eventually accelerated in the final stretch and won by three lengths and a half.

Nishino Flower competed in the Daily Hai Sansai Stakes on November 2nd. Due to the temporary suspension of her jockey Masao Sato, her jockey was replaced by Seiki Tabara for the race. She won by three lengths, and this win earned her a place in the Hanshin Sansai Himba Stakes (G1) on December 1st. At the final straight, she overtook the horses in the lead and won the race by three quarters of a length. This victory gave Nishiyama Farm their first G1 victory.

At the end of the year, Nishino Flower was awarded the JRA Award for Best Two-Year-Old Filly.

=== Three-year-old season (1992) ===

On March 15th, Nishino Flower's win streak ended when she placed 2nd at the Tulip Sho and lost to Adorable. After the Tulip Sho however, her jockey Masao Sato felt responsibility for his defeat and lost confidence in riding her, so Hiroshi Kawachi became her permanent jockey.
