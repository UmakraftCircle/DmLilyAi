## Biography

<blockquote style="font-style: italic; border-left: 3px solid #464647; padding-left: 1em; color: #50a19f;
>"To carve into history a passionate moment with my own legs... That is... my dream...!"</blockquote>

A reserved Umamusume with a passion for studying history. In particular, she is the most interested in the history of racing, and the heartfelt drama weaved together by the 'dreams' of Umamusume and their trainers. She is so fond of it that she likes to get invested in predicting the results of upcoming races, or taking her large camera to racetracks on a weekly basis.

## Appearance

She has long, straight hair colored a cool silvery-gray, which deepens into a shadowy steel blue toward the ends. Her bangs are cut evenly across her forehead with longer strands framing her cheeks, and a small, curved fringe at the center parts slightly. Her long hair cascades freely down her back, nearly reaching her thighs. A golden ornamental hairpiece resembling a compass rests atop her right ear. Two red ribbons are winding into her hair strands behind her head, fixed in place by two gold accessories. Her eyes are large and round, colored a piercing icy blue with a gradient that grows paler near the bottom.

Her race outfit resembles a ceremonial battle dress, blending regality with magical motif. The bodice is white with a high collar, layered beneath a deep plum vest outlined in gold. The vest is adorned with a crimson and white ribbon at the neck and secured by ornate golden pins at the waist. One arm is covered with a flowing white sleeve trimmed in gold, while the other is gloved in a long, fingerless dark violet glove. The skirt is a sharp combination of crimson and navy panels, with diagonal stripes and golden crest details. A large, billowing white sash flows from her left side, layered under a golden hip guard that echoes the design of her headpiece. Her legs are clad in mismatched thigh-highs: one deep red with black diagonal patterns, and the other bare deep red with black. Her footwear consists of sleek, heeled gold-and-white boots, trimmed with red and marked by arcane symbols.

## Trivia

* She was announced during the 4th Anniversary PakaLive TV stream on February 22nd, 2025.
* Her father cooks pintade every Christmas. This is a possible reference to Chrono Genesis' sire, Bago, a French racehorse who won the 2004 Prix de l’Arc de Triomphe.
* Her hair clip was a gift from her grandfather.
* When she was little, she used to put vintage racing magazines under her pillow so she could dream about historical races.
* She dislikes snow, as it could cancel the upcoming races she wants to see.
* During her career mode's senior year, there is an event named "失環" in which her trainer gets struck by a car trying to save a child. This causes them to be in the hospital for a while, until they gain the strength to watch Chrono Genesis' Arima Kinen.
** This is a reference to what happened to her real life jockey, Yuichi Kitamura. During a maiden race at Hanshin Racecourse on May 2nd, 2021, another horse on the track caused the horse he was jockeying to trip, and Kitamura fell in the process. This resulted in multiple bone fractures, he was forced to recuperate for a year, and he was unable to jockey Chrono Genesis for the rest of 2021.
*Throughout her junior and classic year in her career story, Chrono Genesis focuses on an Umamusume named Schon Lilie, an Umamusume who competes in the Takarazuka Kinen and the Arima Kinen.
**This Umamusume is a reference to the real horse Lys Gracieux, who was the first mare to win consecutive Grand Prix races (both the Takarazuka Kinen and the Arima Kinen in 2019).
*In her career story's normal ending, an Umamusume called "Particularly-petite Umamusume" shows up to take revenge on Chrono Genesis, and she vows to beat her in the Arima Kinen coming up.
**This Umamusume is a reference to the real horse Melody Lane, who was known for being the lightest horse in the JRA to win a race. While she did not win a G1 race, she competed in multiple of them, and raced with Chrono Genesis in the 2021 Takarazuka Kinen and Arima Kinen respectively.
*Her mother works as a chronologist. This is a reference to the real life Chrono Genesis' dam, who is named Chronologist.

## Real-life page (excerpt)

Source: https://umamusu.wiki/IRL:Chrono_Genesis

' is a Japanese racehorse who was active from 2018 until 2021. She is the third JRA horse to have won three Grand Prix races (Takarazuka Kinen and Arima Kinen) after Speed Symboli and Grass Wonder, and the first mare to accomplish this feat.

### Name Origin

According to Chrono Genesis's owner, Sunday Racing, her name is a combination of her mother's name, Chronologist, one who studies time, with the Greek word "genesis," which means "beginning." "Chrono Genesis" could be considered to mean "the beginning of time."

### Background

Born on March 6th, 2016, Chrono Genesis was sired by Bago, winner of the 2004 Prix de l'Arc de Triomphe as well as four other French G1s. Prior to Chrono Genesis's success, Bago had not sired a G1 winner in almost a decade. Her dam, Chronologist, was a daughter of the American-bred and Japanese-trained Kurofune, who won the G1 NHK Mile Cup and Japan Cup Dirt among other races on both turf and dirt.

Chrono Genesis has an older half-sister, Normcore, who was sired by Harbinger. She won the Victoria Mile in 2019, becoming Chronologist's first G1-winning foal, and later won the Hong Kong Cup in 2020. During Chrono Genesis's training at Northern Farm, she was considered to have comparable flexibility to Normcore when it came to her racing style, but she was otherwise an average performer.

### Racing career

=== Two-year-old season (2018) ===
Chrono Genesis made her debut on September 2nd, 2018 in an 1,800-meter turf race on Kokura Racecourse, where she won by two lengths. She was supposed to have run her debut race at Niigata Racecourse a week earlier, but due to the World All-Star Jockeys event taking place around the same time, her team could not find a jockey for her and had to delay her entry.

She followed this with a win in the listed Ivy Stakes, where she set a new record for two-year-old runners at Tokyo Racecourse by covering the final three furlongs in a blistering 32.5 seconds. After this, she entered the Hanshin Juvenile Fillies, where she faced young G1 competition for the first time. Chrono Genesis suffered a late start which forced her to race from the very back, and she began to chase at the final straight, but could not reach the winning horse Danon Fantasy. This second-place finish was her first defeat, and her last race of the year.

=== Three-year-old season (2019) ===

After a brief break, Chrono Genesis ran in the Queen Cup (G3), a trial race for the Oka Sho, on February 11th, 2019. She accelerated in the last spurt and won, claiming her first graded victory. Thanks to her win, she was given guaranteed entry to the first leg of the Triple Tiara on April 7th. At the Oka Sho, although Chrono Genesis charged through the pack on the final straight, but could not catch up to the lead horse, Gran Alegria, and placed third. A month later, she entered the Japanese Oaks on May 19th, where her strongest competition included Loves Only You and Curren Bouquetd'or. As Chrono Genesis chased the leader Curren Bouquetd'or, Loves Only You suddenly closed in from behind, overtook both of them, and won the race. Chrono Genesis once again finished in third place.

Chrono Genesis rested until the fall, and proceeded directly to the Shuka Sho on October 13th rather than run in a trial race.
