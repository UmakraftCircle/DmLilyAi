## Appearance

Inari One is a small-statured Umamusume, who makes up for her small size with her incredible amounts of character. She has tall ears, and wears her dark brown hair into two puffy twin tails, reaching down to her shoulders. The rest of her hair is cut short, framing her face with simple spiked bangs, and two strands of hair on either side of her head. On the left side of her head, her twin tail is tied with a yellow ribbon, and on the right side she wears a white kitsune mask with pink and purple accents. She has large round teal eyes, with small bean-shaped eyebrows.

Her racing uniform resembles a kimono, made of various purple hues. The front of the kimono is open, with her chest wrapped in bandages. She wears a purple and red rope choker, with a bow tied on the right side. Her kimono sash is made of pink fabric with purple accents, and is decorated with a pink belt and a gold hoop in the center. Around her waist, she wears a magenta and white rope, with a large ribbon tied on her left hip, with three white pom-poms in the center. Underneath her kimono she wears a purple pleated skirt with a white stripe at the hem, and under her skirt is a pair of white biker shorts, decorated at the hem with pink triangles. Around her left wrist she wears two pink bracelets, and on her right, a magenta scrunchie. She wears wooden open-toed sandals on either foot, both with pink straps. On her right foot she wears a black sock, and on her left, a white sock. Both socks are tied with X-shaped pink ribbons.

## Trivia

*Her initial character profile was uploaded to the official [https://x.com/uma_musu/status/860323767485997056 Twitter] account on May 4, 2017.
**This tweet was a character name prediction quiz, that encouraged fans to try and guess which horse she's named after. The hint was "A lively Edoite Umamusume, despite being from the countryside. Her stature may be small, but she carries endless power and tenacity."
*While she has a distinct way to refer to the male or female trainer in the Japanese version, there is no distinction in Global, as she calls both trainers “Boss”.
