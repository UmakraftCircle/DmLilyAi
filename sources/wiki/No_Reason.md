No Reason is a character in Umamusume: Pretty Derby.

## Biography

<blockquote style="font-style: italic; border-left: 3px solid #9ad2f5; padding-left: 1em; color: #cb243e;
>“In this war-torn race world, the only way forward is to unite the world!”</blockquote>

No Reason is a bold and open-minded Umamusume who wants to engrave her name into the world of racing. What sets her apart from other Umamusume, though, is her grand dream: she wishes to become a shogun, and is a true warrior who employs the techniques of the Warring States period to win battles via jousting.

...Though, apparently she is prone to making big mistakes.

## Appearance

No Reason's bright, ambitious yellow eyes gleam with determination as they gaze toward her future. Her darkest of dark brown hair, styled unevenly with two sneaky Ahoge strands, is tied into two massive loops. Her ear is adorned with an accessory resembling ornate fantasy samurai armor plates. Tied across her forehead is her custom red Hachimaki (鉢巻) proudly emblazoned with an emblem and the Fusa of Shimenawa (標縄) hanging on each side of her face. 

She dons a blazing red and blue samurai robe, adorned with Shimenawa ropes that separates the red into white at the hem. At its center is a striking blue Hiyoku featuring wave patterns—a motif closely tied to samurai tradition. Her shoulders and arms are protected by red armor plates. Beneath the robe lies a red dress bearing her emblem and cinched at the waist is an obi rope. Below the waist, her armor continues with protective plates covering her knees, shins, and feet. Another obi rope ties her shin guards. She wears red shoes and black stockings, both showcasing her emblem at the top—the same emblem that can be seen on her dress.

## Relationships

* Yaeno Muteki - Friend and roommate.
* Tanino Gimlet - Friend, classmate and rival. No Reason views Tanino Gimlet as a rival she must defeat at any means necessary, and Gimlet is intrigued by No Reason's boldness. However, when Gimlet gets injured after the Japanese Derby, No Reason is angered by the fact that she cannot race with her anymore, showing a close rivalry.
* Symboli Kris S - Friend, classmate and rival. No Reason also views Symboli Kris S as a formidable rival, and gets upset when Kris S runs in the Tenno Sho (Autumn) instead of the Kikuka Sho.
* Hishi Miracle - Friend, classmate and rival. No Reason relates to Hishi Miracle, as they are both "underdogs" in the racing world. No Reason sees the potential and strength in Hishi Miracle, and they both train together. No Reason also brings out the confidence in Hishi Miracle, and Hishi Miracle begins to believe in herself with No Reason's help.
* Durandal - Friend. Though they have not raced each other due to taking different paths, they are good friends and get along due to their eccentric behaviors. No Reason goes along with Durandal's belief of being a knight.
** IRL, No Reason, Durandal, Hishi Miracle, Symboli Kris S, and Tanino Gimlet were all a part of the 2002 generation. 
* Narita Brian - Friend and upperclassman. Despite No Reason's eccentric personality clashing with hers, Narita Brian sees the strength in her. When Brian watched No Reason win the Satsuki Sho, it reminded her of her own Satsuki Sho win back then.
* Dantsu Flame - Friend. No Reason connects with her as a fellow "underdog", and encourages her to get stronger. Introduced by Hishi Miracle, Dantsu Flame gets along well with No Reason, and Dantsu also finds No Reason's attitude to be admirable in her own way.
* Phalaenopsis - Friend. 
** IRL, No Reason, Dantsu Flame, Narita Brian, and Phalaenopsis were all sired by Brian's Time. Tanino Gimlet was also sired by Brian's Time.

## Trivia

* She was announced during the 3rd Anniversary PakaLive TV stream on February 22nd, 2024.
* Her samurai theming is a reference to the real horse participating in the Soma Nomaoi, a traditional festival in Fukushima Prefecture after his racing and breeding career. 
* The line about "big mistakes" in her biography is likely a reference to the 2002 Kikuka Sho, where the real horse was the betting favorite, but stumbled at the start of the race, unseating his jockey, disqualifying himself and instantly making 11 billion yen of betting tickets worthless.
* She likes to act out Sengoku-era period dramas. She also likes Kabuki theatre.
* She is skilled at reciting haikus.
* Her career story is the only one to directly reference how Sunday Silence dominated Japanese pedigrees throughout the racing world. A reference to this is No Reason trying to stop the "Silent Clan" from dominating races, which includes Umamusume like Special Week.
* She loves to drink soda, specifically cola.
* No Reason has an Umamusume that she looks up to. She has rosemary earrings, and used to run before an injury forced her to quit. This is a reference to No Reason's half sister in real life, Rosmarinus, who had to quit after the open-class race Shiragiku Sho due to injury.
* As a reference to how her sire Brian's Time and her dam Ambrosine are American horses, No Reason is American in Umamusume.

## Real-life page (excerpt)

Source: https://umamusu.wiki/IRL:No_Reason

' was a Japanese racehorse that was active from 2002 to 2004. He won the 2002 Satsuki Sho as a longshot at odds of 115.9. He was notorious for unseating Yutaka Take at the start of the 2002 Kikuka Sho, while being poised as the favorite to win the race. In retirement, he participated in the Soma Nomaoi Festival in Minamisoma, Fukushima. The event celebrates samurai martial skills and horse riding.

### Name Origin

A common rumor is that when he was transferred to Yasuo Ikee's stable, he was given the name "No Reason", as a North Hills secretary remarked, "a strong horse needs no reason."

### Racing career

=== Three-year-old season (2002) ===
No Reason made his debut on January 5th, in a 1,800-meter turf race on Kyoto Racecourse. After he won his debut, he ran in the open-class Kobushi Sho on February 9th, where he won for the second time in a row. However, on March 16th, he lost and placed 7th in the open-class Wakaba Stakes.

Because of this, he initially lost priority entry to the Satsuki Sho, the first leg of the Triple Crown. However, as there was a draw for the race, No Reason won this draw, and he was allowed entry. As the 15th favorite with the odds of 115.9, he entered the Satsuki Sho on April 14th. Running in a good position, No Reason surged past the other horses and won the race, attaining his first and final G1 victory. He then entered the Japanese Derby on May 26th, where he lost to Tanino Gimlet and placed 8th. 

After months of rest from a fracture, No Reason returned to compete in the Kobe Shimbun Hai (G2), where he placed 2nd to Symboli Kris S. On October 20th, he entered the Kikuka Sho as the overwhelming favorite, with odds of 2.5. However, immediately after exiting the starting gate, No Reason dropped his jockey Yutaka Take, immediately disqualifying him from the race. Not only did he lose, the 10th favorite Hishi Miracle won instead. He continued to not place well, as he placed 8th in the Japan Cup on November 24th. His final race of the year was the Arima Kinen on December 22nd, where he lost and placed 6th to Symboli Kris S, who won at nine lengths. 
=== Four-year-old season (2003) ===
No Reason only ran in two races in his four-year-old season. On February 22nd, he entered the Kyoto Kinen (G2), where he finished in 5th place. He then ran in the Hanshin Daishoten (G2) on March 23rd, where he placed 4th. While there were plans to have him compete in the Tenno Sho (Spring), No Reason developed flexor tendonitis, which forced him to recuperate for the rest of the year.

=== Five-year-old season (2004) ===
After coming back from injury, No Reason ran in the Asahi Challenge Cup (G3) on September 11th, his first race in almost 18 months. He finished 11th place, but soon after his superficial tendonitis reoccurred. Because of this, he was retired from racing shortly after his injury and was transferred to Yushun Stallion Station.

### Retirement

In 2005, he began his career as a stallion, with his first crop debuting in 2008. The performance of his first crop was disappointing and he did not mate with many mares, only receiving 2 mares in 2009. He was retired from stud on January 1st, 2010.

In 2014, he was transferred to Shikagashira Stable. From 2014 to 2023, he participated in the Soma Nomaoi, a traditional event held in Soma, Fukushima. 

On May 7th, 2024, he passed away at the age of 25.
