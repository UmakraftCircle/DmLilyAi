## Appearance

Ikuno Dictus appears to be a sturdy and sharp Umamusume, just by how refined and sleek she appears. Her orange blunt bob is cut perfectly, with not a singular strand of hair out of place. Her narrow and long braid is neatly done, and reaches down to her upper leg. She has a white blaze on the center of her bangs. Her ears are narrow, and an elaborate metal ear decoration around her left ear. She wears large round glasses with silver frames over her pale yellow eyes.

Her racing outfit resembles a dressage outfit. She wears a white button up vest with teal stripes on the outside. Under this is a white collared shirt, with a red tie tucked under the vest. To complete this ensemble, she wears a teal blazer with a long red coattail. The blazer is adorned with silver trim around the entire design, and she has red arm bands on either sleeve, with the tight-fit cuffs being made out of black leather, and reaching up her forearm. The collar of her blazer is held together by a silver metal chain. She wears white pants, with a dark brown belt and a silver decorative buckle. On her legs she wears a pair of dark brown boots that reach above her knees, and have steel toes and heels.

## Relationships

* Nice Nature, Twin Turbo, Matikanetannhauser - Friends and teammates on the anime-only team Team Canopus.
** In Matikanetannhauser's character story, Nice Nature, Twin Turbo, and Ikuno Dictus regularly attend her races to support and cheer her on. The four of them are often seen together as well.
* Sounds of Earth, Royce and Royce - Teammates on the anime-only team Team Canopus starting from Season 3.
* Mejiro McQueen - Roommate and friend.
** The two mated together in real life, giving birth to the foal Kisoji Queen.
*Yamanin Zephyr - Friend. Ikuno taught her how to ride a bicycle a while ago.
*Daiichi Ruby - Friend. They both relate to each other, as they both had leg problems when they were younger. They sometimes discuss how to wear proper cleats for their conditions.

"Ikuno" is the Crown name of her Owner Noriaki Katsuno. While "Dictus" is taken directly from her sire, the French Stallion Dictus.
