## Lead

Zenno Rob Roy is a character in Umamusume: Pretty Derby.

## Biography

<blockquote style="font-style: italic; border-left: 3px solid #F3EA5D; padding-left: 1em; color: #5F9B26;>"To be like the hero of a fantastical tale... I can't imagine any book lover not wishing the same."</blockquote>

One day, she'll write her own epic tale. A voracious reader with a rich imagination.

A bibliophile who serves as a student library assistant at Tracen Academy. Though she comes off as quiet and reserved, within her burns the desire to embody the hero of an epic tale. She had always overcome most hurdles with ease, but rarely stood out. Ever since winning a certain freestyle race, however, the humble dream of becoming like the hero Rob Roy has welled up within her.

## Appearance

Rob Roy has cool gray hair, tied into a braid that rests over her shoulder. She has blunt cut bangs, and a braid across her forehead, with two white clips consisting of two triangles on either side. Her ears are longer than most Umamusume, and have a de-saturated pink inside. She has a band of white flowers with blue centers around her right ear. She wears large glasses with thick black frames, and has downturned blue eyes.

Her race outfit is very reminiscent of a Scottish folktale hero. She wears a dark green brimless hat on her head, with a gold medal on the side. Her outfit is dark green with gold trims, and consists of a long sleeved coat on top of a long skirt. Her jacket's cuffs are yellow with a dark green band around the center, and she wears a green and gold band around her right arm. Over top of her shirt she has a brown belt around her waist and shoulder with gold adornments. The belt holds a green, yellow, blue, and white plaid shawl in place. Her dark green skirt has a yellow zig-zag pattern at the base, with a white stripe underneath, and the hem is decorated with white frills. She wears white knitted knee-high socks with green ribbons on either side. Her shoes are simple loafers with brown buckles, the left shoe being white, and the right is brown.

## Relationships

* Neo Universe - Close friend. Rob Roy was one of the first people who went out of her way to understand Universe's strange mannerisms, they both share interest in books, and Rob Roy often recommends Uni books about space.
* Agnes Digital - Friend. Both enjoy reading, and can often sympathize with each other. Digital also notices Rob Roy while others don't, which surprises Rob Roy.
* Sweep Tosho -  Friend. Rob Roy often teaches Sweep knowledge related to magic. While Rob Roy is obsessed with being a folk hero, Sweep is obsessed with being a witch, and they both like to read fantasy books in the library. Sweep enjoys teaching Rob Roy about magic, and Rob Roy herself seems to believe in her power and strength. Rob Roy admires Sweep as a friend and rival, and vows to defeat her in future races.
** In real life, they challenged each other in the 2005 Takarazuka Kinen, where Sweep Tosho defeated him in an upset win. They also challenged each other in the 2005 Tenno Sho (Autumn), where they both lost to the long-shot mare Heavenly Romance.
* Symboli Kris S - Friend, upperclassman, and mentor. Rob Roy deeply admires Kris S, and wants to emulate her. She is one of the reasons why she wants to take on the Triple Crown route, especially the Derby. She looks up to her strength and her quiet demeanor. Kris S, though not very sociable, acknowledges Rob Roy as a strong racer, and they both train and spend time together. Rob Roy also finds Kris S' ears cute and fluffy.
** In real life, Symboli Kris S and Zenno Rob Roy raced together once in the 2003 Arima Kinen, where Symboli Kris S won by nine lengths. They also had the same trainer, Kazuo Fujisawa, and they have trained with each other at Fantast Club.
* Rice Shower - Roommate and friend. They both enjoy books, Rice's interest being in picture books, and can understand each other as they're both a bit reserved.
* Admire Groove - Friend. Rob Roy admires the assertive behavior of Admire Groove, despite also being concerned over her cold demeanor.
* T.M. Opera O - Friend. Opera O and Rob Roy sometimes hang out other, and Opera O likes to drag Rob Roy into her operas and plays. However, Opera O can sense the true strength yet the insecurities Rob Roy has about herself, so she tries to lift her up and give her confidence. Because of Opera O's odd attitude, it inspires Rob Roy into becoming more confident and assertive like her.
** In real life, both of them won the Senior Autumn Triple Crown, which consists of the Tenno Sho (Autumn), the Japan Cup, and the Arima Kinen. T.M. Opera O won this in 2000, while Zenno Rob Roy won this in 2004.
*Taiki Shuttle - Friend and upperclassman. Taiki's strength and race record puts Rob Roy in awe, and Rob Roy sometimes asks her how it was like for Taiki to be Umamusume of the Year. Taiki likes to invite Rob Roy to her barbecues alongside her other friends.
**In real life, both Taiki Shuttle and Zenno Rob Roy had the same trainer, Kazuo Fujisawa. They were also both Horse of the Year, with Taiki Shuttle winning in 1998, while Zenno Rob Roy won in 2004.
*Manhattan Cafe - Friend and upperclassman. Both Cafe and Rob Roy like to read stories, albeit the stories Cafe likes to read tends to scare Rob Roy.

*Automated doors don't respond to her presence immediately.
*She tends to act out scenes from books out loud in public.
*She likes going to the zoo.

## Real-life page (excerpt)

Source: https://umamusu.wiki/IRL:Zenno_Rob_Roy

' was a Japanese racehorse who was active from 2003 until 2005. He was the second horse to achieve the Senior Autumn Triple Crown after T.M. Opera O in 2000, and is currently one of the only horses to have achieved this. Additionally, Zenno Rob Roy's record time in the 2004 Arima Kinen remains unbroken over two decades later.

### Name Origin

The crown name "Zenno" was chosen by Shinobu Osako, who was the president of Zenrin, a map publishing company. After Shinobu Osako's death in 2005, Kumiko Osako inherited the stable and crown name. "Rob Roy" originates from the Scottish outlaw and folk hero Rob Roy MacGregor.

### Background

Zenno Rob Roy was born on March 27th, 2000, on Shiraoi Farm. He was sired by legendary American sire Sunday Silence (Kentucky Derby, Preakness, Breeders' Cup Classic). His dam, Roamin Rachel, won the American G1 Ballerina Handicap. Since Roamin Rachel was a successful sprinter who won sprint races, it was initially expected that Zenno Rob Roy would also be proficient in short-distance races. 

As Zenno Rob Roy was growing up, he was regarded as the best yearling at Shiraoi Farm, and was described as an "honor student" by the breeding manager at the farm. When he became a yearling, he was purchased by Shinobu Osako, and began training under Kazuo Fujisawa at Miho Training Center.

### Racing career

=== Three-year-old season (2003)===
Zenno Rob Roy made his debut on February 9th, in a 1,600-meter turf race at Nakayama Racecourse which he won by two and a half lengths. On March 2nd, he competed in the open-class Sumire Stakes, where he placed 3rd. On April 12th, he entered and won the open-class Yamabuki Sho. 

On May 3rd, he competed in the Aoba Sho (G2), where he won by two and a half lengths and attained his first graded victory. Because of this victory, he gained entry into the Japanese Derby on June 1st. Despite fighting hard in the stretch, he lost to Neo Universe and placed 2nd. After this, he rested for a few months at Fantast Club, alongside Symboli Kris S. 

After his break, Zenno Rob Roy returned on September 28th and won his second graded victory at the Kobe Shimbun Hai (G2). While his connections initially planned for him to run the Autumn Tenno Sho, where he would have competed against Symboli Kris S, Rob Roy's trainer Kazuo Fujisawa believed that his Kobe Shimbun Hai win indicated that he could handle the distance of the Kikuka Sho. As such, he was entered into the Kikuka Sho instead. 

On October 26th, Zenno Rob Roy challenged the Kikuka Sho. He was in a good position early on in the race, but was blocked and placed 4th; both he and Neo Universe lost to That's the Plenty. Due to his exhaustion after running in the Kikuka Sho, plans to have Rob Roy enter the Japan Cup were scrapped.

His final race of the year was the Arima Kinen on December 28th, where he raced with Symboli Kris S for the first and last time. At a high pace set by Tap Dance City, Zenno Rob Roy ran in a good position throughout the race. However, he could not catch up to Symboli Kris S, who won by nine lengths, and he placed 3rd.

=== Four-year-old season (2004) ===
After a few months of rest, Zenno Rob Roy entered the Nikkei Sho (G2) on March 27th, where he was narrowly beaten by Win Generale. On May 2nd, he entered the Tenno Sho (Spring), where he challenged Neo Universe for the final time, but placed a distant second as the two were once again defeated by the 71-to-1 outsider Ingrandire.
