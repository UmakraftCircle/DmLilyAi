## Biography

<blockquote style="font-style: italic; border-left: 3px solid #f1eb9d; padding-left: 1em; color: #1650e3;>"Maybe one day, the whole world will connect through hope and form one big ring... That is my wish."</blockquote>

An Umamusume with a pure and warm heart who dislikes loneliness or solitude. If she sees anyone troubled by such woes, she'll run up to them right away and keep company until their heart has connected to hope. This act of hers is described as "angelic" by many, or so they say. Her hobbies are stargazing and appreciating art.

## Appearance

Victoire Pisa has very long, curly light-blonde hair that flows below her waist; she has two long bangs framing her face extending below her shoulders. On her right ear is a white ear cover with a frilled, blue-and-white striped bow. She also has a left side ponytail fastened by a frilly white hair tie with blue accents. Her eyes are a vivid gradient, beginning in a magenta color before transitioning into purple and a lighter cyan nearing the bottom.

For her race outfit, Victoire Pisa wears a white backless dress while donning a white beret decorated with gold trim, which features a ribbon tied into a large bow and fastened by a gold button at the back. She wears a red neckerchief with blue and white stripes around her collar underneath a black nylon underlayer that covers her neckline. She wears a primarily white bodice with navy blue accents on the lapels and down the front of the chest, with gold trim, buttons, and ornamentation resembling vines on the lapels. Her detached white sleeves are secured by navy blue cuffs adorned with the same ornamentation on her bodice; the sleeves are puffier at the ends, decorated with a small blue bow and a layer of frills, before tapering into thinner golden cuffs with red and navy blue stripes and white gloves. The bows on her outfit transition from a dark to a vibrant blue color with a glittering starscape pattern along the ends, which form into pairs of wings.

Below her bodice is a long, multi-tiered skirt. The first layer consists of white pleated fabric with a golden border and navy blue lining. The skirt itself is primarily white, just above ankle-length, and flares to form an opening down the front while secured by a pair of ornate gold aiguillettes. A long, banner-shaped piece of fabric with alternating white and navy blue stripes drapes over the opening of the skirt, revealing a navy blue petticoat underneath. The skirt is decorated in a golden trim and stylized vine ornamentation, as well as a hemline adorned with red and navy blue stripes. Underneath the skirt, she wears black pantyhose and high-heeled, navy blue shoes with hazel-colored toe boxes and laces; the left shoe has a white cuff with a navy blue border, while the right shoe has the opposite.

## Relationships

* Sounds of Earth - Roommate. Earth recommends concerts to Pisa, while Pisa recommends art exhibitions to her. Pisa also enjoys Earth's music.
* Neo Universe - Friend. They often stargaze together. Pisa also understands Universe's speech very well.
** IRL, Victoire Pisa and Sounds of Earth were both sired by Neo Universe.
* Eishin Flash - Friend and rival. Pisa looks up to her, as Flash is able to connect people through her victories in races. They get along well, and they train together.
* Rose Kingdom - Friend and rival. While at first, Rose is closed off, Pisa reaches out to her, and shows her that friendship and connection is a good thing. They get along well, and they train together from time to time.
* Rulership - Friend and rival. Victoire Pisa tends to comfort Rulership when she's in a weakened mental state. Rulership looks up to Pisa, and is somewhat envious of her good qualities, such as her kindness. She also views Pisa as inspiring, and was brought to tears when Pisa won the Dubai World Cup. 
** In real life, Eishin Flash, Rose Kingdom, Rulership and Victoire Pisa were rivals throughout their careers, starting with their classic years.
*** Rulership and Victoire Pisa were also stablemates at Sumii Stable while they were active. 
* Wonder Acute - Friend and classmate. They do arts and crafts together, such as beadwork.
* Buena Vista - Friend. Both of them connect with each other due to their unbounding kindness for others. 
* Transcend - Friend and rival.
* Red Desire - Friend. As Umamusume who both strive to be kind to others, they bond well. Red Desire finds Pisa's angelic behavior inspiring and saint-like, while Pisa views her as a sweet and helpful friend. Red Desire is also considerate of the games they play together, as she actively takes out connection puzzles so that Pisa won't be upset.

## Trivia

* She was announced during the fifth anniversary PakaLive TV stream on February 22nd, 2026.
* Victoire Pisa is tied with Kiseki and Calstone Light O, having the 5th shortest time gap from introduction to being playable trainee, with 25 days between when she was first announced and when she was added as a trainee, behind Phalaenopsis at 20 days, Transcend at 19 days, Gran Alegria at 17, and Dream Journey's immediate banner.
* A subtle reference to the real horse being sired by Neo Universe was her hair color. Both Victoire Pisa and Neo Universe were bay horses in real life, but possess a color of the opposite spectrum in their Umamusume designs (Neo Universe has blonde hair while Victoire Pisa has white hair).
* During training, one of the in-game events includes an earthquake that references the same earthquake that happened in Japan on 11th March, 2011 in real life. Because of the scale of the disaster, which may lead the reference to cause discomfort in individuals affected by it, the official Umamusume X account posted a warning about the reference.
*Mackerel sandwiches are her favorite late-night snack. This could be a possible reference to how Victoire Pisa was transferred to Turkey to perform stud duty there. In Turkey, mackerel sandwiches, also known as Balık ekmek, are a common street food.
**Victoire Pisa also likes to eat kebabs, which are commonly eaten in Turkey.
*She has a French penpal that she video calls.
*She likes knitting.
* She has difficulty at doing puzzle rings.
* In both her career and character story, Victoire Pisa remembers the day when her childhood junior club had engulfed in flames, though no one got hurt.
** This event is a reference to the Yamamoto Training Center fire that happened on February 11th, 2000, where 22 horses died in the fire. While he was active, Victoire Pisa rested at Yamamoto Training Center for a month after his Arima Kinen victory in 2010.
*She struggles playing otome games, as she is unable to choose a love interest.
*The turquoise stone on her tail's rubber band tie is a nod to how Victoire Pisa was moved to Turkey to perform stud duty. The turquoise stone is also known as the "Turkish stone", as turquoise means "Turkish" in old French.

## Real-life page (excerpt)

Source: https://umamusu.wiki/IRL:Victoire_Pisa

'  is a Japanese racehorse that was active from 2009 to 2011. He notably became the first Japanese-bred and trained racehorse to win the Dubai World Cup in 2011.

### Name Origin

"Victoire" is derived from the Montagne Sainte-Victoire mountain range in southern France. "Pisa" is the crown name for owner Yoshimi Ichikawa.

### Background

Victoire Pisa was born on March 31st, 2007, on Shadai Farm. He was sired by Neo Universe, the winner of the 2003 Satsuki Sho and Japanese Derby. His dam, Whitewater Affair, won a G2 and a G3 in the United Kingdom, and her sire Machiavellian won two French G1 races. Victoire Pisa's older brother, Asakusa Den'en, won the Yasuda Kinen in 2005. 

Victoire Pisa was then owned by Yoshimi Ichikawa, and as a yearling, he began training under Katsuhiko Sumii at Ritto Training Center. Because of his pedigree, there were high expectations for him as a racehorse, and he trained well. Trainer Sumii was awed by Victoire Pisa's large size and how he ran lightly.

### Racing career

=== Two-year-old season (2009) ===
Victoire Pisa made his debut on October 25th, in a 1,800-meter turf race on Kyoto Racecourse. Though he placed 2nd and lost to Rose Kingdom, Victoire Pisa finally broke his maiden a week later on November 7th. At the end of the month, he won the ungraded Kyoto Nisai Stakes on the 28th. His first graded victory came a month later, winning the Radio Nikkei Hai Nisai Stakes (G3) on December 26th. 

=== Three-year-old season (2010) ===
Victoire Pisa continued his win streak into the new season with victories in the G2 Yayoi Sho and G1 Satsuki Sho. This streak was finally broken in the Japanese Derby, where he finished 3rd behind Eishin Flash and Rose Kingdom. 

He traveled to Europe soon after to participate in two French races: the G2 Prix Niel, in which he placed 4th, and the G1 Prix de l'Arc de Triomphe, where he was 7th while Nakayama Festa finished second, a nose after the winner Workforce.  

His first race back in Japan was his first attempt at the Japan Cup. In a controversial finish, Victoire Pisa came 3rd behind Rose Kingdom and Buena Vista, who was subsequently demoted to 2nd place due to interference in the final straight.  

He capped off the year with his first attempt at the Arima Kinen, where he once again clashed with Buena Vista. The race came down to the final straight, with Victoire Pisa winning by a nose.

=== Four-year-old season (2011) ===
After his victory in the Nakayama Kinen, Victoire Pisa contested the G1 Dubai World Cup. While this was his first race off a turf track, Japanese horses had seen success on the newly built Meydan Racecourse in Dubai, with Red Desire winning the G2 Al Maktoum Challenge Round 3 in 2010. At the time, the track used an all weather synthetic tapeta surface that was suitable for turf horses to adapt to. Victoire Pisa left Japan along with Buena Vista, Transcend and Rulership enroute to the UAE on March 10th.

A day after leaving Japan, the country was struck by a 9.1 magnitude earthquake, east of the Oshika Peninsula of the Tōhoku region, with a tsunami resulting shortly after. The event became known as the Great East Japan Earthquake Disaster. Miyagi, one of the prefectures heavily affected in the disaster, is home of the Yamamoto Training Center, where Victoire Pisa was stabled after running in the Arima Kinen.
