## Appearance

Hishi Miracle has short, yet fluffy, light-gray hair. She wears white ear covers with two blue stripes on both ears. On her right ear cover is a ring, with a yellow tassle-like ribbon attached. Her slightly droopy eyes are a de-saturated green, that fade into light orange.

Her racing outfit consists of a sleeveless white dress with light blue trim around the collar, and two thin white straps on both of her bare shoulders. On the left side of the top is a dark blue stripe, and separating the top from the skirt is a golden trim. Her layered skirt is light blue at the top, with a zig-zag pattern adorned with gold trim, and underneath this layer is a simple white skirt, with a blue line at the bottom. Her puffed sleeves are separated from the dress, and are white with light blue accents. She wears light gray tights underneath her simple boots. Her right boot being white, and the left, black. Both boots have light blue trims at the base.

## Relationships

* Dantsu Flame -  Friend and roommate. Miracle is very comfortable around Dantsu, finding her cute and teasing her at any opportunity. She leverages her status as Dantsu's senior at every opportunity.
** Not only were they contemporaries who ran together often in real life, they were both considered underdogs as they debuted with very strong rivals. They both achieved a win in Takarazuka Kinen. It's likely this is the basis for them being roommates.
*Narita Top Road - Friend. Top Road and Miracle get along very well, often hanging out. They also share a passion for soccer and play all kinds of variations of it together.
**IRL, they are both sired by Soccer Boy. Their close friendship and affinity for soccer is based on this.
* Tanino Gimlet - Friend and classmate. Miracle may not understand what Gimlet is saying most of the time, but she values her as a friend greatly in addition to seeing her as a brilliant and strong Umamusume. She makes a great effort to look up her colorful vocabulary in her own time so that she can understand her better.
**They debuted in the same 2002 generation. Due to Tanino Gimlet's early retirement, they never faced each other, but they are still associated with each other due to being Classics winners.
*No Reason - Friend, classmate and rival. No Reason relates to Hishi Miracle, as they are both "underdogs" in the racing world. No Reason sees the potential and strength in Hishi Miracle, and they both train together. No Reason also brings out the confidence in Hishi Miracle, and Hishi Miracle begins to believe in herself with No Reason's help.
**They debuted in the same 2002 generation and raced each other often across their respective careers. They are also fellow Classics winners.
*Symboli Kris S - Friend and classmate. Miracle finds Kris S's strong presence overwhelming at times, but they are good friends who hang out often. Due to Kris S being strong, there are moments where Miracle compares herself to her, wondering if she could measure up to the dazzling Umamusume around her.
**They debuted in the same 2002 generation and raced each other often across their respective careers.
*Fine Motion - Friend.
**They debuted in the same 2002 generation and raced each other once, in 2002 Arima Kinen.
*Neo Universe - Friend. They have a sweet and amicable friendship. Miracle tries her best to communicate with Universe and vice versa.
**They were contemporaries who ran together often in real life.
*Tap Dance City - Friend. Miracle receives encouragement from Tap, who in contrast to own her average nature, is quite bold and larger-than-life in her demeanor. They bond over being "self made" Umamusume, a sentiment proposed by Tap.
**They were contemporaries who ran together often in real life.
*K.S.Miracle - Friend. In contrast to Hishi Miracle, K.S.Miracle is more serious. However, despite their differing personalities, they get along well. While their friendship is at first strained when Hishi Miracle discovers that K.S.Miracle has numerous health conditions, Hishi Miracle brings comfort to her in her own way.
*Hishi Akebono - Friend. They go out to eat okonomiyaki together. Akebono is also impressed by how skilled Hishi Miracle is at cooking okonomiyaki.

## Trivia

* She was announced during the 2nd Anniversary PakaLive TV stream on February 22nd, 2023.
* Character Design Notes from Umamusume: Pretty Derby Artworks Vol. 4:
** Though the motif horse had more dark when active, her hair is gray to match the gray haired horse.
** As the motif horse had a blue diamond shape on his hood, the gems and the small pendant she wears are diamond shaped. There are also diamonds on the bottom of the shoe.
** As the motif horse only had a marking on his right hind leg, the racing outfit shoes are black and white to reflect this.
*Her hatred for swimming is a reference to the real life horse, in which he was infamous for being a poor swimmer.
*After she wins the Takarazuka Kinen, there's an old man in the audience who is particularly excited over her victory.
**This old man is a reference to a man who was a better in the 2003 Takarazuka Kinen, in which he won a higher amount of money when Hishi Miracle won the race. 
*She has a general love for food, and especially likes okonomiyaki. Her love for food is likely a nod to how the real horse has a strong appetite.  
**Her love for okonomiyaki is likely a reference to a 2014 JRA commercial, where the Japanese actor Yutaka Takenouchi compares the 2003 Takarazuka Kinen that Hishi Miracle won to these okonomiyaki ingredients; shrimp, squid, Ezo scallop, and opilio crab.
***This commercial is also referenced in Hishi Miracle's career, as she compares the competitors in the senior year Takarazuka Kinen to okonomiyaki ingredients.  
*Her family taking her to soccer matches when she was young is a reference to her real life sire's name, Soccer Boy. 
*She is good at making drinks with numerous fruits mixed in.

## Real-life page (excerpt)

Source: https://umamusu.wiki/IRL:Hishi_Miracle

'  is a Japanese racehorse that was active during 2001 to 2005. A late blooming racehorse who would go on to win The Kikuka Sho in 2002, Spring Tenno Sho and the Takarazuka Kinen in 2003 against heavy favorites.

### Name Origin

Crown name "Hishi" is from the owner Masaichiro Abe.

### Racing career

=== Two-year-old season (2001) ===

==== Debut and maiden race struggles (2001 - mid 2002) ====

Hishi Miracle made his debut on August 11th, 2001 in a 1,200-meter turf race at Kokura Racecourse. His jockey, Koichi Tsunoda, said at the time, "He'd be better with a longer distance". With three more losses on 1200 meters, he was put on longer (1800 - 2000m) races, and finally won on his 10<sup>th</sup> maiden race in the spring of his three year old season in 2002. He would start slowly climbing the race classes with top three placements, and his team would set sights on the Kikuka Sho after his win at the Nowaki Tokubetsu.

=== Three-year-old season (2002) ===

==== The Kikuka Sho (late 2002) ====
After the decision from the team, he ran in the Kikuka Sho trial race, the Kobe Shinbun Hai, as his first graded race. He placed 6th and failed to get priority entry for the Kikuka Sho, which is given to the top three placers. Without priority or prize money to enter the Kikuka Sho, trainer Sayama made the bold decision to pay the 2 million yen late fee for classic registration without informing the owner Abe. There were 8 horses competing for only 3 slots available, and the additional entry fee would not be refunded if he failed the lottery draw. Hishi Miracle won the draw and was given a slot at the Kikuka sho. 

The favorite at the Kikuka Sho was the Satsuki Sho winner, No Reason, as the Japanese Derby winner Tanino Gimlet had already been retired from injury, and the 2<sup>nd</sup> place at the derby Symboli Kris S was aiming for the Tenno Sho (Autumn). Hishi Miracle was the 10th favorite as his performance at the Kobe Shinbun Hai did not inspire confidence. At the race, Yutaka Take was dismounted from No Reason out of the gate and was disqualified, while Hishi Miracle stayed at the back of the pack. In a fast paced race, he utilized his spurt early and used the slope on the 3rd corner of the Kyoto Racecourse to overtake the entire pack and stand at the front, in a manner similar the Kikuka Sho wins of Mr. C.B. in 1983 and Gold Ship in 2012. Despite the end closer Fast Tateyama making it within a nose, Hishi Miracle crossed the finish line in 1<sup>st</sup>, making it his first G1 victory and first graded victory as well.

=== Four-year-old season (2003) ===

==== The Tenno Sho (Spring) ====
After the Kikuka Sho, he lost three races in a row in the Arima Kinen, Hanshin Daishoten, and Sankei Osaka Hai. In the Tenno Sho (Spring), there were only two previous G1 winners, Hishi Miracle and the 2002 Takarazuka Kinen winner Dantsu Flame. Though the level of the race was said to be quite low, but Hishi Miracle was an underestimated 7th favorite behind graded winners Tsurumaru Boy, Daitaku Bertram, and Toho Shiden. Hishi Miracle raced and won with the same strategy as the Kikuka Sho, spurting early and overtaking the pack over the 3rd and 4th corners of the Kyoto Racecourse. 

Trainer Sayama first aimed for the Yasuda Kinen to utilize Hishi Miracle's long spurt length on the longer straight of Tokyo Racecourse, but pivoted to the Takarazuka Kinen as to not cause too much fatigue to the horse with a tight schedule.
