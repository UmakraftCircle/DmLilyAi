## Appearance

Biwa Hayahide has thick, highly voluminous white hair that spreads widely behind her head and falls down her back. The ends of her hair are large and curly, contributing to its distinctive fullness. Her white horse ears and white horse tail are also slightly wider than average. 

Below each horse ear is a small gold shield-shaped hair ornament featuring crescent-like indentations along the lower sides. Around the base of her right horse ear is a small gold crown accessory.

Her bangs are straight and white, ending just above her eyes. The far left side of her forehead remains exposed, while a single long strand extends from the left side of her bangs, curving outward and away from her face. Two long white strands frame her face and reach down to her chest. The left strand is noticeably wider, while the right strand is thinner and hangs farther outward. Both strands curl slightly away from the face near their ends.

On the right side of the top of her head is a white ahoge that curves upward and inward in a C-like shape.

She has light, shiny gold eyes and wears a pair of red under-rimless glasses.

Her race outfit consists of a fitted dark magenta jacket, adorned with gold buttons, and a pink collar and light pink accent. Her long sleeves have a pink triangular spike pattern on the cuffs. Underneath the jacket is a black button-up blouse with a red ribbon. She wears a dark magenta pleated skirt with a spiked pink design at the top. The skirt has waist cutouts on either side, and there's a red ribbon tied around her waist. Paired with thigh-high stockings with the same pink spikes—one leg featuring a cut-out circle at the top. She wears cool gray heeled loafers with buckles at the top.

## Trivia

* Her initial character profile was uploaded to the official [https://x.com/uma_musu/status/867934971117264896 Twitter] account on May 25, 2017.
** This tweet was a character name prediction quiz that encouraged fans to try and guess which horse she's named after. The hint was "She is a cool, intelligent, and talented Umamusume. When someone points out that she has a big head, she snaps, 'It's just because of my hair color!'"
* Race uniform design notes from Umamusume Pretty Derby Artworks Vol.02:
** Biwa Hayahide's glasses are a reference to times where the horse would wear a red [https://www.jra.go.jp/kouza/yougo/w397.html menko] (hood/blinkers).
** Black and pink sawtooth patterns on the stockings and skirt are based on Biwa's racing silks.
** Her red belt references the red band on IRL Biwa's saddle, as seen in the [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LfERKn8XppM 1994 Takarazuka Kinen].
* The real Biwa Hayahide had two socks, one on his right foreleg and one on his left hind leg. These were not adapted into the design of his Umamusume counterpart. The real Hayahide was a gray horse, so his coat gradually turned white over time; the decision may simply be based on his appearance later in life, at which point his socks were no longer visible.
* She loves bananas, as shown in the 10th episode of Season 3, where she competes against the other members of BNW in a banana-eating contest. She also appears eating bananas in the cafeteria in other episodes of the season.
** The real horse also loved bananas, [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_QQL2JwXUMo to the point of dropping carrots out of his mouth to eat them.]
* One of Biwa Hayahide's most notable personality traits is her self-consciousness about the size of her head, which she insists is not big.
** In fact, she is so insecure about it that whenever “big” and/or “head” (or synonyms of both words) were put together in a sentence, she will respond with denial even when it wasn't referring to her or her head. She can even sense when someone is talking about her from afar or in a loud crowded area like a racetrack and just so happens to use the word "head" when speaking about her, and insists that someone was talking about it, even when no one else finds it believable, like in the 12th episode of Season 2.
** IRL Hayahide was teased for having a noticeably bigger head than most horses; his white face made it look even larger. Former Kansai TV race announcer Kiyoshi Sugimoto even joked with his friends that his big head would give him an advantage in a photo finish.
* Hayahide is also self-conscious about her voluminous hair. She compares it to a "raging dragon", impossible to tame; she rants about how much trouble it creates for her and laments the fact that she doesn't have smooth and silky hair like Brian.
** She reacts negatively when Ticket says her hair looks like Santa's beard.
*** In her Christmas costume's unique skill animation and in the event Hungry for a Miracle, a pair of prop glasses placed behind her makes her hair look like Santa Claus's face when viewed from behind.
** IRL Hayahide grew a fluffy and unruly winter coat every year.
* Hayahide was injured as a child while running with Brian, leaving her unable to run for a while. This may reference the fact that IRL Hayahide suffered a wound to his leg as a yearling after running into a fence. It may also foreshadow later events in which he developed tendonitis after the 1994 Tenno Sho (Autumn) and was forced to retire, preventing him from racing against Brian in the 1994 Arima Kinen.
