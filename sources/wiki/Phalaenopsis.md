She is prepared to become the flower of the Twinkle Series’ battleground so that she can repay her gratitude to the training club that turned her from an Umamusume with a weak constitution to a strong racer. She is the cousin and childhood friend of Biwa Hayahide and Narita Brian.

## Appearance

Phalaenopsis has long, curly, dark purple hair that fades into a paler heliotrope color at the ends, and is tied into a side ponytail on top of her head. Her eyes are a vivid gradient that transitions from lavender, hot pink, and into a brilliant golden hue, complemented by red eyelashes. She wears matching white ear covers with a pink checkerboard pattern, while her left ear is adorned with a pair of gold rings and three phalaenopsis flowers, fastened together by a ribbon with the same pattern as her ear covers.

## Relationships

* Biwa Hayahide - Childhood friend and cousin. During Phalaenopsis' classic year, Biwa Hayahide acts as her mentor, specifically during the Japanese Oaks as to prepare her stamina. They both train together, and Hayahide looks out for Phalaeno as her family.
* Narita Brian - Childhood friend and cousin. Phalaeno looks up to Narita Brian. Though Brian acts cold towards Phalaeno, she cares about her, and watches her race. Brian also likes the ohagi Phalaeno makes.
** IRL, Phalaenopsis' dam, Catequil, was the half-sibling of the dam of Narita Brian and Biwa Hayahide, Pacificus.
** Additionally, Phalaenopsis and Narita Brian were both sired by Brian's Time.
* Special Week - Classmate, friend and rival. Phalaeno helps Special Week with classic literature classes and tea ceremonies. Phalaeno also looks up to Special Week as a strong runner in their generation, and wants to surpass her. 
** IRL, the two had a foal together named Ranunculus.
* El Condor Pasa - Classmate, friend and rival. They watch lucha libre matches together in their free time. Phalaeno also knows about El Condor Pasa's pet bird Mambo.
* Grass Wonder - Classmate, friend and rival. Phalaeno and Grass Wonder spar, and Grass gets along with her well because Phalaeno is one of the few friends she can discuss martial arts with.
* Seiun Sky - Classmate, friend and rival. They play shogi together.
* King Halo - Classmate, friend and rival. They write poetry together in class.
* Tsurumaru Tsuyoshi - Classmate, friend and rival. They both make health drinks together, and Tsurumaru Tsuyoshi relates to Phalaeno as someone who also has health issues.  
** IRL, Phalaenopsis, Special Week, Grass Wonder, El Condor Pasa, King Halo, Seiun Sky, and Tsurumaru Tsuyoshi were members of the Golden Generation, a group of horses that began racing in 1997 who dominated the majority of the Classics and their four year old races.
* Samson Big - Friend and roommate. They both get along, and they became roommates because they both admire Brian in some way.
* No Reason - Friend.

## Trivia

* She was announced during the 5.5th anniversary PakaLive TV stream on August 22nd 2026.
*Phalaenopsis has the 4th shortest time gap from introduction to being playable trainee, with 20 days between when she was first announced and when she was added as a trainee, behind Transcend at 19 days, Gran Alegria at 17, and Dream Journey's immediate banner.
* Phalaenopsis is the first Umamusume introduced to be the cousin of another Umamusume, being the cousin of both Narita Brian and Biwa Hayahide.
* Phalaenopsis is the first, and currently only, Umamusume from the Golden Generation to be based on a mare in real life.
* The training club that Phalaenopsis belongs to represents North Hills (Maeko Farm at the time), a racing group for whom the IRL horse earned their first G1 win.
** Several Umamusume from later IRL time periods also belonged to North Hills or members of the family who owned it, including Believe (Koji Maeda), No Reason (Shinji Maeda), Still in Love (North Hills Management), and Transcend (Koji Maeda).
*** All of these Umamusume appear in Phalaenopsis' good ending, and many of them appear elsewhere in her career as well.
* Phalaenopsis' weak constitution and her reverse temperature condition is a reference to how in real life, Phalaenopsis had a weak constitution and body as a foal, though she healed from it.
* Her casual outfit is based on the gyaru subculture. This is because she admires the gyaru subculture, and it reminds her of basara.

## Real-life page (excerpt)

Source: https://umamusu.wiki/IRL:Phalaenopsis

' was a Japanese racehorse that was active from 1997 to 2000. She is best known for winning both the Oka Sho and the Shuka Sho in 1998, and for winning the Queen Elizabeth II Cup in 2000.

### Name Origin

Her name comes from the flower moth orchids, scientifically known as Phalaenopsis.

### Background

Phalaenopsis was born on April 4th, 1995, on Maeko Farm. Her sire, Brian's Time, was an American racehorse who sired many successful horses in Japan. Her dam, Catequil, was a Canadian racehorse that was brought by North Hills to commence breeding duties in Japan. Catequil's nephews were Biwa Hayahide and Narita Brian, which made them Phalaenopsis' cousins. Fifteen years later, Catequil also had a son named Kizuna, who ended up winning the Japanese Derby in 2013.

Because of her pedigree, there were high expectations for Phalaenopsis. However, as a foal, she had a weak constitution, and she struggled with training. It was to the point where it was assumed that she could not become a racehorse. However, as a two-year-old, her condition began to improve, and she began training under Mitsumasa Hamada at Ritto Training Center.

### Racing career

=== Two-year-old season (1997) ===
Phalaenopsis made her debut on November 30th, 1997, in a maiden race at Hanshin Racecourse, where she was ridden by Shigeru Ishiyama, a young jockey who frequently rode horses from Mitsumasa Hamada's stable. She was entered in a race on Hanshin's dirt course to reduce the strain on her legs yet scored a decisive victory, finishing nine lengths ahead of runner-up Judi Urban. A month later, she won the Pre-Open-class Sazanka Sho, her first turf race, by two and a half lengths.

=== Three-year-old season (1998) ===
After scoring her third consecutive victory in her first race of the year, the Open-class Elfin Stakes, Phalaenopsis became regarded as the top contender for the fillies' Classics set to take place in the spring. She made her graded stakes debut at the G3 Tulip Sho in March, and was the favourite to win ahead of Ein Bride, the winner of the G1 Hanshin Sansai Himba Stakes, and Dantsu Sirius, who finished ahead of several colts to win the G3 Shinzan Kinen. Despite this, her weight had decreased by 8kg, and after a slow start, Phalaenopsis was surrounded by the rest of the pack from the middle of the race and through the home stretch, finishing the race in 4th, over a length behind winner Dantsu Sirius. In addition to her poor condition, it was determined that Shigeru Ishiyama had made a jockey error during the race, and was relieved of his duties as Phalaenopsis's jockey. 

Ahead of her first classic race, the G1 Oka Sho, Yutaka Take was brought in as Phalaenopsis's new jockey. Despite her condition improving after she re-gained the weight she lost before the Tulip Sho, she was the third favourite entering the race. During the Oka Sho, she ran at the front of the midpack and overtook London Bridge, who had been holding onto the lead for much of the race, in the home stretch to claim her first G1 victory, and the first G1 victory for North Hills since their farm was founded in 1984. 

Phalaenopsis was then entered into the G1 Yushun Himba (Japanese Oaks), with her team hoping to win the Triple Tiara. Leading up to the Oaks, there were concerns over whether she would be able to handle the 2,400-meter distance.
