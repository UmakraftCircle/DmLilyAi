## Appearance

Tap Dance City is tall and has shoulder-length brown hair with a red and blue dyed braid. On her right ear is a golden shachihoko decoration. She has light purple eyes with blue highlights to them. 

Her racing outfit includes a red top with white stripes, a navy top section, and white belts accented on the sleeves and around the neck to attach to her blue corset. She wears a sweeping white skirt over a dark purple or deep navy bottom, that slowly fades into a bright red when it reaches the far end. The white skirt has gold-yellow designs and patterns, resembling stars and decorative flourishes, visible particularly around the bottom edge and center. Her legs are covered in black tights, and she is wearing white and light blue tap dancing shoes with a golden cleat attached. She also shows off bright red gloves that fade into a darker maroon to match her sleeves that have the same gradient.

## Relationships

* Daitaku Helios - Roommate. With their shared love for partying and having a good time (rather, what some may consider causing a ruckus), they get along like two peas in a pod. Even if it's a great deal of trouble for the dorm head...
** In real life, they were both horses with a big breakaway running style. This is likely the basis for them being roommates. 
** Interestingly, Helios's best friend, Mejiro Palmer, is roommates with Tap's idol, Katsuragi Ace. The four of them share the same running style.
* Katsuragi Ace - Role model. Ace winning Japan Cup against all odds is the reason Tap decided to come to Japan, so it's natural that she idolizes her greatly. She seeks to emulate her coolness, down to her flashy running style.
** Tap Dance City's main jockey Tetsuzo Sato was inspired to become a jockey after watching Katsuragi Ace win 1984's Japan Cup as a child. 19 years later, he would achieve that dream with Tap Dance City, with the same breakaway style. Tap's admiration for Ace is likely based on this.
* Air Shakur - Friend and classmate. Tap sees Shakur as a valuable equal and potential part of her crew, taking a special interest in her. She finds every opportunity to appeal to Shakur and attempt to curry the favor, with no regard to how disruptive she may be. It's unknown if it's actually working, but their relationship is still ostensibly positive. Shakur is able to "decode" Tap's erratic behavior to the trainer in order to improve their compatibility, showing a strong degree of familiarity and understanding.
** They debuted in the same 2000 generation and raced each other once, in 2002's Arima Kinen.
* Symboli Kris S - Friend and rival. Tap feels a strong sense of competitiveness towards her, watching her every move carefully. Given she sees herself as a completely self made Umamusume, she feels diametrically opposed to Kris S, thinking she has it easy because of her relationship with the Symbolis. However this misunderstanding is cleared up and Tap realizes the two of them are not so different after all, breathing new life into their rivalry. As two American Umamusume, they can lower their guard in their downtime and indulge in shared the familiarity of their homeland.
** They were contemporaries in racing who raced each other many times and were seen as heated rivals. In particular, Tap Dance City foiled Symboli Kris S's Autumn Triple Crown bid by winning 2003's Japan Cup. They were also both American bred horses born in Kentucky.
* Agnes Digital - Friend. Tap considers Digital a part of her crew, and wants her to live in her castle in the future, along with Air Shakur.
** They debuted in the same 2000 generation and raced each other several times.
*Hishi Miracle - Friend. While not entirely understanding her, Hishi Miracle feels encouraged by Tap's bold nature.
**In real life, they often competed with each other.

## Trivia

* She was announced during the 2nd Anniversary PakaLive TV stream on February 22, 2023.
* She has a strong background in traditional dance as her mother is a dancer. It's likely the basis for this is Tap Dance City's IRL dam, All Dance.
* Before she came to Japan, she was originally from Kentucky. She even confirms that she has a southern accent when one of her crewmates asks her to teach her English.
** IRL, Tap Dance City was bred and born in Kentucky.
* She and her roommate Daitaku Helios were in possession of a secret and treasured disco ball in their room at some point, until it was found out and confiscated.
* Character Design notes from Umamusume: Pretty Derby Artworks volume 4:
** From the motif horse's name and the character's personality, the design of the racing outfit is very flamboyant and loud. The skirt is cut back to show off the long slender legs, and you can get a peek at her tummy when she moves around. 
** With the Kinko Sho Threepeat and the three white stripes on the horse's jockey silk, the number "3" is sprinkled in as historical reference. The character likes gold so it is used abundantly, and there is a golden shachihoko (Kinko) around her ear.
** She had a dyed braid at the same spot in her hair as the person who she looks up to, Katsuragi Ace.
*The white belts in her outfit may also refer to the IRL horse having white reigns.
*In her career mode's normal ending, there's a scene where she nearly misses the Prix de l'Arc de Triomphe because her flight to France got cancelled at the last minute.
**This is a reference to the real life Tap Dance City's expedition to France in 2004, where his flight was delayed due to a malfunction with the plane he was supposed to board. 
*She is an only child.
*Back in America, she used to slide down her local pond with the kids when it froze over in winter.
*Her tendency to tap dance before races is a reference to the real horse, as he would get agitated in which it would look like he was "tap dancing".

## Real-life page (excerpt)

Source: https://umamusu.wiki/IRL:Tap_Dance_City

' is an American-bred, Japanese-trained racehorse that was active from 2000 to 2005. He is known for a bold frontrunning strategy and holds the record tie in distance to 2nd place in a JRA G1 race.

### Name Origin

"Tap" and "Dance" come from his sire and dam, Pleasant Tap and All Dance, respectively, and "City" is the crown name of owner Yushun Horse Club Co. Ltd.

### Racing Career

=== Three year old to Five year old season (2000 - summer 2002) ===

==== Debut, 10 jockey rotations ====
Tap Dance City debuted on March 4th, 2000 in a 2,000-meter turf race on Hanshin Racecourse. He won his first race on March 19th. In his first graded race attempt, the Kyoto Shinbun Hai (G2) in May, he took home third with about a three length distance to winner Agnes Flight. With 4 defeats in conditional races, his second win came from challenging a higher class (below 9 million yen class) in the Tenryugawa Tokubetsu. In his four year old season, he lost all 6 races he ran and floated around the Open, Pre-open, and below 10 million class. After turning 5, he took 3rd in the Nikkei Shinshun Hai (G2) by 1.5 lengths in another graded challenge, and was finally promoted from open by winning in the Kasuga Tokubetsu and the Midosuji Stakes.  

The first race after his promotion, he lost by a head in the Nikkei Sho, and another three more races. With a record of 22 races and 4 wins, despite showing promise in the Kyoto Shinbun Hai, he was late to be promoted. His trainer, Shozo Sasaki, said that his bad performance is due to his temperament. He was said to be "tap dancing" when he gets agitated, and had to have two people with him in the paddocks. His temperament led to late starts, and he also had to run towards the outer rail, which caused him to lose many races. The fact that he had many jockeys rotate out from his saddle also meant that they have not found the optimal way to ride him, since his father produced runners on all distances in all ages, and they did not have enough time to ride him to find the best strategy.

=== Five year old season (late 2002) ===

==== Tetsuzo Sato, main jockey ====
On the September 7th, he ran in his 6th graded race in the Asahi Challenge Cup (G3). His previous jockeys were not available, and Tetsuzo Sato, who happened to be free, was chosen to ride him. In the race, he settled into 3rd in the field and was pushing in the straight, with the 2nd and 1st favorites Ibuki Government and True Surpass coming from behind. In a three-way battle, Tap Dance City was passed but overtook again and finished first by a head, giving him his first graded victory. His finishing time of 1:58.1 also set a new record by 0.2 seconds. From this point until retirement, Sato remained his jockey. 

His first G1 race came when he was placed 50th in the Arima Kinen fan vote of 2002. He was the 13th favorite in the race. Out of the gate, Fine Motion took first, with Tap Dance City following. They traded places several times in the home straight and first corners, but Sato would up his pace on the back straight, taking a large lead over 2nd going into the 3rd corner.
