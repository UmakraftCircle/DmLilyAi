## Appearance

Verxina has long, dark indigo hair with a white diamond in the middle of her bangs. Her ears are short and round, and on her right ear she has two teal ear piercings. On her left ear she wears a black ear cover, she also sports a dark blue sailor cap that fades into turquoise, decorated with gold trim, as well as her family's crest, a golden anchor. Attached to the hat is a dangling circle earring with a white X-shaped gemstone in the center, and a blue gemstone at the end. Her eyes are dark purple, fading into light blue at the bottom, and she has a mole under her right eye.

Her racing uniform is a stylish outfit with multiple layers. She wears a blue and gold detachable collar(collar-type choker) with golden ornament around her neck. The top of her dress is a rich blue, with a straight across neckline, folded in a "V" shape at the center to reveal a black piece of fabric. Around her waist is a black corset with four gold buttons in the center. Verxina has asymmetrical sleeves, her right arm being bare with a dark blue cuff around her wrist, and her left arm covered with a white half-jacket, and full dark blue sleeve, attached is a turquoise cape that fades into pastel green. She has a layered skirt, the top half being dark blue with golden decor, underneath that is light turquoise translucent fabric that fades into pastel green. Attached to the dark blue section is a blue gradient strap that reads "VERXINA" in white lettering, with a golden "V" emblem at the end. The final layer is a dark blue pleated skirt, each pleat decorated with two light-gray chevrons. She wears plain black tights, and a copper bracelet composed of four-pointed stars around her left ankle. Her court shoes are plain, with golden heels and fan decorations in the center, however the left one is white, whereas the right is blue.

## Trivia

* Her design was shown during the opening of Season 3, but her name was officially announced to the official [https://x.com/uma_musu/status/1726481339560640528 Twitter] account on November 15, 2023.
* In Cheval Grand's career, Cheval mentions that Verxina used to admire an undefeated Triple Crown Umamusume as a kid. This Umamusume is a reference to Deep Impact, the undefeated Triple Crown winner of 2005.
** Deep Impact is also the sire of Verxina and her full sister Vivlos IRL.
*She likes eating oshiruko. She also prefers chunky red bean paste.
*She's talented at trapping insects.
*She likes to do yoga in her free-time. 
* Verxina heavily dislikes the number 2.
** This is a reference to her placing 2nd place in all the Tiara races, losing each one to Gentildonna.
*** Aside from that, Verxina has 2 G1 wins, and is the second foal from her mother, Halwa Sweet.
*She used to take piano lessons when she was younger.
*Despite being the eldest of the three sisters, Verxina is the last one to become a trainable unit and the gap between her and Vivlos's release (489 days) is significantly wider than Cheval Grand and Vivlos' (30 days).
*Her love for baseball is a reference to the real life Verxina, in which her owner, Kazuhiro Sasaki, is a former professional baseball relief pitcher.
