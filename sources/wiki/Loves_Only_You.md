## Appearance

Loves Only You has yellow eyes and red hair, that flows far past her back in a high ponytail style with a long muted pink hair strand at her right side, and a red heart on her left cheek.

Her race outfit consists of a fitted black jacket with green zigzag trim on the sleeves and gold accents on the cuffs. The jacket has a corset-like design with gold lacing at the front, emphasizing her waist. Underneath the jacket, there is a white ruffled blouse with a high collar and frilled cuffs that peek out from the jacket sleeves. She wears a skirt that is multi-layered with white ruffles underneath and a black outer layer that is open in the front, revealing the ruffles and her legs. There are decorative ribbons in green and purple tied around the skirt. She wears thigh-high boots with an asymmetrical design, with one boot is white with gold detailing and a red frill at the top, while the other is black with gold accents and a white frill at the top. She also wears accessories like a small black top hat on her head and white, black gloves and a single black and gold strap with a crimson bow that rests on her left thigh.

## Relationships

* Chrono Genesis - Friend, roommate, and classmate. Loves enjoys Chrono's eccentricity, and enjoys hearing her talk about race history. She also dries Chrono's hair for her when she is engrossed in history. 
* Gran Alegria - Friend and classmate. Loves likes to follow Gran's "MiRules", and likes the salads Gran makes. 
* Curren Bouquetd'or - Friend and classmate.
* Curren Chan - Friend.
* Marche Lorraine - Childhood friend. The two have a very close relationship; according to Loves, Marche is her #1 best friend. Loves is one of the only people Marche is comfortable speaking to without her puppet. Since she was young, Marche was happy to stand behind Loves and support her from the shadows. However, Loves wants Marche to recognize her own strength.
** Both wear matching straps, with Loves on her left thigh and Marche on her right thigh.
** IRL, both horses grew up together at the Northern Farm and were trained by Yoshito Yahagi. They would go on to win their own Breeder's Cup races in 2021, with Loves Only You winning the BC Filly & Mare Turf and Marche Lorraine winning the BC Distaff that year.
* Espoir City - Friend. They play fighting games together from time to time.
* Kiseki - Friend. They both enjoy manga together. Loves also helps Kiseki make a drawing stream.
* Almond Eye - Friend and rival. Almond Eye teaches her how to do yoga, and they attend yoga sessions together. 
* Agnes Digital - Friend. Digital likes to watch Loves' streams, and she uses an anonymous online username to watch them. Loves eventually picks up on who she is, and appreciates her as a fan.

## Trivia

* She was announced during the 4th Anniversary PakaLive TV stream on February 22nd, 2025.
* The mole on her neck is in the same place as a mole on her IRL jockey, Yuga Kawada's, neck.
* While using Love-Linked Aster on Beyond Dreams, the player can start the Career with the 9927 Wishes costume before swapping to the new one before entering Breeders' Cup.
* Victory in the Breeders' Cup during Beyond Dreams unlocks an event with commentary in English even on the Japanese version: "Here comes Loves Only You to win it for Japan!".
** This references the real-life commentary delivered by Larry Collmus for NBC.
* She hates going to hospitals, especially the dentist.
* Her parents give her feedback on her streams.
* There are a total of 26 hearts on her racewear.
* She used to be a tomboy when she was little, and she used to get covered in mud all the time.
* She is very good at watermelon smashing.
* In her solo song, (Oyasumi Haishin) Minna to Love Hojuu Shita~i, Agnes Digital makes a cameo appearance at the 2:51 mark.
* In her senior year, Loves Only You is forced to return to Japan after heading to Dubai, because a pandemic is going around.
** This is a reference to the COVID-19 pandemic that started in 2020. While Loves Only You was initially scheduled to compete in the Dubai Sheema Classic in 2020, it was cancelled days before the race because of growing concerns regarding the pandemic.
*In her classic year, a bacterial infection in her legs puts her plans for the Oka Sho in jeopardy. Because of this, the player can choose to either enter her in the Oka Sho or the Wasurenagusa Sho, a prelude for the Japanese Oaks.
**This happened to Loves Only You in real life, where she contracted a bacterial infection in her hooves. This caused her to miss out on the Oka Sho.
*In the homescreen, she mentions that her older sister likes to hang out with Forever Young. This sister is a reference to the real horse Real Steel, who is Loves Only You's full older brother and the winner of the 2016 Dubai Turf. He is also the sire of Forever Young, hence why they hang out in Umamusume.
* She finds drawing a bit difficult.

## Real-life page (excerpt)

Source: https://umamusu.wiki/IRL:Loves_Only_You

'  is a Japanese racehorse that was active from 2018 to 2021. She is known as an international trailblazing horse, becoming the first Japanese horse since Cesario to win a United States Grade 1 race, and the first Japanese horse to win a Grade 1 in three countries. 

Loves Only You and Marche Lorraine's wins at the Breeders' Cup began an era of Japan's large scale involvement in United States Grade 1 racing, having increased participation of Japanese racehorses in the Breeders' Cup and Kentucky Derby. This eventually led to Forever Young's iconic victory at the 2025 Breeders' Cup Classic.

### Name Origin

Owners DMM Dream Club, officially described her name as: "To show love to everyone. Inspired by the mother's name."

### Background

Loves Only You was born on March 26th, 2016, on Northern Farm. Her sire, Deep Impact, was the 2005 Triple Crown winner, and won seven G1 races in total. Her dam, Loves Only Me, did not run in races, but she was imported from the United States to Japan due to her good pedigree. On July 10th, 2017, Loves Only You was purchased by DMM Dream Club for 1.6 million yen. She was trained under Yoshito Yahagi at Ritto Training Center.

### Racing career

=== Two-year-old season (2018) ===
Loves Only You made her debut on November 3rd in a 1,800-meter turf race on Kyoto Racecourse. While she was originally set to compete earlier in the summer, Yoshito Yahagi decided against this. While she suffered a late start, she gradually began to get a good position, and she eventually won the race by one and one-fourths of a length. 

She was then scheduled to race in the Shiragiku Sho on November 25th, where she won once again. While she initially set to compete in the Hanshin Juvenile Fillies after, due to sudden weight loss, that plan was cancelled quickly. 

=== Three-year-old season (2019) ===

After winning the Wasurenagusa Sho on April 7th, Loves Only You was initially going to prepare for the Oka Sho, the first leg of the Triple Tiara. However, she contracted issues with her hooves, so her plan to compete was quickly cancelled. Instead, Loves Only You was set to compete in the Japanese Oaks on May 19th. 

While Loves Only You was in an unfavorable position during the race, once Curren Bouquetd'or and Chrono Genesis were ahead of her, she quickly chased from behind and overtook them both by a head, achieving her first G1 victory. Her record of 2 minutes and 22.4 seconds also set up a new record for the race, which remained unbeaten. Though scheduled to compete in the Shuka Sho, she dropped out due to inflammation in her right hoof, and she instead competed in the Queen Elizabeth II Cup on November 10th. She was defeated by Lucky Lilac, and placed 3rd, ending off her classic year.

=== Four-year-old season (2020) ===
After receiving an invitation, Loves Only You was set to compete in the Dubai Sheema Classic, a foreign G1. She arrived at Al Maktoum International Airport on March 19th to prepare. While the race was initially going to be a no-spectator race due to COVID-19, it was then announced on the 22nd that the race would be postponed due to safety reasons. Because of this, Loves Only You, alongside other horses, returned to Japan on the 30th.
