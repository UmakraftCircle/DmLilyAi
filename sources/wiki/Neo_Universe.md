## Appearance

Neo Universe has long and wavy blonde hair that almost appears to be floating, with a light blue underdye. Two strands of hair frame her face, the left strand longer than the right. On either side of her head she has two strands of wavy hair, levitated above the rest. She has bangs parted to her left, and a small blue star-shaped blaze, as well as an S-shaped ahoge on the top of her head. She has yellow ears with bright blue insides, and her blonde tail fades into light blue at the end. On either ear she has white, rounded diamond shaped charms with blue gems in the center. On her right ear is a star shaped charm dangling off of her ear. Her blue, half-lidded eyes often seem spaced out.

Neo Universe wears a large white jacket with blue and yellow accents as her race outfit. The jacket has an upturned V-shaped collar with a blue lining. The right side of her jacket is yellow at the upper torso, and has a circular, silver button with her name printed on it as well three long black straps that dangle off her jacket. The jacket has a pocket to the left near the waistline with a shorter black strap hanging off it. Underneath her jacket, she wears a white bodysuit with a triangle-shaped cutout at the neckline and a black tube top. Her shoes are white ankle boots that have fluffy collars connected by black straps in the center. The soles of her boots are deep blue, with a yellow rim, and a yellow strap on the top.

## Relationships

* Still in Love - Roommate and friend. She knows about Still in Love's "alter ego", but doesn't seem to mind it. She is shown to be supportive towards Still in Love, often offering her advice and hearing her out. Sometimes, they stargaze and observe the night sky together.
* Zenno Rob Roy - Close friend. Rob Roy was one of the first people who went out of her way to understand Universe's strange mannerisms, they both share interest in books, and Rob Roy often recommends Uni books about space.
* Admire Groove - Classmate. Neo Universe notices that despite Admire Groove's behavior, she's actually really perceptive of other people, like how she notices Still. She also shows great concern for her, as her universal knowledge of her past as a horse lets her understand what Admire Groove is going through.
** IRL, Neo Universe, Still in Love, Admire Groove, and Zenno Rob Roy are all horses from the 2003 Classics generation.
*** While Neo Universe became a Double Crown champion on that year, Still in Love was that year's Triple Tiara champion.
* Sounds of Earth - Underclassman and friend. Earth is one of the few people who can understand Universe without confusion.
* Victoire Pisa - Friend. They often stargaze together. Pisa can also understand Neo Universe. 
** IRL, Victoire Pisa and Sounds of Earth were both sired by Neo Universe.
* Hishi Miracle - Friend.

## Trivia

* She was announced during the 2nd Anniversary PakaLive TV stream on February 22nd, 2023.
* Her unusual dialogue includes terms in Japanese quotation marks (『』) to emphasize emotional sincerity, English quotation marks ("") for space-related terms, and made-up all-caps words whose meanings are only implied. Her dialogue will sometimes render the words for "sky" and "world" with the kanji for "universe".
** Her inability to communicate normally seems to be inspired by a comment from jockey Mirco Demuro, who said that "the only thing bad about him is that he can't speak human language".
* Character Design Notes from Umamusume Pretty Derby Artworks Vol.04:
** Astronaut-like design from the motif horse's name. The right ear gives a transparent feeling reminiscent of an astronaut helmet, and the texture of the outfit is chosen to represent "near-future".
** From the comment of "the only flaw is not being able to speak human language", the silhouette of the hair and outfit is made to resemble an octopus alien.
** From the same comment, her hair has an antenna shape.
* Neo Universe, alongside Stay Gold, are the only Umamusume who are aware of their in-real-life counterparts and the in-real-life world in general.
* She particularly likes ramen.
* She likes the zoo, because she finds the animals there to be cute.

## Real-life page (excerpt)

Source: https://umamusu.wiki/IRL:Neo_Universe

Neo Universe was a Japanese racehorse that was active from 2002 to 2004. In 2003, he nearly won the Triple Crown but came third to That's the Plenty in the Kikuka Sho.

### Name Origin

Neo Universe is named after the song of the same name by L'arc en Ciel, a Japanese rock band.

### Background

Neo Universe was born on May 21st, 2000, on Shadai Farm. His sire was Sunday Silence, and he came from his 9th crop of foals. His dam, Pointed Path, was sired by the British horse Kris. As a foal, Neo Universe's body was noted to be slender compared to his brothers. At two years old, he was sent to train under Tsutomu Setoguchi at Ritto Training Center.

### Racing career

=== Two year old season (2002) ===
Neo Universe began his racing career on November 9th, 2002 in a short distance debut race at Kyoto Racecourse. He was the favorite to win at 2.5 odds, and lived up to his expectations by winning the race by a length and a half over Mayano Chianti. His second and final start as a two-year-old was the Sports Hochi Hai Chukyo Nisai Stakes, which was a step up in distance at 1800m. Due to the impressive performance of his last race, he was again the favorite but finished third behind Hoshi Commander and Makoto Emperor.

=== Three year old season (2003) ===
Beginning his three-year-old season, Neo Universe first challenged the Shiraume Sho in January, an allowance-class race over 1600 meters of turf. He won this race by a neck, and this begun his winning streak. His next race was his first foray into a graded stakes race in the G3 Kisaragi Sho. Sent off as the third favorite, he won comfortably by half a length over Silent Deal.

Being pointed towards the Classics, he then was raced in the Spring Stakes, a trial for the first leg of the Triple Crown: the Satsuki Sho. In this race, he also had a change of jockey. Mirco Demuro, a foreign jockey, took the reigns and later became Neo Universe's regular jockey. Demuro won the Spring Stakes on Neo Universe, and continued into the Satsuki Sho with him. During the Satsuki Sho, Neo Universe stayed in the middle of the pack almost the entire race, further back than most of his other starts. He broke through the pack in the stretch, and just barely beat out Sakura President by a head.

Following the path of the Triple Crown, Neo Universe made his next start just a month and a half later in the Japan Derby. On the day of the Derby, the track was incredibly heavy and wet due to rainfall from the remnants of a typhoon the previous day. Many other horses erred towards the outside in hopes for better going on the track, but Neo Universe and Mirco Demuro stayed near the middle of the track for the entire race. This proved to be an effective strategy, as the duo prevailed easily by half a length over Zenno Rob Roy. In an interview, Demuro said that, "He's unbelievable. If he did any more he'd be flying." Neo Universe was entered in the Takarazuka Kinen and Kobe Shimbun Hai as preparation for the Kikka Sho, but neither of those starts resulted in a victory, being fourth place and third place respectively. In the Kikka Sho, he stayed near the back of the pack and challenged That's the Plenty in the stretch, but failed to catch up to him and was overtaken in second place meters before the goal by Lincoln.
