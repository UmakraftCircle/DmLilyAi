## Appearance

As described, Fenomeno carries intense and sharp eyes, deep violet and slanted with purpose. Her hair is raven black with strong purple undertones, spiky and wild yet somewhat controlled, descending just to her back. Along the sides of her face, longer strands flow slightly below shoulder length and curve inward, a bit smoother than the rest of her hair and slightly softening her face. She can be seen adjusting her large navy-blue officer's cap, its dark visor accented with yellow stripes. The cap's black band features purple chevron patterns and displays an emblem of her position. And also, relative to other umamusume, her tail is incredibly thick. 

Her uniform is akin to that of a traffic cop combined with a warden, Navy black in color and fitted with utility pockets, thick buttons, heavy reinforcements and red harness straps. These straps secure a semi-reinforced tactical coat to the right side of her outfit. Each sleeve features a yellow stripe running design along the sides, and, near her left shoulder hangs a yellow shoulder cord. A thick, heavy-duty skirt attaches to her left hip. Her straight-cut pants features more yellow stripes running along the sides, with additional pockets and straps hanging from the right side. Her long black boots are zipped up and have noticeable straps wrapping near the ankle.

## Trivia

* She was announced during the 4th Anniversary PakaLive TV stream on February 22, 2025.
* Her dad is a police officer. She would always play as a cop with him and her mother.
** In chapter 3 of the Golden Christmas Eve event story, it was revealed that Fenomeno only wanted a police notebook for Christmas, but she was gifted a whole police set instead, with a uniform, cap, flag and notebook included.
* She is one of the few Umamusume to have different colored ears. Other Umamusume that have different colored ears include Loves Only You and Casino Drive.
* Her family's preference for red bean paste is a nod to her nickname, "Mamechin", which is referring to kuromame (sweetened black soybeans).
** This nickname originates from real life, as his trainer, Hirofumi Toda, nicknamed him this because his coat resembled kuromame.
* Fenomeno's love and care for children is a reference to how in real life, Fenomeno became a lead horse for foals at Oiwake Farm after retirement.
* She likes to watch detective television shows at night.
* She has a strong sense of smell, and can easily detect ingredients from a single sniff.
* She cannot handle spicy food.

### Name Origin

His name, Fenomeno, has several meanings in Portuguese, as the word fenomeno could mean "phenomenon", "monster". His official name meaning is "Paranormal, Monster (Portuguese)". His trainer Hirofumi Toda nicknamed him "Mamechin" due to the fact that his coat colour resembles a type of sweet black soybean served during New Year’s, known as Kuromame.

### Retirement & Breeding Career

After developing tendonitis, Fenomeno was retired from racing to become a breeding stallion at the Shadai Stallion Station. His first foals were born in 2017. In December 2018, Fenomeno was moved from the Shadai Stallion Station to the Rex Stud to continue his stud duties. However, none of his children achieved notable results, and none of them have won a race in JRA.

Fenomeno retired from stud in 2021, and returned back to Oiwake Farm, his birthplace. In August 2022, he was subsequently gelded and became a lead horse for foals that have just weaned from their mother and one-year-olds.
