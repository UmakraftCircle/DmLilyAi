## Biography

<blockquote style="font-style: italic; border-left: 3px solid #EE5340; padding-left: 1em; color: #20A95C;>"Whether about racing or the culture itself, I must learn more about this lovely place! ♪"</blockquote>

A sheltered lady excited to make memories that will last a lifetime.

A lady of a distinguished family that is rumored to be royalty, she has come to Tracen as an exchange student from Ireland. Perhaps due to a sheltered childhood, she's very curious about everything, which can lead to some sticky situations. She hopes to make a lot of memories while at the academy.

## Appearance

Fine Motion's soft, brown hair frames her face with two delicate locks on each side, while a soft white streak runs through the center. The hair is tied at the back with red and white ribbons. On each side, near her ears, she wears two hairpins shaped like shamrocks, their three-leafed clovers symbolizing her Irish roots. Notably, she is one of the few Umamusume who does not sport an ear accessory.  

Fine Motion dons a finely tailored, unbuttoned white jacket. The jacket features a crest near the chest, pockets at the waist, and an accessory belt at the back. Her sleeves are elegantly lined with red and green at the wrists, and she wears white gloves adorned with shamrock designs, another nod to her Irish heritage. Beneath her neck, a neat and unique bow completes the sophisticated look. Under the jacket, she wears a green vest, followed by a sleek black dress that flows down into a graceful skirt. Her pristine white boots are decorated with a string of hearts, and her white socks mirror the red and green lining seen in her sleeves.

## Relationships

* Air Shakur - Friend. Fine is unbothered by Shakur's unique, more harsh way of interacting with others, stating that while she's different from everyone else in Tracen, she loves that part of Shakur too. Fine has a lot of respect for Shakur. Shakur may act as if she is not fond of Fine, but really, Fine does not have much of a hard time swaying her into doing things, and acts quite playful around Shakur at times. The two have a complicated and intertwined relationship with the concepts of destiny and fate, and face it in different ways; Fine's method of facing the future becomes a painfully bright guiding light for Shakur, and when it is revealed that Fine has returned to Japan and is not going to stop racing, Shakur refuses to retire as well.
* Air Groove - Friend and roommate. Air Groove is often worried on Fine Motion's obsession with ramen and her behavior due to Fine Motion's upbringing.
* Durandal - Friend, classmate, and rival. Fine's noble knight, Durandal holds Fine to high regard and addresses her as Her Highness (殿下 Denka).  Fine respects Durandal, and plays into her "Knight" belief. Fine also gives Durandal advice on how to be more knightly, as Durandal struggles with being proper.
** In real life, Durandal and Fine Motion competed with each other numerous times in mile races.
*** In knightly tales, Durendal was a legendary blade wielded by the paladin Roland to protect his King and Kingdom. Also France and Ireland have had a historically friendly [https://www.ireland.ie/en/france/irelands-relationship-with-france/ relationship] since the 17th century.
* Tanino Gimlet - Friend.
* Symboli Kris S - Friend.
* Kawakami Princess - Rival. In the Queen Elizabeth II Cup, it is Kawakami Princess' challenge that causes her to become both even more passionate in racing and more uneasy than before.

## Trivia

* Her initial character profile was uploaded to the official [https://x.com/uma_musu/status/855249418869645312 Twitter] account on April 20, 2017.
** This tweet was a character name prediction quiz, that encouraged fans to try and guess which horse she's named after. The hint was "She is a foreign exchange student from Ireland, and is considered the "Top Lady" of a noble family. According to the school officials, she is a "a real young lady"."
* On October 10, 2025, a significant amount of dialogue involving Fine was rewritten to clarify that she and her family are not actual Irish royalty. 
** This is likely because Ireland is a republic in real life following a revolution that resulted in it leaving the British Empire (excluding Northern Ireland, which remains a part of the United Kingdom). Due to this and the game's growing global popularity, this change was likely made to avoid potentially offending any Irish players. Currently, she is the only Umamusume whose backstory was actively changed after the game's launch.
* Instead of having one hair accessory near her left or right ear, she instead has two on each of her ears. This is a reference to how the real Fine Motion has atypical chromosomes, with the possibility that she is intersex.
** Fine Motion is a mare, appears externally female, and does not have any external male reproductive organs. The condition does make her infertile, however, and it was not discovered until she was sent to serve as a broodmare. 
* Fine Motion's sister is obsessed with Air Groove, and even freaked out when Fine told her that Air Groove also has wedding-themed racewear. 
** This is a reference to Pilsudski, her real life older half-brother. In the 1997 Japan Cup, he noticeably got excited around Air Groove while in the paddock, hence why his Umamusume counterpart is obsessed with Air Groove.
* The Security Captain of Fine's detail goes unnamed, but her prevalence throughout Fine's story chapters and career, as well as having been around since Fine was young, have led to her being suspected as a reference to [https://www.keibalab.jp/db/breed/12249321/ Piccolo Player], Fine's real-life attendant horse for 16 years.
* Her Ramen Scenario racewear’s unique skill animation has ten different variants depending on what ramen is shown in it.
