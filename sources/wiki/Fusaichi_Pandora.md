## Appearance

Fusaichi Pandora has light blonde hair, with fluffy bangs parted to the left. In the center of her bangs is a prominent white streak of hair. She wears her hair into two curly ponytails that reach her shoulder, tied by two white fluffy scrunchie. Both of her ears have ear covers, with the right side being red, and the left side being yellow with two white beads at the top. She has bright red irises, each with dark red pupils, with a white highlight in the center.

Her race outfit consists of two main components, a cropped frilled top, and a pleated skirt. Around her neck, she wears a pink shirt collar with white frills at the base, in the center is a large red ribbon with a heart gem in the center, and a pink pleated ascot, adorned with yellow trimming and white frills, attached to the base. Her sleeveless top is white with a pink trim, but is decorated with pastel yellow laced off-shoulder sleeves, decorated around the perimeter with white trim and red pleated frills. Her top also has white translucent sleeves, tied at the cuffs with a red band and a yellow stripe around the center. Pandora's red and light red striped skirt is decorated with translucent scalloped trim. The skirt is decorated with a yellow scalloped decoration around the top half, adorned with white trim. She wears a bright pink belt atop her skirt, a large ribbon split into two sections (one being hot pink, the other being yellow and red stripes) adorns her right hip. Attached to this ribbon is a pearly white charm that says "FP", and many other pearly white chains and accessories adorn the perimeter of the belt including a white key, and a pink star. She wears a hot pink thigh belt around her left leg, attached to it are two white chains. Her shoes are simple heels decorated with ribbons and square emblems with the letter "F" in the center. Her left heel is hot pink with pink ribbons, and her right heel is white with light blue ribbons. She wears a translucent white sock on her right foot, and a yellow sock on her left foot, both frilled at the top.

## Relationships

*Daring Heart - Friend and roommate. Heart tends to spoil her sometimes, and the two of them hang out together. However, Heart makes note of Pandora's intellect and strength, and looks forward to seeing her grow. They also go mountaineering together from time to time.
*Kawakami Princess - Friend, rival, and upperclassman. Fusaichi Pandora views Kawakami Princess as a rival she must surpass, as they are both rivals in the Triple Tiara route. Though she loses to her multiple times, she eventually finds the strength to beat her. Outside of racing, they are good friends. They both enjoy watching Princess Fighter, and have nostalgic memories of watching the show.
*Almond Eye - Close friend. Fusaichi Pandora is often looking out for Almond Eye, and vice versa. Pandora also tends to pamper her for time to time, while Almond Eye makes bento lunches for her.
**IRL, Almond Eye was foaled by Fusaichi Pandora.
*King Halo, Rhein Kraft, Cesario - Friends. Teammates on the game-only team, Team Ascella.
**Fusaichi Pandora deeply admires Rhein Kraft as a racer and friend, to the point where she calls her "Kuu-chan-senpai" out of respect. As Kraft brought her up when she was down and always looked out for her, Pandora works her hardest to win for her sake, and trains seriously because of her mentorship.
**King Halo acts as the mentor for Team Ascella, which includes Pandora. She makes note of Pandora's carefree yet her talented strength, and looks out for her.
**Cesario helps Pandora with her training when Kraft isn't there to mentor her. Cesario also teaches Pandora how to be a mentor to other racers as well, such as Almond Eye for example.
*Hishi Akebono, Hishi Miracle, North Flight and Fuji Kiseki - Friends and her former teammates.
**Akebono and Hishi Miracle worry about Pandora due to her carefree nature, but they enjoy hanging out with her. Fuji finds her attitude intriguing, and notices her talent. North Flight also notices her strength, and likes to hang out with her on occasion.
***IRL, all of them have been jockeyed by Koichi Tsunada. However, Pandora switched jockeys after the 2006 Oka Sho, hence why they are her former teammates.
*Jungle Pocket - Friend, upperclassman, and her former teammate. Jungle Pocket is very strict with her, and dislikes Pandora's carefree attitude when it comes to racing. She wants her to take it seriously instead of like a joke. However, Pocket does like it when Pandora is genuinely determined about something, like when she continues to pester her into going to the convenience store with her. Pocket also lets Pandora borrow delinquent manga, which Pandora has grown to like.
*Daitaku Helios - Friend and upperclassman. They sing karaoke together, and Fusaichi Pandora admires her as a fellow gyaru.
*Sweep Tosho - Friend and rival. While Sweep gets annoyed with Pandora sometimes, they can get along from time to time. At times, Sweep teaches her how to be a proper witch, in which Pandora affectionately nicknames her "grandma" because Sweep has also mentored Rhein Kraft before. During summer camp, Sweep teaches her a "magic spell" so that Pandora could cool off, which in reality was just her spraying her with water.
*Buena Vista - Friend and classmate. They hang out together on occasion.
*Rulership - Friend. They sometimes play board games together. When Pandora wins a match, Rulership immediately sulks in her room.
**IRL, Fusaichi Pandora and Rulership had a foal named Satono Esperanza together in 2017.

## Trivia

* She was first revealed in Act 2 of the game's Main Story, and later got officially revealed during the PakaLive TV Vol.45 stream on September 27th, 2024.
* Before her release, she was alluded to as "Chestnut-haired Umamusume" in Kawakami Princess' career mode.
* She has been scouted for modelling before.
* Her design is based off hime-gyaru ("princess gyaru"), a sub-style of gyaru fashion that focuses on a princess-look. This can be especially seen in her casual outfit. This style was chosen for her character because the real life counterpart raced during the 2000s, and hime-gyaru was a popular trend at the time.
* In her career story, Rhein Kraft is hospitalized during Pandora's classic year. This is a reference to real life events, as the real Rhein Kraft passed away in 2006, which was also Fusaichi Pandora's classic year.
* She dislikes vegetables. 
* Pink is her favorite color.
* She enjoys hiking. 
* She loves to eat sweets, much to her detriment. 
* She enjoys renting out movies, since her grandmother used to be a film actress. 
* She likes to do dances on Umatok. 
* Fusaichi Pandora's "marshmallow temperament" in contrast with her talent is a reference to the real life Fusaichi Pandora, in which despite her strength in races, she was also quite moody. 
* Fusaichi Pandora's family spoiling her and being very wealthy is a reference to her owner, Fusao Sekiguchi, who was known to invest large sums of money into horses and horse racing. 
** Her secret of purchasing every pillow that seems right to her is a reference to Fusao Sekiguchi's spending habits.
*She likes to watch television dramas with others. 
*She also likes to watch a show called "Totally Agents!" with Daring Heart.
**This show is a reference to the French-animated cartoon "Totally Spies!", which ran from 2001-2013. Fusaichi Pandora ran from 2005-2007, which is coincidentally when the show was in its popularity. 
*Fusaichi Pandora's confidence in that she is a genius is a reference to Yoichi Fukunaga, who is the father of Yuichi Fukunaga and was believed to be a genius jockey before his injury.
**In her story, her sudden claim that she wasn't as much as a genius as she thought is a reference to Yuichi Fukunaga, who acknowledged that he wasn't a genius like his father. 
*Her swimsuit racewear's base golden skills mirrors those given by Almond Eye's SSR Speed card {{Game Support|30242}}, referencing their real life mother and daughter relationship. 
*Her family owns a private beach. 
*On occasion, she does yoga in the mornings as part of her morning routine.

## Real-life page (excerpt)

Source: https://umamusu.wiki/IRL:Fusaichi_Pandora

'  was a Japanese racehorse that was active from 2005 to 2007. She was part of Sunday Silence's last crop in 2005, and is known for being the mother of JRA Hall of Fame inductee Almond Eye, a nine G1 winning Japanese racehorse.

### Name Origin

Her crown name, Fusaichi, is based on owner Fusao Sekiguchi, a controversial figure in Japanese racing during the 2000s. The crown name is a combination of "Fusao" and "ichiban", meaning "Fusao is number one". "Pandora" comes from Greek mythology, who was the first human woman to be created.

### Background

Fusaichi Pandora was born on February 27th, 2003, on Northern Farm. She was sired by Sunday Silence, and came from his last crop of foals after he died in 2002. Her dam, Lotta Lace, was sired by Nureyev, an American-bred French-trained racehorse. She was purchased for 91.35 million yen by Fusao Sekiguchi at a select sale, and was sent to train under Toshiaki Shirai at Ritto Training Center.

### Racing career

=== Two-year-old season (2005) ===
Fusaichi made her debut on November 12th, in a 1,800-meter turf race at Kyoto Racecourse. She won her debut by 6 lengths, making her a contender for her next race in the Hanshin Juvenile Fillies.

On December 4th in the Hanshin Juvenile Fillies, she was rated as the 2nd favorite in the race. Fusaichi Pandora, placed in the outer barrier, ran near the back of the pack for most of the race before making a bid for the lead on the final corner. She had too much ground to make up and ended up finishing 3rd behind the winner T.M. Precure. Fusaichi Pandora ran one more race in an allowance race on December 25th, but finished 3rd.

=== Three-year-old season (2006) ===
To begin the year, Fusaichi Pandora participated in the open-class Elfin Stakes on February 4th. Thought rated as a 1.7 favorite in the race, she had a troubled run and finished 6th. She then rebounded her form by winning the open-class Kinsenka Sho on February 25th. A month later, on March 18th, she ran in the  Flower Cup (G3) and finished 2nd, which earned her a spot in the Oka Sho.

On April 9th, Fusaichi Pandora participated in the Oka Sho, the first crown of the Fillies Classic series. Despite her drop in form, she was rated as the 2nd favorite in the race. Starting from the outer gate, Fusaichi Pandora and her jockey Koichi Tsunoda rushed to the front of the pack on a wet track but she quickly lost ground on the final stretch and faded to finish a disastrous 14th. After this race, Tsunoda would be replaced as Fusaichi Pandora's jockey by Yuichi Fukunaga. Her next race was scheduled to be the Japanese Oaks.

In the Japanese Oaks on May 21st, Fusaichi Pandora was the fifth favorite after her defeat at the Oka Sho. During the race, Pandora sat mid-pack before making a run on the final straight. Though she would make up ground, she was unable to pass Kawakami Princess, and finished 2nd. After the summer break, Fusaichi Pandora continued to place in races with Fukunaga as her jockey, finishing 3rd in both the Rose Stakes (G2) and the Shuka Sho, which she lost once again to Kawakami Princess.

On November 12th, Fusaichi Pandora ran in the G1 Queen Elizabeth II Cup. She was the 7th favorite in the race, behind the favorites Sweep Tosho and her rival Kawakami Princess. During the race, Fusaichi Pandora, once again made a good run in the final stretch, but a late surging Kawakami Princess denied her victory, and she placed 2nd.
