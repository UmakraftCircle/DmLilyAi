## Biography

<blockquote style="font-style: italic; border-left: 3px solid #EFDB28; padding-left: 1em; color: #DD4E31;
>"Soaring towards victory with the heart of a wrestler! El's the world's strooongest!"</blockquote>

The North American Phantom Bird! A vivacious and quirky lucha libre.

A masked luchadora, she's always hyped up and ready to wrestle. She has ultimate confidence that she's the world's strongest Umamusume, and loves to proclaim that fact to anyone who'll lend her an ear. Her mask is a present from her pro wrestler father and she insists on keeping it on all the timeーno matter what. Her best friend and roommate, Grass Wonder, constantly scolds her for being too reckless.

## Relationships

* Grass Wonder - Classmate, best friend and roommate. Teammates on the anime-only team, Team Rigil. Grass has a habit of pulling on her tail to keep El in check, much to the disdain and slight fear of El and despite that, Grass cares for her deeply. They both seem to be the ones that understand one another the most, and have a healthy rivalry on the track. Grass also keeps El's secret of owning a hawk in their dorm room under wraps.
* Special Week -  Classmate, friend and rival. Due to their similarly energetic personalities, El and Spe are very close friends, as El teaches Spe how to do special wrestling moves and how to name them, shares food sent from her family with her, such as cacti used with steak or as a smoothie, and even offered to share her strategy on how to beat Montjeu, who beat El in the Arc. El was also the one who taught Spe the French phrase she uses in the Japan Cup, though Spe is clueless about its actual meaning.
* Phalaenopsis - Classmate, friend and rival. They watch lucha libre matches together in their free time. Phalaeno also knows about El Condor Pasa's pet bird Mambo.
* King Halo, Seiun Sky, Tsurumaru Tsuyoshi - Friends and classmates.
** IRL, El Condor Pasa, Phalaenopsis, Special Week, Seiun Sky, Grass Wonder, King Halo, and Tsurumaru Tsuyoshi make up the Golden Generation, a group of horses that began racing in 1997 who dominated the majority of the Classics and their four year races.
** IRL, Grass Wonder and El Condor Pasa were American-bred and Japanese-trained horses who shared the same jockeys, Hitoshi Matoba and Masayoshi Ebina.
* Seeking the Pearl - Senior. El looks up to Pearl.
* Taiki Shuttle -  Friend and Senior. Teammates on the anime-only team, Team Rigil. Taiki usually shares the steak she gets from her family with El during her barbecues, which the latter is reminded of her home in the States.
** IRL, Seeking the Pearl, Taiki Shuttle and El Condor Pasa were the first American-born, Japanese-trained horses who won a international G1 in France. During El's expedition in France in 1999, El stayed in the same stable Taiki Shuttle resided in the previous year.
* Air Groove, Symboli Rudolf, T.M. Opera O, Narita Brian, Hishi Amazon, Fuji Kiseki, Maruzensky - Teammates on the anime-only team, Team Rigil.

## Trivia

* Her initial character profile was uploaded to the official [https://x.com/uma_musu/status/852355687791435778 Twitter] account on April 12, 2017.
* "Umamusume: Pretty Derby Artworks Vol.1" explains why her character designer made specific choices when designing her in relation to her real-life counterpart, which were as follows:
** Due to her real life counterpart wearing a blue belly band with red lines, the belt she wears around her waist is reminiscent of that.
** Due to her name being of Latin descent, her design features a lot of motifs reminiscent of a [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucha_libre Luchador] outfit.
** Due to winning many left-turn races at the Tokyo race course, she wears a brown band on her left leg.
*Her pet bird is named Mambo, which may be a reference to her real life counterpart's sire - Kingmambo.
* El can speak four languages, being English, Spanish, Japanese and French.
* When unmasked, El possesses a plain face that could mistake her for a mob Umamusume and displays a very shy personality, referencing her in-real-life counterpart's early years as a shy horse and the impressive track record, race aptitude and pedigree under his belt.
** This element of El also ties in with her Luchador inspiration. In Lucha libre, the majority of Luchadors wear masks, which evokes the identity the Luchador takes on during their career. Many Luchadors ensure they keep their personal identities hidden. Losing a mask to an opponent is considered the ultimate insult, and can seriously hurt the career of the unmasked.
* In the fifth episode of the first season of the anime and in the game, El Condor Pasa participates in the Japanese Derby, which was impossible in real life due to foreign-bred horses being unable to participate in the Classics prior to the JRA loosening restrictions in 2001.
** Due to the fact that the real El Condor Pasa never lost a race until the Mainichi Okan, where he and Grass Wonder lost to Silence Suzuka, in the anime, it was made so that both El and Special Week won the Derby by declaring it a dead heat, though it removed the real Special Week's impressive 5 length victory.
** Her inclusion in the Japanese Derby is due to the almost exact finish times Special Week and El Condor Pasa performed in the 1998 Japanese Derby and the 1998 Japan Cup respectively.
* Despite having a white marking in real life, this was not adapted in her Umamusume design.
* in {{Game Support|30161}} and {{Game Support|30163}}, in the card illustration both Nakayama and El wear matching flower-like keychain.
