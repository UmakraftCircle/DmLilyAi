## Appearance

Matikanefukukitaru has short orange hair that frays out at the ends, and bangs growing past her eyes. Her amber eyes display perpetual starry pupils. This Umamusume's ears are far longer than most, and she has tan inner-ears. Fuku's left ear has a Daruma doll at the base with its left eye painted and white tassels that resemble shide hanging below it. On her right ear, she wears a yellow four-leaf clover shaped accessory with striped ribbons attached to it. Also on her right ear is a white ear cover with dark blue at the top and a thin red stripe.

Her racing outfit consists primarily of a simple white, red, and blue sailor-style school uniform. The collar of her top is blue with white stripes, with a bagua motif on the back, and is tied at the front with a red necktie bearing a yellow knot with white five-point stars. Her top has open shoulders and is decorated with red bows on the sleeves. She wears a blue pleated skirt with white and red stripes around the perimeter. She also wears a belt with three omamori and two ema dangling from it, one saying "Great Luck" and the other saying "Assured Victory." On her back is a backpack shaped like a white maneki-neko with a red and green collar. Its left paw is raised while the other holds a large koban with the character "福" (Romaji: fuku) inscribed. On her legs, Fuku wears white stockings with alternating red and blue dashes across the top, and sports simple brown loafers on her feet.

## Trivia

* Her initial character profile was uploaded to the official [https://x.com/uma_musu/status/852713291637850112 Twitter] account on April 13, 2017.
** This tweet was a character name prediction quiz, that encouraged fans to try and guess which horse she's named after. The hint was "A superstitious Umamusume that loves fortune-telling. When she is overcome with "great fortune", she shows the power to overtake the fleeing Silence Suzuka!"
* "Umamusume: Pretty Derby Artworks Vol.1" explains why her character designer made specific choices when designing her in relation to her real-life counterpart, which were as follows:
** "Fukukitaru" means "good fortune comes", and her lucky cat backpack, "Nyaa-san", her left daruma hair tie, "Daruman", and her right golden four-leafed clover hair tie are known to be symbols of good luck.
** Since her real-life counterpart's jockey wore silks that were red with blue stripes, a serafuku was chosen for her racing outfit, as red and blue is a common color scheme for serafuku. 
** The two [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ema_(Shinto) ema] around her waist represent her consecutive victories in the Kobe Shimbun Hai and the Kyoto Shimbun Hai, the preliminary races for the Kikuka Sho.
* Shiraoki, the god she worships, is the name of a real life racehorse who is an ancestor of the real life Matikanefukukitaru. Other horses in Umamusume who are descended from Shiraoki are Vodka and Special Week.
* In the game, due to her being dependent on luck, two of her objectives are randomly selected, with either the Yayoi Sho, Spring Stakes or Mainichi Hai as the second objective and the Hakodate Kinen, Sapporo Kinen or Kokura Kinen as the second to last objective.
* She performs kagura dances back at her family's home, though she trips and falls every time she does so.
* Her older sister passed away when she was young.
** This is a reference to the real life Matikanefukukitaru's older brother, simply named Atheno Tosho 1991 due to never receiving a racing name. This is because he suffered a fatal neck injury as a foal, and he had to be euthanized.
*Despite the real-life counterpart having a marking and a white sock on his right hind leg, this was not incorporated into Matikanefukukitaru's design.
*Her username on Umatter is @Funnya_happy.
