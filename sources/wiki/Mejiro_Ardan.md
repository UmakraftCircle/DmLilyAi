## Appearance

Mejiro Ardan has long, light blue hair, styled in loose waves at the end. She wears a crown braid atop her head, and has blunt cut bangs, parted primarily to the right. On her ear, she wears a teal cuff, lined with golden trim, and attached to it is a pleated red ribbon. She has purple down turned eyes, showcasing her gentle demeanor.

She wears a black dress, adorned with white ruffles both along the neckline, and down the center of the dress, with a golden horseshoe with a circle in the center where the two pieces intersect. It is held up by two teal ruffled straps. Around her waist is a black sash with gold trim, tied at the center with a small red ribbon. The skirt of Ardan's dress is asymmetrical, the left half sporting a thigh-length black skirt with ruffles at the end, and the right side, a white underskirt with teal ruffles and stripes around the base. Around her tail is a light teal ribbon, cut into a scallop pattern at the ends. Despite the dress exposing her shoulders, her right sleeve is long, with a yellow tassel tied around her upper arm, and has a teal stripe down the center. This sleeve has a white ruffle at the end, along with a black glove underneath. Her left arm is bare, aside from a white glove tied with a thin golden ribbon. Both of her shoes consist of white boots with a black base, and teal ribbon tied up her leg. However, her left shoe is ankle-height, and her right shoe is knee-height.

## Trivia

* Her first appearance was in a chapter of Cinderella Gray on October 21st, 2020, but she made her appearance on the website on February 9th, 2021.
* Mejiro Ardan, alongside Mejiro Ramonu and Mejiro Bright, are the only members of the Mejiro Family that have never appeared in the main anime series.
** While Ardan did not appear in the main anime series, she did appear in Cinderella Gray, which is a spin-off.
*Because she is very used to hospital food, she tends to worry if the food she makes is bland.
*She likes going to theaters to watch plays.
*She is not easily scared of scary things, like tests of courage and eerie restaurants.
*She likes eating konpeito.
*She enjoys kite flying, as she never got to do it as a child.
*She is able to make healthy drinks.
*Her love for going to her favorite Chinese grocery store is a possible reference to how in real life, Mejiro Ardan was transferred to China in October 2000.
*In her character story, Mejiro Ardan briefly mentions that she had a twin who was stillborn. This is a reference to the real horse, in which he had a twin in the womb, but the foal came out stillborn.
*The player has the option of either entering Mejiro Ardan into the Kikuka Sho or the Tenno Sho (Autumn) in her classic year. This is because in real life, Mejiro Ardan did not run in other races after the Japanese Derby in 1988 due to a fracture, and made his return in 1989.
*She is able to withstand spicy food because she had to eat many bitter things as a child.
