# Part 4: Inheritance, Parenting and Gene Generation

Master index and conflict register: `AdvanceCareerGuide.md`. Tags: S1 JP Reference (5th anniversary), S3 Global Reference (Erzzy), S6 Crazyfellow's Parenting & Gene guide (updated through Aug 2026; most detailed and newest), S7 fan-farm guide. S6's caveat: proc-rate data collection is noisy and compatibility numbers are hidden; much of the newer data comes from Polaris (@BourBon_Polaris), Shoppo (@shoppo_ura), Aya (hakuraku.moe), aoneko_uma and umamusustation. ⚖️ = sources disagree.

## 4.1 Vocabulary

- **Sparks / Factors / Genes (因子, 遺伝子) / Legacies:** what an uma passes on. S6 notes the official JP word is "Inheritance", not "legacy"; Global localisation is called bad. Parents ("legacy umas") = the two umas you pick; each has their own two parents (grandparents). **Effects stop at grandparent level**; there is no hidden mechanic for generations behind (S6).
- Colours: **Blue** = stat; **Red/Pink** = aptitude (surface, distance, strategy); **Green** = unique skill; **White** = skills, race genes, scenario genes, hidden/awakening genes, aptitude genes.
- **Rentals/Guests:** you may borrow a friend's (or rental) parent: 3 borrows per day (S3); S6 says Uma Plan adds +5 rentals a day.
- **Hall of fame / inheritance-only umas (継承専用ウマ娘):** umas created by the Bulk Inheritance system are separate and cannot be used in daily races, trainer exchange, legend races etc. (S6).

## 4.2 What each gene does

| Gene | Initial (start of run) | Mid-run inheritance (two events: early April of Year 2 and Year 3; S3) |
|---|---|---|
| Blue ★1 / ★2 / ★3 | +5 / +12 / +21 base stat to that stat (S6); a 9★ blue = 63 (S3, three 3★ = 63) | +1-10 / +1-16 / +1-28 (S6) |
| Scenario gene ★1-3 | - | 10/20/30 to their stats (rolled independently for each stat, e.g. URA: +10 Stamina and +30 Speed is possible) |
| Race gene (G1) ★1-3 | - | 3/6/9 stat and/or a skill hint for certain G1s (e.g. Queen Elizabeth Cup gives stats plus a non-400m skill hint) |
| Green (unique) ★1-3 | Lv +1 hint level of the parent's unique | hint +1 to +3 (more ★ more often +3); a 3★ uma can pass her unique |
| White (skill) ★1-3 | hint Lv +1 | hint Lv +1 to +5 |
| Red (aptitude) | raises the aptitude by rank according to total stars (4.5) | hidden value +1 to +5 points per proc |
- Higher ★ helps twice: more chance to proc and more chance of the upper end of the range (S6). The older theory "★1 = 5%, ★2 = 10%, ★3 = 15%" is outdated (S6); current numbers in 4.4.
- Grandparent genes proc roughly **half** as often as the parent's (because GP compatibility is weighted lower) (S6, confirmed by Polaris).
- **Stat uncap mechanic (JP, 2500 cap; Global n/a until cap exceeds 1200):** blue genes raise the stat cap in the initial and mid-run stages; a mid-run green inheritance also uncaps stats by the source uma's bonus (Seiun's "Angling" uncaps Stamina and Int; Kitasan's "Wasshoi" uncaps Speed and Stamina; Agnes Digital's unique raises Speed/Stamina/Power, not Int despite her Int bonus); the amount is single digits. Clicking a green gene shows which stat is uncapped (S6).
- **Mid-run inheritance table (S6):** with ◎ compatibility all 6 blue stats are hit (average 4-6); with ○ an average floor of 4 (0 theoretically possible); whites and greens give "minimum +1 hint" and a 1-5 bonus; red gives 1-5 points. A missing blue is possible because of the individual compatibility theory (4.3). The initial green inheritance is special: it happens just before turn 1 and is affected by compatibility, but it isn't RNG: the same lowered green hint level always appears for △ parents.
- **Inheritance caps:** whites no limit; red no limit; scenario genes no limit (theoretical max from scenario genes: 60 stats × 6 procs = 360) (S6, confirmed by a user).
- **Gold Inheritance event (lucky proc):** ⚖️ S6's earlier view: flat ~20% chance that raises overall gene chance and differs by compatibility grade; S6's later view (Polaris data): it is only an indicator that a ★3 gene proc'd; no extra mechanic; there is no activation rate difference between gold and normal events; 0 gold events with a full ★1 set of parents.
- Genes can proc in **both** inheritance events (S6 corrects the "each gene procs once" myth).

## 4.3 Compatibility (affinity)

- **Display:** overall symbol ◎ great (>150 points), ○ good (51-150), △ poor (<50) (S6; S3: ◎ = 150+). The symbol sums the **individual compatibility** of each parent and grandparent link; each lineage member has a hidden point value. A ◎ built from many weak links (50+25+25+50+25+25 = 150) is poor; ideal >300, optimal parents ~400-500; don't start a run without ◎ (S6).
- **JP August 2026 (5.5 anniversary):** the JP client now shows the compatibility numbers and each individual breakdown (S6).
- **Hard-coded base compatibility** exists between every pair (0 with itself); use mee1080 umaishow, gametora compatibility (switch server top-left), U-tools (JP all-in-one), design.u-ma.org lineage planner (S6), derpiederp's sheet for the old Global system (no longer updated), and uma.pwnation.net race planner (S6).
- **Current system (JP post-2.0 anniversary/Grand Masters onward; Global since 24 June 2026; S6):** only overlapping **G1** wins grant compatibility, **+3 each** (previously +1); G2/G3 and title bonuses (Triple Crown/Tiara) no longer count; base compatibility across umas was raised; parent-to-parent overlapping races count. Run every G1 available; **ideal 22-26 races including dirt, about 20 turf-only**; with enough overlap a ◎ from only one side of the lineage is possible. There is no bonus for running the same race twice (Arima in Classic and Senior counts once).
- **Legacy system (obsolete everywhere since 24 June 2026; archived, S6/S3):** each identical race win (G1, G2 or G3) shared between a parent/GP and the target was +1; each title/epithet +1 (Classic Triple Crown, Triple Tiara, Spring Senior Triple Crown, Autumn Senior Triple Crown, Tenno Sweep, Dual Grand Prix, Dual Miles, Dual Sprints, Dual Dirt; U-tools has a longer list); it was possible to build "super parents" (e.g. 63 overlapping wins, 150+ points; best in MANT). S3 (older text): similar wording ("+1 per identical graded win", later "+3 per matching G1" and G2/G3 dropped, retroactive) and "only G1, G2 and G3 count; OP races don't".
- **Race overlap rules by scenario (S6):** URA/Aoharu/UAF/GFF/Mecha/DYI share URA-style final races; **URA Finals do not count** (they are scenario races; Cygames FAQ; corrected 11 Sep 2025, earlier 2021 text said they did); MANT, L'Arc, Grand Live and Grand Masters have scenario-specific races that don't help compatibility across scenarios; Twinkle Series has no extra races. Breeders' Cup scenario races are true G1s and do give compatibility. The G1 classification applies to scenario races.
- S6 recommendation: don't over-weight uma-to-uma base compatibility after the 2.0 overhaul: race bonuses account for most; race every G1 (20+ turf-only, 26+ with dirt); be careful mixing Tiara and Crown rotations (fewer overlaps); bridge the gap via dirt aptitudes or modern scenarios with no objective races. Parents should be trained with the same deck and races as the lineage (S6).
- S1 (JP) agrees: every G1 shared by all parents/grandparents adds compatibility; it suggests a dirt rotation (parents at Dirt D-C) so that more G1s overlap.
- **L'Arc bridge:** L'Arc sets all umas to A Turf and A Mid distance, bridging incompatible ranges (e.g. Curren Chan Short with Mid parents like Narita Brian; dirt-only umas); but it is shortened (caps at 16 overlapping races) so the compatibility ceiling is much lower, and red genes for Turf/Mid still need natural A aptitudes; scenario buff is temporary (S6).

## 4.4 Proc rates and formulas

**Base inheritance odds at 0 compatibility (Polaris, Aug 2025; S6, matches S3):**
| Gene type | ★1 | ★2 | ★3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Blue (stats) | 70% | 80% | 90% |
| Red genes and aptitude whites (遺伝子) | 1% | 3% | 5% |
| Green (unique skill) | 5% | 10% | 15% |
| Race whites | 1% | 2% | 3% |
| Skills, scenario, hidden (目覚め 隠し) whites | 3% | 6% | 9% |

**Formula:** `inheritance_odds = base_odds × (1 + individual_compatibility / 100)`. Example: ★3 green with 200 individual compatibility: 15% × (1 + 2) = 45%. S3 shows a 30-affinity example → ×1.3. Verified against a 100-run sample; grandparents proc about half as much because their compatibility is weighted lower; the same formula applies in all scenarios (S6, from a 2021 Cygames patent surfaced by Shoppo and tested by Polaris). Because compatibility is per lineage member, one weak grandparent drags its own genes (Fuji Kiseki example: poor base compatibility with Kitasan and E in Long plus different objective races meant at least 3 races mismatch: Kikka Sho, Arima, Haruten; her ★3 green tended to give +1-2 hints) (S6).
Earlier unconfirmed theories about "overall" compatibility influencing quality (e.g. ★1 white giving +3 hints with ◎◎) were probably misattributed individual compatibility effects (S6).

## 4.5 Red (aptitude) genes

- **Generation:** a red gene can only be generated from aptitudes where the uma has **A or better** (S does not increase odds). The pick is uniformly random among those A aptitudes; rarity is fixed: ★3 10%, ★2 70%, ★1 20% (umamusustation, S6). Therefore raising an unwanted aptitude to A lowers your chance of the gene you want.
- Examples: Mayano Top Gun's Late/End (B) won't be picked unless raised to A; El Condor Pasa B Dirt won't generate Dirt unless raised; Urara has 3 native A (Late, Dirt, Short): Dirt chance 33% if you add no other A; Mayano with 5 A + raised B's could be a 7-way pick (~17%). Early 2021 S6 table: chance of inheriting desired red gene unmodified: Agnes Tachyon 33.33%, Winning Ticket 33.33%, Vodka 25%, King Halo 33.33%, Haru Urara 33.33% and 25%, Agnes Digital 16.67%, Sakura Bakushin O 25%, Grass Wonder 20%, Gold Ship 25%, Smart Falcon 25%, Curren Chan 33.33%, Symboli Rudolf 20%, Super Creek 25%, Special Week 20%, Hishi Akebono 33.33%, Daiwa Scarlet 20%, Tokai Teio 33.33%, Nice Nature 25%, Biwa Hayahide 25%, Matikanefukukitaru 25% (chart partly garbled in extraction).
- **Conflicting-strategy trap (S6):** Symboli Rudolf's unique needs 3 overtakes in the last leg, so a Runner red gene on her is useless (her Runner B became A after a Mayano parent had Nige red, making it eligible). Gold Ship's unique needs the back half, so giving her a Senkou red gene is wasteful. Pair umas with the same style and distance so the lineage shares genes and you have usable inherited uniques.
- **Initial effects of stars (from S1/S3/S6):** 1★ total → 1 rank; 4★ → 2 ranks; 7★ → 3 ranks; 10+★ → 4 ranks (S3: one star for the first rank, then +3 stars per rank; max A; **S only from mid-run inheritance**). S6: A→S counts as a grade increase in the mid-run chart.
- **Hidden red values (S6, inferred, 2022):** red procs follow +1 to +5 points like white/green, so a ★3 can still roll only +1. Thresholds (mid-run): C→A needs ≥4 points, B→S ≥4 (via A), C→S in one go ≥7; C→B with at least 1 proc then B→A needs 1 point or B→S at least 4. Examples: G→C initially with 10★ of red: one proc rolling +4/+5 can reach A; F→B initially with 10★ then one proc ≥4 can reach S; a low roll can leave G→C at only B even after two ★3 procs. Extreme case (Agnes Digital G→S in Long): at least 10★ of red Long to lift G→C at start, then enough mid-run procs for A/S in the two events.
- **Priority for red genes (S6):** **Distance > Ground > Strategy**; distance S is vital for PvP (+5% Speed) and surface S gives +5% acceleration. S6's 2022 parent advice: 1-2 ground genes (versatile for every uma), 2-3 distance genes, at most one position gene (a Nige-gene Rudolf is a bad parent). Older picks (2021): Mile: Vodka/Daiwa Scarlet; Mid/Long: Maruzensky (Mid only; ideal accel for many mid tracks) and Symboli Rudolf (compatibility + unique); Dirt: Smart Falcon or any B-dirt uma raised to A; 2022: accel-unique parents are meta (OG Maruzensky, OG/Dance Seiun Sky, Mejiro Ryan, Mejiro Dober, OG Kitasan Black/White); older viable inherits obsolete: Meisho Doto, Gold City (risky random ult), Monk El Condor Pasa, Bridal Mayano Top Gun.
- **Aptitude genes (4th anniversary "idenshi" whites):** if your lineage has the red gene, its aptitude white can appear. Only the **total stars** of that red gene within the lineage matter (Shoppo): 6★ minimum for any chance (about 40%); 12★ guarantees it (100%); 6-11★ ≈ 38-43% (sample rows: 6★ ≈ 38-40%, 9★ ≈ 40-42%, 11★ 38.5%, 12★ and 18★ 100%). A greedy parent with 3×2 Mile + 3×2 Turf + 3×2 Late red genes could roll all three, but the value is abysmal since you'd drop dirt rotations; most aim for one, and only mile/dirt events justify two or more (S6).
- **Awakening/seasonal/directional/stat whites (目覚め):** Speed/Stamina/Power/Guts/Wisdom genes need **12+ successful trainings** of that stat (failed trainings don't count; two types is realistic, three is hard; auto focusing two stats helps). Directional (left/right turn) and seasonal (spring, summer, autumn, winter) genes need **6 race wins** under that condition (summer needs 5 beyond the debut race); any race type counts; only per-condition wins matter; all-surface/all-distance umas get directional genes by running each G1 once; summer is hard (consider junior OP races; summer races: JDD, Ibis Summer Dash ×2, late July-early September junior races, early September Classic/Senior races, summer camp races). Hidden factors: random stat, +6/+12/+18 by ★ (two Japanese notes documented them; S6 eventually rates these genes strong for low effort in auto parenting). **None of these are inheritable and lineage doesn't raise their odds.**

## 4.6 Blue genes and stat rolls

- **Generation:** at the end of a run **one stat of the five** is chosen at random (20% each); then a second roll decides ★ by that stat's value (S6, S3, umamusustation data). **3★ requires the stat ≥600 (B rank)**.
- **3★ odds by ability score (umamusustation sample):** 1200 (SS+): 10.39% (n=1033 chances); 1150-1199: 10.65%; 1100-1149: 10.97%; 1050-1099: 4.49%; 1000-1049: 5.22%; 900-999: 6.40%; 800-899: 6.01%; 700-799: 6.47%; 600-699: 5.50%; 500-599: 0% (n=2232); 400-499: 0%; 300-399: 0%; ≤299: 0%. Conclusion: **≥1100 roughly doubles 3★ odds**.
- S3 version: per-stat star table: <600: ★1 ~90%, ★2 ~10%, ★3 0%; 600-1100: ★1 ~50%, ★2 ~45%, ★3 ~5-6%; >1100: ★1 ~20%, ★2 ~70%, ★3 ~10%. ⚖️ S3's breeding text mentions "1% per 600 stat" and "2★ ~50%" that contradict its own table: unresolved; the umamusustation data above is the most detailed. S3: when a stat reaches 1100 the 3★ chance doubles.
- Example (S6): 1 SS stat + 1 B stat: (10% × 20%) + (5% × 20%) = 2% (Speed) + 1% (Power) = 3% overall; previously SS in all stats was impossible, now with GM/L'Arc and later scenarios an all-SS line is possible (maximum 10%).
- **Mid-run blue ★** follows the compatibility formula (4.4) and Stat gains (4.2).
- **Deck planning for blue:** bring a card of the stat you want blue on (e.g. a Stamina card for better odds of Stamina blue); U-tools (JP) has a card planner. Favourite blues: DYI: Guts; Onsen: Speed/Power; Global: Speed/Stamina/Power for a long time under URA/Aoharu/MANT (S6).
- **Beginner 9★ target (S3, S6):** roll a 3★ blue grandparent, pair with a second uma with a 3★ (from a rental or another internal parent). Total ★ counted across the uma and its two parents ("9★ Speed" = the uma has 3★ and both parents have 3★). S3 beginner path: own 2★ base parent + guest 3★ base parent → 7★ (45 stats) → 8★ (54) → 9★ (63) → then two 9★ umas allow 18★ without guests; loop breeding does not raise odds. S3 also: initial +stat from 9★ = 63 and from a rental uma ~189 at inspiration (S3: parent +15 stats at start vs borrowed +63; inspiration ~45 total vs ~189).
- **Parent blue priorities:** S1 (JP): blue factors matter little; people look for strong uniques, distance pinks and large white pools. ⚖️ S3/S6: 9★ blue is the first goal; S6 (JP terms): blue is for shoring up weak stats but CM-specific whites like Essence of Stamina matter more now.

## 4.7 White, green and race gene generation

- **Skills:** only skills you **learned during the run** can become skill genes; if all six lineage members have a skill but you didn't buy it, it isn't generated. Only the basic version can be passed down (never a gold); but buying a gold raises odds. Inherited unique skills (green) that you learned cannot be passed on as a white gene (wasting SP in a parent run except to win races).
- If a skill hint is inherited for a skill you already learned, the hint converts to stats (small value 1-9, amount unknown). A skill already learned (e.g. 良バ場○ then ◎) can no longer be hinted by "!" hints or end-of-race hints; you need a unique event with choices or a card event (S6).
- **Generation chance (S6, linear approximation, Polaris/Aoneko):**
| Type | Min (no lineage) | Max | Per lineage member |
|---|---|---|---|
| White skill (○/basic) | 20% | 35% | +2.5% |
| White skill ◎ learned | 25% | 40% | +2.5% |
| Gold skill learned | 40% | 70% | +5% |
| Race skills | 20% | 35% | +2.5% |
| Mini scenario whites | ~20% | ~30% | (same as white skills) |
  All skills have a flat 5% chance of ★3 (95% ★1-2) before grade effects. S3: +2.5% per ancestor that holds the gene (+5% gold); race/scenario whites 20%.
- **Exponential model (Aoneko; confirmed by uma.moe/Aya, n = 26,532,199 Team Trials umas; Aya March 2026 hakuraku.moe/notes/spark_generation):** probability = base × 1.1^N, N = number of lineage members with the gene; bases: white 20%, white ◎ 25%, gold 40%, race 20%. The linear model overestimates low lineage counts and underestimates high ones.
- **Race genes:** must **win the G1 in 1st place**; you only need to win it once (repeats give no benefit); 20-30% chance per run for a G1/race gene, 5% for ★3; no bias among G1s. Certain G1s give skill hints (Queen Elizabeth Cup: stats + non-400m skill). The number of race factors vs G1s won is unclear from a dev interview. The Aoharu scenario gene can appear even if you fail Aoharu races or fail the final URA portion (S6, S3).
- **Scenario genes (S6, Aya data):** probability by number of common lineage members with the scenario gene: 0 → 19.89% (13,311 runs); 1 → 21.75%; 2 → 24.93%; 3 → 27.81%; 4 → 30.39%; 5 → 28.88% (low sample); same min and max as other genes. Higher ★ likely gives better rolls (Gamewith <30 samples supports the ascending principle). Big scenario genes from finishing the scenario's race; L'Arc main + mini genes need level-3 debuffs maxed (single-digit stats); UAF (3 mini genes), GFF and Mecha (5 mini genes, one per stat) no longer make the big scenario gene; mini genes are the only ones that give Skill Points; inheriting at least 3 of a scenario's genes mid-run (not necessarily in the same turn) gives that scenario's unique skill (underwhelming and not inheritable).
- **Scenario gene stat leanings (S6, Y = big 10/20/30 gene, y = small):** URA Speed/Stamina; Aoharu Power and Int (Int gene still relevant to Nige meta); MANT Y; Grand Live Y (does not exist per S6); Grand Masters Y; L'Arc main genes Y + mini genes y (Stamina aptitude grants Stamina etc.); UAF mini genes y; GFF and Mecha individual stat genes (all stats); Twinkle Legends Power and Guts; DYI Stamina and Int; Yukoma/Onsen Guts and Int; Breeders' Cup ("Dreams") Speed and Guts; Tracen-ken/Ramen Power and Guts.
- **Uma grade effect on white/green quality (Aoneko 2022-2025; Aya 2026):** ending the run at SS or above raises ★ quality of white/green genes (blue and red unaffected, and appearance chance unchanged). Table for white ★ (★1 / ★2 / ★3): **below B** 90% / 10% / 0%; **below SS** (<17,500 score) 50% / 45% / 5%; **SS to below UE** (≥17,500) 20% / 70% / 10%; **UE and above** 17.5% / 70% / 12.5%. UB and above add nothing further (Jan 2025): UE is the ceiling. Stats above 1300 don't affect blue genes. March 2026: a tiny sample suggested different white appearance rates at low grades (B and below); not pursued. S3: SS or higher → 20/70/10.
- **Green genes (unique skills):** guaranteed to generate if the uma is 3★+; a magazine claim that ult level affects odds was debunked; **28 April 2026 (Aya): green genes follow the same mechanics as white genes**, including the grade table (Global data then only reached SS/UG; UE band extrapolated 17.5/70/12.5). Inherited unique strength: −0.2 (lower levels) and duration −40% (see Part 5).

## 4.8 Building parents ("supergene mummy")

- **Order of importance for a strong parent (S6, 2024):** 3 Red > good skills not found in your training deck > 3 Blue. Earlier ideas (2023 screenshots) are outdated.
- **Supergene strategy:** train with the same support deck and the same races as your lineage: same deck lets you buy the same skills (gene chance up to 70% for gold), same races raise common-G1 gene chances (up to 30%); you must win the G1; there's no benefit to repeating a G1 in Classic and Senior. Don't learn inherited uniques unless needed to win (waste of SP; no gene). Save race presets to avoid forgetting which races were run. Choose: jack of all trades (wide spread of skills) vs specialist (reinforcing loops for specific skill focuses and ★3 factors, e.g. URA essence skills specialist).
- **S3 "Advanced Breeding":** intentionally make parents win more races (affinity); use a rental with ideal representative sparks; be greedy on grandparents (they are bred out); keep unwanted aptitudes at B so pinks aren't diluted; trade blue ★ for scenario factors and better pinks to save time. Parent types (S3): **Distance parent** with 2★ distance pink base → 25-30% chance of S; with 3★ → 35-40%. **Dirt parent:** Mile 3★ on the parent, Dirt on grandparents → S Mile ~20%, S Dirt ~15-20%. Other: End Closer (straightaway spurt), Front Runner (Groundwork, Tail Held High), 6★ style parent (style pinks on grandparents, distance pinks on the parent). "Parents to Try Making" section lists Sprint/Mile/Dirt/Medium/Long targets with pink/blue/green needs.
- **Planning questions (S6 ch. 8):** What is this parent for (CM/LoH, GGP, GP, main)? Which route and which uma can generate the needed red? Do I have the parenting pass to lock a gene (Factor lock or combined pass), or let RNG pick? Which rental raises the aptitudes I need (dirt/mile/mid)? C grade is usually the bare minimum to win. For F2P/Global (no gene passes): raise dirt/mile for max G1s but don't accidentally raise unwanted aptitudes to A. Support deck: plan which stats and skills; U-tools planner (JP).
- **Good-enough rules (S6):** don't chase perfect genes early; a minimally viable parent (aligned reds, weak blues, plus 1 strong borrow) gets you through most content; running a conflicting-strategy parent with zero usable whites for the red alone is "absolutely stupid"; better one run with a strong rental accepting what comes out; don't rent junk parents with 100 whites of which only 10 are usable; always borrow the strongest borrowable parent (**UmaPureDB** uma.pure-db.com, Global uma-global.pure-db.com): finding a good one takes <5 minutes versus grinding mediocre parents. Blue genes are foundations for weak stats but CM-specific whites are more important now (Essence of Stamina 「レースの真髄・体」 etc., unobtainable from cards; Speed Eishin Flash and Power Fine Motion later gave access). Red genes for what your main uma needs (e.g. Mile, Turf); Global: first 9★ blue plus at least ★2 Distance on the main parent.
- **Where to spend (S6):** PvE content (Stadium/Team Trials, Legend races, Daily races, Master challenges) doesn't need strong parents; PvP events (Champions Meeting, League of Heroes, the short-lived Monthly Matching Beta) do; white skill genes grow in importance as very strong speed/accel skills appear.
- **Friend search and follow list (S1, S3, S6):** UmaPureDB and Discord friend-ID channels; look for: 9★ Speed (Long), 9★ Stamina (Mile/Medium), 9★ Power (Sprint), Mile/Dirt/Sprint pinks (Haru Urara, Pasa, Oguri, Air Groove); MLB Kitasan and Super Creek from 9★ umas (S3); CM: strong track uniques, 9★ distance, high race count; Stadium: highest-cap stat blue (Summer Gold Ship rental so a failed gold can be retried). Mark 3★ blue or 2★ blue + 3★ pink umas as protected parents (S3).
- **JP parents are extremely high quality** (S1); Global players are "so far behind" in scenario/parenting tools (S3). S1 (JP): one strong rental is all you need for most content.
- **Uma Plan (JP 5th anniversary subscription, 24 Feb 2026):** extra roll on the mid-run inheritance (pick one of two rolls), useful for double-G aptitude parents (Stay Gold parenting etc.); bulk inheritance farming (5 rolls, 10 with the gene/factor pack); +5 rentals a day; +100 follow slots on first purchase (S6). To pick your re-roll in auto runs tick "Pause at the inheritance turn" (S6).

## 4.9 Bulk Inheritance (Uma Plan; JP)

- Premium-only; one free set of 10 tickets after the 5th anniversary; uses its own rules; the rest of S6's gene rules don't apply to these rolls, but renting an inheritance-only uma for standard parenting follows usual rules.
- Steps (S6 from Cygames' help page, 27 Feb 2026): create a plan (up to 5 saved); choose a scenario; pick an uma; set up the parents you rent (a friend's inheritance-only uma doesn't consume rentals or money); set race rotations (win rate from aptitude: A/A = 110%; B or lower reduces it without declared rates; 3+ consecutive turns of racing reduces it more per turn; before the 20 May 2026 patch a trophy for the race added +30% and trophies can't be earned by bulk-made umas, so do one normal run first); choose up to 10 prioritised genes (skill gene odds depend on support card LB; lineage genes ~5% chance; training guide ON gives 1.1× for the "SUPER recommended" genes; rental decks not allowed); with the Factor lock/combined pass you pick the gene to lock (Blue: unknown how determined; Red: random among A aptitudes); execute.
- Inheritance-only umas cannot be used in daily races, legend races etc. S6 notes that for Breeders' Cup (bad for normal parenting) bulk inheritance gives better results than auto or manual (abbreviated; the tail of the S6 explanation was cut off in extraction).

## 4.10 Parent-farming scenarios: S6 ratings (as of June 2026) and notes

Every scenario guide in S6 assumes **auto-kun controls the run** and is for parenting, not Ace runs. Auto enabled for scenarios after Feb 2025 (URA got auto at the 4th anniversary). Scenario launch cadence changed to every 4 months (from 6) in 2024.
- **Still relevant (June 2026):** **Breeders' Cup** and **Tracen-ken/Ramen**. **Obsolete or superseded:** URA, Aoharu, MANT, Grand Live, Grand Masters, L'Arc (only to bridge compatibility), UAF, GFF, Mecha, Twinkle Legends, DYI (superseded by Tracen-ken), Yukoma Onsen (superseded by Tracen-ken; higher SP ceiling and stronger hint mechanics).
| Scenario | Key facts |
|---|---|

## 4.11 Summary of key parenting recommendations across sources

1. Plan before the run: purpose, route, rental, deck. Red genes first (Distance > Ground > Strategy), then whites not in your deck, then blue (S6, S1, S3).
2. Borrow the strongest rental you can find; don't build junk parents (S1, S3, S6).
3. Maximise overlapping G1s (current system): 22-26 with dirt; don't mix Crown/Tiara rotations without dirt/Breeders' Cup bridging (S6).
4. Keep unwanted aptitudes below A (S6, S3).
5. Win each G1 at least once for race genes; don't repeat races for odds (S6).
6. SS+ end grade raises white/green ★ quality; UE is the ceiling (S6).
7. 3★ blue needs ≥600 in the picked stat and doubles at ≥1100 (S6, S3).
