## 5.1 Skill types and colour (S1, S3)

- **Activating conditions:** every skill has conditions; after meeting them, non-green skills do a Wit check. `@` = "or", `&` = "and"; `&` comes before `@` in the order of operations. Common conditions: `running_style`, `distance_type`, `order` (order>=2 means 2nd or worse), `order_rate` (order_rate<=50 top half), `distance_rate` (>=50 latter half), `change_order_onetime` (positive = you were passed, negative = you passed someone), `straight_random`, `corner_random`, `phase_random` (0 opening, 1 middle, 2 final leg; checks a random spot in that phase), `accumulatetime` (seconds before activation allowed), `near_count`, `is_finalcorner` (includes the final straight), `corner` (corner!=0 corner; corner==0 straight), `slope` (1 uphill, 2 downhill), `is_overtake`. A `phase_random==0` skill with `accumulatetime` may pick a spot so early that not enough time has passed, making it impossible to activate (less desirable). Activation engine details: Part 2, 2.7.
- **Skills with angle brackets** require a strategy or distance; don't buy ones that don't match your uma (they do nothing) (S1). S3: pick one distance and one style that you are A or better in for a run.
- **Green (stat) and purple skills (S1):** small bonus (green) or penalty (purple) to a stat if conditions are met; the bonus ignores the stat cap. Level 1 = +40, level 2 = +60 (purple −40): level only once if at all; lowest buying priority. Icon shows the stat (Speed, Stamina, Power, Guts, Wisdom, All, Speed). Value depends on how often it activates; example: core-distance greens: 90% of Dirt races are on non-core distances while 75% of Mid races are on core. In PvP, **Speed green is typically the only type worth getting** (more value per SP); Stamina green can be good in Long because Stamina above 1200 is converted to speed via Stamina Showdown (スタミナ勝負, Part 2). Green skills skip the activation check.
- **Blue (recovery) skills (S1):** restore HP in the race (Stamina stat is not HP); commonly 1.5%-7.5% HP. ⚖️ S1: in JP, stat inflation lets everyone reach minimum Stamina for Short to Medium and to survive HP-drain debuffs, so recoveries are mostly for Long; S3 (Global) needs them for Mid and Mile as well (required Stamina chart in Part 2). Best ones restore at a consistent time, usually before the end of the Middle Leg.
- **Red (debuff) skills (S1):** affect other umas, not friends. Icons: Speed down, Acceleration down, Kakari extension, HP down, Vision down. In the current JP meta **Speed down is the only relevant type**; HP down does nothing when everyone has enough HP; Acceleration down is good in theory but too rare/hard to time; Kakari extension is mostly griefing (the HP increase is trivial). ⚖️ S3 (Global, where stamina is scarce): stamina debuffs matter (each gold stamina debuff −3% HP, white −1%; Nice Nature alone could remove 8% on Medium; a gold recovery is +5.5%); speed debuffs: gold −0.25 m/s (≈ a negative Medium Distance Corner ◎), "Hesitant" skills −0.15 m/s (≈ a negative white speed skill).
- **Orange skills (S1):** Speed and Acceleration (best ones have good activation times and long durations); a lane-change skill (outward in the last leg, inward otherwise); start skills (white weak, **gold very good and used often, especially for Runners**; a purple version that makes starts worse exists and Gold Ship can get it by winning Takarazuka Kinen twice); vision skills (do nothing unless you were vision-debuffed, due to the hard cap at the default distance).
- **Speed skill effect types:** Target Speed (useless until top speed is reached) and Current Speed (never wasted). **Acceleration skills only help while accelerating;** they do nothing if the uma is already at top speed (S1).
- **Uniques (S1, S3):** per-uma; a 3★ uma can pass hers. Inherited uniques are much worse: strength −0.2 and duration −40% (0.35 over 5 s becomes 0.15 over 3 s; strong 0.45/5 s becomes 0.25/3 s; dual uniques +0.25 speed/+0.3 accel become 0.05/0.1; Gold Ship 0.25 over 6 s becomes 0.05 over 3.6 s); recovery uniques 5.5% → 1.5% (strong ones like Super Creek and Fantasy Grass 7.5% → 3.5%). Level scaling: recovery +2% per level (level 4 = 5.83% instead of 5.5%); other skills +1% at level 2 then +3% per level. ⚖️ S3 (GL): unique recovery below 3★ restores 3.5%, else 5.5%.
- **Hard-to-inherit exception (S1):** one inherit used by nearly all top rankers is **Gold Ship (Summer)'s unique**, which triggers a random gold skill you have (a second chance at a failed Wit check).
- **Skills stack (S3):** debuffs stack; all skills stack even with the same name; skills bought work anywhere; debuffs don't affect your teammates.

## 5.2 Skill effectiveness math (S1 examples)

- **Speed skills:** distance = effect × duration × (track length/1000). Speed Star (スピードスター) 0.35 m/s × 1.8 s = 0.63 m (1.512 m at 2400 m). Heart and Soul (全身全霊) 0.35 × 2.4 = 0.84 m (2.016 m at 2400 m). Medium Distance Corner ◎ 0.25 × 3 = 0.75 m (1.8 m at 2400 m).
- **Acceleration skills:** compute the time to reach top speed with and without the skill. At 1000 Power, accel = 0.424 m/s² (ignoring phase multipliers); time = (Top − Start)/Accel; distance = 0.5 × time × (Top + Start).
  - **Groundwork (地固め)** 0.2 over 3 s: start 17 m/s, top 20.3 m/s (Runner in Mile): base 7.78 s/145.1 m; with skill 5.29 s/98.66 m + 2.49 s at top speed 50.55 m = 149.21 m → **+4.11 m**.
  - **Seiun Sky unique (アングリング×スキーミング)** 0.4 over 4 s: start 20, top 24.2: base 9.9 s/218.8 m; with skill (accel 0.842) 4.99 s/110.28 m + 4.91 s × 24.2 = 118.82 m → 229.1 m → **+10.3 m**.
  - **Imminent Shadow (迫る影)** 0.4 over 0.9 s (1.8 s at 2000 m; Chaser: base 19.9, top 24.4): base 10.61 s/235 m; with skill: 21.38 m/s after 1.8 s (37.15 m) then 7.12 s/162.98 m + 1.69 s × 24.4 = 41.24 m → 241.37 m → **+6.37 m**.
- **Spurt speed carry-over (S3):** speed skills raise top speed; if a speed skill is active when the last spurt begins you get a head start on acceleration as if you accelerated 0.15 m/s earlier; even if the skill ends 0.1 s later it doesn't lower speed. Comparison (spurt from 20 to 25 m/s; base 207.46 m over 9.43 s): white accel 213.50 m (+6.04; also inherited accel unique); gold accel 216.62 m (+9.16; also non-inherited accel unique); white speed 208.84 m (+1.38; also inherited speed unique); gold speed 210.63 m (+3.17; also speed unique); dual unique 216.61 m (+9.15; +3.97 m when inherited). Speed skills that activate before the spurt also give their speed (in 2000 m a 3 s skill lasts 6 s; a gold used 5 s before the spurt adds ≈ 0.35 × 5 = +1.75 m). Important on tracks where final-corner accels work (Scorpio Cup: Late Surger McQueen viable through her unique's carry-over).
- **Wit and activation:** S3 notes Wit-based activation is important enough that two golds needing both procs is unreliable (Part 2 recovery table).

## 5.3 Racing styles and phases (S1)

- Four strategies plus Great Escape (Oonige), a Runner subset. Phase strength: Great Escape ★★★★★/★★☆☆☆/★☆☆☆☆; Runner ★★★★☆/★★★☆☆/★★☆☆☆; Leader ★★★☆☆/★★★★☆/★★★☆☆; Betweener ★★☆☆☆/★★★★☆/★★★★★; Chaser ★☆☆☆☆/★★★★★/★★★★★ (opening/middle/final). Exact coefficients are in Part 2, 2.5.
- **Position Keep:** each strategy wants a position relative to the 1st Runner; they speed up (Pace Up) or slow down (Pace Down) to hold it. The better the 1st-place uma the faster everyone runs and the more Stamina the race needs; the pace-down chance falls. Exception: no Runner in the race, then the Leader who takes first ignores Position Keep and acts like a Runner (then Betweener etc.) (S1, S3).
- **Race flow (S1):** four phases: Opening Leg first 1/6, Middle Leg 3/6, Final Leg 1/6, Last Spurt 1/6. Before the Final Leg your Speed stat doesn't affect target speed (Wit slightly does); after the spurt starts Speed and Guts affect speed. The spurt is when the uma spends all her energy. Starting stationary she accelerates to opening speed, then middle-leg speed, then final-leg speed; if she doesn't spurt immediately when the Final Leg starts she later accelerates to spurt speed; acceleration skills are useful only in those periods; speed skills are useless while accelerating but last beyond it.
- **Final-leg HP decision:** at the Final Leg she decides how fast and when to start running based on HP, with higher Wit making more optimal decisions. If HP is lacking she delays the spurt (red line in the graphs) and the dip at the end is running out of HP; severely lacking HP means she may not spurt at all (Speed is wasted). A recovery used in the last two phases makes her reconsider (example: Breath of Fresh Air restoring 5.5% lets her start the spurt right then, less optimal than a recovery that triggers earlier but better than not having it). A well-timed accel skill (Sprint Turbo example) can raise time at top speed from ~50% of the Last Spurt to 80-90%; the meta revolves around acceleration skills for this reason.
- **Track features (S1, S3):** corners (closer to inside is shorter; passing on a corner costs distance; four corners per capsule track), uphills (slow umas; Power lowers the loss), downhills (Wit-based speed boost and lower HP use; lower Stamina requirement for high-Wit umas), Final Corner (umas spread out to avoid blocking), Final Straight (needs a preceding corner; Ibis Summer Dash is special-cased).
- **Gate blocks:** see Part 2, 2.6 (skill odds like Lucky Seven 1/9 in CM, 2/12 in Stadium, 3/18 in large races).

## 5.4 Analysing tracks and using tools (S1, S3)

- **Track visualiser** shows elevation, hills, straights/corners and leg starts. The most important question: **does the last spurt start on a corner?** The spurt starts at the Final Leg (not the Last Spurt phase). Acceleration skills at spurt start help a lot; Speed skills are then useless.
- **Takamatsu Kinen example (S3, Chukyo 1200m):** Maruzensky's accel unique triggers on the final corner, so it is too early; the same for Seiun Sky and Taiki Shuttle. El Condor Pasa's unique triggers on the final straight (good). Narita Taishin's Encroaching Shadow is strong; last-straight speed uniques (Silence Suzuka, Mihono Bourbon) are wasted. Hill skills trigger on the first hill: Highlander (登山家) strong, Restless (じゃじゃウマ娘) useless (no recovery needed in 1200 m), 1,500,000 CC (十万バリキ) triggers too early here. Opening straight ~300 m and ending straight 412 m: straight skills are more likely to trigger in the final leg; on longer tracks Breath of Fresh Air (一息) or Cooldown (クールダウン) would be inconsistent.
- **Umalator (S3; GL `alpha123.github.io/uma-tools/umalator-global/`):** simulates races between two umas in a sterile environment; good for skill effectiveness and HP checks; real races have randomness (blocking, Wit rolls, skill triggers, nearby umas, position keep), so it finds average performance, not any single race. **Check Stamina:** enter your uma, click Compare; a dip at the end = not enough HP; if both dip the track is too long for the default opponent. Add debuffs to uma 2 (Mystifying Murmur on Medium; All-Seeing Eyes with uma 2 set to Late Surger). To find requirement ahead of time: enter projected stats and recoveries, copy to side 2 with ">" and set one uma to the target Stamina and the other +100 and compare; 0 difference means enough; lower the other uma's Speed to see how much lack of Stamina costs (used for the Stamina vs Speed tests in Part 2).
- **Finding good skills (S3):** choose the track, pick the style, set mode "Skill Chart" and run; if heals/debuffs rank high your uma lacks Stamina, so set Stamina to 8000 for a general chart; add the best unique skills you have available (inherited), then rerun (other accel skills drop, max-speed greens rise); check minimum/maximum values (Unyielding may trigger too early); verify requirements (e.g. Behold Thine Emperor's Divine Might needs passing three umas: fine for Pace Chasers normally, but in the first Derby CM Front Runners were weak so Pace Chasers had fewer to pass); consider SP; compare ◎ to single circle and golds to whites.
- **G1 track spurt starts (S3):** "Late Final Corner" = too far into the final corner for final corner accels like Maruzensky's or Dober's; "Very Late" = even inherited Taiki (after her rework) fails. Sprint: Nakayama 1200 (Sprinters) Late Final Corner; Chukyo 1200 (Takamatsunomiya) Final Straight, very inconsistent Downhill/Uphill. Mile: Hanshin 1600 (Asahi Hai, Hanshin JF, Oka Sho) Very Late Final Corner; Tokyo 1600 (NHK Mile, Yasuda, Victoria) Very Late Final Corner then Final Straight; Kyoto 1600 (Mile CS) Late Final Corner. Medium: Nakayama 2000 (Hopeful, Satsuki) Corner; Kyoto 2000 (Shuuka Sho) Downhill + Corner, Final Corner shortly after; Tokyo 2000 (Tenno Sho Fall) Late Final Corner; Hanshin 2000 (Osaka Hai) Corner, Final Corner shortly after, inconsistent Downhill; Kyoto 2200 (QE II Cup) Downhill + Corner, Final Corner shortly after; Hanshin 2200 (Takarazuka) Corner, Final Corner after, inconsistent Downhill; Tokyo 2400 (Oaks, Derby, Japan Cup) just before Final Corner. Long: Nakayama 2500 (Arima) Straight, Corner shortly after; Kyoto 3000 (Kikka Sho) Straight, Corner shortly after; Kyoto 3200 (Tenno Sho Spring) Straight. Dirt: Ooi 1200 (JBC Sprint) Final Straight; Tokyo 1600 (February Stakes) Very Late Final Corner then Final Straight; Ooi 1800 (JBC Ladies Classic) Corner, Final Corner shortly after; Chukyo 1800 (Champions Cup) Final Corner, inconsistent Downhill; Ooi 2000 (JBC Classic, Japan Dirt Derby, Tokyo Daishoten, Teio Sho) Corner. Tally: Straight 23.3% (Final Straight 13.3%); Corner 76.7% (Final Corner 63.3%; Early 30%, Late 10%, Very Late 23.3%).
- **S1 pointer:** KuromiAK's technical document (S2) for the nitty gritty; Gametora skills page for skill lists/conditions; vtuberstart article on Stadium scoring/frequencies.

## 5.5 Building umas by strategy (S3)

- **How to win:** the biggest factor is acceleration; extremely powerful at a good time (a 3 s gold speed on 1600 m gains 1.68 m; Seiun Sky's unique 10.3 m), so the meta revolves around uma acceleration skills. Also strive for **S distance aptitude** (+10% Speed ... S3 says +10%; S2 gives +5% stat multiplier, about +10.25% after conversion, see Part 2 ⚖️) which helps Front Runners hold the lead and others catch up; Speed greens help too.
- **Front Runners:** key skill **Groundwork** (acceleration at start after activating three skills, letting them take the front); get three greens that suit the track; Focus/Concentration also works but not 100%; aim for Groundwork white sparks on Front parents (troublesome otherwise). **Seiun Sky's unique** is the other key accel skill ("what makes Front Runners viable in PvP at all"); works when the last spurt starts on a corner; must be in first place, so stack middle-leg speed skills (Professor of Curvature, Mile/Front Runner Corners, Summer Maruzensky's unique, Tail Held High, Escape Artist...). All Medium and Mile G1 tracks start the spurt on a corner; all Long G1 tracks on a straight (in Long use Kitasan's unique). Front Runners value Wit highly (early speed and activation).
- **Defeating Front Runners (Seiun covers):** cap Power and Wit, ignore other stats (e.g. 600/400/1200/200/1200), stack middle-leg skills to keep the lead and/or debuffs; Summer Maruzensky's unique always activates in the middle; Bakushin, Smart Falcon and V. Bourbon also good; distance aptitude only needs D+.
- **Pace Chasers:** all good accels are on the Final Corner or Final Straight; if the spurt starts just before/early into the final corner, Maruzensky's and Taiki's uniques are useful; if late into the final corner or before/into the final straight Nishino Flower's unique is good (and to a lesser extent H. Creek's); Pace Chasers also have a downhill accel skill (good on Kyoto Medium).
- **Late Surgers:** "a big dice roll": many accel skills but none consistent; take as many as possible. General accels: On Your Left! (Nice Nature SSR; borrow if lacking; Gold City, Grass Wonder at level 5), Mejiro Ryan/Dober's unique, Burning Spirit PWR (Unity Cup). Mejiro Ryan's unique matches Seiun Sky's activation point but requires being 6th (not 1st), so don't get too far ahead; Dober's is often the same as Ryan's but allows 5th. Gold City's unique, El Condor Pasa (Fantasy), Mayano Top Gun (Wedding) sometimes. Track-specific: Mile: Furious Feat (Oguri Cap SSR; Vodka level 5); Dirt: Trending in the Charts! (Agnes Digital card); Medium: Rise from the Ashes.
- **End Closers:** similar to Late Surgers (no On Your Left!); get Straightaway Spurt/Encroaching Shadow (accel on straights in the last spurt; gold version exclusive to Narita Taishin).
- **Debuff builds:** umas that barely pass the Stamina requirement are prime targets, especially Medium+. Wit is all that matters (500/300/900/300/1200 is fine for any distance); focus on the strongest roles for the track (usually Front Runner and Late Surger). Easy to raise if you don't want to train a third good uma. (S1 adds that in LoH debuffers are a poor idea because of placement scoring.)
- **Christmas Oguri:** her unique accelerates after three recoveries, so you choose where via the recoveries; high Wit is key. On 2000-2500 use Triple 7s (triggers after spurt start on 2400/2500 but still strong); Kyoto Medium: End Closer with a downhill recovery; if there is an uphill: Front Runner with the uphill recovery; if the spurt starts on the first corner after halfway: U=ma2 (Agnes unique) as Pace Chaser; if the final corner: Super-Duper Climax! (Urara unique) as Late Surger/End Closer. Gamble: three phase_random recoveries (Race Planner, Gourmand, Burning Spirit STA, Super Creek unique) or Drain for Rose (Halloween Rice) as Pace Chaser. Late Surger fallback: A Small Breather (early gives the accel; late gives some speed); First Star is decent on any style but can trigger early.
- **Team composition (S3):** synergy rarely matters as long as they don't work against each other; two key facts: Front Runners perform worse when alone (two Front Runners compete and both run faster); with zero Front Runners one random Pace Chaser gets a huge buff (assigned as the front runner with Pace stats), so bring a Front Runner to reduce it or if your Pace Chaser can't accelerate in first (Maruzensky can ult from 1st to 5th but Taiki only 3rd to 5th). Supporters: Debuffers (Speed debuffers on Sprint/Mile; End Closers best; Stamina debuffers on Medium/Long; Late Surgers best there; or a Front Runner debuffer motivating your carry); Sacrificial Front Runner (low stamina that dies in the final leg to trigger uniques that need passing); Seiun Bully (see above).
- **Unusual strategy (S3):** Urara + McQueen: put Urara on a Turf track and she falls so far behind that `distance_diff_rate` skills go haywire; McQueen's unique has `distance_diff_rate<=30`, so she can activate even as a Late Surger/End Closer; strongest when McQueen's unique lasts into the spurt (carry-over); you can also inherit McQueen's unique; you can raise a debuffer to have 100 Power by failing Power trainings in Year 3 (e.g. Grass Wonder with Dominator); this ruins End Closer skills like Masterful Gambit (仕掛け抜群) and Sleeping Lion (眠れる獅子).
- **Lazy CM preparation (S3):** in most CMs prepare one parent with a good unique and a 2★ pink for the distance (no 3★ blue; race whites and affinity matter little: +50 stats won't change win rate much). Gemini Cup (3200 m): Rudolf with 2★ Long, borrow a 3★ Long + 3★ Blue (Stamina) as grandparent; End Closer conversions want 3★ End pink on the grandparent. Getting a 2★ pink takes about twice as many runs as your uma has A aptitudes (~10 on Oguri, ~6 on Teio); doubling for a 2★ blue; ×5 for a specific blue. Required skills (Gemini: Straightaway Spurt; annoying for non-Taishin) should be on the grandparent (reduce pink to 2★, then white; never lower the blue). Example: Taurus Cup team Taiki/Daiwa/Nature made with a 7★ Stamina parent and a borrowed Maruzensky; runs: 3 Nature, 2 Daiwa, 4 Taiki; stop when a run hits Distance S plus any mandatory skills; in the final room (2 Pace 1 Front) an Oguri won; consistently reaches A Finals.

## 5.6 Stadium / Team Trials (S1, S3)

- **Format (S1):** starts as five 1v1 races; with weekly promotions becomes five 3v3 races. Whoever places first in a category wins it; win the match by winning ≥3 of 5 categories. Categories: Short, Mile, Medium, Long, Dirt (Dirt always Mile; never Short or Mid Dirt). Can't duplicate umas within a team or use two versions of the same uma (e.g. Mejiro McQueen and Mejiro McQueen (End of Sky)); so you eventually need 15 unique umas.
- **Scoring (S1):** not simply win/loss; points from a good start, using many skills, a close race, a dominant race, the best-performing uma of your position, etc. Different strategies on the team give "Nice Position" bonus points. Start dash bonus if start delay <0.02 s (S2).
- **1-2★ team ideas (S1):** Short: Runner/Leader Sakura Bakushin O; Leader/Betweener Air Groove (inherit Short); Betweener/Chaser King Halo. Mile: Runner Twin Turbo; Leader Ikuno Dictus; Betweener Vodka. Medium: Runner Daiwa Scarlet; Leader Agnes Tachyon/Tsurumaru Tsuyoshi/Royce and Royce; Betweener Winning Ticket/Mejiro Ryan. Long: Leader Super Creek; Betweener Matikanefukukitaru/Matikanetannhauser; Chaser Gold Ship. Dirt: Runner Mayano Top Gun (inherit Dirt and Mile); Leader El Condor Pasa (inherit Dirt); Betweener/Chaser Haru Urara (inherit Mile). No 1★/2★ Dirt umas in five years; raising Mayano's aptitude is hard so most use the select-3★ ticket (Oguri Cap or Taiki Shuttle).
- **S3 new player team (per-distance):** Bakushin Sprint, Vodka Mile, Daiwa Medium, Gold Ship Long, Urara Dirt. S3: in Team Trials Oguri is almost always used for Mile or Dirt; in Champions Meeting you can use her freely; no incentive to keep styles separate in CM (feel free to duplicate).
- **Promotion/demotion:** weekly at Monday 05:00 JST reset; the golden number is placement in class; cutoffs under it (example Class 6 stay above 137,217th = 493,950 points); a small band exists for remaining in class, not displayed. Items: parfait (max mood for friendly umas, +4% all stats), sunny, rainy, first-three gate blocks, last-three gate blocks (small speed boost); the latter four mostly help with conditional green skills; parfait helps generally.
- **Race frequencies (S1 JP; S3 has a Global version not re-read):** Short: 1000 m 8%, 1200 m 54%, 1400 m 38%; core distance 50%; clockwise 52%, counter 43%, 5% no corners; tight-turn tracks 29%; uphills 12/18 (7 before spurt); downhills 10/18 (all before spurt); common locations Niigata, Hanshin, Chukyo. Mile: 1500 m 5.3%, 1600 m 36.7%, 1800 m 58%; core 43%; clockwise 70%/counter 30%; tight turn 29%; uphills 15/17 (11 before spurt); downhills 13/17; common Kyoto, Nakayama, Sapporo. Medium: 2000 m 62.6%, 2200 m 20%, 2300 m 4.7%, 2400 m 12.7%; core 73%; clockwise 52%/counter 48%; tight turn 19%; uphills 17/22; downhills 15/22; common Niigata, Tokyo, Kyoto; Longchamp 2400 m not in rotation. Long: 2500 m 18%, 2600 m 37.3%, 3000 m 16.7%, 3200 m 8%, 3400 m 10.7%, 3600 m 9.3%; core 19%; clockwise 79%/counter 21%; tight turn 33%; uphills 2500-2600: 6/7; downhills 4/7; 3000+: 6/6 each; common Tokyo, Kyoto, Nakayama. Dirt: 1600 m 25%, 1700 m 25%, 1800 m 50%; core 25%; clockwise 50%/50%; tight turn 43%; uphills 9/16 (8 before spurt); downhills 8/16; common Funabashi, Morioka. Seasons Spring 40%, Summer 22%, Fall 12%, Winter 26%; weather Sunny 58%, Cloudy 30%, Raining 11%, Snowing 1%; track condition Good 77%, Slightly Heavy 11%, Heavy 7%, Bad 5%. Nocturnal cannot activate (daytime races); Exchange Race skills cannot activate; mobs in Short/Mile/Dirt don't have Lone Wolf, 80% chance in Medium/Long; NPCs have Lone Wolf and Sympathy (more with longer distance).

## 5.7 Champions Meeting (CM) (S1, S3)

- Monthly 3v3v3 tournament on one track (specialised umas); rewards very good. You only bring 3 umas (vs 15 in Team Trials).
- **Brackets:** Graded League (any uma; better rewards: 3rd in Graded finals ≈ 1st in Open finals) and Open League. ⚖️ Open League eligibility: S1 (JP) UC or worse; S3 (Global) B or worse. After the tournament starts you can't change the choice. Recommended to join Graded even as a new player (S1).
- **Rounds:** Rounds 1 and 2 last two days each with up to 4 entries/day of 5 races each (8 opportunities per round; best score counts, so keep using entries after reaching Group A). Final Round is one race; there is a 12-hour window after Round 2 to register the umas (else the umas of your last entry), then a matching period; results after.
- **Win-rate stats:** after a set of races you can see each uma's win rate; before a race via the booklet icon top-left.
- **Strategy (S3):** since track details are known, buy greens you normally wouldn't (Left-Handed and Fall greens give +40 Speed each; both on a left-handed fall track = +80); other track details (final corner timing, hills, final straight length) matter; debuffs are popular so bring more Stamina or extra recoveries; in CM winning is all that matters (unlike Team Trials points), so skills that accelerate you faster are especially desirable.
- **Open League (S3):** stats are usually more desirable than lots of skills (Taurus Cup 2400 m: many B-rank umas took only Swinging Maestro with the rest in stats); fail the fan checkpoints to avoid levelling the unique skill (adds points); leave umas at low stars; S aptitude adds no points so is very desirable; purple skills reduce score (S1, useful for A+ builds); rank calculators available.
- **Prep (S1):** analyse the track, spurt start, use the Umalator, use win-rate stats, build specialised umas with S distance and suitable uniques; S3 "preparing for CM (for lazy people)" is in 5.5.

## 5.8 League of Heroes (LoH) (S1)

- Monthly; build is similar to CM, but **points come from each uma's placement**, so debuffers/sacrificial Urara are generally poor. Reaching Platinum with an uma gives an uma-specific title (not hard; Stadium umas are usually enough); repeating gives stars on the title. Since the 14th LoH the top 30,000 get extra jewels, shiny animated titles and physical rewards for the top 96 (not shipped outside Japan).
- **Timeline:** 6 days, 5 tickets/day, each item usable 3 times per day; entries don't cost tickets until Silver (start with 4000 points); 30 tickets total so max 34,000 points. **Optimisation:** save 5 tickets per day: day 1 nothing; day 2 do 5 runs (parfaits for 3), repeat until day 6 when you use 10; lets others rank up first so matchups are easier; once Platinum you are paired with any Platinum player; below that you'll see many A-S rank umas if you save tickets; later in the day may add to this.
- **Gates:** 12 umas → 25% inner gates, 50% outer, Lucky Seven 16.6%; the inner gate green is just Wit and inner gates are generally preferred by Runners; making multiple teams for different items isn't worth it.

## 5.9 Popularity (S1)

- The game's evaluation of an uma's strength (like betting odds); larger relative stat differences raise popularity. Symbols from best to worst: ◎, ○, ▲, △ (only the top 5 get symbols; △ is shown twice). Evaluations are T-scores (mean 50, SD 10); e.g. Archline Professor's adjusted speed skill value at 1200 Wit is 55.5; Guts and Wit skill values aren't reflected.
