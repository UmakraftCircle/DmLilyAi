## Understanding cards (READ ME)

Before heading into the list, one should be able to understand what I consider notable bonuses and also be able to start building one's own criteria for pulling cards based on the neccessities of one's account

### Understanding cards

There are 7 types of cards, one for each attribute (speed, power, stamina, guts, wit) plus pal and group cards

Each card allows friendship (rainbow) training on their dedicated attribute, with group cards having friendship training on wit, and with the exception of pal cards that do not have friendship training

Cards also come with passive bonuses, support hints and event hints, R cards only have skill hints and random events, SR cards have both, plus 2-chain events, and SSRs have both and 3-chain events

Support hints, as the name indicates, give a chance at discounts during careers of said skills, or extra stats, when an *[hint icon]* appears on a training facility

These hints are based off Hint Frequency% bonus, and give more hints off Hints Levels bonus (2 hints for level 1, 3 for level 2, and up to 5 at level 4)

Event hints are similar, but are predefined by the card's events, which could be given away by random events (shared across all rarities of the same uma — so for example, R Silence Suzuka will have the same random events as SSR Silence Suzuka) or by chain events (unique to each SR or SSR card)

SSR cards, additionally, have 3-chain events, with the last event in the chain usually giving gold skills, most cards do give a gold skill, but some have a gamble feature, also called coinflip, where you have the chance of getting the white skill version instead

These coinflip events also have a hidden "stat-check", where the chance of getting the gold skill depends on how high the card's stat is at the time of triggering — for example, Special Week SSR is more likely to give Gourmand if you have 1000+ speed

Passive bonuses increase your stats throughout a career run, and can affect the the amount of stats you get from mood bonus, training, and rainbows, as well as the extra stats you get after races and goals, the amount of fans, the chance of appearing at their designated training facility, and other beneficial effects like energy reduction, failure protection, and others

Friendship Bonus amplifies the amount of stats you're getting from a rainbow training, so this bonus is only applied once you reach orange bond and that card is in its dedicated training facility.

Friendship Bonus from a unique passive is multiplicative (for example, Sweep Tosho SSR, has 35% Friendship bonus at MLB, and her unique (10%) amplifies it to 48.5%)

Mood Effect boosts the already-existing mood bonuses you get during career (20% extra training stats and 4% race bonus while on great, 10% and 2% on good, -10% and -2% on bad, and -20% and -4% on awful), this bonus is added multiplicatively, this means for a card with 30% mood bonus, you'll be getting 26% extra stats when training with that card, on any facility. This is also added negatively for bad or below moods, so be careful (-10% training could turn into -13%!)

Mood bonus from a unique passive is additive (for example, Winning Ticket SSR, has 30% Mood bonus at MLB, and her unique (10%) amplifies it to 45%)

Training Effectiveness grants extra stats on every training you do with that card, regardless of the facility it lands on

Training Effectiveness from a unique passive is additive (for example, Daiwa Scarlet SSR has 5% Training Effectiveness at MLB, and her unique (5%) amplifies it to 10%)

These three bonuses are the trifecta of stat bonuses, and you can calculate your training bonus multiplying all three bonuses (for example, Kitasan Black SSR has 5% Unique Training Effectiveness, 10% Training Effectiveness, 30% Mood Bonus and

25% Friendship Bonus at MLB, assuming a Great mood, we'll be looking at 15% Training effectiveness, 26% Mood Bonus and 25% Friendship Bonus, for a total of 1.15 * 1.26 * 1.25 = 1.81, or 81% extra stats on your base speed facility numbers)

Stat Bonus +x is a flat increase of x to a training increasing that stat before applying any other bonuses, or the skill point gain if that's the case (for example, Mejiro Ryan SSR has Power Bonus +1 and Guts Bonus +1 at MLB, this means she'll apply an extra +1 base to power when training in speed, power or guts, and an extra +1 base to guts when training in stamina or guts with the card)

Specialty Priority is an increase of a card to land in its designated training. The way your supports assist in training works this way: each card has a weight of 100 of landing in each facility, and 50 of landing in none, therefore, each turn, a card with 0 specialty priority has a 100/550 (18.18%) chance of landing on either of the 5 facilities, or 50/550 (11%) of not participating in training at all.

Specialty Priority from a unique passive is multiplicative (for example, Vodka SSR has 65 Training Effectiveness at MLB, and an extra 20 from her unique, so her total weight would be [(100+65)*1.2]/550 = 198/550, or 36% chance of training in power each turn)

Race Bonus is a percentage increase in the rewards you get after finishing races (skill points, and stats if you win) and when doing career goals (including URA Qualifier, Semifinals and Final stat increases)

Fan Bonus is also a percentage increase of gained fans you get after finishing races

Initial Stat +x is a flat increase of x at the start of a run in said stat

Initial Friendship Gauge +x is also a flat increase of x of a card's friendship gauge, where higher Initial Friendship Gauge decreases the turns needed to reach orange bond for friendship training

Wit Friendship Recovery is a Wit and Group card unique bonus that increases that amount of energy recovered during friendship training with those type of cards, usually, Group Cards have +2, SRs have +4 (except Ikuno Dictus SR) and SSRs have +5 (except Matikanetannhauser SSR)

Event Recovery and Event Effectiveness are both percentage increases in Pal cards' events, increasing energy, stats and skill points gained through all of their events

Energy Cost Reduction and Failure Protection are both percentage reductions in energy cost and failure when training, respectively. These bonuses are usually given to Pal cards, with the exception of Matikanefukukitaru SSR

### What makes a card good

Every card has a niche use of some sort, people who played the Japanese version used to joke around saying Air Shakur SSR being the only gacha cleat-able card, but, right now, it's the only card in the game that gives a guaranteed Straightaway Spurt hint from her event chain. Every account is different, every user and their situation is different, some people may have the trio of Kitasan Black, Super Creek and Fine Motion MLB, others may have only a couple copies of some SRs, and some other players have every card in MLB status. But all suffer from the same issue: not knowing what to use in each deck. You do not want to be bringing Super Creek to a mile deck, or have more than four speed cards for an uma with 20% growth in speed, or an SSR when you have a better SR in your hands.

So let's look at bonuses, skills, and more:

Let's start by looking, slowly, at the one of the Three Goddesses three great SSRs of early game, Kitasan Black

- Kitasan Black (0LB): 5% Training Effectiveness + 20 Specialty Priority (Unique), 20% Friendship Bonus, 5% Training Effectiveness, 20% Mood Bonus, 5% Race Bonus, 25 Initial Friendship Gauge
- Kitasan Black (1LB): 5% Training Effectiveness + 20 Specialty Priority (Unique), 21% Friendship Bonus, 6% Training Effectiveness, 23% Mood Bonus, 5% Race Bonus, 27 Initial Friendship Gauge, Power Bonus +1
- Kitasan Black (2LB): 5% Training Effectiveness + 20 Specialty Priority (Unique), 23% Friendship Bonus, 8% Training Effectiveness, 26% Mood Bonus, 5% Race Bonus, 30 Initial Friendship Gauge, Power Bonus +1
- Kitasan Black (3LB): 5% Training Effectiveness + 20 Specialty Priority (Unique), 25% Friendship Bonus, 10% Training Effectiveness, 30% Mood Bonus, 5% Race Bonus, 32 Initial Friendship Gauge, Power Bonus +1, Specialty Priority 40
- Kitasan Black (MLB): 5% Training Effectiveness + 20 Specialty Priority (Unique), 25% Friendship Bonus, 10% Training Effectiveness, 30% Mood Bonus, 5% Race Bonus, 35 Initial Friendship Gauge, Power Bonus +1, Specialty Priority 80

We're seeing a steady buff in all of the trifecta stat bonuses until 3LB, then she gets a massive increase in her Specialty Priority, let's translate this to numbers

0 turns (41.6% chance of 1.383 bonus training elsewhere, including not training)

0 turns (8.3% chance of 1.449 bonus training elsewhere, inclduing not training)

From 0LB to 2LB, we see around a 10% stat increase, but no stability in training speed with Kitasan at all

From 2LB to 3LB, we have another 7% stat increase (total from 0LB to 3LB: 17.35%), but the chances of not training in speed go down by 25%, whilst 2 and 3 turns of speed training get a considerable boost to happen (+9% and +7%, respectively)

From 3LB to MLB, the stats remain the same, but the chance of not training speed have gone down by 33% from 0LB, and chances higher turns in training spike considerably (almost 19 times more likely to get 5 continuous turns of speed than 0LB!)

Thus, we can conclude Kitasan is a good card based on numbers alone, a card that provides consistent high numbers all around — with and without rainbow, and in and out of friendship training

Now, we should be looking at the second most important aspect of cards, skills.

One of the best examples for how important gold skills are on SSR cards is none other than Super Creek

Besides great stat bonuses (15% training effectiveness, 37.5% friendship bonus and 10% race bonus at MLB), we're looking at a card that offers one of the greatest recoveries on any track, Swinging Maestro

There are many skills in the game and more will be implemented as time goes, but some cards give more generalist skills that are good everywhere (the aforementioned Super Creek, or others like Kitasan Black, Yukino Bijin, Matikanetannhauser or Tokai Teio)

Soon enough, we'll be looking at cards that specialize in a specific style or track type (Mr C.B for end closers, Maruzensky for front runners, Nice Nature for late surgers, Agnes Tachyon for pace chasers, Taiki Shuttle for miles, The Throne's Assemblage for mediums...)

For skill rankings, conditions, and general information, refer to Tools in Links to get a better grasp at working out skills for your umas

Last but not least, there are certain cards that become more useful on certain scenarios, such as scenario link and pal cards. Most pal cards are really strong in their designated scenario and give great value even at 0 or 1LB. Scenario link cards also provide extra skill hints that should be taken into account when making a deck for an specific scenario to train on

## Grand Masters

**Scenario release:** Early Dec '26  
**Sheet:** GM

- **Scenario gimmick:** every turn you can acquire fragments (one out of three colors - blue, yellow, red; and six types, one for each stat and one for skill points, totals 18 types of fragments)
- doing any action (including resting and dating), fragments give +1 bonus stat to their respective type, then, two fragments combine into a shard, that gives +2 or 3 bonus to its type, then
- two shards will combine into a crystal, and two crystals combine into a Goddess' Wisdom, which gives permanent bonuses (training effectiveness, hint chances, extra events,
- discounted energy, support event buffs...) and a one-turn buff depending on the color of the Goddess' Wisdom, then your fragments will reset and you can start getting them again
- Besides, you'll run in three different races each at the end of each year, getting extra bonuses based off the amount of Goddess' Wisdom you get before the races
- **Scenario link:** Ancestors & Guides
- **Scenario skills:** Wisdom of the Sun (Blessing of the Sun), Wisdom of the Ocean (Blessing of the Ocean), Wisdom of the Land (Blessing of the Land), Firm Track Demon,
- In Body and Mind
- **Scenario spark:** Grand Masters Scenario (SPD/PWR)

## Project L'Arc

**Scenario release:** Early Apr '27  
**Sheet:** LArc

- **Scenario gimmick:** prepare for international racing with extra training partners (similar to Unity Cup!) that each have a Star Gauge to fill up. When it's full, they'll join an SS Match where
- you'll have to race against them to level up their Star Gauges, gain stats, an assortment of buffs and Support Points that fill up an Expectation Gauge. When more than 5 people are present
- in an SS Match, you'll have the chance to pop an SSS Match, getting extra stats. Summer camp is replaced by Overseas Expedition, where you'll raise your overseas aptitudes through
- special tasks and trainings, as well as races like Prix Foy and Prix Niel, getting special buffs and preparing to participate in the L'Arc races (early October in Classic and Senior year)
- **Scenario link:** Mei Satake, El Condor Pasa, Manhattan Cafe, Gold Ship, Orfevre, Nakayama Festa, Tap Dance City, Sirius Symboli, Satono Diamond
- **Scenario skills:** Dreaming of the Peak (Bearer of Hope), See Ya Later!, and specific-event skills that depend on scenario link trainee or support card, and can be random if no
- **scenario links are present, every listed skill:** Child of Longchamp (Longchamp Racecourse), Photon Flash (Medium Straightaways), It's On! (Ramp Up), Innate Experience (Inside Scoop),
- Miraculuous Step (Soft Step), Nothing Ventured (Risky Business), Center Stage (Prudent Positioning), Overwhelming Pressure (Pressure)
- **Scenario spark:** L'Arc Scenario (PWR/STA), Overseas Turf (PWR), Longchamp (Skill Pts.), Everyday Rhythm (GUTS), Nutrition Management (STA), French Fluency (WIT),
- Overseas Expedition (SPD), Boldness (STA/GUTS), Mental Strength (PWR/WIT), L'Arc Hopes (SPD, Corner Adept), L'Arc Domination Dreams (Downhill Speedster)

- a level that goes from 1 to 100. Each turn, your facilities will shuffle between the 15 disciplines, and whenever two or more disciplines appear, you'll be able to do link training, which allows

**Sheet:** DYI

- **Scenario gimmick:** build your facilities and train in a deserted island. Easy work for an Umamusume, duh. You have a Construction Plan that refreshes 6 months, as well as a Development
- Gauge that keeps tracks of your facilities' development. You get to choose the construction plan (and therefore, the facilities you're upgrading) at the start of each construction phase. From
- level 3 onwards, your facilities can take an Instinct Full Throttle build (more stat gains, takes more to build) or Skilled Technique Route (more skill points and hints, cheaper to build). You can
- also build a Beach House that allows Island Training, a combination of all your trainings together, giving increased stats depending on your construction plan, as well as bond levels to all
- your supports. You can access island training after acquiring its tickets, two are given throughout each construction plan phase. During summer, facilities are replaced by their Island Versions
- which boosts several stats at the same time, with stronger clicks depending on the upgrades of your facilities. After each construction plan phase, an Evaluation Meeting will be held, which
- will provide stats and extra bonuses based on your development points.
