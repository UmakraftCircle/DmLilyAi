## 2.1 Stat definitions and caps

- **Raw stats** are the stat panel values. **Base stats** = (Raw + Aoharu team rank bonus) × Motivation coefficient (絶好調 1.04, 好調 1.02, 普通 1.00, 不調 0.98, 絶不調 0.96). **Raw above 1200 is halved before becoming base stat** (raw 1600 → base 1400) (S2, S1). Green skills that give stats add their full value to raw and base.
- **Adjusted stats** (each also clamped):
  - AdjustedSpeed = BaseSpeed × RaceCourseModifier + GroundModifier + SingleModeModifier
  - AdjustedStamina = BaseStamina + SingleModeModifier
  - AdjustedPower = BasePower + GroundModifier + SingleModeModifier
  - AdjustedGuts = BaseGuts + SingleModeModifier
  - AdjustedWiz = BaseWiz × StrategyProficiencyModifier + SingleModeModifier
- **Final stat = AdjustedStat + SkillModifier**; it is the stat used in formulas unless stated (S2). Activation chance uses **base** Wit.
- **Caps:** ⚖️ S2: each stat is clamped to 1-2000, and this and all later stat caps were raised to **2500 on the 5th anniversary (JP)**. S3/S6: **Global still 1200**; S6 says uncap mechanics don't apply to Global until the cap moves past 1200. S3 FAQ: "internal stat caps are 2000, green skills can raise stats to 2000".
- **Single Mode Modifier:** in training all uma gain +400 adjusted stats (S2). S3: the invisible +400 makes Stamina requirements much lower in Career.
- **Ground modifier, Speed:** all conditions 0 except Heavy (不良) −50 for turf and dirt.
- **Ground modifier, Power:** Turf: Firm 0, Good −50, Soft −50, Heavy −50. Dirt: Firm −100, Good −50, Soft −100, Heavy −100.
- **Strategy Proficiency Modifier (Wiz):** S 1.1, A 1.0, B 0.85, C 0.75, D 0.6, E 0.4, F 0.2, G 0.1.
- **Race Course Modifier:** some courses have 1-2 threshold stats. Base stat above the threshold gives adjusted Speed a bonus: ≤300 +0.05×, 301-600 +0.1×, 601-900 +0.15×, >900 +0.2×. With two threshold stats the modifier is averaged, so max is +0.2×. S3 calls these "secret stat checks": +5% speed per 300 in the stat (1-300 +5%, 301-600 +10%, 601-900 +15%, 901+ +20%), halved when there are two; mood moves the thresholds (stats needed for a boost, Highest 290/578/867; Good 296/590/884; Normal 301/601/901; Low 308/614/920; Bottom 314/627/939). The list of courses comes from the reverse-engineering community (umamusu_reveng tweet) and isn't shown in game.

## 2.3 What each stat does

**Speed (S1, S2, S3):** the only stat affecting your top speed and only in the last spurt (final leg after the spurt begins); early/mid target speed ignores it. S3 formulas:
`BaseSpeed = (20 − (CourseLength − 2000)/1000) × PhaseModifier`;
`FinalLegSpeed = BaseSpeed + sqrt(500 × Speed) × DistanceAptitude × 0.002`;
`MaxSpurtSpeed = (FinalLegSpeed + 0.01 × BaseSpeed) × 1.05 + sqrt(500 × Speed) × DistanceAptitude × 0.002`.
⚖️ S2 (newer) adds a Guts term: `LastSpurtSpeed_max = (BaseTargetSpeed_phase2 + 0.01×BaseSpeed)×1.05 + sqrt(500×Speed)×DistanceProficiency×0.002 + (450×Guts)^0.597×0.0001` (the Guts component was added after the 1st anniversary). S1 (5th anniversary): with raw Speed above 2000, the excess feeds **Zenkai Spurt (全開スパート)**; how impactful is unknown but maxing speed is a top priority.

**Stamina:** HP = 0.8 × StrategyCoef × Stamina + CourseDistance. Strategy coefficients: Front Runner 0.95, Pace Chaser 0.89, Late Surger 1.0, End Closer 0.995, Oonige 0.86. S3: Pace Chasers effectively have an 11% stamina penalty (the 0.89 coefficient). Running out of HP sets you to minimum speed. Stamina above the requirement matters via Stamina Limit Break (2.9) and as insurance vs Rushed and debuffers.

**Power:** `Accel = BaseAccel × sqrt(500 × Power) × StrategyPhaseCoef × GroundTypeProficiencyModifier × DistanceProficiencyModifier + SkillModifier + StartDashModifier`; BaseAccel 0.0006 m/s², **0.0004 uphill**. Also: uphill speed penalty `|SlopePer| × 200/Power` (S2/S3 same), lane change speed `0.02 × (0.3 + 0.001 × Power)` m/s (S3, S2), conserve power/release if above 1200 (JP), Compete Before Spurt scaling, Zenkai Spurt acceleration. S3: Power matters more in short races and when there are uphills; less in long races where you have time to accelerate. S3: "for the most part, if you meet your stamina goal, focus on speed as much as possible, then power second". S3 notes that Speed training also raises Power, and Power training raises Stamina, so even in Sprint Speed wins out in the end; Power is stronger the more Speed you have and vice versa.

**Guts (S1, S2, S3):** effects: late-race (final leg/last spurt) HP consumption multiplier `1 + 200/sqrt(600×Guts)` (200 → 1.577×, 400 → 1.408×, 600 → 1.333×); spurt speed (post-1st-anniversary); minimum speed `MinSpeed = 0.85 × BaseSpeed + sqrt(200×Guts)×0.001`; Spot Struggle, Dueling, Compete Before Spurt, Secure Lead. Overall smaller than other stats; not generally to be prioritised (S1). S3 targets: ~300 Guts, rarely over 400.

**Wit (S1, S2, S3):** (a) skill activation `max(100 − 9000/BaseWiz, 20)%`: 200 → 55%, 300 → 70%, 400 → 77.5%, 500 → 82%, 600 → 85%, 700 → 87%, 900 → 90%, 1200 → 92.5% (S2, S3 tables agree); (b) Rushed chance; (c) chance to enter downhill mode; (d) Pace Up and Speed Up/Overtake entry chance; (e) per-section random target speed modifier; (f) last spurt candidate acceptance `15 + 0.05×Wiz`%; (g) Stamina Keep and Compete Before Spurt decisions; (h) JP 5th anniversary: excess over 1200 boosts unique/gold/evolved skills (2.10). ⚖️ Wit target: S3 (GL) 300-500 ("more than that starts to fall off too much"); S1 (JP) "always pushing 1200+".

## 2.4 Stamina, Guts and required Stamina

**Stamina vs Guts (S3, from ottoh's tweet):** each distance has a target Guts. Below it each Guts point is worth more than a Stamina point; above, less. Thresholds: Sprint 210, Mile/Dirt 260, Medium 320, Long 3000m 380, 3600m 440. 50 Guts above the threshold is worth 43-46 Stamina; the next 50 worth 33-37; the next 50 worth 30-31. 50 below adds worth 55-62 Stamina; 100 below adds 66-83; 150 below 82-106 (more impactful in shorter distances).

**HP consumption (S2, S3):** `HPConsumptionPerSecond = 20 × (CurrentSpeed − BaseSpeed + 12)² / 144 × StatusModifier × DownhillAccelModeModifier × GroundModifier`.
- Status modifier: Front Runner Spot Struggle + Rushed 3.6×; Oonige Spot Struggle + Rushed 7.7×; Front Runner Spot Struggle 1.4×; Oonige Spot Struggle 3.5×; Rushed 1.6×; Pace Down 0.6×; otherwise 1.0×. Downhill accel mode ×0.4.
- Ground modifier by track condition: Turf Firm 1, Good 1, Soft 1.02, Heavy 1.02; Dirt Firm 1, Good 1, Soft 1.01, Heavy 1.02.
- In the late-race and last spurt HP consumption is multiplied by the Guts modifier above.
- HP recovery: all recovery skills instantly restore a percentage of max HP (excess is wasted) and function identically apart from trigger and amount.
- S3: gold 5.5%, white 1.5%; uniques below 3★ 3.5% else 5.5%; recoveries also reduce needed Stamina for the track. ⚖️ S1 says inherited uniques drop 5.5% → 1.5% (strong 7.5% → 3.5%); S1 range of recovery skills 1.5% to 7.5%. S1 level scaling: recovery unique +2% per level (Lv4 → 5.83% instead of 5.5%).
- Out of HP: target speed = minimum speed; deceleration −1.2 m/s² (overrides).

## 2.5 Speed model

- **Target speed** (trend-toward): `(BaseTargetSpeed | LastSpurtSpeed) × PositionKeepCoef + ForceInModifier + SkillModifier + SlopeModifier + MoveLaneModifier`. Cannot go below Minimum Speed nor above 30 m/s. Below target: accelerate; above: decelerate.
- **Force-in modifier:** applied in the early-race when >0.12 course width away from the inner fence and inside is blocked. `Random(0.1) + StrategyModifier` m/s, random rolled once at race start; strategy modifier: Front 0.02, Pace 0.01, Late 0.01, End 0.03.
- **Move-lane modifier:** when affected by a skill that modifies lane move speed and lane movement occurred in the previous frame (post 1st anniversary): `(0.0002 × PowerStat)^0.5` m/s.
- **Slope modifier:** uphill: minus `SlopePer × 200/Power`. Downhill: each second a `Wiz × 0.04%` chance to enter downhill accel mode: +`0.3 + abs(SlopePer)/10` m/s and HP consumption −60%; each second 20% to end.
- **Base speed:** `20.0 − (CourseDistance − 2000m)/1000` m/s (1200m 20.8, 2000m 20.0, 2500m 19.5).
- **Base target speed** early and mid race = BaseSpeed × StrategyPhaseCoef (speed stat has no effect). Late-race and last spurt use the last-spurt formula.
- **Strategy phase coefficient (speed):** Front 1.0 / 0.98 / 0.962; Pace 0.978 / 0.991 / 0.975; Late 0.938 / 0.998 / 0.994; End 0.931 / 1.0 / 1.0; Oonige 1.063 / 0.962 / 0.95 (early / mid / late & last spurt). Acceleration coefficients: Front 1.0 / 1.0 / 0.996; Pace 0.985 / 1.0 / 0.996; Late 0.975 / 1.0 / 1.0; End 0.945 / 1.0 / 0.997; Oonige 1.17 / 0.94 / 0.956. S3 and S1 give the same numbers.
- **Randomness per section:** each of the 24 sections, base speed × random% is added to target speed, random in [Min, Max]: `Max = Wiz/5500 × log10(0.1×Wiz)%`, `Min = Max − 0.65%`. Not applied during last spurt. Examples: 400 Wit +0.117%/−0.533%; 800 Wit +0.277%/−0.373% (S2). S3 table: 200 → 0.047%/−0.603%; 300 → 0.081/−0.569; 400 → 0.117/−0.533; 500 → 0.154/−0.496; 600 → 0.194/−0.456; 700 → 0.235/−0.415.
- **Last spurt:** distance and speed are computed at the start of the late race (without per-section randomness); not the same as the "last spurt phase". If HP suffices to run the remaining distance at max target speed the uma spurts immediately. If HP is insufficient, candidates are generated by lowering target speed by 0.1 m/s each step until it reaches the late-race base target speed, sorted by completion time (ignoring acceleration); going best to worst each is accepted with `15 + 0.05×Wiz`%; if none passes, the slowest is chosen. The estimate covers stamina use up to 60 m before the goal. If a recovery skill triggers after entering late-race the spurt is recalculated (post 1st anniv.); debuffs do not trigger a recalculation. S3 chart examples: an uma lacking HP delays the spurt; an HP recovery lets her start immediately but is worse than a recovery that triggers earlier; if severely lacking she might not spurt at all.
- **Starting speed** 3 m/s. **Start dash** +24.0 m/s² until speed reaches 0.85 × BaseSpeed. Launch bug (extra acceleration carried past the threshold) was fixed by capping speed at 0.85×BaseSpeed until the modifier is removed; that fix later caused a bug where a speed debuff during the dash could leave the uma stuck under the threshold; now fixed by making the trigger ignore current speed modifiers. An uma on a course starting uphill with low power can be stuck in start dash until the hill ends. After the dash an uma usually can't accelerate to minimum speed within a frame, so her speed is raised to the minimum.
- **Minimum speed** = `0.85×BaseSpeed + sqrt(200×Guts)×0.001` m/s; applies after the start dash; if after a frame's acceleration an uma is below it, her speed is raised instantly.
- **Blocking:** while blocked her speed is capped between 0.988× (0 m) and 1.0× (2 m) of the blocker's speed (linear in distance).
- **Current vs Actual speed:** "Current Speed" (LastSelfSpeed) is the direct result after acceleration and is fed into the next acceleration and HP consumption; "Actual Speed" (LastSpeed) is current speed with current-speed modifiers applied (immediately, without accelerating), used for distance and relative-speed calculations (blocking, overtaking).
- **CurrentSpeedWithNaturalDeceleration (ability 22):** a modifier that raises actual speed (like CurrentSpeed) plus, when it expires, a one-time modifier of equal size raising current speed.
- **Deceleration:** early −1.2, mid −0.8, late −1.0 m/s²; Pace Down (after 1.5th anniversary) −0.5; out of HP −1.2.

## 2.6 Phases, sections and racing styles

- Race is 24 equal sections; phases: Early-race 序盤 (0) sections 1-4; Mid-race 中盤 (1) sections 5-16; Late-race 終盤 (2) sections 17-20; Last Spurt 最終盤 (3) sections 21-24 (S2). S1/S3: Opening Leg = first 1/6, Middle Leg = next 3/6, Final Leg = next 1/6, Last Spurt = final 1/6; the Final Leg and Last Spurt share numbers and can be treated as one phase; skills reference these phases.
- Progress bar ticks between early and mid, and mid and late; the UI fades in the last spurt.
- Frame rate: simulated at 0.0666 s per tick (~15 fps). Replay records positions each frame within 1 s of start or within 25 m of goal, else every 1 s (16 frames).
- 1 Bashin (horse length) = 2.5 m.
- **Style shape (S1, S3):** Great Escape ★★★★★/★★/★; Runner/Front ★★★★☆/★★★☆☆/★★☆☆☆; Leader/Pace ★★★☆☆/★★★★☆/★★★☆☆; Betweener/Late ★★☆☆☆/★★★★☆/★★★★★; Chaser/End ★☆☆☆☆/★★★★★/★★★★★ (opening/middle/final).
- **Position keeping rule (S1, S3):** in the first half, each style tries to hold a position relative to the front runner (Pace Up if too far, Pace Down if too far ahead). Numbers are in metres; those lower than −3 scale with track length. If there is no Front Runner (or he's too bad to lead) a random Pace Chaser (then Late, etc.) takes the lead and ignores position keeping (S3); S1 says the Leader who takes 1st. Great Escape behaves like Runner but doesn't slow down until 17.5 m ahead of 2nd.
- **Racing on corners/slopes/final corner (S3):** the closer to the inside of a corner the shorter the path, so umas drift inside; passing on corners costs extra distance; each capsule-shaped track has four corners; uphill slows umas (Power reduces the loss); downhill gives a Wit-based speed boost and HP saving; final corner spreads umas out (helps avoid blocking); a final straight needs a preceding corner (Ibis Summer Dash has none).
- **Gate blocks (S1, S3):** 8 gate blocks; extra umas fill from the outside. CM (9 runners): two in block 8; 18 runners: three in blocks 7 and 8; Stadium/Team Trials (12): two in blocks 5-8. Effects: Lucky Seven is 1/9 in CM, 3/18 (1/6) in large races, 2/12 in Stadium (S3: only a 50% chance to do something even in gate seven); Outer Gate 4/9 CM, 8/18 large, 6/12 Stadium; Inner Gate 3/9 CM, 6/18 large, 3/12 Stadium. S1 LoH: 12 runners: 25% inner, 50% outer, Lucky Seven 16.6%.
- **Initial lane:** `(HorseIndex + Adjustor) × HorseLane`: adjustor 0 for Ooi/Kawasaki/Funabashi; 1.86 if gate ≥15 for Longchamp; 0.6 if gate ≥10 on other JRA courses.

## 2.7 Skill mechanics (S2, S1, S3)

- **Activation chance:** many skills need a pre-race Wit check; formula in 2.3. Green skills skip the check (S1). Activation is checked before the phase updates (so `phase==1&corner==0` can activate on the frame she enters late-race).
- **Duration and cooldown scale with distance:** `Duration = BaseDuration × CourseDistance/1000`; `Cooldown = BaseCooldown × CourseDistance/1000`.
- **Random-position conditions (S2):** `x_random` picks a 10 m segment before the race starts and activates when other conditions are met on that segment. `straight_random`: first randomly picks a straight (equal chance regardless of length) then a 10 m segment. `corner_random`: if the race passes the same corner multiple times, activates on the last lap. `all_corner_random`: up to 4 triggers placed as follows: all corners are candidates; pick one (equal chance regardless of length); pick a 10 m segment inside it; if ≥10 m remains after the trigger, the candidate is replaced by that remainder, else discarded; remove all candidate corners before the selected; repeat until 4 triggers or none left. `phase_corner_random`: all corner segments of the phase are stitched together, so chance is proportional to length.
- **remain_distance:** computed as course distance (integer) minus current position rounded down, so `remain_distance>=399` can trigger at ≥398.000001.
- **order_rate:** converted to `order` by rounding to nearest (9 runners, order_rate>50 → 4.5 ≈ 5 → order>5, i.e. 6th or worse). `order_rate_inXX_continue` requires staying in the top XX% until activation; the first 5 seconds don't count.
- **near_count:** near if |DistanceGap| < 3 m and |LaneGap| < 3 HorseLane (was 1.5 m and 1.5 lanes before the 1st anniversary).
- **is_surrounded:** true if all three directions have another uma (one uma can fill several): Out |gap|<1.5 m and 0<LaneGap<3 lanes; Front 0<gap<3.0 m and |LaneGap|<1.5 lanes; Behind −3.0<gap<0 and |LaneGap|<1.5 lanes.
- **behind_near_lane_time / infront_near_lane_time:** closely behind if |DistanceGap| < 2.5 m and |LaneGap| < 1 lane; checked against the uma one place ahead/behind; timer resets when placement changes.
- **activate_count_x:** skills are checked in ID order; a lower ID can trigger a higher ID in the same frame, not vice versa.
- **Targets:** skills don't distinguish friend and foe (teammates can trigger and be targeted and count toward max targets) but teammates are excluded from debuff effects.
- **Skill level scaling (S2):** Lv 1-10: Target speed 1.00, 1.01, 1.04, 1.07, 1.10, 1.13, 1.16, 1.19, 1.22, 1.25; Accel 1.00, 1.02, 1.04, 1.06, 1.08, 1.10, 1.125, 1.15, 1.175, 1.20; Stat 1.00, 1.01, 1.02, 1.03, 1.04, 1.05, 1.06, 1.07, 1.08, 1.10; Everything else (recovery, current speed, lane move) 1.00, 1.02, 1.04, 1.06, 1.08, 1.10, 1.12, 1.14, 1.16, 1.18.

## 2.8 Lane, blocking, vision, overtaking

- **Units:** lane = distance from the inner fence in course widths; 1 course width = 11.25 m; 1 horse lane = 1/18 course width. Min lane distance 0; max differs by course: Tokyo turf 1.5 course width (widest), the three dirt courses Sapporo/Hakodate/Niigata 1.1 (narrowest). Max may change in a race (Nakayama 2000m, some Hanshin distances); effect unclear.
- **Lane change speed:** `TargetSpeed = 0.02 × (0.3 + 0.001×Power) × FirstMoveLanePointModifier × OrderModifier`; first-move-lane-point modifier (early/mid, before the move lane point) `1 + LaneDistance/MaxLaneDistance × 0.05`; order modifier (late/last spurt) `1 + Order × 0.01`. Current lane speed accelerates at `0.02×1.5` and resets to 0 at target lane; actual speed `clamp(CurrentSpeed + SkillModifier, 0, 0.6) × DirectionModifier`; DirectionModifier 1 moving out and `1 + LaneDistance` when moving in (faster inward). If blocked in the desired direction the lane doesn't change but current lane speed keeps building.
- **Target lane** updates when less than 0.5 horse lane from the target, or when blocked on the side she tries to move. Three modes: normal, fixed, overtake.
- **Overtake targets:** visible umas 1-20 m ahead whose distance gap ÷ speed gap < 15 s (catchable within 15 s) and either with lower target speed than yours, or blocked with lower current speed than your target speed; the closest blocker in front is automatically a target.
- **Normal mode** (no targets, or within 200 m of the move-lane point in early/mid): 1) out of HP: keep current lane; 2) position keep pace-down: target lane 0.18; 3) during/after the final corner if the extra move lane is outside: go to it if not blocked; 4) early/mid: current lane − 0.05 if not blocked; 5) mid and <1.75 horse lane from the inside uma: target 2.0 lanes from that uma; 6) otherwise straight.
- **Overtake mode:** entered with targets; stays 1.5 s after all targets are lost. Candidates: for each target, find the innermost and outermost uma of its crowd (0 ≤ DistanceGap ≤ 3.0, 0 < LaneGap < 2 lanes); each inner/outer uma's lane ±1 horse lane is a candidate if there is no visible uma within OwnerDistance ≤ Distance ≤ Inner/OutermostDistance + 0.5 and CandidateLane ± 0.8 lane. Extra candidate: early/mid and ≥1 lane from the fence: 1 lane in; else straight. Discard candidates with no lane room. Score = `|CandidateLane − LaneDistance| × PhaseCoef`: early In 1.0/Out 100.0; mid In 1.0/Out 1.0; late/spurt In 1.0/Out 1.15; lowest wins; if none good, stay. If the accepted candidate is inside the extra move lane and there's room, use the extra lane.
- **Fixed mode:** skill-specified lane while not blocked toward it.
- **Extra move lane (final corner lane):** on entering the final corner `clamp(LaneDistance/0.1, 0, 1) × 0.5 + random(0.1)` (before the 1st anniversary it activated on the final straight).
- **Front blocking:** 0 < DistanceGap < 2 m and |LaneGap| ≤ (1.0 − 0.6×gap/2 m) × 0.75 horse lane (0.3 lane at 2 m, 0.75 at 0 m); lowest gap is the blocker; speed limited to `(0.988 + 0.012×gap/2m) × SpeedBlockFront`. **Side blocking:** |DistanceGap| < 1.05 m and |LaneGap| < 2 lanes; the lowest lane gap sets available space. Skills requiring "all sides blocked" mean front plus at least one side. **Overlap:** |DistanceGap| < 0.4 m and |LaneGap| < 0.4 lane; the outer uma is bumped 0.4 lane away (triggers a target-lane update).
- **Vision:** visible if DistanceGap ≤ VisibleDistance and |LaneGap| ≤ ((DistanceGap/VisibleDistance)×11.5 + 2 lanes)/2; visible distance starts at 20 m and is modified only by skills; the cone's max width is constant (S1: vision skills do nothing unless you've been debuffed, due to the hard cap).

## 2.9 Position keeping, temptation and mid-race mechanics

- **Start delay:** random up to 0.1 s (not affected by Wit). >0.08 s is a late start; <0.02 s gives a start-dash score bonus in team stadium. Skills that fix the delay: アタシに指図しないで！！ (0.085) and 超遊び癖 (0.1). Skills that multiply it: Concentration ×0.4, Focus ×0.9, Gatekept ×1.5. If delay < 1 frame, acceleration and HP are unaffected and first-frame distance is `SpeedAfterAccel × (1 frame − StartDelay)`; if > 1 frame, no acceleration/HP/distance for that frame (loss of acceleration is significant and can cause blocking); 1 frame (0.066 s) to 0.08 s gives no late-start warning.
- **Position keeping** affects target speed in sections 1-10. Five modes besides normal. Normal mode: no modifier; every 2 s a check enters non-normal modes (entry condition + Wit check for specific modes); exits when the exit condition is met or after running 1 section (3 for Oonige; section length floored before multiplying); 1 s cooldown after exit (3 s total to the next check).
- **Pacemaker:** non-front modes are set by distance from the pacemaker; exact selection unclear (range parameter 10.0, count parameter 2). Before the 1.5 anniversary the pacemaker was the first-place uma among the most forward strategy.
- **Front Runner:** Speed Up: ×1.04, enter if first and < 4.5 m (Front) / 17.5 m (Oonige) ahead of the uma behind (12.5 m if she's the only front runner, post 1.5th anniv.); pass Wit check chance `20 × log10(0.1×Wiz)%`; exit when > 4.5 m (Front)/17.5 m ahead of 2nd (12.5 m if exactly 1 front runner excluding Oonige and rushed-style changes). Overtake mode: ×1.05, enter if not first within own strategy and pass Wit check; exit when 10 m (27.5 m Oonige) ahead of 2nd of the same strategy.
- **Non-front:** thresholds (CourseFactor = 0.0008×(CourseLength − 1000) + 1.0): Pace Chaser min 3.0 m, max 5.0×CF; Late Surger min 6.5×CF, max 7.0×CF; End Closer min 7.5×CF, max 8.0×CF. Pace Up: ×1.04, enter above max distance from pacemaker with Wit check `15×log10(0.1×Wiz)%`, exit below a random value between thresholds. Pace Down: ×0.945 (mid-race after 1.5th anniversary; ×0.915 otherwise), enter when below min distance and no target/current-speed-up skill active; exit when above a random value between thresholds (after 1.5th anniv. in mid-race the max is replaced with `lerp(Min, Max, 0.5)`), or when a skill raises current or target speed (debuffs used to also remove pace down; fixed 2022-08-09). **Pace Up Ex:** ×2.0 (post 1.5th anniv.), prioritised over all other position-keep modes: entry for front runners when another uma whose strategy should be behind is ahead; for others when the pacemaker's strategy should be behind her; exit when no such uma is ahead.
- **Rushed / Kakari / Temptation:** rolled before the race; chance `(6.5 / log10(0.1×Wiz + 1))²%`, effectively rounded up to the next integer percent by the RNG: 300 Wit 19.00%, 600 → 13.26 → 14%, 900 → 11.01 → 12%, 1200 → 9.74 → 10%; 自制心 reduces by a flat 3% (19% → 16%). S3 table: 200 24.17%, 300 19%, 400 16.24%, 500 14.49%, 600 13.26%, 700 12.32%. Occurs in a random section 2-9 (S3: mostly random point in the first half). HP consumption ×1.6, forces the AI to change position-keeping strategy and succeed in all position-keep Wit rolls (it doesn't change strategy coefficient for base target speed). Conversions: Front Runners enter Speed Up if someone is close behind; Pace Chasers become runners; Late Surgers 75% Front/25% Pace; End Closers 70% Front/20% Pace/10% Late. Every 3 s while rushed 55% to snap out; ends by 12 s; debuffs that worsen Rushed set a fixed 5 s timer (no exit rolls).
- **Spot Struggle / Lead Competition (位置取り争い):** may trigger once any uma has run 150 m, between ≥2 front runners (or ≥2 Oonige; a regular Front doesn't struggle against an Oonige) where one hasn't reached section 6 and they're close to the frontmost of their strategy (DistanceGap < 3.75 m, LaneGap < 0.165 course width); can't trigger if the frontmost is overtaken by an uma of a strategy that should be behind. Effect: `TargetSpeed += (500×Guts)^0.6 × 0.0001` m/s for `(700×Guts)^0.5 × 0.012 × StrategyProficiencyModifier` s; leaves early if >5.0 m ahead/behind or >0.416 course width apart laterally; always ends at section 9; HP consumption raised (see 2.4).
- **Dueling / Compete Fight (追い比べ):** on the final straight; candidates within 3.0 m and 0.25 course width for 2 s, both ≥15% HP, at least one in top 50% and one entered the final straight, neither has completed an earlier duel. Effect: `TargetSpeed += (200×Guts)^0.708 × 0.0001` m/s, `Accel += (160×Guts)^0.59 × 0.0001` m/s². Multi-uma duels allowed (a new uma can join by qualifying against any active member; multiple groups per race). Exit: HP <5% or >5 m from every other member; an uma that has exited can still keep others going but not admit new members.
- **Conserve Power / Release (足を貯める / 脚色十分), JP:** power stat used `= BasePower + 0.5×max(RawPower − 1200, 0) + SkillModifier` (both halved past 1200, so >1200 is almost 1:1 but motivation interacts differently); requires >1200 Power; credit only in the first 10 sections; per second gains `PositionKeepCoef × StyleCoef`: position-keep coef 4.2 (normal) or 6.7 (pace down); style coef Front 1.0, Pace 0.75, Late 1.0, End 0.7. Blocked when in Speed Up, Overtake, Pace Up or Pace Up Ex; Rushed; Spot Struggle; each of these imposes a 1.5 s cooldown on exit; entering Spot Struggle multiplies conserved power by 0.95 and Rushed by 0.8. On entering the last spurt, if enough power has been conserved, acceleration is increased from the next frame: strategy-distance coefficient (Short/Mile/Mid/Long): Front 1.0/1.0/1.0/1.0; Pace 0.7/0.8/0.9/0.9; Late 0.75/0.7/0.875/1.0; End 0.7/0.75/0.86/0.9; activity coefficient 1.0 × 0.98 if the uma entered Spot Struggle × 0.8 if she was Rushed. Duration `= ConservedPower × sqrt(CourseDistance) × DistanceTypeCoef / 1450` (Short 0.45, Mile 1.0, Mid 0.875, Long 0.8); no release if duration < 0.2 s.
- **Compete Before Spurt (位置取り調整), from parameter file, less confirmed:** between sections 11-15; every 2 s a check (too far from the lead, scaling with Wit, or other umas nearby, scaling with Guts and counts of nearby umas/same-strategy umas); success → competition mode: speeds up at HP cost for 2 s then 1 s cooldown; distance-from-lead thresholds (scale with course distance): Oonige 0, Nige 0, Senkou 2.5, Sashi 5.0, Oikomi 10.0. `TargetSpeedIncrease = ((Power/1500)^0.5 × 2.0 + (Guts/3000)^0.2) × 0.1 × StrategyCoef`; strategy coefficient Oonige 0.2?, see below. Stamina consumption `20 × (StrategyCoef × DistanceCoef + NearFactor)`: strategy coef Oonige 1.5, Nige 1.2, Senkou/Sashi/Oikomi 1.0; course distance coef: <1401 0.3, <1801 0.3 [sic], <2101 0.5, <2201 0.8, <2401 1.0, <2601 1.1, ≥2601 1.2; NearFactor 0.5 if the uma is NOT far from first (triggered by nearby umas only). A front runner without another same-strategy uma within 10 m (there's an unexplained 20% threshold) gets the bonus multiplied: Oonige 2.0, Nige 1.1 (extraction of a table with values Oonige 0.2 / Nige 0.8 / Senkou 1.0 / Sashi 1.0 / Oikomi 1.0 for another coefficient row is preserved in S2 but its column heading was lost in the PDF text).
- **Stamina Keep (持久力温存), speculative:** the uma tries to conserve a random HP amount 1.035-1.04× the amount needed to finish. Every 2 s she checks if she has enough; if not, chance `30% × (Wisdom/1000 + Wisdom^0.03)` to notice and enter stamina-keep mode, during which she doesn't participate in the competition (packet-capture test: 93/100 immediate activations at 1000 Wit, higher than the formula predicts). Activating an HP recovery resets the stamina-keeping state.
- **Secure Lead (リード確保), from parameter file:** between sections 11-15; occurs when her lead against an uma that should be further behind by strategy is smaller than desirable. Every 2 s if the lead is less than desirable, 20% chance (should scale with Wit; unknown) to try. `DesirableLead = StrategyCoef + 0.0003×(CourseDistance + 1000)` m; strategy coef table (rows = own strategy, columns = opponent): Oonige vs Nige 2.0, Senkou 7.0, Sashi 8.0, Oikomi 8.0; Nige vs Senkou 4.0, Sashi 8.0, Oikomi 8.0; Senkou vs Sashi 5.0, Oikomi 6.0; Sashi vs Oikomi 3.0. Effect: speed up 2 s at HP cost then 1 s cooldown: `TargetSpeedIncrease = (Guts/2000)^0.5 × 0.3 × StrategyCoef` m/s with strategy coef Oonige 0.2, Nige 1.0, Senkou 1.0, Sashi 0.8; stamina consumption `20 × StrategyCoef × CourseDistanceCoef` with strategy coef Oonige 1.2, Nige 1.0, Senkou 0.8, Sashi 0.8 and the same course-distance table; if a nige uma has no other same-strategy uma within 10 m (20% threshold) her bonus is multiplied: Oonige 7.0, Nige 2.0 (4.0 after the 5.0 anniversary).
- **Stamina Limit Break (スタミナ勝負), from parameter file:** if base Stamina + skills > 1200, an extra target-speed buff on reaching max spurt speed until the end: `TargetSpeedBuff = sqrt(Stamina − 1200) × 0.0085 × DistanceFactor × RandomFactor` m/s. DistanceFactor: <2101m 0×; <2201 0.5×; <2401 1.0×; <2601 1.5× (1.2× before 2024-10-29); ≥2601 1.8× (1.5× before). Random factor: table type 0 (50%) 0.98-1.00; type 1 (30%) 0.95-0.98; type 2 (20%) 1.00-1.02 (`TargetSpeedRandomTableChangeProbabilityByPower: 500`).
- **Zenkai Spurt (全開スパート), JP 5th anniversary:** with >2000 Speed and enough HP, after reaching target speed during the last spurt she continuously gains additional (target?) speed; the max gain scales with Speed; acceleration differs from the usual formula and scales with Power; affected by skills designed for it (ID 48) at about 10% of listed value; unaffected by normal accel skills or conserve/release; tests show the acceleration continues until the finish or until HP runs out; `Accel ≈ 0.068 × AccelFromPower / (CourseDistance/1000)^1.5 + SkillModifier`; running out of HP drops her to minimum speed (bug, confirmed).
- **Wiz Limit Break Buff (JP 5th anniversary):** see 2.10.
- **Finish time:** the last frame is interpolated for sub-frame precision. Displayed time = ActualTime × 1.18, clamped by lower/upper bounds (e.g. Tokyo turf 2400m lower bound 2:21.6; breaking it displays the bound ± up to 1 s). Margin conversion uses scaled time difference × 15 m/s: Nose 0.2 m, Head 0.4 m, Neck 0.8 m.

## 2.11 Course events and simulation notes

- Each course has 1001 keyframes (1000 equal segments) with 3D coordinates and a rotation quaternion; position at Lane=0 is lerp between keyframes; lane offset moves outside by 11.25 m per course width (left of rotation for clockwise, right for counter-clockwise). Ratio of course distance to world transform (horizontal) at lane origin between 0 m and 1 m (using 3D distance) gives the base ratio, from 0.96 to 1.06 across courses. Per frame, if the previous frame's ratio exceeds the base ratio, `DistanceAdd_world = DistanceAdd_course / max(1, ratio_prev/ratio_base)`. Running wide on a corner or diagonally therefore costs extra distance.
- Slope per second: `SlopePer = tan(slopeAngle) × 100`; uphill if SlopePer > 1.0, downhill if < −1.0. The rotation-based slope was outdated after the 1st anniversary; slopes are now Course Events (ID 11: SlopePer; Length).
- Course Event IDs: 0 Corner (number 1-4; distance), 1 Ground change (turf→dirt), 2 Straight (start/end; front type Front=1, Across=2, False straight=3), 3 Lane Max change, 4/5 First/Second BGM Landscape, 6 Jikkyo trigger (unused), 7 Move Lane Point (only Niigata 1000m; else the entry of the initial corner), 8/9 First/Second BGM, 10 Event Crowd (unused), 11 Slope.
- **Initialization order:** 1) base stats; 2) skill activation chance and whether skills can activate; 3) activate skills that fire out of the gate; 4) use item; 5) set start location by gate; 6) start delay; 7) adjusted stats; 8) stamina→HP; 9) starting speed; 10) rushed determination.
- **Frame order (incomplete, per gate order):** Pre-Update: check end of corner; Update: activate skills → recover HP by skills → update last-spurt state → update target speed → calculate acceleration → update phase → calculate distance and position → check slope; Post-Update; Events: check start of corner.
