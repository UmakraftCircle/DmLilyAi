## 6.1 Gacha rates, pity and expected results (S1, S3)

- **Rates (S1):** SSR/3★ 3%; rate-up cards/umas usually 0.75% each (two rate-ups or one: each stays 0.75%, it does not become 1.5%). You get the rate-up item about 1 in 4 times you get an SSR/3★.
- **No truly limited characters:** summer and Christmas umas can be pulled any time; some welfare cards are event-exclusive but otherwise no limited support cards. A few umas are semi-limited (released on anniversary/half-anniversary): you can't get them from normal selectors; those banners' 3★ rate is usually 4.5% (rate-up still 0.75%).
- **Spark (pity) at 200 pulls:** pick any rate-up item. To get an MLB SSR you need on average two sparks (400 pulls). Don't pull on banners without at least 200 pulls available (S1). S3 agrees: roll only with a spark ready and in multiples of 200; pity does not carry between banners.
- **Table for a single rate-up SSR (sparks included; S1):**
| Pulls | None | 0LB | 1LB | 2LB | 3LB | MLB |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 50 | 69% | 26% | 5% | 0.6% | 0.1% | 0% |
| 100 | 47% | 36% | 13% | 3% | 0.6% | 0.1% |
| 200 | 0% | 22% | 34% | 25% | 13% | 6% |
| 300 | 0% | 11% | 24% | 27% | 20% | 19% |
| 400 | 0% | 0% | 5% | 15% | 22% | 58% |
| 500 | 0% | 0% | 2% | 9% | 17% | 72% |
| 600 | 0% | 0% | 0% | 1% | 5% | 94% |
- **Banners with no rate-up** (New Year 2021, just before Anniversary 2025, Anniversary 2026): you can spark any uma; if a selector for the uma you want exists, buying it is cheaper.
- **Banner rules (S3):** an MLB card needs two pities; skill/other cards one; free-roll banners first; dual-SSR banners preferred. S3 tips: two sparks ≈ 50/50 to MLB an SSR (~400 rolls) when on a banner you're committed to; S1 says ~400 pulls on average.
- S3 guidance in the new-player section (Global): save for a spark (30,000 jewels); reroll on the current banner; "30-50 uma rolls to collect 1-2★". Dual-spark planning per S1: see 6.3.

## 6.2 Currencies (S1)

### Jewels (Carrots/Carats)
- Used mainly for gacha; energy or race-point refills are not recommended. Paid vs free jewels: paid jewels are needed for the 50-jewel daily pull and certain "scamcha" banners guaranteeing a random 3★ or SSR in 10 pulls; a source taking either type takes free first.
- **Non-renewable sources:** Missions; Trophies (first-time G3+ wins: 30 G1 × 100, 36 G2 × 50, 69 G3 × 30 = 6,870 jewels); Levels (50 jewels per level, later 75, then 100; EXP from high scores on your umas (top 3 per uma), talking to umas, seeing events in runs, etc.; collect in the Uma Directory); Stories (first four 20 jewels each, last three 50 each; first four readable during the uma's banner for 80 free jewels; main stories also unlock free SSR cards); Shop deals (1500 paid-jewel package ×2, 7500 ×3, refreshing now and then); DMM first login 500; Cygames link 300.
- **Renewable:** Daily missions 50/day; daily login 110/week; weekly Stadium rewards up to 250; monthly Circle rewards up to 3,000 (realistically 150-1,200); CM or LoH up to 3,600/month in Graded (1,940 Open; LoH gives more); Legend Races 250 each; story events (~1/month; 450 from bingo cards + 1,500 from event points + 210 from stories = 2,160); monthly pass (500 paid immediately + 50 free/day for a month); random monthly events (e.g. Tachyon's Inheritance Lab); special events (anniversary, half-anniversary, Valentine's, New Year: a few hundred to a few thousand each); G1 missions (150 per real-life G1 set); Pakalive rewards (a few hundred/month).
- **Pink tickets:** roll either umas or support cards; treat as 150 jewels each; use them on banners you are already committed to; ~200 of each type per year (story events give 2 of each; Graded A CM gives 5 of each); sold in shops monthly.
- **Income and pull strategy (S1):** about 258,000 jewels per year plus 200 of each pink ticket (2024 data; confirmed by the author's 2025 numbers); excluding uma tickets that is ~288,000 jewels or 1,920 pulls per year for support cards. Best to pull on support banners with free rolls for cards that stay relevant for months. "Golden standard": February Anniversary (+ new scenario) 280 rolls (+120 free); June new scenario 320 (+80); August half-anniversary 300 (+100); October new scenario 320 (+80); December New Year 300 (+100); that uses 1,520 of the 1,920 rolls, leaving 400 rolls (+200 uma tickets) for triple sparks, other banners or umas; occasionally single spark to free rolls. S3: F2P income about 300k carats/year (older figure; same ballpark). ⚖️ Free-roll timing: S1 New Year = December; S5: beginning of January.

### Friend Points
- Gained when others use your profile support card in a run (they rarely use cards below MLB SSR), and from PvP (5,000 for staying in Class 6, 4,000 in Class 5), plus Stadium: each race gives 1/10,000 of your uma's points, usually enough to clear the monthly Friend Point shop. Shop: older event cards, the **Haru Urara** card early, pink tickets (150 jewels each), hint books; sometimes Pieces (~20,000 points for 50).

### Trainer Medals
- Sources: rental rewards 10 each time someone uses your profile uma as a parent (up to 100/day); monthly Circle rank; Circle item requests (5 points per item up to 100/day) and daily Circle missions; Stadium races, weekly rewards and first-time class promotions; Daily Races up to 30/day; Daily Legend Race 20 per race; training run completion 200 each; run race rewards 10 per race up to 100 per run.
- Main uses (JP): SSR limit break crystal fragment (8,000 medals) and random 3★ uma ticket (15,000 medals), buyable monthly; limit breaking story welfare SSRs (extra copy prices 100, 900, 2,000, 3,000); Pieces sometimes at 600 per 5 (up to ten times). Priorities (S1 JP): Rhein Kraft > Special Week > Silence Suzuka 3LB = Mejiro McQueen 3LB > Narita Brian 3LB > Special Week MLB ≫ Winning Ticket ≫ Rice Shower; Team Sirius is awkward since most scenarios have a Friend/Group card. ⚖️ S3 (Global): Mejiro McQueen 3LB (Long) > Narita Brian 3LB > Rice Shower 2LB (debuffers) > MLB Brian/McQueen > Winning Ticket.

### Clovers, Horseshoes, Statues, Blue tickets
- **Clovers:** 1 per pull when a banner changes (no clovers for the 200 pulls of a spark); also from converting extra Pieces once 1★/2★ umas are maxed; buy pink tickets.
- **Horseshoes/Cleats:** from destroying duplicate support cards after maxing them; buy pink tickets and hint books; rainbow horseshoes mostly from extra welfare copies. S3: one SR ticket per month per Cleat type plus two R and 1★ tickets; never destroy the Urara Guts SSR.
- **Statues:** from duplicate umas; exchange for Pieces at 1:1, then 2:1 after 25, 3:1 after another 25, capping at 5:1 (first 100 Pieces cost 250 Statues, each additional Piece 5). Use sparingly, typically when ≤25 Pieces from a star upgrade. Totals: to 4★ (200 Pieces) 750 (no bonus pieces), 450 (off-banner 3★, 60 Pieces), 300 (rate-up 3★, 90 Pieces); to 5★ (300 Pieces) 2,250 / 1,950 / 1,800; from 4★ (Legend Race) to 5★ 1,250.
- **Blue tickets:** from the training pass checkpoints; 10 tickets = 1 card from the ticket shop (dated cards; drip-fed additions).
- **Shoes / Banners / Dream Glitter:** level up umas to unlock skills bought in training. Shoes from G3+ races by category (Pink Short, Green Mile, Orange Mid, Blue Long, Red Dirt); banners from winning the race named on the banner (usually from mandatory races; obtainable from other currencies); Dream Glitter as a random URA Finals drop and event rewards, buyable in shops.
- **Hint books:** permanently raise the discount of skills the uma starts with (e.g. from level-ups); Green books for white skills, Gold books for gold skills; up to Hint Level 3 (10% discount per level). Cost: White L1 4 books + 5 Dream Glitter; L2 8 + 10; L3 6 Big Books + 15. Gold L1 8 + 20; L2 16 + 40; L3 12 Big Books + 60. Sources: Friend Point, Trainer Medal, Horseshoe shops (monthly), events, anniversary shops. Only do this for umas you run in PvP, ideally several; prefer skills you use often (Mejiro Dober's Betweener Straights over her Kyoto green; repeatable skills like Betweener Straights can be bought twice, so the discount counts twice).
- **Limit Break crystals:** yellow for SR, rainbow for SSR; 20 fragments = 1 crystal at the shop; fragments come from events (4-6 per month) so a crystal every 4-5 months.
- **Passes (S1):** monthly training pass (premium comes with 350 paid jewels); points per turn (stats 100%, skill points 50%, 5% of displayed stat sum on completion; rounded up). Clearing the pass: support card tickets 5 + 20 = 25; free jewels 500 + 1,350 = 1,850; uma gacha tickets 2 + 2 = 4; support gacha tickets 2 + 2 = 4; rainbow crystal fragment 0 + 1. Separate monthly parenting passes: one gives extra rerolls of the end-of-run factors; one lets you lock a factor beforehand (usually the distance factor). The 5th-anniversary subscription (Uma Plan) is in Part 4, 4.8.

## 6.3 Circles / Clubs (S1, S3)

- Guild equivalent: monthly reward for being in one; trade shoes with members for Trainer Medals; no downside, so join asap. Circle rank is by monthly fan gain; serious circles have monthly fan requirements (e.g. 30,000,000 per month), tracked by leaders on spreadsheets (not visible in game). **You must be in the circle ≥2 weeks to receive rewards**; if leaving for a better circle with <2 weeks left in the month wait for the month to end. (Fan farming method: Part 1, 1.14.) Club Points (Global) buy hints/shoes (S3).

## 6.4 Legend Races, Daily Legend, Trainer Exchange, Stories (S1)

- **Legend Race:** race against a specific uma on a track where her real-life counterpart did well; close to the real G1 schedule; each lasts three days and usually three back to back. Rewards: 40,000 Money, 15,000 Support Points, 250 Jewels, 79-88 Pieces for the Legend uma; limit break fragments from doing many. Each has a unique trophy (collect with 9 different umas for self-satisfaction).
- **Daily Legend Races:** one of each per day for a Piece of that uma; 150 Pieces obtain the uma; sometimes two per day; a bit under 5 months to acquire an uma from nothing; first clear all undone Legend Races for first-completion jewels; target new umas rather than star upgrades. Button states: no text = already 5★; piece icon first = enough to upgrade/obtain; number after "才" = Pieces needed for the next star. Parent-worthy umas obtainable this way (S1): Oguri Cap (non-Long tracks for non-Runners), Seiun Sky (mandatory for Runners on all Mid and all but one Mile track), Mejiro Dober (backliners on most Medium and some Mile), Taiki Shuttle (Leaders on some Mile/Medium), Maruzensky (specific tracks), Narita Brian (generic for non-Runners), Nishino Flower (some Mile/Medium), Kitasan Black (Runners, mostly Long), Seiun Sky (Ballroom) (Runners, mostly Long), Fuji Kiseki (generic speed parent for non-Short), TM Opera O (New Year) (generic speed, Medium/Long), Gold Ship (Summer) (ideal for all Stadium umas).
- **Trainer Exchange (JP):** weekly; can send four umas for increased rewards (money, support points, some Pieces); umas are lost forever but it is more rewarding than selling from the hall of fame; requirements are an aptitude/stat threshold (e.g. Mid Distance B+).
- **Story events (roulette):** train to get event points and tokens; spin the roulette to fill a bingo card (reward per line); after 25 spins missing rewards are guaranteed; multi-spin options (until a line, until all 8 lines, 10 spins); the first three bingo cards give a welfare SSR copy each (later cards infinite but worse). To "complete" the event finish three bingo cards and gain 1,500,000 event points (enough to MLB the SSR, read the story and get all jewels). Three SSR uncap fragments at 920,000 points; 20 fragments limit break any SSR. Bonus event points from related umas/cards: MLB R 12%, MLB SR 18%, MLB SSR 25%; banner SSRs 45%; welfare 35%; new umas 10%/12%/15% at 3/4/5★; old umas 1%/3%/5%/7%/10% per star level; highest bonus applies; option to double TP to double EP and tokens.

## 6.5 Other JP events (S1)

- **Monthly Match:** not rerun since introduction; a month of fixed race conditions across 5 categories (Regular Races, weekly rotation); 12 umas with up to 11 player-trained opponents as you progress through Classes 1-5; RP entries (weekday 3 free daily, weekend 5); distance aptitude A is auto-raised to S; motivation randomised below class 8 (fixed max otherwise); items only below class 8; class points by placement/opponent strength; Finale Race by your highest category; rentals (max limit break cards from the support exchange shop; 3 uses/day; occupy one of five personal slots).
- **Trainer Skills Test:** accompanies a new scenario (every four months); exams scored on races won, stats, skills; aptitude below A penalises (0.4× at G); S gives no benefit; "free" exams don't penalise; trial coins shop (450 jewels, SR and SSR LB fragments, three gacha tickets each of umas and support, hint books; total 48,000 coins); top-30 umas can be rented free as parents.
- **Racing Carnival:** PvE like Legend Races using RP; four "HARD" races (umas ~600 stats) fill the bar; two Hard races unlock a Very Hard or Extreme race (3× Very Hard in a row guarantees Extreme); retry gets +25% all stats; Carnival Points awarded like Stadium; top five yields a **Carnival Factor** (free guaranteed inherited Carnival Skill with level loops: level-1 inheritance → level-2 factor → ... effects vary per event; e.g. stats); 100,000 points clears it (two SSR LB shards and much of the shop); ~1,500 points per race (~1,000 Hard, ~2,000 Very Hard); ~119 RP in a 7-day event; Extreme opponents ~900 stats and four golds; tracks vary so check greens.
- **Scouting Event:** race players like Stadium; scout one of their umas with coins (the top uma is the one you take, the bottom the one you give up); choose a captain from your trained umas (change every race to fill the umadex); the track changes every 3 races (odd ones like Short Dirt); a new uma gives +15 to a stat and more scout coins; pairs give green skills when raced together; reach team rank SS for all rewards; save coins to scout Uf umas.
- **Uma Strolls:** main-menu option: +15 affection each time.
- **Tachyon's Inheritance Research:** fill a bar by runs (40%-400% each by how far you get; double TP doubles it), Stadium/Daily races 30%, Daily Legend Race 35%; three slips raise the ★ of a Blue, Pink or Green factor on an uma; use before the reward period ends (cannot hold).
- **Master's Challenge:** PvE on a couple of G1s with fixed conditions vs stronger opponents; first win unlocks the next difficulty; only first-time-win rewards (jewels, limit break fragments, a title for the final difficulty); items usable; almost full gates favour frontliners; any hall-of-fame uma can race up to 3 times on a single difficulty.
- **Oonige tech and lane tech (S1):** In Global Oonige often denies Runners their "first place" accels like Angling x Scheming; in JP less important: Oonige forces other Runners/Leaders further from the backline, giving frontlines a better chance, so they are often paired with other Runners/Leaders. With the anniversary buffs lane movement skills raise target speed while changing lanes; **Danger Avoidance** (Runner lane skill, immediate major lane change) stacks with Position Sense/Aoharu Int; you need only one other lane skill; Aoharu Int is better if affordable.

## 6.6 Uma collection, stars and levels (S1, S3)

- Rarity 1★/2★/3★ (R/SR/SSR-equivalent for umas). Stars via Pieces; you can't "star up" a past run. Collection screen shows stats; the Stories screen lets you read stories as bond rises; Profile gender affects some lines (e.g. Dober is ruder to male trainers); birthday lines; "Key item" unlocks all lines.
- The SSRs you get from the main story can be limit broken with Trainer Medals. A new uma's first four story parts can be read during the banner (free gems).

## 6.7 New player and first-month plan (S1, S3; partly in Part 1, 1.15)

- Reroll, then prioritise spending on support cards; save the 3★ pick ticket until missions are done; use rental decks early; link the Cygames ID; join a Circle; welfare selector picks per Part 3.7; don't buy from random shops except clocks (S3); do each graded race once for first-time jewels; mark protected parents; stadium team of five per category.
- **Coming from Global (S1):** wait for a free-pull event to reroll; then save jewels for the next scenario banner; use the latest scenario; Global and JP saves use the same folder on PC (use an emulator for both); free-roll events happen at least every four months.
- **Link Password:** make it early; Cygames ID link = 300 jewels (S1).

## 6.8 Training-run screen facts (S1)

- Resting raises Energy, dating raises Mood, racing raises Fans, training raises Stats. You can suspend a run and return; abandoning deletes it. Conditions (bad ones) are visible in the Status screen; the hospital is available for bad conditions.
- Relevant sections: Telling Time (turn calendar), Fan Goals, Optional Races, Conditions.

MLB = max limit break (4LB); Ult = unique skill; 0LB-4LB; agemasen; Spark (pity); Sparking = picking the rate-up after 200 pulls; Scamcha = banner guaranteeing a 3★/SSR in 10 pulls (paid jewels only); Uma Plan; Uma Pieces; Trainer Medals; Clovers; Horseshoes/Cleats; Statues; Blue tickets; Pink tickets; Hint books; Dream Glitter; Parfait; RP (race points, Stadium resource); TP (training points); SP (skill points); EP (event points); CM (Champions Meeting); LoH (League of Heroes); GP/GGP (grandparent/great-grandparent); MB/TB/FB (motivation/training/friendship bonus).
