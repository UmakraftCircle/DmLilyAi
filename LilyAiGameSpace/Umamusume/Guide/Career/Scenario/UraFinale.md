**The Beginning: URA Finale** (新設！URAファイナルズ, Shisensetsu! URA Finals!) is a playable [scenario](https://umamusu.wiki/Game:Career_Mode "Game:Career Mode") in the [_Umamusume: Pretty Derby_ mobile game](https://umamusu.wiki/Umamusume:_Pretty_Derby_\(mobile_game\) "Umamusume: Pretty Derby (mobile game)"). It was the 1st scenario introduced with the game's launch, before being updated on December 12, 2022 (July 1, 2026 in Global Version) to expand on existing features and adding new mechanics.

Story
-----



Director [Yayoi Akikawa](https://umamusu.wiki/Yayoi_Akikawa "Yayoi Akikawa") has identified a deficit in [Tracen Academy](https://umamusu.wiki/Tracen_Academy "Tracen Academy")'s offerings: [Umamusume](https://umamusu.wiki/Umamusume "Umamusume") frequently have their potential to stand on a large stage squandered when their aptitudes do not overlap with the year's most famous races. Therefore, she invents the URA Finale: an all-new event covering every track and distance.

The player works with Director Akikawa, [Tazuna Hayakawa](https://umamusu.wiki/Tazuna_Hayakawa "Tazuna Hayakawa"), [Etsuko Otonashi](https://umamusu.wiki/Etsuko_Otonashi "Etsuko Otonashi"), and a new friend, trainer [Aoi Kiryuin](https://umamusu.wiki/Aoi_Kiryuin "Aoi Kiryuin"), to prepare for the URA Finale and bring it to life amidst trials and tribulations. The player and Trainer Kiryuin evolve as trainers together over the course of the scenario, as Kiryuin learns to open herself up to her trainee, [Happy Meek](https://umamusu.wiki/Happy_Meek "Happy Meek").

[Happy Meek](https://umamusu.wiki/Happy_Meek "Happy Meek") has a random chance to show up as a support on [stat training](https://umamusu.wiki/Game:Stat_Training "Game:Stat Training"). Her appearance is indicated by an orange icon on the training button and on the [facility](https://umamusu.wiki/Game:Stat_Training#Facility "Game:Stat Training") in which she is present.

When a training facility with Happy Meek has been selected, an event will occur that prompts the player to select one of three options. The player has the choice to compete with Happy Meek in one of the 5 different stat categories, or energy. One of the three options will always be same stat as the selected facility. The remaining two options are randomly selected.

The likelihood of winning the duel is indicated by the corresponding probability indicator.

Winning the duel against Happy Meek will provide a reward and raise Happy Meek's level, increasing the difficulty of subsequent duels.

### Finale Season: URA Finale

[![](https://umamusu.wiki/w/thumb.php?f=Happy_Meek_Boss.jpg&width=158)](/File:Happy_Meek_Boss.jpg)

Happy Meek during the URA Finale Finals

After three years of training, 6 extra turns are provided for the URA Finale. During this period, random after training events, condition events and Support Card events will not appear.

There are 3 races of the URA Finale. The Qualifier, the Semifinal, and the Finals. Before each race, 1 turn of training is provided. Each race must be won to advance to the next race or the scenario will end with a normal ending for the trainee.

The races used for the URA Finale is based on the most commonly ran distance type during the career playthrough, with ties favouring the shortest distance and differing track types counting separately. 5 different trophies can be obtained, 4 for every distance type for races on turf and 1 for any distance on dirt.

Happy Meek will appear in the URA Finale Finals. If Happy Meek is on maximum level after her duels in this scenario, she will appear in the URA Finale Finals with stronger stats. Winning against this Happy Meek will improve the awards and increase the odds of receiving the URA Finale spark at the end of the run.

Every scenario will feature the URA Finale and the 3 events associated with winning each race, unless it is overridden by the scenario. Unlike other scenarios, this one has different dialogue for the URA Finale.
