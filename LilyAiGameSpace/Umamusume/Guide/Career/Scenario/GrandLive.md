The Blue Sparks gained from Inheritance also raise the Stat caps, depending on the Spark rating. These are the Stat uncaps gained at the start of the career:

|------------|----------|

|3*          |+16       |

|9*          |+48       |


During Inheritance Events, the Stat uncaps gained are random.

Inheriting a parent's Unique Skill via green Sparks also raises the Stat caps (but does not award direct Stats), depending on the parent's Stat bonuses. For example: Oguri Cap has a bonus in Speed and Power, and inheriting Triumphant Pulse from her awards an additional Stat uncap in those two Stats. Only three stat caps can be raised this way, even if the parent has four different stat bonuses.

Points [](#points)
-------------------

The currency used to buy Lessons is Performance points. There are five different kinds of Points:

![](https://uma.guide/icon/gl-tokens/utx_ico_performance_s_00.png)Dance, ![](https://uma.guide/icon/gl-tokens/utx_ico_performance_s_01.png)Passion, ![](https://uma.guide/icon/gl-tokens/utx_ico_performance_s_02.png)Vocal, ![](https://uma.guide/icon/gl-tokens/utx_ico_performance_s_03.png)Visual, and ![](https://uma.guide/icon/gl-tokens/utx_ico_performance_s_05.png)Composure.

Your current Performance Points are displayed in the upper left side of the screen.

![](https://uma.guide/img/guides/article/grand_concert/gc_pointbar.png)

Every Training has a primary Performance Points associated with it. A Training yields the primary Performance Point type 60% of the time and the secondary Performance Point type 30% of the time. The remaining 10% is split randomly among the other three Point types.


|Training|Primary Point Type|Secondary Point Type|
|--------|------------------|--------------------|
|Speed   |                  |                    |
|Stamina |                  |                    |
|Power   |                  |                    |
|Guts    |                  |                    |
|Wit     |                  |                    |


Friendship Training gives you two types of Performance Points. One is determined by the Training you perform, while the other is very likely to be the Performance Point type you have the fewest of at the start of the turn.

![The Rainbow Training here awards both Dance and Passion Performance points](https://uma.guide/img/guides/article/grand_concert/gc_training_rainbow.png)

The Rainbow Training here awards both Dance and Passion Performance points

![pal](https://uma.guide/icon/type/pal.svg)Light Hello's Training Together event has a 45% chance to grant 20 additional Points of the type you currently have the least of.

Each Performance Point type has an initial cap of 200.

### Performance points Calculation [](#performance-points-calculation)

The exact number of Performance Points you receive depends on the Facility level and the number of Support Cards on the Facility, and is increased by Scenario-linked characters.

This is the formula:

PerformanceToken\=⌊(S+F)×1.15C+2L⌋

S: Base value determined by the Training type (Wit = 5, others = 9)  
F: Facility level (1 for level 1, 2 for level 2, and so on)  
C: Number of Support Cards on the Facility  
L: Number of Scenario-link Cards on the Facility

Examples: A Speed Training on a Level 1 Facility with no Supports gives 10 Performance Points.  
The same Training with two Support Cards gives 13 Performance Points.  
If one of those Support Cards is a Scenario-linked character, it would be 15 Performance Points.

Lessons [](#lessons)
---------------------

When you open the Shop, you will be offered three Lessons to buy. They do not expire. Once you buy one Lesson, three new ones will appear.

![](https://uma.guide/img/guides/article/grand_concert/gc_ui_button.webp)

If you do not have enough Performance points to buy a Lesson, you can schedule it. The outstanding Points are then displayed directly on the Point bar. You also receive a notification when you have gained enough Performance Points to buy it.

![](https://uma.guide/img/guides/article/grand_concert/gc_ui_point_reserved.webp)

There are two kinds of Lessons: Techniques and Songs.

### Techniques Needed for a Song [](#techniques-needed-for-a-song)

After you buy a specific number of Techniques, you get a selection of Songs to buy. This specific number depends on where you are in your career.

Below you can see the number of Techniques required before you are offered a new Song.


|Concerts                 |Number of Techniques Required|
|-------------------------|-----------------------------|
|Before 1st Concert       |1-2-3-4-4-2-3                |
|Before 2nd to 4th Concert|2-2-2-4-5-2-2                |
|Before Grand Concert     |2-2-2-4-3-2-2                |


Before your first Concert, you need to buy a Technique to unlock your first Song. Another two Techniques will unlock the second Song, and three more for the third. To get a fourth Song, you need four Techniques.

The number of Techniques required resets after each Concert.

### Song Saving [](#song-saving)

Song Saving occurs when you have reached a selection of Songs to buy, but do not buy one before your next Concert. Since the shop does not change, you can buy one of those Songs after the Concert. This counts as a Lesson in the progression to the next Song (see Gameplay). The drawback is that you get the ![](https://uma.guide/icon/gl-tokens/utx_ico_performance_s_07.png)Concert Bonus after the next Concert. This is done primarily when the Songs do not have a desirable bonus, so you get to the next Songs a bit quicker.

### Concerts [](#concerts)

Songs raise your Hype Level. You need to learn three Songs before every Concert to achieve a Great Success. A great success rewards you with +10 to all Stats, while having fewer Songs only gives you +3 to all Stats.

Additionally, you receive Skill Points for every Lesson you have learned in the time between Concerts. A Song is worth +25 SP, a Technique is worth +5 SP.

In total, you will perform in five Concerts:


|Concert            |Turn                      |
|-------------------|--------------------------|
|1st Concert        |Junior Year Late December |
|2nd Concert        |Classic Year Late June    |
|3rd Concert        |Classic Year Late December|
|4th Concert        |Senior Year Late June     |
|Grand Concert (5th)|Senior Year Late December |


INFO

Performing in a Concert will also raise each of your Performance Points caps by 50.

Gameplay [](#gameplay)
-----------------------

The gameplay loop of Grand Concert is all about acquiring Points through Training and then spending them to buy Techniques and Songs.

The most important aspect is understanding how many Techniques you need for a new Song, and that this resets after each Concert.

Since ![pal](https://uma.guide/icon/type/pal.svg)Light Hello has a chance to give you 20 Points, you should prioritize training with her.

WARNING

Since Races do not give you any Points, it is highly recommended not to do extra Races unless you need them to unlock your Uma's secret event!
