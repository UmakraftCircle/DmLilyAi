# Uma Musume Legacies (detailed fact reference)

**Source:** https://gametora.com/umamusume/legacies
**Author:** Gertas, published on GameTora (page last updated 2025-06-28)

## 1. Core concepts

- **Legacy** (also called inheritance) is one of the most important systems for building strong characters.
- Before a Career, the player chooses **two Legacies** (community term: "parents") from previously trained characters. The character being trained cannot be her own Legacy, because a girl cannot be inspired by her own past Career.
- Each Legacy has its own two Legacies, shown on the selection screen. These are **sub-legacies** ("grandparents") and also contribute to inspiration bonuses. A girl cannot be her own Legacy, but can be her own sub-legacy.
- **Sparks** are attributes generated for a character at the end of her Career. They determine what she can pass on when used as a Legacy.
- **Guest Legacy:** a Veteran borrowed from the Follow List, usable up to 3 times per day.

## 2. What Legacies do

### Initial bonus (applied when the Career starts)
1. **Stat bonus** from the blue sparks of the Legacies and sub-legacies. On the selection screen, blue numbers come from the left Legacy's lineage and pink numbers from the right's.
2. **Aptitude bonus** to a track type, track length or running strategy, coming from pink sparks. Improved aptitudes are highlighted in yellow.
   - Example given: Haru Urara has a B in Mile, which Legacies can raise to A at the start.

### Inspiration events
- Happen **twice per Career**, in the **first half of April of Year 2 and Year 3**. A short cutscene shows the trainee running after her Legacies.
- Always give **extra stats**, based on blue sparks of the Legacies and sub-legacies (more stars means more stats).
- May also trigger inspiration from **green, white or pink** sparks. The chance depends on overall compatibility.

### Compatibility
- Shown as a symbol at the top right after picking Legacies. It affects inspiration event bonuses.
- Three tiers (the underlying value is a hidden number, the symbols only show a range):

| Symbol | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| ◎ (double circle) | Great |
| 〇 (circle) | Good |
| △ (triangle) | Poor |

- Rule of thumb: pick girls with a matching track-length aptitude (for example both the Legacy and trainee rated A in Medium).
- Each pair of characters has a fixed base compatibility rating. The game combines several numbers when Legacies are picked, and crossing the first threshold gives 〇 and the second gives ◎. Exact formula and numbers are on the compatibility calculator page (https://gametora.com/umamusume/compatibility).
- Characters with similar affinities usually get good compatibility. Sub-legacies should match too where possible. Racing more (especially G1 races) in a Career raises the final compatibility bonus.

## 3. Spark types

Every spark has 1, 2 or 3 stars. Every character is guaranteed **one blue** and **one pink** spark. Green and white sparks have specific conditions.

### Blue sparks (stats)
- Exactly one per Career. The stat type is random among Speed, Stamina, Power, Guts and Wit (Japanese names: スピード, スタミナ, パワー, 根性, 賢さ).
- Star rating depends on the final value of that stat:

| Final stat value | 1 star | 2 stars | 3 stars |
|------------------|--------|---------|---------|
| Under 600 | 90% | 10% | 0% |
| 600 to 1100 | 50% | 45% | 5% |
| Over 1100 | 20% | 70% | 10% |

- Starting bonus per star rating: 1 star gives +5, 2 stars +12, 3 stars +21. Bonuses **add up** across every Legacy and sub-legacy.
  - Example: one Legacy at 3 stars, another at 2 stars, four sub-legacies at 1 star each gives 21+12+5+5+5+5 = **53** speed.

### Pink sparks (aptitude)
- One per Career, randomly one of three categories: track type, track length or running strategy. It always rolls something the character actually prefers (for example never Turf if she has a G in Turf).
- Categories and Japanese names:

| Category | Options |
|----------|---------|
| Track type | Turf (芝), Dirt (ダート) |
| Length | Sprint (短距離), Mile (マイル), Medium (中距離), Long (長距離) |
| Strategy | Front Runner (逃げ), Pace Chaser (先行), Late Surger (差し), End Closer (追込) |

- Can boost aptitude at Career start, and can trigger again in an inspiration event to push aptitude higher (for example A to S).
- The star count appears completely random. Even a girl that loses immediately without training can get 3 stars.

### Green sparks (unique skills)
- Give access to a Legacy's **unique skill** (special animation and rainbow background in the skill list, for example Maruzensky's LP1211-M).
- Only appear if the Legacy's rarity is **3 stars or higher** at the Career's start. In that case it is guaranteed. Raising rarity with character pieces can eventually qualify lower-rarity characters.
- A Legacy's green spark is granted at the start. Re-rolling it in an inspiration event gives a discount on the skill (if not yet bought). Sub-legacy green sparks can also come from inspiration events.

### White sparks (skills and races)
Two variants:
- **Skill sparks:** correspond to one of the trainee's normal-rarity skills. Random chance at Career end. If gained during inspiration, the skill becomes available at a discount that scales with stars.
- **Race sparks:** chance from **winning G1 races**, so the player can influence the odds by winning. Through inspiration they give two bonuses: usually a skill hint plus a small stat boost, or small boosts to two different stats. There are many, listed at https://gametora.com/umamusume/g1-race-factor-list. Careers (scenarios) also have their own spark of this type.
- Generation chances at Career end: each normal skill, each G1 win and the Career itself have a **20%** chance of producing a white spark. Double-circle (◎) skills have **25%** and rare (gold) skills **40%**.
- Stars: 1 star 50%, 2 stars 45%, 3 stars 5%. The 2 and 3 star chances rise if the uma is SS rank or above.

## 4. Building strong Legacies

- Early on, almost any non-terrible combination is fine. Later, aim for a strong lineup.
- **Blue sparks** are the top priority because they give the most stats, especially when the support pool is weak. Start by aiming for 3-star blue sparks.
- **Stamina and Power** are the most popular blue spark stats. Speed is already heavily raised in training, while Guts and Wit are often less valuable.
- **Goal ("9 star"):** a Veteran with a 3-star blue spark whose two Legacies also both have 3-star blue sparks. This gives 63 stat points at the start (21 x 3) plus much more from inspiration events.
- **Pink sparks:** length-based ones are usually best, since reaching S aptitude in your distance gives a lot of raw speed. Surface-based ones are more universal (only two options versus four lengths). Strategy-based ones are least valuable but useful for off-strategy trainees.
- **White sparks:** the URA Scenario spark from winning the URA Finale is the most desirable. After that, aim for quantity by having many skills and G1 wins.
- Chance-based rolls dominate, but understanding the rules improves your odds.

## 5. Farming tips

- **Use borrowed Veterans.** A full build needs two Legacies trained first. A good guest Legacy saves a third of the work and is often better than one you would train yourself. Guest use is limited to 3 per day.
- Aim for **600+ (ideally 1100+)** in the stat you want as a blue spark. If any 3-star blue is fine, build 600 across the board and hope for a high roll. You also need enough stats to win the Career, which varies per uma.
- Once stats are adequate, **focus on racing, preferably G1**. It gives chances at race sparks and raises final compatibility.
- Stats need not be perfect on farming runs, so the support setup can include cards with interesting skill hints. Buying the skill and rolling it as a white spark makes the Veteran more valuable.

## 6. Legacy loops (outdated strategy)

The source labels this strategy popular around the Japanese launch but mostly out of favor since, kept for historical reasons, and says there is no need to do it.

- **Loop:** pick usually four high-compatibility characters and repeatedly use them only as Legacies for each other until all reach satisfying builds.
- Rationale: there are two kinds of good Career outcomes: a strong character for PvP, or a character with great sparks (even if the run failed). Random play often yields great sparks on two incompatible characters. Loops try to concentrate everything into one set.
- Drawbacks per the source: tedious, long, and they do not actually improve spark odds. Playing normally gives a wider variety of usable characters.
- **Example rotation:** the source uses Vodka, Daiwa Scarlet, Grass Wonder and El Condor Pasa as characters A to D. Round 1: A and B are Legacies, C is the trainee. Then shift everything one position right each round, wrapping around. After the loop closes, use the strongest version so far instead of the previous step's result. Once compatibility is consistently double circle, you can repeat Careers for one character with fixed Legacies until the desired sparks appear.
