Stamina![](https://uma.guide/icon/type/stamina.svg) will once again be the most difficult stat to acquire for this CM. Although there is a longer downhill compared to the previous CM, the longer distance combined with the Stamina/Guts Racetrack Speed threshold makes 867-901 the optimal amount of Stamina. Similarly, at least 577-601 Guts![](https://uma.guide/icon/type/guts.svg) is recommended for the additional Speed threshold, while 867-901 would be optimal if possible.

### Front Runners ![](https://uma.guide/icon/type/style_front.svg) [​](#front-runners)

Similar to the previous Long CMs, CM20’s late-race begins on the backstretch, with a corner shortly following in 33 meters. Therefore, the viable Accels are ![](https://uma.guide/icon/skill/utx_ico_skill_20041.webp) Angling and Scheming, ![](https://uma.guide/icon/skill/utx_ico_skill_20011.webp) Victory Cheer!, and ![](https://uma.guide/icon/skill/utx_ico_skill_20011.webp) Break It Down!.

WARNING

Due to both ![](https://uma.guide/icon/skill/utx_ico_skill_20041.webp) Angling and Scheming and ![](https://uma.guide/icon/skill/utx_ico_skill_20011.webp) Break It Down! being Seiun Sky Unique Skills, one of the two Skills can only be acquired from the sub-Legacy layer on one Legacy. Due to the difficulty of obtaining all 3 Accel Uniques along with the subsequent SP costs, players can consider only taking 2 out of the 3 Skills.

### Pace Chasers ![](https://uma.guide/icon/type/style_pace.svg) [​](#pace-chasers)

Like most CMs, Pace Chasers have very little Accel in terms of inherited Unique Skills, and must rely on Front Runner Acceleration Skills, such as ![](https://uma.guide/icon/skill/utx_ico_skill_20011.webp) Victory Cheer! and ![](https://uma.guide/icon/skill/utx_ico_skill_20041.webp) Angling and Scheming. However, Pace Chasers do have a generic option in ![](https://uma.guide/icon/skill/utx_ico_skill_20041.webp) My True Strength, a Pace/Long White Acceleration Skill.

WARNING

​ ![](https://uma.guide/icon/skill/utx_ico_skill_20041.webp) My True Strength has a pre-condition of being within 4 Lengths of the front-most Uma towards the end of the mid-race. If there is a Runaway ![](https://uma.guide/icon/skill/utx_ico_skill_40012.webp) in the lobby, there is a high chance that the Uma will be unable to meet this pre-condition.

### Late Surgers ![](https://uma.guide/icon/type/style_late.svg) [​](#late-surgers)

Late Surgers have a new Acceleration Skill in ![](https://uma.guide/icon/skill/utx_ico_skill_20042.webp) Claw Forward/ ![](https://uma.guide/icon/skill/utx_ico_skill_20041.webp) Scramble, which provides instant Acceleration in exchange for a 2% HP Drain. While ![](https://uma.guide/icon/skill/utx_ico_skill_20041.webp) Moving Past, and Beyond is delayed, it can still be considered for a secondary Accel source. However, due to the difficulty in making a Long Mejiro Dober Legacy, players can also choose to omit this step.

### End Closers ![](https://uma.guide/icon/type/style_end.svg) [​](#end-closers)

Similar to the previous Arima Kinen CM, the most important Accel Skill for End Closers is ![](https://uma.guide/icon/skill/utx_ico_skill_20041.webp) Straightaway Spurt. While ![](https://uma.guide/icon/skill/utx_ico_skill_20041.webp) Moving Past, and Beyond is delayed, it can still be considered for a secondary Accel source. However, due to the difficulty in making a Long Mejiro Dober Legacy, players can also choose to omit this step.
