### Emulate Races in Room Match

![Champions Meeting Practice in Room Match](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIAAAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAAALAAAAAABAAEAAAIBRAA7)

The best way to see how well your choice Champions Meeting Umamusume will be doing is by racing others in **Room Match**. You can participate in **public lobbies** where the held matches are set to closely emulate an upcoming Champions Meeting race.

You can also do this with private lobbies to train together with friends, or even in [Clubs](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/538281). The most common rule for setting these type of matches is:

*   Rounds will **always have only up to 9 players** in a race.
*   **Random mood** if you wish to emulate 1st and 2nd rounds.
*   **Great mood** if you wish to emulate the final round.

### Use Practice Mode

![Practice Mode](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIAAAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAAALAAAAAABAAEAAAIBRAA7)

**Practice** mode, under Exhibition of the Races menu, became available on the release of the [Half Anniversary](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/554733) in global. Compared to Room Matches, practice mode allows you to precisely pick the entrants of the race itself from a catalogue of saved **Practice Partners**.

Practice Partners can be obtained from partner shares in a Club, or by looking through an Uma's stats and checking if they have an available **Partner ID**. You can search for these Umas anywhere, but one of the more common places to obtain these IDs are through leaderboards in [Team Trials](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536831).

Champions Meeting Mechanics
---------------------------

### Participation Requirements



* Champions Meeting Participation Requirements: ① Register 3 Veteran Umamusume to participate in the race.    ② Have a team rank of E2 or higher.    ⓘ You cannot have 2 identical Umamusume in the team. This includes the same Umamusume in different costumes. For example, you cannot have both Tokai Teio (Peak Joy) and Tokai Teio (Beyond the Horizon) in the same team.    ⓘ You can change teams and racers per Entry Uma registration in an ongoing Champions Meeting. You can only swap during the Entry phase and not in the middle of the 5 race attempts after the entry.


### Choosing a League: Open or Graded



* Open League: Graded League
  *     ・Only A+ or below Umamusume can participate.    ・Little difference in rewards between rankings.: ・No training grade restrictions.    ・Better rewards compared to Open League.


To participate in Chamipions Meetings, you will need to **choose a league** and have 3 Veteran Umamusume of an eligible rank ready to participate. **Graded League** is the best choice for casual or competitive participation if you have a full team of B+ Umamusume and above.

With limitaitons placed in Open League, you will need invest more of careful min-maxing to sustain your rank while also outclassing your opponents. This means more careful consideration of your Support Cards and their Limit Break levels.

### Consists of 3 Rounds and 2 Groups



* Round 1: Goes to A ↗    3 or more Wins        Goes to B ↘    2 or less Wins
  * Round 2: Goes to A Finals →    3 or more Wins    Goes to B Finals ↘    2 or less Wins
  * Round 3: A Group Finals    1st Place Winner
* Round 1: Goes to B Finals →    2 or more Wins
  * Round 2: B Group Finals    1st Place Winner
  * Round 3: 


Only A Group in the 2nd round can drop to B bracket. Those already in the B bracket cannot ascend to A in remaining rounds.

Each league will be composed of 3 rounds of races, with the whole group splitting into A and B groups depending on their performance.

*   Teams that perform well with enough wins will go into the A group bracket. The rest of the teams will fall into the B group.
*   Teams in A group that do not win enough in the 2nd round will fall into the B group bracket.
*   Teams are locked into their brackets when they enter the final round.

#### Participating in Rounds 1 and 2

Rounds 1 and 2 of the Champions Meeting can be entered for free in a day up to a maximum of 4 times. Further entries will require an **entry ticket** or 30 Carats. Win as much as you can to get into group A.

#### Final Round will be a Single Match

The final round of the Champions Meeting will be comprised of a single race between teams. Players who advance to this round will enter for free, only needing to register their choice Umamusume for the final round.

### Champions Meeting Round Details



* Round 1 Details: ・You can enter 5 races.    ・3 Trainers (3 teams) are matched for each race.    ・Win 3 or more times to advance in Round 2 of Group A.    ・Win 2 or less to advance in Round 2 of Group B.
* Round 1 Details: Round 2 Details

Group B:    ・1 Win or more to advance to the Final Round of Group B.    ・0 wins will cause your loss on this round.
* Round 1 Details: Final Round Details
* Round 1 Details: ・Trainers who make it to this round will participate free of any charges.    ・A winner will be determined in a single race with the finalists.    ⓘ Once you have registered an Umamusume, you cannot change it.    ⓘ If you do not register an Umamusume within the registration period, the one registered in the previous round will be your automatic pick for the final round.
