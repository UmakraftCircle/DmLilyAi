# Champions Meeting | Uma Musume | GameTora
Champions Meeting (CM) is a tournament event in which you'll be matched against other trainers. Advance through the rounds to win a plethora of prizes, including premium currency and gacha tickets!

Tournament System
-----------------

Before the event itself starts, you'll be asked to select what league you want to participate in; either **Open League** (オープンリーグ) or **Graded League** (グレードリーグ). You can change the league type any time during the three-day selection period before Round 1. After the first round starts, your choice is finalized and cannot be changed again for this Champions Meeting.

If you haven't chosen anything during the selection period, you can still do late registration during either of the first two rounds. You will select the league type then, and your choice will be permanent for the current event.

You can only field girls of rank B or lower in the Open League (A+ or lower on the Japanese server). It will also yield fewer rewards than the Graded League.

Usually, this means that the average opponent will be weaker than those you encounter in Graded League matches. However, you may still face highly optimized characters made to squeeze out as much strength within the rank limitations as possible, so don't expect the Open League to always be objectively easier than the Graded League.

The tournament itself consists of three rounds. The first two rounds are qualifiers. They both last two days, and you can make up to four attempts per day, each consisting of 5 individual races. You will be matched against new opponents for every race. The best attempt will count.

The finals are a single race. It's the only round where you won't be matched with your opponents in real-time. You just need to select your team and wait for the results.

In each race during the tournament, you can field three girls from your roster and will be randomly matched against two other players. Every race will thus have nine participating uma musume.

The races are held in an "all or nothing" fashion. The winning horsegirl will get a victory for her trainer, the other two players will get a defeat.

![](https://gametora.com/images/umamusume/events/2021/06_gemini_cup_system.png)

### Round 1

Round 1 lasts for two days. You can make up to 4 attempts (each consisting of 5 races) per day, for a total maximum of 8.

If you win three or more times (in any of the attempts), you will advance to **Round 2, Group A.**

If you win less than three times, you will proceed to **Round 2, Group B.**

It's possible to get up to four entry tickets per day. The first ticket is free, two you can get from a daily mission, and the last one can be purchased for 30 gems. The reset for this is at game's daily reset (noon EST for EN, noon JST for the JP server).

While any unused tickets won't disappear after day 1, please note that you can still only make 4 attempts per day, no matter how many tickets you have saved.

Every attempt gives you a chance to do all five races again, and you can field a different set of characters if you want to. Only the best result will count, so you only have to win three races in one of the sets.

Winning races during the event gives you rewards, so it's a good idea to participate as much as you can, even if you've already qualified.

### Round 2

Round 2 lasts for two days. You can make up to 4 attempts per day, same as in Round 1.

If you're in **Group A** and win three or more times, you will advance to **Finals, Group A.**

If you get one or two wins, you will be delegated to **Finals, Group B.**

Winning 0 races eliminates you from the tournament.

For **Group B** participants, getting at least one win will guarantee them a spot in **Finals, Group B.**

Getting 0 wins will eliminate them from the tournament.

Similar to Round 1, you can make 4 attempts every day.

### Finals

Unlike the qualifier rounds, there will only be one race available in the finals. Your final tournament rewards will depend on the result of that race.

Good luck!
