## 1. What Champions Meeting is

Champions Meeting (CM) is a recurring tournament event in which you are matched against other trainers. Advancing through the rounds earns prizes, including premium currency and gacha tickets. The page also hosts schedules, race conditions, green (recommended) skills and a history of every CM in release order.

## 2. League choice

Before the event starts, each player picks one of two leagues:

| League | Japanese name | Restrictions and rewards |
|--------|---------------|--------------------------|
| Open League | オープンリーグ | Only girls of **rank B or lower** may be fielded (A+ or lower on the Japanese server). Gives **fewer rewards** than Graded. |
| Graded League | グレードリーグ | No such rank cap. Gives more rewards. |

Rules for choosing:
- The league can be changed any time during the **three-day selection period** before Round 1.
- Once Round 1 starts, the choice is final for that Champions Meeting.
- Players who did not choose can still **late-register during either of the first two rounds**. That choice is also permanent for the event.
- Open League opponents are usually weaker on average, but highly optimized builds squeezed into the rank limit exist, so it is not guaranteed to be easier.

## 3. Race format

- Each tournament race uses **three girls from your roster**.
- You are randomly matched against **two other players**, so **nine uma musume** run in each race.
- Results are "all or nothing": the winning horsegirl gives her trainer a win, and the other two players each get a loss.
- Each attempt is **5 races**, and you face new opponents for every race.

## 4. Rounds

The tournament has three rounds: two qualifier rounds and a finals.

### Round 1
- Lasts **2 days**, with up to **4 attempts per day** (8 total), each attempt being 5 races.
- Only your **best attempt** counts, so you need three wins within a single attempt.
- **3 or more wins** puts you in **Round 2, Group A**.
- **Fewer than 3 wins** puts you in **Round 2, Group B**.
- Attempts can use a different set of characters each time.
- Winning races gives rewards, so playing more is worthwhile even after qualifying.

### Entry tickets
- Up to **4 tickets per day**: 1 free, 2 from a daily mission, and 1 purchasable for **30 gems**.
- Reset happens at the game's daily reset (noon EST on the English server, noon JST on Japan).
- Unused tickets carry over past day 1, but the cap of **4 attempts per day** still applies however many are saved.

### Round 2
- Lasts **2 days**, with up to **4 attempts per day**, same as Round 1.

| Group | Result | Outcome |
|-------|--------|---------|
| Group A | 3 or more wins | Finals, Group A |
| Group A | 1 or 2 wins | Finals, Group B |
| Group A | 0 wins | Eliminated |
| Group B | at least 1 win | Finals, Group B (guaranteed) |
| Group B | 0 wins | Eliminated |

### Finals
- **One race only.** This is the only round without real-time opponent matching: you pick your team and wait for the result.
- Final tournament rewards depend on that race's outcome.
