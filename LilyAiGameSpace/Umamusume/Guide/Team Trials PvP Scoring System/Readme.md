# Team Trials (PvP) Scoring System (detailed fact reference)

**Source:** https://gametora.com/umamusume/team-trials-pvp-scoring
**Author:** Gertas, published on GameTora (created 2021-04-30)
**Note:** This is an original, fact-focused reference written from that article for LilyAi to read. It is reorganized and reworded, not a copy. Full credit belongs to the original author and GameTora. Game materials are copyright Cygames, Inc.
**Time sensitivity:** The article dates from 2021 (Japanese server era). Score values may have been adjusted since. Check the live page before relying on exact numbers for the Global server.

---

## 1. How scoring is structured

A match is 5 races. Every score in this article is a **base score** that later gets **modifiers** applied:

1. Per-character scores: race result, skills, positioning and other bonuses (sections 3 to 9).
2. Team-level scores: victory and team result bonuses (section 4).
3. Modifiers: Ace, Opponent Rating, Support and Consecutive Win bonuses (section 2). Penalties are not modified.

The score details for a finished match are on the **Score Info** button (スコア情報 on JP), which shows which character earned how many points and for what.

## 2. Score modifiers

Modifiers are **additive, not multiplicative**. Example: base 10,000 with a 20% opponent bonus and 5% support bonus gives 10,000 + 2,000 + 500 = **12,500**.

| Modifier | How it works |
|----------|--------------|
| **Ace Bonus** | The character in position 1 on each team (labelled "Ace", エース on JP) gets **+10%** on every score she earns. Put your strongest uma in slot 1. |
| **Opponent Rating Bonus** | More points for beating stronger opponents. Varies widely with rating range. Ballpark: about **50%** against a 100,000 team rating and about **80%** at 150,000. |
| **Support Bonus** | From leveling your support cards. Total and breakdown shown on the main Team Trials screen. Usually a few percent at most. |
| **Consecutive Win Bonus** | Winning several of the 5 races in a row. On a streak of three, every character in the third win gets **+3%** (the streak length equals the bonus percent). |

## 4. Team points

| Bonus | Condition | Points | Modifiers that apply |
|-------|-----------|--------|----------------------|
| **Victory** | Win at least 3 of the 5 races | 10,000 | Only the Opponent bonus. Example: a 60% opponent bonus gives 16,000. |
| **1st, 2nd and 3rd (Trio / Trifecta)** | Your three girls take the top 3 places in a race | 5,000 | (see section 2) |
| **1st and 2nd (Quinella)** | Only when you fielded two characters (lower-tier classes) and they took 1st and 2nd | 3,000 | (see section 2) |
| **All Members Placed** | All three of your characters finish in the top 5, without a Trio | 4,000 | (see section 2) |

- You can get either the Top 3 bonus or the Top 5 bonus for a race, never both.
- Victory is awarded after all races, on the match result screen.

## 5. Skill points

- Each activation of a **normal rarity** skill: **500**.
- Each activation of a **gold rarity** skill: **1,200**.
- So upgrading to a gold skill is better for score than adding another normal skill, and gold skills are usually stronger than two normal skills of the same category too.

### Unique skills
Value depends on the skill level and the character's star rarity. Reaching 3-star rarity upgrades the unique skill, and PvP pays **500 more** for the upgraded version.

| Skill level | 1 or 2 star | 3 star or higher |
|-------------|-------------|------------------|
| 1 | 1,500 | 2,000 |
| 2 | 1,700 | 2,200 |
| 3 | 1,800 | 2,300 |
| 4 | 1,900 | 2,400 |
| 5 | 2,000 | 2,500 |
| 6 | 2,100 | 2,600 |
| 7 | 2,200 | 2,700 |
| 8 | 2,300 | 2,800 |
| 9 | 2,400 | 2,900 |
| 10 | 2,500 | 3,000 |

## 6. Good Positioning

- It does not relate to positioning skills. The character must be **1st among girls with the same running strategy** (for example a Late Surger ahead of all other Late Surgers).
- **1,000 points** if she achieves this on entering the middle phase of the race, and another **1,000** if she does it on entering the final phase.
- Teammates with the **same strategy compete for the same bonus**. Each duplicated strategy in the lineup can cost you nearly 4,000 points at the maximum with bonuses included. Use varied strategies.

## 7. Beat Target Time

- Each Team Trials course has a preset **Standard Course Time** (reference time), the time a runner is expected to finish in.
- Beating it earns **100 points per 0.1 second**, capped at **2,000 points for 2.0 seconds** faster.

| Finish time vs standard | Score |
|-------------------------|-------|
| -0.1 s | 100 |
| -0.5 s | 500 |
| -1.0 s | 1,000 |
| -1.5 s | 1,500 |
| -2.0 s or better | 2,000 |

(Every 0.1 s step in between is worth the matching 100-point increment.)

## 8. Strong Start

- **1,000 points** for an exceptionally low start delay.
- The source describes start delay as random and not stat-dependent. It puts the chance of a slow start at about **20%** and a strong start at about **20%**.
- Skills can shift the odds: the **Focus** skill multiplies start delay by **0.9**, and the gold **Concentration** skill multiplies it by **0.4**, which makes late starts impossible.
- The Race Mechanics Handbook (sibling folder) gives the numeric thresholds: a delay below 0.02 s earns Strong Start, and 0.066 s or more is a Late Start.

## 9. Dark Horse and penalties

### Dark Horse
- If your uma starts the race as the **8th favorite or worse** and finishes **3rd or better**, you get **4,000 points**.
- The source calls it rarely worth aiming for, since it would mean weakening your build and still placing well.

### Rushed penalty
- Rushing (掛かり, kakari) is a random negative state. A higher Wit stat lowers its chance.
- Penalty: **-500** flat when it starts, plus **-100 per second** of rushing.
- Rushing lasts up to 12 seconds naturally, but enemy debuff skills can extend it.
- It is the **only penalty** in Team Trials, and score modifiers like the Ace bonus do **not** affect it.

## 10. Worked example from the source

A losing long-distance team outscored a winning dirt team. The reasons:

- Everyone on the losing team finished in the top 5, earning the All Members Placed bonus. On the dirt team, one girl (Taiki Shuttle) finished 6th and denied the team any bonus.
- Taiki Shuttle also scored low individually: 6th place, one gold skill, and no positioning bonuses.
- Two dirt team members (El Condor Pasa and Haru Urara) shared the same strategy, so they only split one set of Good Positioning bonuses between them.
- The long-distance team had more leveled unique skills, all at 3-star. The two 2-star dirt girls each got 500 fewer points for unique skill activations.
- Takeaways: team balance, different strategies, gold skills and higher star rarity all matter.
