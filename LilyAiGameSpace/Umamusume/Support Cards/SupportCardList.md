# Support Card List

Support cards are split by rarity, then by type inside each file. Tables hold stat bonuses only; hints, unique effects and events go in separate detail files.

- [SSR.md](SSR.md): Speed, Power, Stamina, Guts, Wit, Group, Friend
- [SR.md](SR.md): Speed, Power, Stamina, Guts, Wit, Friend
- [R.md](R.md): Speed, Power, Stamina, Guts, Wit, Friend
- [SSR-Details.md](SSR-Details.md): unique effects, hints and training events for SSR cards

Notes:
- The wiki calls the Friend type "Pal". Group cards only exist as SSR.
- Stats are shown at max level (SSR Lv50). New bonus columns are added when a card introduces them. "-" means the card has no such bonus, "?" means not confirmed yet, and (+N) values come from the card's unique effect.
- Card list source: https://umamusu.wiki/Game:List_of_Support_Cards
