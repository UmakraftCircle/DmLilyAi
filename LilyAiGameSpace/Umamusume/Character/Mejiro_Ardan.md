## Overview

Mejiro Ardan is a character in the Umamusume: Pretty Derby franchise who serves as both a friend and a rival to Sakura Chiyono O and Yaeno Muteki. She belongs to the esteemed Mejiro family and is the younger sister of Mejiro Ramonu. Despite a frail health that keeps her frequently hospitalized, she possesses great skill and dreams of becoming a legendary racer.

## Background

- **Origins:** Member of the prestigious Mejiro family and younger sister of Mejiro Ramonu.
- **Personality:** Determined to achieve legendary status as a racer despite her weak constitution.
- **On the track:** Recognized for her high skill level as a competitor.
