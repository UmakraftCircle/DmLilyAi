## Overview

Oguri Cap is a coolheaded dreamer from the countryside who enrolled at Tracen Academy after dominating the racing scene back home. She works to make her hometown proud, though her clumsy side tends to come out. At the academy she is known as a fearsome eater who can empty a whole pot of rice in an instant. She is the main protagonist of the Cinderella Gray manga and anime.

## Real-life counterpart

Oguri Cap is named after and based on the Japanese racehorse of the same name. The real horse loved hot springs and was a poor swimmer, which the game references in her Stamina training. (The umamusu wiki also notes the real-life "Oguri Cap Rule" that let T.M. Opera O enter the Classics late.)
