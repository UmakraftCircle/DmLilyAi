## Overview

Gold Ship is a mischievous, eccentric and capricious Umamusume who loves playing pranks, especially on Mejiro McQueen, yet is a very skilled racer when it counts. She is the oldest member of Team Spica in the anime and one of the first members alongside Vodka and Daiwa Scarlet. She is prone to sudden swings in mood and motivation, and in the game she may perform very well or very poorly depending on how she feels. She is the protagonist of the spin-off PisuPisu☆SupiSupi Golshi-chan and runs the franchise's PAKA TUBE channel as a VTuber. Wikipedia describes her as one of the most popular characters in the franchise.

## Appearance

Long straight silver-gray hair with straight bangs, rosewood-colored eyes and a purple bow on her right ear. She has a muscular, athletic build and is among the taller Umamusume. Her race outfit is a short red dress with gold stripes and six gold buttons, which represent the real horse's six G1 wins (per the Fandom wiki).

## Real-life counterpart

Gold Ship is named after and based on the Japanese racehorse of the same name, who was similarly temperamental. Wikipedia notes the horse's 2015 Takarazuka Kinen, where a bad start in the gate caused about 12 billion yen in lost bets.

**Unique skill (per Game8):** activates once the race is 50 to 60% complete while she is in the second half of the pack, then gives a velocity increase over a long stretch. Game8 rates her one of the best free trainees, strong especially in Long races. She is one of the few End Closers able to move up mid-pack, so she is viable in Medium and Mile too, and her innate skills include the early-race velocity skill Uma Stan.
