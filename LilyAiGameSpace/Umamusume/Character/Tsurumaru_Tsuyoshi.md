## Overview

Tsurumaru Tsuyoshi is an Umamusume character featured in the Pretty Derby franchise. She is the sixth and final addition to the Golden Generation and is known for her optimistic outlook despite a frail health. Determined to achieve the Triple Crown, she carefully manages her condition, often preparing smoothies to combat her illnesses.

## Background

- **Team:** The sixth and final member of the Golden Generation added to the game.
- **Personality:** Optimistic and highly conscious about her health, frequently making smoothies to address her ailments.
- **On the track:** Aspires to win the Triple Crown despite a weak constitution, with legs that often require hospitalization and a tendency toward violent coughing fits.
