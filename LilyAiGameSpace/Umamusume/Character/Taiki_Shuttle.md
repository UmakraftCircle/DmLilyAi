## Overview

Taiki Shuttle is a tall, friendly Umamusume born in the United States who arrives with a cowgirl attitude and a heart as big as her strength. She is known as the fastest short-distance Umamusume in Japan and a hug machine who gets lonely easily, which may be why she is also a great party planner. Her powerful dash has caught the eye of her friend Silence Suzuka.

## Background

- **Appearance:** Light brown hair in a high ponytail with a green star-shaped hair band, a matching star pin on the right side, and cobalt-blue eyes.
- **Relationships:** Mejiro Dober is her roommate (per the Fandom wiki).
- **On the track:** A Pace Chaser with high Sprint and Mile aptitude who can run on both Turf and Dirt.

**Unique skill: Shooting for Victory! (per Game8):** triggers in the second half of the final corner when she is 3rd to 4th in Champions Meeting or 3rd to 5th in Team Trials. It is a strong acceleration skill but situational, since on some tracks it can activate at a poor spot. Game8 places her on the Dirt team in Team Trials as a Pace Chaser and recommends maxing Potential; Mile Maven and Changing Gears are strong for her.
