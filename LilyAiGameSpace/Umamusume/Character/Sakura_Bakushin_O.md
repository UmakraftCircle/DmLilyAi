## Overview

Sakura Bakushin O serves as the assertive class representative of Team Alkes. She is a childhood friend and teammate of Sakura Laurel and Sakura Chiyono O, and is recognized as one of the top sprinters in the series. Although she aims to master races of all distances, her strong focus on speed and limited stamina keep her competing mainly in mile‑length events.

## Background

- **Team:** Member of Team Alkes, working alongside Sakura Laurel and Sakura Chiyono O.
- **Personality:** Described as headstrong and determined.
- **On the track:** Known for being one of the best sprinters, with a clear preference for speed over stamina, which restricts her to races no longer than a mile.
