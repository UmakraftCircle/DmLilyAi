## Overview

Super Creek is an Umamusume featured in the Pretty Derby franchise and is recognized as one of the trio known as “The Three Strongest Eternals.” She is characterized by a nurturing, mother‑like disposition, often tending to younger companions even when it leaves her exhausted.

## Background

- **Origins:** She was raised in a family that worked as daycare providers, which shaped her caring nature.
- **Team:** She is grouped with Oguri Cap and Inari One as the celebrated “Three Strongest Eternals.”
- **Personality:** She loves looking after smaller Umamusume such as her roommate Narita Taishin, Tamamo Cross, and her trainer Fumino Nase, frequently sacrificing her own well‑being.
