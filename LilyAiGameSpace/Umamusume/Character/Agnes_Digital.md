## Overview

Agnes Digital is a cheerful, super-versatile otaku who loves Umamusume and is here to support all the sparkling racers around her. In the game she is the only Umamusume with good aptitude on both track surfaces, and Game8 calls her an all-around pick whose silly personality is part of the appeal.

**Unique skill: OMG! ☆ The Final Sprint (ﾟ∀ﾟ) (per Game8):** a velocity and navigation-speed skill that is fairly easy to trigger, since she only needs to have overtaken twice in the late race. It works best for non-Front styles, which usually have runners ahead of them. Her kit includes the early-race skills Uma Stan and Superstan. Game8 also notes a secret event: winning 7 of the listed Mile races gives a large Speed and Power boost plus extra hints for Updrafters and Unyielding Spirit.
