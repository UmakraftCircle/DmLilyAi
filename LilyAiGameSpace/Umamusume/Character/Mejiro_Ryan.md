## Overview

Mejiro Ryan is a character from the Umamusume: Pretty Derby franchise, belonging to the distinguished Mejiro family. She is devoted to her relatives and younger members such as Mejiro Dober and Mejiro Bright, often acting as a supportive figure. Though she presents a tomboyish exterior and enjoys weight training, she secretly reads romance manga and wishes to appear more feminine, a desire she feels too shy to express.

## Background

- **Origins:** A member of the prestigious Mejiro family.
