## Overview

Mihono Bourbon is a character from the Umamusume: Pretty Derby franchise who serves as both a friend and a rival to Rice Shower. She is noted for her robotic mannerisms, which have earned her the nickname “Cyborg.” Despite this mechanical persona, electronic devices tend to malfunction around her, leading to a ban from campus vending machines and an analog lifestyle for her family.

## Background

- **Personality:** Displays a robotic demeanor that has led others to label her “Cyborg,” and she inadvertently causes electronic devices to break down.
