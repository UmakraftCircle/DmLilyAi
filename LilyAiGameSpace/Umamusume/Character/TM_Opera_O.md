## Overview

T.M. Opera O is an opera queen who is so self-absorbed it is comical. Her mannerisms are over the top, and she is forever proclaiming her own greatness. She believes that people who do not know their own beauty and strength are unhappy, and she works tirelessly every day toward her stage ambitions. She is an antagonist in the Beginning of a New Era movie, appearing as Jungle Pocket's ultimate opponent, and one of the protagonists of the Road to the Top OVA alongside Admire Vega and Narita Top Road.

## Real-life counterpart

T.M. Opera O is named after and based on the Japanese racehorse of the same name. The umamusu wiki notes that the real horse benefited from the "Oguri Cap Rule", which let him enter the Classics late by paying a large fee, and that he was the first to win a Classic crown that way.
