## Overview

Almond Eye is a Junior Division student at Tracen Academy and a member of Miho Dormitory. She is the model student: strong in both academics and athletics, polished in every respect, and with the presence of a future star. All of it rests on one defining trait, an extreme hatred of losing. She competes to the point of perfectionism, and her one flaw is that she often pushes herself past what her body can take. In her game introduction she says she will beat even a legend, because she is the one who believes she can.

She is named after and based on the real racehorse Almond Eye, foaled on 10 March 2015 at Northern Farm. The real mare is regarded as the most successful Japanese racemare of all time. She was the fifth horse to win the fillies' Triple Tiara, twice Japanese Horse of the Year (2018 and 2020), and was inducted into the JRA Hall of Fame in 2023. Her nine Grade 1 wins earned her the fan title "Nine-Crown Queen", which the game echoes: the ninth-ranked tagline and the nine stars in her eyes. The real horse's name refers to an almond-shaped eye that is considered beautiful.

## Background

- **Personality:** Competitive, disciplined and gifted, with a perfectionist streak. She is friendly with her peers but treats rivals as targets to beat again and again.
- **Habits and likes:** She likes eye contact and spot-the-difference puzzles, prefers rice porridge in the morning, bakes brownies, watches murder mystery shows at night, and puts on good puppet shows with Cuties plushies. She is a big fan of the mascot Kyumaru, a cucumber-loving kappa. Her disguises and saunas are the things she handles worst.
- **Health:** She is prone to heatstroke and fevers, which the wiki links to the real mare falling ill in Hong Kong ahead of the 2019 Hong Kong Cup.
- **Relationships:**
  - Blast Onepiece is her friend and roommate. In real life both were owned by Silk Racing.
  - Fusaichi Pandora is a close friend whom she looks up to. Pandora pampers her while trying to set a good example, and Almond Eye makes her bento lunches. In real life Pandora is her dam.
  - Curren Bouquetd'or is a friend and underclassman. Almond Eye looks out for her, and Bouquet aspires to be as strong as her. They were stablemates in real life.
  - Lucky Lilac is a friend, classmate and rival. Almond Eye wants to beat her over and over, and Lilac is annoyed but fired up by that drive. In real life Lilac was her main rival during the Triple Tiara.
  - Gran Alegria is a friend and rival. Gran Alegria admires her running, while Almond Eye is frustrated when she loses to her in miles. In real life Gran Alegria was one of the few horses to beat her.
  - Kiseki is a friend and rival who sees her as a born protagonist and offers to teach her how to draw manga.
  - Gentildonna, Vivlos, Daring Tact, Rhein Kraft and Smart Falcon are friends. Smart Falcon thinks Almond Eye has the makings of an Umadol and wants to support her.
  - Mejiro Bright and Seiun Sky are friends and upperclassmen who teach her how to relax and have fun, and she learns that rest can lead to better results.
- **Trivia:**
  - She was announced during the 4th Anniversary PakaLive TV stream on 22 February 2025 and is a semi-limited character: she is in the permanent gacha pool but cannot be picked with paid 3-star trainee vouchers.
  - In her career mode, the Senior Year Japan Cup features every Umamusume whose real counterpart won 7 G1 races (Vodka, Symboli Rudolf, T.M. Opera O, Kitasan Black and Gentildonna). This nods to her record nine G1 wins, set in 2020.
  - Like Haru Urara, Mejiro McQueen and Smart Falcon, she has an alternate event if she loses her debut race, because the real mare lost her debut to Nishino Urara.
  - Her fluffy hairband represents the shadow roll worn by the real horse, and her race outfit is based on a drawing she made as a child.
  - Her love of morning porridge refers to her real trainer, Sakae Kunieda, who made porridge for the horses he trained.
  - Her fan nickname 九冠馬 (Nine-Crown Horse) sounds like the Japanese word for cucumber, which is where the Kyumaru fandom comes from.
  - In her career story she is upset to hear that another Umamusume has beaten her record. This refers to the 2025 Japan Cup, where Calandagan broke her record time by 0.3 seconds.

## Appearances

- **Design:** Long, smooth light-brown hair with a gentle inward curl at the tips. The inner layer is light blue fading to lavender, with small sparkles. A single tight braid runs from the back to the side of her head, tied with a black ribbon. She wears a headband with a large white bow with black tips beside her left ear. Her bangs part in the middle, showing a small white cross-like highlight on her forehead. Her eyes are lavender with blue gradients and star-shaped pupils. Her race outfit is a pastel blue idol dress: a corset-style bodice with white frills and a choker ribbon, red button accents, puffy off-shoulder white sleeves with yellow bows, blue fingerless gloves, and a layered skirt of white, blue and black ruffles with a red bow at the waist. Two diamond-patterned tails hang from her hips. She finishes the look with white thigh-high stockings and blue high-top sneakers with red bows.
- **Anime:** No anime appearance is listed on her wiki page.
- **Manga:** Umamusumeshi.
- **Game story events:** That Summer's Que Sera Sera (main story event), the 4th Anniversary Story and the 5th Anniversary Story.
- **Songs:** Solo: Glory Eyes, plus an Almond Eye version of Irodori Phantasia. Group: Legend-Changer, Reimei ni Saku Hana, Shake The World!!, STARTING FORCE, Umapyoi Densetsu, VOLTAGE and You! Aim! A!!.
