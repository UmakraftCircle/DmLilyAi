## Overview

Tamamo Cross is a small but fierce runner from Kansai who talks fast with a strong Kansai tone. She depends on effort and stubbornness, and once she starts moving forward she does not slow down. Her quick back-and-forth with Oguri Cap has made the two a popular duo. MyAnimeList lists her as a main character in Umayuru: Pretty Gray and in Cinderella Gray Part 2, and as a supporting character in other anime and manga such as Umayon.

**Unique skill: White Lightning Comin' Through! (per Game8 and Lootbar):** raises both velocity and acceleration when she is on a straight in the second half of the race and is either near the front (about 2nd to 3rd or 4th) or mid-pack (about 6th to 9th) with a runner she can overtake. Game8 builds her as a Long End Closer.
