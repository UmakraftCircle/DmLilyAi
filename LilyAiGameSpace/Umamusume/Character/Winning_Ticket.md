## Overview

Winning Ticket is a central character in the Umamusume: Pretty Derby mobile game, serving as the protagonist of Chapter 3 in Part 1 of the main storyline. She belongs to Team BNW alongside Biwa Hayahide and Narita Taishin. Her demeanor is straightforward, yet she is emotionally sensitive and often cries, driven by a strong admiration for and ambition to compete in the Japanese Derby.

## Background

- **Team:** Member of Team BNW together with Biwa Hayahide and Narita Taishin.
- **Personality:** Straightforward in attitude, emotionally sensitive, prone to crying frequently, and deeply admires the Japanese Derby.
