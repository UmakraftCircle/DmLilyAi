## Overview

Cesario is a central character in the Umamusume: Pretty Derby video game, taking the lead role in Chapter 2 of Part 2 of the main storyline. She competes as a rival to Rhein Kraft while being part of the same generation, and she shows admiration for King Halo and her trainer. Her demeanor includes respect for the veteran horse Special Week.

## Background

- **Team:** Joined Team Ascella together with Rhein Kraft after being impressed by King Halo and her new trainer.
