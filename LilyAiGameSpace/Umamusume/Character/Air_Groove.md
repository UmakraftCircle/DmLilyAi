## Overview

Air Groove is the co-vice-president of Tracen Academy's student council and a member of Team Rigil, praised as the "Empress" for striving to fulfill every responsibility perfectly. She is a close friend and rival of Silence Suzuka and takes good care of her childhood friend Duramente. She first appears in the anime's first season.
