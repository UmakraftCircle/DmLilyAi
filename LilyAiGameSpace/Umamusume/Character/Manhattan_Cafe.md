Manhattan Cafe is a character in the Umamusume: Pretty Derby franchise who serves as both a friend and a rival to several other characters. She is driven by the goal of surpassing an unseen ghost friend that accompanies her. Although she is frequently irritated by Agnes Tachyon's behavior, she still offers reluctant assistance when needed.
