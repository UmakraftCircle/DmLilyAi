## Overview

Aston Machan is an Umamusume character who is often seen carrying a Ma‑chan doll. She enjoys approaching anyone holding a camera, which leads her to appear spontaneously in anime scenes where she looks directly at the lens. In the mobile game, she greets players with a reminder to always remember her.
