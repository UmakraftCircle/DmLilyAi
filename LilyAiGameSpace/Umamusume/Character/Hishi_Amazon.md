## Overview

Hishi Amazon is the hot-blooded, hot-tempered leader of Miho Dormitory and a racer who loves nothing more than a one-on-one fight. Most see her as a fierce competitor, but she has the good grace to congratulate opponents who beat her. She is proficient at cooking, cleaning and laundry, and generally reliable, though you should avoid mentioning ghosts or thunder around her. In the anime she debuts in season 1 as a member of Team Rigil.

## Background

- **Appearance:** Sun-kissed skin, amber eyes, long azure hair reaching her knees, a small white clip and a red tiger-stripe band, plus a small fang.
- **Relationships:** Narita Brian is her rival and the student council vice president.
- **Running gag:** In the first anime season she is repeatedly asked to stand at the practice track to mark the finish line; later seasons use a standee of her for the same purpose.

## Real-life counterpart

Hishi Amazon is named after and based on the racehorse of the same name, who was born in the United States and raced in Japan. The Fandom wiki notes that the real horse could not enter the races that now form the Triple Tiara, but in the game she can.

**Unique skill (per Game8):** gives a velocity boost on the final straight. She must be 5th or worse in Team Trials (4th or worse in Champions Meeting) on the final corner or later, must have overtaken a runner there, and that runner must be closer to the rail than she is. The overtaking and positioning requirements make it hard to trigger. Game8 rates her an okay End Closer whose 20% Power growth helps, and suggests Mile sparks to raise her Mile aptitude from A to S for Team Trials and Champions Meeting. She must run the Arima Kinen twice in her career, so Game8 suggests about 400 Stamina for the Classic year and 500 or more for the Senior year. Uma Stan helps her early-race pickup.
