## Overview

Tokai Teio is one of the main characters of the franchise and the protagonist of the anime's second season. She is a bright, bubbly Umamusume with a natural knack for racing. She has long looked up to the student council president, Symboli Rudolf, and that admiration is her main motivation. Her goal is to become an undefeated Triple Crown champion. Fans call her upbeat march the "Teio Step".

## Background

- **Anime:** She first aims for Team Rigil and hangs out with Team Spica as a non-member, then decides to join Team Spica. Season 2 follows her career as repeated injuries threaten her racing and her dream of following Symboli Rudolf.
- **Relationships:** Friend and rival of Mejiro McQueen and Nice Nature, roommate of Mayano Top Gun, and rival and friend of Twin Turbo. Symboli Rudolf is her idol (in real life, Symboli Rudolf is her sire).
- **Appearance:** A small Umamusume with long amber-brown hair in a ponytail, a pink ribbon, a white streak in her bangs and sky-blue eyes.
- **On the track:** In the game she is a Pace Chaser with strong speed and a good finishing kick.

## Real-life counterpart

Tokai Teio is named after and based on the Japanese racehorse of the same name, known as the "Miracle Horse" of the early 1990s. After her debut she quickly won the first two Triple Crown races undefeated, but bone fractures cost her the third leg and two Tenno Sho races. She returned after over a year of rest and won the Arima Kinen, beating the favorite Biwa Hayahide.
