## Overview

Symboli Rudolf is the president of Tracen Academy's student council and a recipient of the Triple Crown. As the only Umamusume in history to have won seven crowns, she is known as the "Emperor", and her authority stays unshaken even after her retirement. She is evenhanded, seeks to be a model Umamusume and dreams of an era in which all Umamusume can live in true happiness. She looks dignified but has a playful side. Tokai Teio especially admires her. In the anime she debuts in season 1 as a member of Team Rigil.

## Background

- **Appearance:** Light brown hair reaching below her waist, with the front section dark brown.
- **On the track:** NamuWiki notes she was popular early in the game's life for her strong unique skill and growth rates, and that she is often used as a parent for factor (legacy) work.

## Real-life counterpart

Symboli Rudolf is named after and based on the Japanese racehorse of the same name, the first horse to win the Triple Crown undefeated in the Classics (per NamuWiki's summary of her title conditions). He is also Tokai Teio's sire.
