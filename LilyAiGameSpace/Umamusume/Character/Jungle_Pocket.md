## Overview

Jungle Pocket is the main character of the story titled “Beginning of a New Era.” She entered the Tracen academy after being inspired by Fuji Kiseki’s race and aims to become the top Umamusume. Her drive is fueled by a desire to surpass the strongest peers of her generation, particularly Agnes Tachyon and T. M. Opera O, while maintaining friendships with rivals such as Agnes Tachyon, Manhattan Cafe, and Dantsu Flame.
