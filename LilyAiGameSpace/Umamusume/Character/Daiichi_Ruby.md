## Overview

Daiichi Ruby is a sprint‑type Umamusume belonging to the generation of Mejiro McQueen. She is introduced in the third season of the Umamusume: Pretty Derby anime, where she is seen attending a school festival performance. Coming from a distinguished family, she consistently turns down the romantic interest shown by Daitaku Helios.
