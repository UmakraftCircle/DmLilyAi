## Overview

Air Messiah is a Junior Division student at Tracen Academy and a member of Ritto Dormitory, where her roommate is Vivlos. The wiki sums her up as honest, steadfast and hardworking. She wants to prove to her mother that a bright future lies ahead by winning races for her sake. Her quote in the game ties this together: her strength is being steadfast and sure, even if it is simple.

She is named after and based on the real racehorse Air Messiah, who won the 2005 Shuka Sho. She was announced at the 5th EVENT ARENA TOUR GO BEYOND, on day two of the YELL leg held on 4 February 2024, which is also her birthday.

## Background

- **Personality:** Honest, steadfast and hardworking. She wants to break out of her shell and get past her insecurities by trying new things, but she tends to stay a quiet presence.
- **Motivation:** Her mother placed in the top three of her Triple Tiara races without ever winning one. Air Messiah wants to show her mother a great future by winning for her.
- **Family:** Everyone in her family keeps some kind of diary. She has an older brother studying in South Korea, whom she sometimes phones. The wiki reads this as a nod to her real-life full brother Air Shady, who was sent to South Korea after his racing career.
- **Everyday details:** She likes chocolate sauce on French fries, reads manga on days off, and as a child left sweets by her bed for Santa because she felt sorry for him. She enjoys Muscat grapes, which the wiki suggests may nod to her real-life foal Air Muscat.
- **Address:** She calls herself Watashi and calls the player "Trainer-san". Vivlos calls her "Mecchan-senpai", which she dislikes.
- **Relationships:**
  - Vivlos is her roommate and they get along well.
  - Rhein Kraft, Cesario and Daring Heart are friends and rivals. Rhein Kraft taught her to sew and shares tips on good sunbathing spots.
  - Air Shakur is a friend and upperclassman who helps calculate her future race results. Messiah looks up to her and likes her taste in music. In real life Shakur is her uncle, since her dam Air Deja Vu is Shakur's older half-sister.
  - Air Groove is a friend and upperclassman whom she admires as a strong racer, especially for winning the Japanese Oaks.
  - Jungle Pocket is a friend despite their clashing personalities. In real life the two produced a filly, Air Muscat, in 2010.

## Appearances

- **Design:** Light olive-green hair and tail. Her slightly wavy hair is cut bluntly at the shoulders, and her bangs are parted to the right and held by a blue and gold brooch, leaving her forehead clear. She has purple eyes, orange round glasses, and a dark blue ear cover with gold trim, a teal frill and two white pom-poms on her left ear. Her default outfit is a dress resembling an Italian folk dress with pale green frills and a white flower at the collar.
- **Anime:** Cameo in Beginning of a New Era.
- **Game story events:** June's Vibrant Statices (main story event), the 3rd Anniversary Story and the 3.5th Anniversary Story.
- **Career story:** An NPC Umamusume with a blue hairpin looks up to her. The wiki reads this as a reference to Air Messiah's real-life foal Air Spinel.
- **Music:**
  - Solo song: Tokaeri no Hana (WINNING LIVE 18).
  - Group songs: Umapyoi Densetsu (WINNING LIVE 18 and 19) and Unite!! (WINNING LIVE 19).
  - Gift, Air Messiah ver., on Solo Vocal Tracks & Talk 6th EVENT The New Frontier Vol.2.
