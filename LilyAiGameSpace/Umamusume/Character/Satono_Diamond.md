## Overview

Satono Diamond is an Umamusume in the Pretty Derby franchise who first appears in the second season of the anime and becomes a co‑protagonist in the third season. She is a devoted fan of Mejiro McQueen and enrolls at Tracen to follow her idol’s footsteps. Determined to end her family’s long‑standing Grade 1 winless streak, she strives to become an acclaimed racer. She shares a friendly rivalry with her childhood friend Kitasan Black.

## Background

- **Origins:** Member of the prestigious Satono family, which has never produced a Grade 1 winner.
- **Team:** Enrolls in the Tracen training school to emulate Mejiro McQueen.
- **Personality:** Admirer of Mejiro McQueen and maintains a friendly rivalry with Kitasan Black.
- **On the track:** Aspires to break her family’s curse by winning a Grade 1 race.
