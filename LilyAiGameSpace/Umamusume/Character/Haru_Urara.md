## Overview

Haru Urara is a character in the Umamusume: Pretty Derby franchise who appears in both the anime and the mobile game. She acts as the co‑protagonist of the anime’s second season and leads Chapter 1 of Part 1 in the game’s main storyline. Although she first comes across as cold and blunt, she genuinely cares for others and is ready to help when needed.

## Background

- **Origins:** member of the prestigious Mejiro family.
- **Team:** reluctantly joins Team Spica in the anime.
- **Personality:** initially seems cold and blunt, but truly cares for others and offers help.
