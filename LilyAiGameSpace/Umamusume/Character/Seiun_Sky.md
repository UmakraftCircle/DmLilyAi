## Overview

Seiun Sky is one of the most easygoing girls at Tracen Academy and lives at her own pace, but on the inside she is a schemer, and her laziness is often an act to get others to let their guard down. She likes afternoon naps and fishing, and loves cats. She is a classmate of Special Week.

## Background

- **Appearance:** Short powder-mint hair, a sunflower hairpin on the left side of her bangs, a green ear cover with a cloud pattern and a cotton tail on the right ear, and tired-looking blue eyes.
- **Real-life rival:** King Halo. The Fandom wiki notes the two raced several times and Seiun Sky always came out ahead.

**Unique skill: Angling and Scheming (per Game8):** activates when she is in the lead in a late-race corner, so she is a Front Runner through and through. Her Fast-Paced skill and its upgrade Escape Artist are both Front Runner skills, and maxing Potential unlocks Escape Artist. Tail Held High is another good mid-race velocity skill, triggering when she uses 3 skills mid-race. Game8 suggests improving her Medium aptitude for Team Trials; for Long she can inherit Victory Cheer! or Break It Down!.

**Unique skill: Break It Down! (per Game8):** can activate when she is at or near the front (1st to 2nd), in the late race and on a straight. Its extra velocity applies only on a straight that is not the backstretch. It is best on Long tracks and is valuable to pass down to Long Front Runners, though Game8 notes she lacks a gold recovery skill and has 0% Stamina and Wit growth.
