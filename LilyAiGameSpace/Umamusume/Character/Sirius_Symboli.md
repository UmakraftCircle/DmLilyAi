## Overview

Sirius Symboli is a former childhood friend of Symboli Rudolf who returns to Japan after a European tour to compete in the Twinkle Series. She debuted in the third season of the Umamusume: Pretty Derby anime and is listed as a possible participant in the Prix de l'Arc de Triomphe.
