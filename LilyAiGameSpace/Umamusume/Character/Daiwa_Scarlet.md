## Overview

Daiwa Scarlet is a proud, competitive junior of Special Week who hates to lose and is set on being number one. She is Vodka's close friend and main rival, and in the anime she is a member of Team Spica. She is also the character players train in the game's tutorial, after which she joins the roster as a 2-star trainee (per TheGamer).

**Unique skill (per Game8):** activates in the second half of the race when she is 1st with a runner right behind her, or 2nd with someone in front she can overtake. She is a Pace Chaser by default because of some of her skills, but Game8 suggests running her as a Front Runner to trigger the skill more reliably. Her skills such as Up-Tempo and Killer Tunes lean toward Medium, where she does better than in Mile. She fits a Medium Front Runner slot in Team Trials.
