## Overview

Hishi Akebono is a foreign exchange student who pursues a career as a chef while also competing as a racehorse. Standing at 180 cm, she is the tallest playable Umamusume and is noted for her exceptionally large appetite.

## Background

- **Origins:** She is a foreign exchange student.
- **Personality:** She possesses a pronounced appetite, often eating portions that are too large for smaller Umamusume such as her roommate Biko Pegasus.
- **On the track:** After initially aspiring to become a sumo wrestler, she chose to race while continuing her culinary ambitions.
