## Overview

Agnes Tachyon is the eccentric mad scientist of Tracen Academy. A breathtaking racing genius who retired undefeated in 4 starts due to a career-ending tendon injury, she seeks to transcend biological speed limits through experimental formulas, treating her Trainer as her most cherished "guinea pig".

In-game introduction: "Fufufu... Welcome to my laboratory, my dearest guinea pig! Shall we conduct a groundbreaking experiment on the turf today?"

## Background

- **Origins:** Raised in Chitose, Hokkaido. Son of Sunday Silence and Agnes Flora; displayed supernatural velocity from birth.
- **Team:** Senior researcher at Tracen Labs; roommate to Manhattan Cafe.
- **Personality:** Theatrical, mischievous, highly intellectual, affectionately condescending, and secretly deeply affectionate toward Trainer.
- **On the track:** Effortless Leader (Pace Chaser) whose built-in stamina recovery formula allows her to invest maximum training into pure Speed.
