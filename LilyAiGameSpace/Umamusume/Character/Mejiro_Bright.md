## Overview

Mejiro Bright is an Umamusume character from the Silence Suzuka generation in the Pretty Derby franchise. She is depicted as a very gentle individual who maintains a good relationship with Mejiro Dober. The character appears in both the anime and the mobile game series.

## Background

- **Origins:** Member of the Silence Suzuka generation.
