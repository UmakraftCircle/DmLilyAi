## Overview

Special Week is one of the main characters of the Umamusume franchise. She is the co-protagonist of the first anime season and serves as the franchise mascot. She is a bright, hardworking country girl from Hokkaido who enrolls at Tracen Academy in Tokyo with the dream of becoming the top Umamusume in Japan and making her mothers proud. Her admiration for her senior Silence Suzuka is a driving force in the story.

In-game introduction: "My name's Special Week! My dream is to be the top Umamusume in all of Japan! I gotta work hard to make my moms proud!"

## Background

- **Origins:** Raised in Hokkaido. She lost her birth mother early on and was raised by her mother's friend. She had not met another Umamusume until enrolling at Tracen.
- **Team:** In the anime she joins Team Spica alongside Vodka, Daiwa Scarlet and Gold Ship, and Silence Suzuka also becomes part of her circle.
- **Personality:** Cheerful, sensitive and encouraging in daily school life. Known for a huge appetite that leads her to assume others love food as much as she does.
- **On the track:** Described as having strong stamina and speed, and an instinct for when to hold back and when to open up.

**Unique skill (per Game8):** requires her to be in the front half of the pack in the late race and beyond (1st to 5th in Champions Meeting races, 1st to 6th in Team Trials) and activates after she overtakes another runner. **How to get her (per the Fandom wiki):** she is a special trainee that cannot be pulled from the gacha. She is a reward for completing hidden requirements in the main story, and her star level can only be raised with statues. The listed steps are:
1. Create a Veteran using the Team Sirius support card "Passing the Dream On".
2. View all of that card's recreation events.
3. Buy the skill "Best in Japan" before the run ends.
4. Advance through Main Story Act 1, Finale Part 2, until the Arima Kinen goal race appears.
