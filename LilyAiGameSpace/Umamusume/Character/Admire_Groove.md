## Overview

Admire Groove is the stoic, elegant prodigy of Tracen Academy. Daughter of "The Empress" Air Groove and Sunday Silence, she was burdened by immense generational expectations. Through sheer pride and solitary resolve, she captured consecutive Queen Elizabeth II Cups and later foaled the dual-classic champion Duramente.

In-game introduction: "I am Admire Groove. Other people's expectations are just noise. I will run the race I chose, and claim victory on my own terms."

## Background

- **Origins:** Raised in Hayakita, Hokkaido. Born of royal bloodlines (Sunday Silence x Air Groove).
- **Team:** Ritto Dormitory; respected as a solitary lone wolf who avoids cliques.
- **Personality:** Cool, detached, intellectually rigorous, secretly harbors a sweet tooth and gazes into aquariums.
- **On the track:** Premier Middle-Distance Late Surger (Betweener) who tracks the pace effortlessly before launching an icy, surgical closing spurt.
