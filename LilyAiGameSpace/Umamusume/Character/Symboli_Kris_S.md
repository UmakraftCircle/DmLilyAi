## Overview

Symboli Kris S is a prominent Umamusume introduced in the anime series Umayuru. She is characterized by a quiet demeanor and a large stature, sharing her generation with Tanino Gimlet. Originally from the United States, she came to Japan to study abroad and hopes to repay the Symboli family. In the story she frequently confronts Tanino Gimlet as a central figure.

## Background

- **Origins:** Arrived from the United States to study abroad and aims to give back to the Symboli family.
