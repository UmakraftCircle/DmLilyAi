## Overview

Sakura Laurel is the main character of the Star Blossom storyline within the Umamusume franchise. She dreams of surpassing the legendary Narita Brian even though she is physically delicate. Her determination drives her to train hard despite her fragility.

## Background

- **Team:** She grew up alongside and now competes with Sakura Bakushin O and Sakura Chiyono O as members of Team Alkes.
- **Personality:** Although she lacks physical strength, she remains resolute in her goal to outshine Narita Brian.
