## Overview

Stay Gold serves as the founder and president of Tracen Academy's Expeditionary Support Group and lives as Gold Ship's largely absent roommate. She is one of the few characters who understand the true nature of the Umamusume world, retaining the memories of the real horse Stay Gold and the ability to share those memories. Motivated by this knowledge, she subtly influences events to improve the lives of the Umamusume counterparts.

## Background

- **Origins:** Holds the memories of the real-world racehorse Stay Gold and can convey the memories of horses that raced against him.
- **Team:** Leads the Expeditionary Support Group at Tracen Academy and is Gold Ship's roommate.
- **Personality:** Aware of the hidden truth of the world and works behind the scenes to ensure better outcomes for the Umamusume.
