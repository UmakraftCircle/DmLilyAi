## Overview

Vodka is a headstrong, energetic junior of Special Week who lives by a policy of being the coolest at all times. In the anime she is one of the first three members of Team Spica, alongside her friend and rival Daiwa Scarlet and Gold Ship. Her rivalry with Daiwa Scarlet is central to both characters.
