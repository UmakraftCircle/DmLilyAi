## Overview

Fine Motion is a sheltered lady who is excited to make memories that will last a lifetime at Tracen Academy. She is eager to hear about popular culture and is always on the lookout for a good local ramen spot.

## Profile details (game)

| Tail | The "Royal Tail" is of noble quality. |
| Family | She is better at handling her uninhibited sister than her parents. |
| Trivia 1 | She is a pro at keep-ups. |
| Trivia 2 | She recently learned to play "Umapyoi Legend" on the violin. |

## Real-life counterpart

Fine Motion is named after and based on the racehorse of the same name, who was born in Ireland and raced in Japan.
