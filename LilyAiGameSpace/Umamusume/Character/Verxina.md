## Overview

Verxina is a character from the Umamusume: Pretty Derby franchise, depicted as the oldest of the three V Sisters. She aspires to claim the title of queen but often feels eclipsed by others, especially her rival Gentildonna. The mere mention of being second provokes irritation, yet she remains resolute in her effort to surpass her competitor.

## Background

- **Origins:** The eldest member of the trio known as the V Sisters.
- **Personality:** Feels overshadowed by others, becomes annoyed when called "second," and is driven to defeat her arch‑rival Gentildonna.
