## Overview

Air Shakur is a Senior Division student at Tracen Academy and a member of Ritto Dormitory, where her roommate is Meisho Doto. She is a brilliant but eccentric racer who always takes the logical approach and trusts only the data she has collected. In her view, a win comes from calculation and never from chance. Her quote in the game says a race comes down to mathematical aggregation.

She is named after and based on the real racehorse Air Shakur, who won the Satsuki Sho and the Kikuka Sho in 2000 but lost the Japanese Derby by a margin of 7 cm. That gap became the core of her story: she built a race-prediction program called Parcae, and no matter how she recalculates, it shows her falling 7 cm short of the Triple Crown. Her first character profile was posted on the official Umamusume Twitter account on 27 April 2017, as a quiz asking fans to guess which horse she was based on.

## Background

- **Personality:** Logical, blunt and guarded. She trusts data over luck and is slow to answer when called before a race, because she is deep in thought. Her self-introduction says her simulations are sound and everyone should be ready to be beaten by her logic.
- **Motivation:** She is searching for an error in her calculations that would prove the Triple Crown was within reach. Parcae predicts that she will never win again after the Kikuka Sho, and she accepts this grudgingly. She shuts out people who try to encourage her and even hesitates to debut at all. In her career story she decides not to retire after Fine Motion returns to Japan.
- **Habits:** She turns off all social media notifications, and her phone background is a black plaid pattern. Her shortened training events show her inner thoughts as C++-style code comments.
- **Family:** She has never opened the packages her mother sends her.
- **Relationships:**
  - Meisho Doto is her roommate, friend and rival. Doto knows how much pain Shakur has been through and wants others to see how strong, hardworking and kind she is. Shakur keeps Doto out of trouble. In real life both were known as second-placers, which may be the basis for pairing them.
  - Air Messiah is a friend and underclassman whom she supports and respects. In real life Shakur is Messiah's maternal uncle, because Messiah's dam Air Deja Vu is Shakur's older half-sister.
  - Agnes Tachyon is a research partner. They share an interest in science, but Shakur mocks Tachyon's romanticism, and Tachyon likes to provoke her. In real life Shakur lost the Japanese Derby to Agnes Flight, Tachyon's older full brother.
  - Agnes Digital and Tap Dance City are friends from the same generation. Tap is very forward with her, finds her endearing and wants her to join her group.
  - Fine Motion is a friend. Both live with a sense of destiny, but Fine faces it cheerfully while Shakur accepts it bitterly. Fine admires Shakur's honesty and finds her bluntness refreshing.
  - Manhattan Cafe is a friend, and the two are mutually annoyed by Tachyon.
  - T.M. Opera O is the strongest rival of the previous generation and shapes how spectators see Shakur. In real life the 2000 Classic generation was nicknamed the "weakest generation" for failing to beat Opera O.
- **Trivia:**
  - Her name comes from the rapper Tupac Shakur, and her training events after certain G1 races reference his songs. She has references to other rappers too.
  - The real Air Shakur was famously hard to handle and was ridden by many trainers. His usual jockey, Yutaka Take, said he never knew what was going on in Shakur's mind. The wiki suggests this may explain her rough manner.
  - Before the Classic-year Japan Cup in career mode, she gets a debuff skill that lowers Guts and Wit, because the real horse finished 14th in the 2000 Japan Cup.
  - The value Parcae shows before the Japanese Derby matches the dates of the real horse's first and last races.

## Appearances

- **Design:** Short, spiky, tousled black hair with a center parting and two longer strands at the back. She has golden-yellow eyes, subtle piercings above her left eyebrow, and a gold or bronze ear piece on her right ear chained to an ear cuff. Her race outfit is a deliberate mix of clashing pieces: a short-sleeved black jacket with blue accents and yellow studs, a torn yellow paint-splatter shirt, and ripped black leggings decorated with blue flames.
- **Anime:** Umamusume: Pretty Derby Season 1 (OVA), a cameo in Season 3, Umayon, Umayuru and Beginning of a New Era.
- **Manga:** Umamusumeshi and Umayon.
- **Game story events:** Tonight at the Ligne Droite and Tapestry of Farewell's Everlasting.
