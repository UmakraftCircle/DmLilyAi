## Overview

Bamboo Memory is a character in the Umamusume: Pretty Derby franchise. She acts as both a friend and a rival to Oguri Cap, and she serves on Tracen's disciplinary committee. She hopes to challenge Oguri Cap in races.

## Background

- **Team:** She is a member of Tracen's disciplinary committee.
- **Personality:** She maintains a friendly yet competitive relationship with Oguri Cap, acting as his rival.
- **On the track:** She intends to race against Oguri Cap.

## Appearances

- **Design:** Short brown hair with a white streak down the middle, a teal ribbon around her right ear, and a headband marked with the kanji 夢 ("dream"). She has blue eyes and sharp brows that suit her competitive nature. Her race outfit is a black military-style jacket with a green lining, gold trim and bamboo-like patterns near the hem, white buckles, and a waist chain. An armband on her left arm reads 風紀 ("discipline"), matching her committee role. Under the jacket she wears a white vest edged in gold, a gray shirt and a green skirt with gold hems. She also wears long white gloves with three straps, white boots with gold toe details and a green stripe, and black socks with gold trim.
- **Anime:** Cinderella Gray.
- **Manga:** Cinderella Gray, Umayon and Umamusumeshi.
- **Game story events:** Let Those Cheers Roar! Tracen Cheer Squad, #Summer #Besties and My Dear Dancing Orchid. She also appears in the seasonal event The White Era and has cameos in Days Flying By and Hark Back, Run Forward.
- **Songs:** Solo versions of DRAMATIC JOURNEY, Ms. VICTORIA and Tracen Ondo. Group: Lightning Fast and Umapyoi Densetsu.
- **Fan notes:** Her first character profile was posted on the official Umamusume Twitter account on 12 May 2017 and reposted on 6 March 2018 for a giveaway campaign. Fans often pair her with Yaeno Muteki, since both love martial arts and share a similar outlook. Her "Hot Blooded Training" changes name depending on the situation.
