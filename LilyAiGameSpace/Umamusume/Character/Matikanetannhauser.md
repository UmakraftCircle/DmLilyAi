## Overview

Matikanetannhauser is a character from the Umamusume: Pretty Derby franchise who appears as a rival to Rice Shower. She is portrayed as unlucky, frequently suffering injuries and nosebleeds, yet she manages to win the affection of her teammates. In the anime's second season, she belongs to Team Canopus.
