## Overview

Biwa Hayahide is a character in the Umamusume: Pretty Derby franchise, known as the older sister of Narita Brian. She belongs to Team BNW alongside Narita Taishin and Winning Ticket, presenting herself as a composed and clever student. Despite this demeanor, she has a fondness for bananas and feels unusually self‑conscious about the size of her head.
