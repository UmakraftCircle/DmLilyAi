## Overview

El Condor Pasa is an Umamusume from the United States who trained abroad and aspires to compete in foreign races. She is one of Special Week's friends and sees her as a rival, though they stay on friendly terms. In the anime she debuts in season 1 as a member of Team Rigil. Her personality shifts with her mask: with it on she is confident, passionate and energetic, and with it off she is timid and meek. The game bills her as a vivacious, quirky lucha libre.
