## Overview

King Halo is a character from the Umamusume: Pretty Derby franchise who befriends the protagonist Special Week. She competes with a strong sense of pride despite achieving only modest results, establishing herself as a notable rival early on. In the game's second main story segment, she takes on a central role as part of Team Ascella.

## Background

- **Team:** Joins Team Ascella during the second part of the game's main storyline.
- **Personality:** Shows a proud attitude toward racing, maintaining confidence even when her performances are average.
- **On the track:** Known for delivering respectable but not top‑tier results, positioning her as a determined yet modest competitor.
