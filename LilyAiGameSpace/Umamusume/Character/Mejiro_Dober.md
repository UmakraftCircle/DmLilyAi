## Overview

Mejiro Dober is a trainee character in the Umamusume: Pretty Derby franchise, belonging to the distinguished Mejiro family. She is the younger sister of Mejiro Bright and looks up to the senior Mejiro Ryan. Beneath her composed demeanor she is a socially anxious, closeted shōjo manga artist, and she reacts differently to trainers depending on their gender, being warm toward females while extremely shy and somewhat fearful of males.

## Background

- **Origins:** Member of the prestigious Mejiro family and sister of Mejiro Bright.
- **Team:** Holds respect for senior Mejiro Ryan.
- **Personality:** Socially anxious, closeted shōjo manga artist hidden behind a cool exterior; displays enthusiasm toward female trainers and extreme shyness with male trainers.
