## Overview

Vivlos is the youngest of the three V Sisters in the Umamusume: Pretty Derby franchise. She grew up in a wealthy, pampered household and is known for her impish and flirtatious personality. Her goal is to live in Dubai and compete in races there, and she appears as a main character in the original net animation Road to the Top while also showing up as a secondary character in Beginning of a New Era.

## Background

- **Origins:** The youngest of the three V Sisters, raised in a privileged and pampered family environment.
- **Personality:** Displays a highly impish and flirtatious demeanor.
- **On the track:** Aspires to reside in and race in Dubai.
