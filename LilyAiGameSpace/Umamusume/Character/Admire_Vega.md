## Overview

Admire Vega is the melancholic, fiercely resolved protagonist of "Umamusume: Road to the Top". Tormented by the memory of her unborn twin sister, she views every race as an act of atonement to fulfill the life her sister never had. Guided by jockey Yutaka Take, she conquered the 1999 Tokyo Yushun (Derby) in record time before a tendon injury cut her brilliant career short.

In-game introduction: "I don't need sympathy or companions. I only run for one person... and that star is watching from above."

## Background

- **Origins:** Raised in Hayakita, Hokkaido. Born under tragic circumstances where her twin sister passed away before birth.
- **Team:** Protagonist of "Road to the Top" alongside Narita Top Road and T.M. Opera O; roommate to Curren Chan.
- **Personality:** Withdrawn, severe, protective of Curren Chan despite herself, and harborer of intense internal guilt.
- **On the track:** Premier End Closer (Oikomi) whose lightning late-spurt acceleration tears past opponents on the outer stretch.
