**Admire Vega** is a character in _Umamusume: Pretty Derby_. She's one of the protagonists of the OVA [ROAD TO THE TOP](https://umamusu.wiki/ROAD_TO_THE_TOP "ROAD TO THE TOP"), alongside **[T.M. Opera O](https://umamusu.wiki/T.M._Opera_O "T.M. Opera O")** and **[Narita Top Road](https://umamusu.wiki/Narita_Top_Road "Narita Top Road")**.

Biography
---------

> "I made a promise... to that shining star. That I would win, even if I have to do it alone."

_The lone star that shines brightest. She carries the weight of her immense resolve._

A somewhat gloomy Umamusume who tends to avoid company. A certain tragedy that took place early in her life affects her deeply, even making her racing activities seem like an attempt to atone. Though she doesn't intend it, her stoic pessimism garners the interest and concern of those around her, and she often gets caught up in the antics of [T.M. Opera O](https://umamusu.wiki/T.M._Opera_O "T.M. Opera O"), one of her racing peers.

Appearance
----------

Admire Vega fashions her long brown hair into a low ponytail, tied at the back by a white bow. She has two black star-shaped accessories at the base of her ears. Her right ear has a blue cover on it, bearing a star design. Her eyes are a pink color, which surround her round, small pupils.

Her race outfit consists of a blue dress with gold trim, ornaments, and belts at the front, in addition to two golden zipper pulls. Two star-shaped ornaments hang down at her blue skirt, which has black stripes separating streaks of blue. A white necktie hangs down over her chest, bearing a familiar star design. Her left shoulder has a cloth-like navy blue pauldron with gold trim. Her arms and legs both sport black tights. An elbow pad sits on each arm, held in place by a blue ribbon, and jeweled with a blue gem. She wears a white gauntlet on her left hand. A belt lies over her leggings on both legs, at the thigh. She wears black and white color-mismatched, golden-heeled shoes, each with a blue trim.

Relationships
-------------

*   **[Narita Top Road](https://umamusu.wiki/Narita_Top_Road "Narita Top Road")** - Classmate and rival. Top Road's persistence in approaching her has greatly changed her.
*   **[T.M. Opera O](https://umamusu.wiki/T.M._Opera_O "T.M. Opera O")** - Rival. Ayabe finds her grandiose overconfidence annoying.

*   **[Curren Chan](https://umamusu.wiki/Curren_Chan "Curren Chan")** - Roommate and close friend. Curren has been friends with Ayabe for long enough to adapt to her standoffish behavior, so she is one of the few people who notice when something is seriously off with her.
    *   IRL, Curren Chan lost her half-brother, Spring Song, to colic. While never confirmed, it is most likely that the two were paired as roommates as they both lost someone considered close to them.
*   **[Meisho Doto](https://umamusu.wiki/Meisho_Doto "Meisho Doto")** - Friend of the same generation. Ayabe is willing to tell Doto what she needs to hear.
*   **[Silence Suzuka](https://umamusu.wiki/Silence_Suzuka "Silence Suzuka")** - Friend. Ayabe and Suzuka occasionally stargaze together on the school roof.

Trivia
------

*   Her initial character profile was uploaded to the official [Twitter](https://x.com/uma_musu/status/873007768449335296) account on June 8, 2017.
    *   This tweet was a character name prediction quiz, that encouraged fans to try and guess which horse she's named after. The hint was "She aims to rise above adversity and reach the top of the Twinkle Series for the sake of her twin sister, who died shortly after her birth."
*   Her nickname is Ayabe, but in real life, it was Adobe (a portmanteau of "**Ado**maiya **Be**ga").
    *   The nickname Ayabe is still a portmanteau of her full name, just in a different order (**A**domai**ya** **Be**ga). It was likely changed as Ayabe is pronounced the same as an existing Japanese surname, 綾部.
*   She is very fond of fluffy things.
    *   This is shown multiple times, including an event where she forgets to add softener to her favorite towel, in a cameo appearance during [Beginning of a New Era](https://umamusu.wiki/Beginning_of_a_New_Era "Beginning of a New Era"), and more.
*   Ayabe's IRL counterpart had a similar story of their twin sibling dying before their birth.
    *   IRL, a mare carrying twin horses is highly risky and usually, one has to be aborted to save the other. The real-life Admire Vega therefore technically had a twin brother, but Ayabe was chosen to survive instead. Both [ROAD TO THE TOP](https://umamusu.wiki/ROAD_TO_THE_TOP "ROAD TO THE TOP") and the game reference this, but the gender is swapped to female as Ayabe's sibling was also an Umamusume.
*   In astronomy, the star Vega of the constellation Lyra is a first-magnitude star, which is also why Ayabe often references wanting to become one.
*   Ayabe's IRL dam, Vega, has multiple stand-ins within the franchise.
    *   In the [final episode](https://umamusu.wiki/Galloping_After_Our_Dreams "Galloping After Our Dreams") of [Season 2](https://umamusu.wiki/Season_2 "Season 2"), the mob Umamusume Pella acts as a representation of Vega, who placed 9th in the 1993 Arima Kinen.
    *   Additionally, in the [game](https://umamusu.wiki/Umamusume:_Pretty_Derby_\(mobile_game\) "Umamusume: Pretty Derby (mobile game)"), the unique mob Harp Alpha appears in the training stories of [Yukino Bijin](https://umamusu.wiki/Yukino_Bijin "Yukino Bijin") and [North Flight](https://umamusu.wiki/North_Flight "North Flight").
        *   Harp Alpha's name is a reference to the Lyra (Lyre or Harp) constellation, of which the star Vega is its alpha, the brightest star in the constellation.
    *   A flashback in Ayabe's character story (Episode 2) reveals that her mother is known as an accomplished racer.

## Overview

Admire Vega is the melancholic, fiercely resolved protagonist of "Umamusume: Road to the Top". Tormented by the memory of her unborn twin sister, she views every race as an act of atonement to fulfill the life her sister never had. Guided by jockey Yutaka Take, she conquered the 1999 Tokyo Yushun (Derby) in record time before a tendon injury cut her brilliant career short.

In-game introduction: "I don't need sympathy or companions. I only run for one person... and that star is watching from above."

## Background

- **Origins:** Raised in Hayakita, Hokkaido. Born under tragic circumstances where her twin sister passed away before birth.
- **Team:** Protagonist of "Road to the Top" alongside Narita Top Road and T.M. Opera O; roommate to Curren Chan.
- **Personality:** Withdrawn, severe, protective of Curren Chan despite herself, and harborer of intense internal guilt.
- **On the track:** Premier End Closer (Oikomi) whose lightning late-spurt acceleration tears past opponents on the outer stretch.
