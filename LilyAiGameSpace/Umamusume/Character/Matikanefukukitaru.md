## Overview

Matikanefukitaru is a fictional Umamusume in the Pretty Derby franchise. She is depicted as both a friend and a rival of Silence Suzuka. She possesses a talent for fortune‑telling and often provides future predictions to other Umamusume. She struggles with low self‑esteem, attributing her successes to luck or the guidance of a deity she calls the great Shiraoki.

## Background

- **Personality:** She has low self‑esteem and believes her victories stem from luck or divine influence from the great Shiraoki.
