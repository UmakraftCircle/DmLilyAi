## Overview

Sakura Chiyono O is a character in the Umamusume: Pretty Derby series who competes as a racehorse. She is depicted as both a friend and a rival to other characters, showing a blend of camaraderie and competitive drive. After achieving victory in the Japanese Derby, she suffers an injury that sidelines her.

## Background

- **Team:** Protégé of Maruzensky; later joins Team Alkes alongside Sakura Laurel and Sakura Bakushin O in Star Blossom.
- **Personality:** Shown as a friendly rival, maintaining close bonds while also competing fiercely with peers.
- **On the track:** Secures a win at the Japanese Derby before being forced to withdraw due to injury.
