## Overview

Cheval Grand is the middle sister among the three V Sisters in the Umamusume: Pretty Derby franchise. She feels insecure and lacks confidence when compared to her sisters Verxina and Vivlos, which makes her withdrawn and uneasy in conversation. Despite these challenges, she is a friend and rival of Kitasan Black and possesses abilities that her sisters do not display.

## Background

- **Origins:** Middle child of the three V Sisters, alongside Verxina and Vivlos.
- **Personality:** Insecure and low in self‑esteem relative to her sisters, highly withdrawn, struggles when speaking to others, yet harbors unique talents not seen in her sisters.
