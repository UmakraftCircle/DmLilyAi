## Overview

Mejiro McQueen is a member of the prestigious Mejiro family and a poised, classy young lady set on fulfilling her family's ambition of winning the Tenno Sho. She is the co-protagonist of the anime's second season and the protagonist of Chapter 1 of Part 1 of the game's main story. In the anime she reluctantly joins Team Spica but later forms a close friendship with Tokai Teio. She can seem cold and blunt at first, but she cares about others and helps when needed.
