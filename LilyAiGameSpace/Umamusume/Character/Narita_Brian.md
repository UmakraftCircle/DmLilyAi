## Overview

Narita Brian is a tough, stubborn lone wolf who lets her running do the talking. She serves as a student council vice president alongside Air Groove.

**Unique skill: Shadow Break (per Game8):** a velocity skill that activates on the final corner or beyond when conditions are met. Its strength depends on whether she was blocked on a side for a time (a "challenge") in the mid-race. The position window is wide (2nd to 7th in a 9-gate race, 2nd to 9th in a 12-gate race), so it suits Pace Chasers and Late Surgers, and it works in both Medium and Long. Game8 rates her a good Pace Chaser for Long races and a versatile Legacy parent for most styles other than Front Runner.
