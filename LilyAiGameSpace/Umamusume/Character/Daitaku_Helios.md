## Overview

Daitaku Helios is a character in the Umamusume: Pretty Derby franchise who debuted in the anime's second season. She is portrayed as a gyaru who frequently uses trendy slang and is known for her skill at escaping. She harbors an unrequited crush on Daiichi Ruby, addressing her as "Lady," and is a close friend of Mejiro Palmer. She also serves as the protagonist of the stage play Sprinters' Story.

## Background

- **Origins:** She belongs to the generation of Mejiro McQueen.
- **Personality:** She is a gyaru who uses gyaru slang (or zoomer slang in the English version), is adept at escaping, and has a one‑sided crush on Daiichi Ruby, calling her "Lady."
