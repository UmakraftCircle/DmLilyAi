## Overview

Silence Suzuka is a co-protagonist of the first Umamusume: Pretty Derby anime season and the protagonist of Chapter 5 of Part 1 of the game's main story. She is an accomplished Umamusume and Special Week's roommate and teammate, and her racing and friendship inspire Special Week. Her goal is to run as fast and as far as possible, ahead of every rival, which she believes is what it means to be the best. Later in the anime, she goes to the United States.

In-game introduction: "I'm Silence Suzuka. I love to run, and I won't give up my lead to anyone. Uh... that's about it."

## Background

- **Personality:** Seen as a prodigy who is always ahead of the pack, both figuratively and literally. She gets so absorbed in running that she can seem aloof, but she is dedicated. She is a bit awkward at making friends but gradually learns to relax and be herself.
- **Appearance:** Long, silky reddish-orange hair down to her waist, with neatly trimmed straight bangs and two even side strands.
- **Team history:** A former member of the elite Team Rigil who transferred to Team Spica, whose trainer understands her way of running.
- **Relationships:** Special Week (roommate, teammate and admirer), upperclassman Air Groove and Matikanefukukitaru. Air Groove was her teammate in Team Rigil.
- **Anime:** She fractures her leg during the Tenno Sho (Autumn), then recovers over roughly nine months and trains with her teammates again.

## Real-life counterpart

Silence Suzuka is named after and based on the Japanese racehorse of the same name (a chestnut horse foaled in 1994, sired by Sunday Silence). She is known for front-running speed. In the 1998 Tenno Sho (Autumn), ridden by Yutaka Take, she did not finish, and that date is listed as her date of death.

**Unique skill:** Sources differ on the exact trigger, likely because of balance changes over time.
- Game8 (2026): activates in the second half of the race while she leads by a large margin. It works as a mid-race velocity skill and usually triggers around the halfway point, especially when she runs as a Runaway.
- Dbltap (July 2025 guide): "The View From the Lead is Mine!", which raises velocity using all remaining strength when leading by a fair margin on the final straight.

**Build notes (per Game8, Dbltap and NamuWiki, as of their write-ups):**
- Build as a Front Runner, specifically a Runaway. She can gain the Runaway style through a hidden event (see below).
- Prioritize Speed, then Power, then Stamina. Skills should help her take the lead early and keep it, for example velocity skills plus stamina recovery. Final Push and Unrestrained were suggested for defending the lead on the final straight.
- Game8 recommends the Grand Live scenario for its high Speed stat cap and shorter length, and notes it is easier to train for PvP than the Trackblazer scenario.
- Game8 advises maxing her potential levels, and rates her kit as generally good across the board.
- NamuWiki notes a late-game acceleration skill gives her more reach than typical runaway horses, letting her act as a semi-ace as well as a "lid" that keeps other runaways in check.

**Hidden events:**
- **An Inspiring Streak** (added after the Nov 11, 2025 balance adjustments): win the Tenno Sho (Autumn) after winning six graded races in a row (G1, G2 or G3) with the streak unbroken. Gives a skill hint for **Runaway**, letting her run as a Runaway.
- **After the Mainichi Okan: Onward, to Light**: finish 1st in the Mainichi Okan in Senior year. Rewards: +3 to 4 random stats, 35 skill points, extra friendship with Yayoi Akikawa and a skill hint level for Early Lead.

**Title:** Her unique title is "Fugitive from Another Dimension" (異次元の逃亡者). Per NamuWiki (Japanese server info), the conditions are a set of consecutive wins in graded races, including winning the Takarazuka Kinen from the front.
