## Overview

Marvelous Sunday is a character from the Umamusume: Pretty Derby franchise. She is portrayed as both a friend and a rival to Sakura Laurel and Narita Brian. Her outlook is that each day is wonderful, and she possesses mysterious abilities such as telepathy and the capacity to transport others into a personal pocket dimension known as the "Marvelous Dimension".
