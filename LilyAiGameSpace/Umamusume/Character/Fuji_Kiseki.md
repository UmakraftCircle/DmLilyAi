## Overview

Fuji Kiseki is a senior Umamusume presented as a jet-black entertainer and the dorm's classiest racer. She comes from a theater family and treats every appearance as a show, putting smiles on faces wherever she goes.

## Profile details (game)

| Detail | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Ears | She has perfect pitch. |
| Tail | Sways gracefully like the feather accessories of a stage costume. |
| Family | Her parents are in the theater and travel all over the country. |
| Trivia 1 | She can make a dove appear from a top hat, and the top hat from nothing. |
| Trivia 2 | She often attends tea parties organized by her fan club. |

## Real-life counterpart

Fuji Kiseki is named after and based on the Japanese racehorse of the same name. NamuWiki notes that the horse's only G1 win was at Mile distance, and the game version is likewise built as a Mile runner.
