## Overview

Rice Shower is the central character for the second chapter of the first part of the main storyline in the Umamusume: Pretty Derby video game. She is portrayed with a gloomy demeanor and often puts herself down. Despite her self‑critical nature, she is a talented racer who strives for victory, even when her successes hinder her fellow competitors from reaching key achievements.

## Background

- **Personality:** Exhibits a gloomy outlook and frequently belittles herself.
- **On the track:** Recognized as a skilled racer who aims to win, though her triumphs can prevent peers from attaining major milestones.
