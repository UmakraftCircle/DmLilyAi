## Overview

Maruzensky is a senior Umamusume known as the "Supercar" or "Monster" for her overwhelming speed. She comes across as a friendly, bright and gentle older sister, though her sense of style is old-fashioned and a little out of step with the times. Her car-themed personality runs through her profile: she drifts, she loves Eurobeat and her outfit is a red hot rod.

**Unique skill (per the umamusu wiki and Game8):** raises acceleration by shifting gears when she is toward the front on the final corner or later. Game8 calls her an easy-to-build Front Runner whose kit (Shifting Gears, Acceleration, Changing Gears) suits Mile. Potential Level 3 unlocks Changing Gears and Level 4 unlocks Focus. Winning the Radio Nikkei Sho in early July of the Classic year triggers a secret event.
