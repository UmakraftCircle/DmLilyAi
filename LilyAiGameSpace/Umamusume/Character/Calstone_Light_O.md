## Overview

Calstone Light O is a character from the Umamusume: Pretty Derby franchise. She appears calm and expressionless, yet she is driven by an unusual fixation on speed and straight lines. This focus causes her to act with single‑minded efficiency and makes it difficult for her to understand anything that involves curves or corners.
