## Overview

Rhein Kraft is a central character in the Umamusume: Pretty Derby video game, acting as the main protagonist for the opening chapter and the concluding chapter of the second part of the story. She is positioned between the generations of Sweep Tosho and Kawakami Princess, indicating her place in the series' timeline. Her admiration for King Halo and her new trainer highlights a respectful and eager aspect of her personality.
