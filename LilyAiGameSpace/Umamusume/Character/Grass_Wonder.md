## Overview

Grass Wonder was born in America, but her parents' love of Japanese culture shaped her into someone often called the spitting image of an ideal Japanese beauty. She is friendly and easy to get along with, yet her will is firm and her determination to win is second to none. In the story she was one of the great Umamusume on the track before a leg injury kept her out for about ten months, and she returned fully healed. She shares a room with her close friend El Condor Pasa.

## Background

- **Appearance:** Tawny waist-length hair with a white spot above her bangs, blue eyes, and a badge with two tails under her right ear.
- **Anime:** Introduced in season 1 as a member of Team Rigil, and a friend of Special Week.
- **On the track:** A Mile and Long runner who works as a Pace Chaser or Late Surger.

**Unique skill (per Game8):** activates when she is 3rd or lower and has overtaken another runner during the final straight. It is easy to trigger for a Late Surger and can help secure a win in the last moments. Game8 recommends her for the Mile team in Team Trials, and maxing Potential unlocks the Mile skill On Your Left!. As a 2-star character she is easy to get from the gacha.
