## Overview

Mejiro Palmer is an Umamusume from the distinguished Mejiro lineage featured in the Umamusume: Pretty Derby franchise. She is known for her friendly and tactful nature, which makes her well-liked, though she struggles with feelings of inferiority compared to her family members. Her close friendship with Daitaku Helios leads her to adopt his slang despite often being confused by it.

## Background

- **Origins:** Belongs to the prestigious Mejiro family.
- **Personality:** Friendly and tactful, popular among peers, yet harbors an inferiority complex toward her relatives.
