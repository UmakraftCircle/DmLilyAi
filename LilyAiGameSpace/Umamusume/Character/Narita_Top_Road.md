## Overview

Narita Top Road is the central figure of the "Road to the Top" storyline in the Umamusume franchise. She serves as class president, maintains a friendly rivalry with Admire Vega and T.M. Opera O, and strives to capture the Triple Crown alongside her trainer Okita.

- **Team:** Collaborates with trainer Okita in pursuit of the Triple Crown.
