## Overview

Nice Nature is a character in the Umamusume: Pretty Derby franchise who competes as a racehorse girl. She is portrayed as a determined competitor who keeps aiming for victory despite repeatedly finishing third. Her experiences on the track have made her somewhat cynical and hesitant to accept high expectations, yet she remains a supportive friend and mentor to other characters.

## Background

- **Team:** Member of Team Canopus during the series' second season.
- **Personality:** Displays cynicism and pessimism, often avoiding lofty expectations, while also acting as a close friend and mentor to Kitasan Black.
- **On the track:** Acts as both a friend and rival to Tokai Teio and continues to strive for wins after multiple third‑place results.
